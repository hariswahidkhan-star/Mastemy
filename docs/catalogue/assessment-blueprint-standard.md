# Assessment Blueprint Standard (static)

1. **20% rule.** Assessment time (quizzes, module tests, drills and mocks) equals 20% of `est_learner_hours`. The validator enforces this per course, and per curriculum file for courses that have a full curriculum.
2. **Formats.** Only single-answer MCQ (4 options) and multiple-response ("choose N" or "choose all that apply", with N stated) are used. Drag-and-drop, hot-spot, simulations, essays and speaking are not delivered. If the official exam uses them, the course states this in `assessment_note` and covers the underlying knowledge with MCQ/MR.
3. **Rationale for every option.** Every option, correct or not, carries a rationale that explains why it is right or wrong. Items without full rationales cannot be published.
4. **Full-length mocks.** Every certification-prep course has at least 3 independent full-length mock forms (A/B/C) with no shared items. Each mock matches the official question count and time limit **where these were verified against an official source**. Otherwise the length is marked "DESIGN ASSUMPTION" in the curriculum and must be confirmed before production.
5. **Blueprint weighting.** Mock items are allocated to official domains at the midpoint of the published weighting range, and every mock form totals exactly the mock length.
6. **Item tagging.** Each item is tagged with its course_id, official domain, lesson ID, cognitive level (recall / apply / analyse) and content_version.
7. **Originality.** Items must be original. Recalled ("brain dump") or leaked exam content is forbidden.
8. **Review.** An item moves from draft to SME-reviewed and then to approved. Any change to the official syllabus triggers a re-review of the affected items.
9. **Scoring.** Mastemy reports percentage correct and per-domain mastery. It never claims to predict an official scaled score.
