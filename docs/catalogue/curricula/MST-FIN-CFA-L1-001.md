## Learning outcomes

1. Apply the CFA Institute Code and Standards to ethical dilemmas
2. Use quantitative, economic and financial-statement tools to analyse issuers
3. Value equity, fixed-income and derivative instruments at a foundational level
4. Describe alternative investments and portfolio-management principles

## Sample MCQs (original items, illustrative)

**Q1.** A research analyst gets a valuable gift from a company she covers, and it could reasonably compromise her independence. Under the Standards she should:

- A. Accept it and disclose it only if asked   
  _Rationale:_ Gifts that could compromise independence must be refused, or disclosed and approved as the Standards require.
- B. Decline the gift **(correct)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: under Independence and Objectivity, members must not accept gifts that could reasonably compromise independence.
- C. Accept it as long as the research rating stays the same   
  _Rationale:_ The test is whether the gift could reasonably compromise independence, not what actually happens to the rating.
- D. Pass it on to her employer   
  _Rationale:_ Giving it to the employer does not remove the conflict.

**Q2.** All else equal, which bond has the highest Macaulay duration?

- A. A 5-year 8% coupon bond   
  _Rationale:_ A shorter maturity and a higher coupon both lower duration.
- B. A 10-year 8% coupon bond   
  _Rationale:_ A higher coupon lowers duration compared with a zero.
- C. A 10-year zero-coupon bond **(correct)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a zero's duration equals its maturity, the highest of these options.
- D. A 10-year 4% coupon bond   
  _Rationale:_ Coupons pull duration below maturity.

**Q3.** A 1-year call and put on the same non-dividend stock share a strike of 100. The stock is 100 and the risk-free rate is 5%. Put-call parity implies c - p is about:

- A. 0   
  _Rationale:_ That ignores the present value of the strike.
- B. 4.76 **(correct)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: c - p = S - K/(1+r)^T = 100 - 100/1.05 = 4.76.
- C. 5.00   
  _Rationale:_ This uses K x r instead of discounting the strike.
- D. -4.76   
  _Rationale:_ The sign is reversed. With S equal to K, calls are worth more than puts.
