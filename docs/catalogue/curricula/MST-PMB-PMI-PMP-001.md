## Learning outcomes

1. Lead and develop high-performing project teams across predictive, agile and hybrid approaches
2. Plan, execute and control scope, schedule, cost, quality, risk and procurement
3. Connect projects to organisational strategy, value delivery and compliance
4. Choose a delivery approach that fits the context

## Sample MCQs (original items, illustrative)

**Q1.** Two senior team members keep clashing over technical design and it is delaying the work. What should the project manager do FIRST?

- A. Escalate to the sponsor   
  _Rationale:_ Escalating straight away skips the PM's own job of resolving team conflict.
- B. Meet both members to understand the root cause and work together on a solution **(correct)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: collaborating / problem-solving deals with the cause and is the preferred first approach.
- C. Assign the design decision to the more senior member   
  _Rationale:_ Forcing a result may resolve things in the short term but damages team cohesion.
- D. Remove one member from the project   
  _Rationale:_ This is a last resort and premature here.

**Q2.** In an adaptive project, who is mainly responsible for maximising product value by ordering the backlog?

- A. Project sponsor   
  _Rationale:_ The sponsor funds the work and champions it, but does not order the backlog.
- B. Product owner **(correct)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the product owner orders the backlog to maximise value.
- C. Scrum master / team facilitator   
  _Rationale:_ The facilitator serves the team and removes impediments.
- D. Developers   
  _Rationale:_ Developers estimate and deliver items. They do not own backlog order.

**Q3.** A new regulation affects the project's deliverables halfway through. Which domain does handling this mainly fall under?

- A. People   
  _Rationale:_ This is not mainly a team or leadership issue.
- B. Process   
  _Rationale:_ Process covers the change control that follows, but recognising external compliance impacts belongs to Business Environment.
- C. Business Environment **(correct)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: compliance and changes in the external environment sit in Business Environment.
- D. None - regulations are out of PMP scope   
  _Rationale:_ Compliance is explicitly within PMP scope.
