## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0098-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Privacy by design means that privacy is:

- A. Built into systems and processes from the outset **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: privacy by design embeds privacy from the start, not as an afterthought.
- B. Added only after a breach occurs  
  _Rationale:_ Reactive addition is the opposite of privacy by design.
- C. The sole responsibility of the marketing team  
  _Rationale:_ Privacy is a cross-functional, not marketing-only, concern.
- D. Optional for personal data  
  _Rationale:_ Privacy by design applies to personal-data processing.

**MST-0098-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Data minimization as a privacy principle requires that organizations:

- A. Collect only the personal data necessary for a stated purpose **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: data minimization limits collection to what is necessary.
- B. Collect as much data as technically possible  
  _Rationale:_ That contradicts minimization.
- C. Keep all data forever  
  _Rationale:_ Retention limits are part of minimization, not indefinite storage.
- D. Share data with any third party on request  
  _Rationale:_ Uncontrolled sharing violates privacy principles.

**MST-0098-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO techniques that help protect personal data during processing.

- A. Pseudonymization of identifiers **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: pseudonymization reduces the linkability of data to individuals.
- B. Encryption of data at rest and in transit **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: encryption protects data confidentiality.
- C. Publishing raw identifiers openly  
  _Rationale:_ Publishing identifiers increases privacy risk.
- D. Granting all employees access to all data  
  _Rationale:_ Over-broad access violates least-privilege.
- E. Disabling all access logging  
  _Rationale:_ Removing logging weakens accountability and security.
