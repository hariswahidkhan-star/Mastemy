## Learning outcomes

1. Explain data-protection principles and key GDPR concepts
2. Identify roles, lawful bases and data-subject rights
3. Apply data-protection-by-design and minimisation
4. Describe handling of breaches and international transfers at a high level
5. Recognise accountability and record-keeping obligations

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1675-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What does the data-minimisation principle require?

- A. Collecting only the personal data necessary for the stated purpose **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: minimisation limits data to what the purpose needs.
- B. Collecting as much data as possible for future use  
  _Rationale:_ That is the opposite of minimisation.
- C. Keeping all data indefinitely  
  _Rationale:_ Retention should be limited, not indefinite.
- D. Never collecting any personal data  
  _Rationale:_ Minimisation limits, it does not prohibit, collection.

**MST-1675-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are rights data subjects commonly have under GDPR? (Select TWO.)

- A. The right of access to their personal data **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: individuals can request access to their data.
- B. The right to erasure in certain circumstances **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: erasure applies under defined conditions.
- C. The right to demand a company never keep any records  
  _Rationale:_ Organisations may need to retain certain records lawfully.
- D. The right to set national law  
  _Rationale:_ Individuals do not legislate through data requests.

**MST-1675-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Is consent always the required lawful basis for processing personal data?

- A. No; several lawful bases exist and consent is only one of them **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: contract, legal obligation and legitimate interests are among the alternatives.
- B. Yes; consent is always mandatory  
  _Rationale:_ Other lawful bases can apply depending on the purpose.
- C. No; a lawful basis is never needed  
  _Rationale:_ A lawful basis is required for processing.
- D. Yes; but only for public bodies  
  _Rationale:_ The range of lawful bases is not limited this way.
