## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the regulatory framework governing human-subjects research
2. Apply informed consent, IRB and protocol requirements to studies
3. Evaluate research billing, conflicts of interest and data integrity
4. Analyse research compliance programme oversight, auditing and misconduct response

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Integrative case

A study procedure is accidentally billed to a patient's insurer while a protocol amendment awaits IRB approval: untangle the billing error, determine the correct coverage analysis, assess the protocol deviation, and tighten the research compliance programme's controls.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0115-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An investigator wants to change a study's eligibility criteria to enrol faster. Before implementing the change, the investigator must usually:

- A. Obtain IRB approval of the protocol amendment first **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: substantive protocol changes require prospective IRB approval.
- B. Implement immediately and inform the IRB next year  
  _Rationale:_ Substantive changes require approval before implementation.
- C. Ask the study sponsor's marketing team  
  _Rationale:_ Marketing has no role in protocol approval.
- D. Change it only if no one notices  
  _Rationale:_ Compliance is not optional based on detection.

**MST-0115-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A procedure performed solely for research purposes is billed to the participant's insurer. This most likely indicates a problem with:

- A. The study's coverage analysis and research billing **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: research-only procedures generally should not be billed to insurers; this is a billing-compliance issue.
- B. The consent form's font size  
  _Rationale:_ Font size is unrelated to billing compliance.
- C. The IRB's meeting schedule  
  _Rationale:_ The meeting schedule does not cause mis-billing.
- D. The participant's parking fee  
  _Rationale:_ Parking is irrelevant to the billing error.

**MST-0115-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO elements must a valid informed-consent process include? (Select TWO)

- A. A description of the study's risks and benefits **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: risks and benefits are required consent elements.
- B. A statement that participation is voluntary **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: voluntariness is a required element of consent.
- C. A guarantee the study will succeed  
  _Rationale:_ No study can be guaranteed; such a claim is improper.
- D. The investigator's salary  
  _Rationale:_ Salary is not a required consent element.
- E. A requirement to waive all legal rights  
  _Rationale:_ Consent cannot require waiving legal rights.
