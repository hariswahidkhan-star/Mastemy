## Learning outcomes

1. Work efficiently in Canva as a professional
2. Apply brand controls and templates
3. Collaborate, export and publish at quality

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A professional must produce on-brand marketing assets efficiently in Canva. They must work at correct dimensions, apply a Brand Kit and reusable templates, and collaborate and export at quality, defending brand controls and resizing over rebuilding each asset.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1878-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Resizing a template in Canva (rather than rebuilding) mainly:

- A. Adapts a design to several formats quickly and consistently **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: resizing reuses the design across channels.
- B. Improves the microphone audio  
  _Rationale:_ Unrelated to Canva export.
- C. Deletes the Brand Kit  
  _Rationale:_ Resizing does not remove the Brand Kit.
- D. Lowers the design to print-only  
  _Rationale:_ Not what resizing does.

**MST-1878-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO keep professional Canva output consistent and efficient? (Select TWO.)

- A. Applying a shared Brand Kit across designs **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the Brand Kit enforces consistency.
- B. Using reusable templates for recurring formats **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: templates save time and hold the brand.
- C. Overriding brand colours on each new design  
  _Rationale:_ That breaks consistency.
- D. Exporting print files at the lowest resolution  
  _Rationale:_ Low-res fails in print.

**MST-1878-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Setting roles and approvals on a shared Canva design helps by:

- A. Controlling who can edit and ensuring brand review before publishing **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: roles and approvals protect quality and brand.
- B. Increasing the export resolution automatically  
  _Rationale:_ Roles do not change resolution.
- C. Writing the marketing strategy  
  _Rationale:_ Unrelated to sharing roles.
- D. Recording the voiceover  
  _Rationale:_ Not a Canva collaboration feature.
