## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0003-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) The accounting equation is best expressed as:

- A. Assets = Liabilities + Equity **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: assets are financed by liabilities and equity.
- B. Assets = Liabilities - Equity  
  _Rationale:_ Equity is added, not subtracted.
- C. Assets + Equity = Liabilities  
  _Rationale:_ This rearrangement is incorrect.
- D. Assets = Revenue - Expenses  
  _Rationale:_ That describes profit, not the accounting equation.

**MST-0003-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A business receives cash from a credit customer settling their account. What is the double entry?

- A. Debit cash, credit receivables **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: cash increases (debit) and the receivable is cleared (credit).
- B. Debit receivables, credit cash  
  _Rationale:_ This reverses the correct entry.
- C. Debit cash, credit sales  
  _Rationale:_ The sale was already recorded when made on credit.
- D. Debit sales, credit cash  
  _Rationale:_ Sales are not reduced by a customer payment.

**MST-0003-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO items that appear in a statement of financial position.

- A. Trade payables **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: trade payables are a liability shown in the statement of financial position.
- B. Property, plant and equipment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: PPE is a non-current asset shown in the statement of financial position.
- C. Cost of sales  
  _Rationale:_ Cost of sales is in the statement of profit or loss.
- D. Revenue  
  _Rationale:_ Revenue is in the statement of profit or loss.
- E. Dividends paid  
  _Rationale:_ Dividends paid are shown in the statement of changes in equity / cash flows.
