## Learning outcomes

1. Apply health-and-safety management-system and risk-assessment knowledge
2. Analyse general workplace, fire and physical hazard controls
3. Evaluate chemical, biological and psychosocial hazard controls
4. Integrate hazard knowledge into a workplace risk-assessment plan

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0335-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) The three broad reasons commonly given for managing health and safety are:

- A. Moral, legal and financial **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the standard reasons are moral, legal and financial.
- B. Moral, political and social  
  _Rationale:_ Political and social are not the standard trio.
- C. Legal, technical and commercial  
  _Rationale:_ Technical and commercial are not the standard reasons.
- D. Ethical, ecological and economic  
  _Rationale:_ Ecological is not part of the standard trio.

**MST-0335-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) The first step of a general risk assessment is to:

- A. Identify the hazards **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a risk assessment begins by identifying hazards.
- B. Review the assessment  
  _Rationale:_ Review is the final step, not the first.
- C. Record the significant findings  
  _Rationale:_ Recording comes after evaluating risk.
- D. Select personal protective equipment  
  _Rationale:_ PPE selection is a control decision made later.

**MST-0335-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) To control exposure to a hazardous solvent, select TWO measures that rank above personal protective equipment.

- A. Substitute a less hazardous solvent **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: substitution ranks high and removes much of the hazard.
- B. Install local exhaust ventilation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: engineering control (LEV) ranks above PPE.
- C. Issue respirators to all staff  
  _Rationale:_ Respirators are PPE, the lowest control tier.
- D. Put up a hazard warning sign  
  _Rationale:_ Signage is a weak administrative control.
- E. Ask staff to wash hands more often  
  _Rationale:_ Hygiene advice is administrative and ranks below engineering controls.
