## Learning outcomes

1. Explain core AI concepts in plain language
2. Describe the main capabilities of today's AI systems
3. Identify the practical limits and risks of AI
4. Match a task to whether AI is a reasonable fit
5. Communicate AI capability honestly to non-experts

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1305-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What distinguishes generative AI from predictive AI?

- A. Generative AI produces new content; predictive AI estimates a value or label **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: generation versus estimation is the key difference.
- B. Generative AI never makes mistakes  
  _Rationale:_ Both kinds can err.
- C. Predictive AI cannot use data  
  _Rationale:_ Predictive AI is built from data.
- D. They are two names for the same thing  
  _Rationale:_ They serve different purposes.

**MST-1305-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are genuine limits of current AI? (Select TWO.)

- A. It can produce confident but false statements **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: hallucination is a real limit.
- B. It may miss context a human would notice **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: nuance and context are weak spots.
- C. It can guarantee exact answers every time  
  _Rationale:_ Probabilistic systems cannot guarantee exactness.
- D. It fully understands human intent  
  _Rationale:_ It does not truly understand intent.

**MST-1305-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which task is the best fit for AI?

- A. Drafting a first version of routine text for human review **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: drafting with review suits AI well.
- B. Signing off a legal contract with no human check  
  _Rationale:_ High-stakes sign-off needs human judgement.
- C. Guaranteeing a medical diagnosis unaided  
  _Rationale:_ Unaided high-stakes diagnosis is inappropriate.
- D. Replacing all human judgement in hiring  
  _Rationale:_ Hiring needs human oversight and fairness checks.
