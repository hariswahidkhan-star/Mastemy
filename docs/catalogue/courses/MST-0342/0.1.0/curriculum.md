## Learning outcomes

1. Apply coordinated care within the practical nurse scope
2. Maintain safety and infection control
3. Support health promotion and maintenance
4. Support psychosocial integrity
5. Provide basic care and comfort
6. Assist with pharmacological therapies within scope
7. Reduce risk potential and support physiological adaptation

> Outcomes are DESIGN ASSUMPTIONS derived without a verified official outline; confirm against the issuer's exam page at blueprint review.

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: NCLEX includes alternate and next-generation clinical-judgment item formats; Mastemy uses MCQ/MR only and does not replicate clinical performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0342-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A practical nurse notices a client's blood pressure has dropped sharply and the client is dizzy. What is the priority action?

- A. Protect the client from falling and notify the supervising RN **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ensure safety and escalate, since assessment and orders exceed the practical-nurse scope here.
- B. Independently prescribe a fluid bolus  
  _Rationale:_ Prescribing is outside the practical-nurse scope.
- C. Document and reassess at the next scheduled round  
  _Rationale:_ An acute change needs immediate action, not delay.
- D. Reassure the client it is nothing to worry about  
  _Rationale:_ False reassurance delays needed escalation.

**MST-0342-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which task is appropriate to delegate to unlicensed assistive personnel?

- A. Assisting a stable client with bathing **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: routine hygiene for a stable client is within assistive-personnel scope.
- B. Performing the initial admission assessment  
  _Rationale:_ Initial assessment is a licensed responsibility.
- C. Administering an intravenous medication  
  _Rationale:_ IV medication is outside assistive-personnel scope.
- D. Developing the client's care plan  
  _Rationale:_ Care planning is a licensed nursing responsibility.

**MST-0342-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO observations should a practical nurse report promptly to the supervising RN?

- A. A new onset of confusion in a previously alert client **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an acute mental-status change needs prompt escalation.
- B. A wound with new purulent drainage and spreading redness **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: signs of infection require escalation.
- C. A client sleeping comfortably through the night  
  _Rationale:_ Normal rest does not require escalation.
- D. A client eating a full breakfast  
  _Rationale:_ Expected intake needs no escalation.
- E. Stable vital signs within the client's baseline  
  _Rationale:_ Baseline stable signs do not require reporting.
