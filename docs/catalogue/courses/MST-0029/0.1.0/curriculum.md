## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0029-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under absorption costing, if production exceeds sales in a period, reported profit compared with marginal costing will generally be:

- A. Higher, because fixed overhead is carried forward in closing inventory **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: absorption costing defers fixed overhead in inventory, raising profit when inventory increases.
- B. Lower, because fixed overhead is written off immediately  
  _Rationale:_ Writing off fixed overhead immediately is marginal costing, which gives lower profit here.
- C. The same, because inventory valuation does not affect profit  
  _Rationale:_ Inventory valuation differs between the methods and does affect profit.
- D. Always zero  
  _Rationale:_ Profit is not forced to zero by the costing method.

**MST-0029-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) In short-term limiting-factor decision making, products should be ranked by:

- A. Contribution per unit of the limiting factor **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ranking by contribution per unit of the scarce resource maximises total contribution.
- B. Total sales revenue per unit  
  _Rationale:_ Revenue ignores variable costs and the scarce resource constraint.
- C. Selling price alone  
  _Rationale:_ Price alone ignores cost and the limiting factor.
- D. Fixed cost per unit  
  _Rationale:_ Fixed cost per unit is not the basis for short-term ranking.

**MST-0029-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO valid approaches to dealing with risk and uncertainty in short-term decisions.

- A. Expected value analysis using outcome probabilities **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: expected values summarise risky outcomes by probability weighting.
- B. Maximin or maximax decision rules **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: these rules reflect pessimistic or optimistic attitudes to uncertainty.
- C. Assuming the future will exactly equal last year  
  _Rationale:_ Assuming no change ignores uncertainty rather than dealing with it.
- D. Using only sunk costs in the analysis  
  _Rationale:_ Sunk costs are irrelevant to decisions.
- E. Deleting all unfavourable outcomes from the model  
  _Rationale:_ Removing bad outcomes distorts rather than addresses risk.
