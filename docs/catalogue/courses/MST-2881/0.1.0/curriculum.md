## Learning outcomes

1. Develop rounded characters with clear motivations
2. Build a plot using conflict, rising action and resolution
3. Choose an effective narrative point of view
4. Use setting and atmosphere to support the story
5. Craft engaging openings and satisfying endings
6. Revise narrative writing for pace and impact

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2881-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In a story, what is 'conflict'?

- A. The problem or struggle the character must face **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: conflict is the central struggle driving the plot.
- B. The title written at the top  
  _Rationale:_ A title is not conflict.
- C. A list of characters' names  
  _Rationale:_ Naming characters is not conflict.
- D. The page number  
  _Rationale:_ Page numbers are unrelated to conflict.

**MST-2881-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which opening 'hooks' the reader most effectively?

- A. The floorboard creaked, and Mia froze mid-step. **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it drops us into tense action and raises questions.
- B. This is a story about a girl called Mia.  
  _Rationale:_ This merely announces the story with no tension.
- C. Hello, I will now tell you a tale.  
  _Rationale:_ This stalls before anything happens.
- D. Mia is eleven years old and has brown hair.  
  _Rationale:_ A plain fact-list is not an engaging hook.

**MST-2881-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO techniques help you 'show' a character rather than just 'tell'? (Select TWO.)

- A. Describe what the character does **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: actions reveal character.
- B. Use the character's own dialogue **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: speech shows personality.
- C. State 'she was kind' and move on  
  _Rationale:_ That is telling, not showing.
- D. List the character's height and weight  
  _Rationale:_ A measurements list does not reveal personality.
