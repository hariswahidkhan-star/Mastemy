## Learning outcomes

1. Explain that sounds are made by objects vibrating
2. Describe how sound travels through materials to reach our ears
3. Relate loudness to the size of a vibration and pitch to how fast it vibrates
4. Investigate how to make sounds louder, quieter, higher or lower
5. Describe simply how the ear detects sound
6. Protect hearing by keeping loud sounds away from ears

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance. Any experiment ideas are optional and require adult supervision; they are not assessed.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2846-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is happening whenever you hear a sound?

- A. Something is vibrating **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: all sounds come from vibrating objects.
- B. Something is glowing  
  _Rationale:_ Glowing makes light, not sound.
- C. The air is getting colder  
  _Rationale:_ Temperature change does not make the sound.
- D. Nothing needs to move  
  _Rationale:_ Sound always comes from a vibration, which is movement.

**MST-2846-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO changes would make a plucked rubber band sound HIGHER in pitch? (Select TWO.)

- A. Making the band shorter **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a shorter band vibrates faster, giving a higher pitch.
- B. Making the band tighter **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a tighter band vibrates faster, raising the pitch.
- C. Plucking it more gently  
  _Rationale:_ Plucking gently changes loudness, not pitch.
- D. Making the band longer  
  _Rationale:_ A longer band vibrates slower, giving a lower pitch.

**MST-2846-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why does a voice sound quieter the further away the person is?

- A. The sound spreads out and loses energy as it travels **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: sound energy spreads and weakens with distance.
- B. The person is vibrating less far away  
  _Rationale:_ Their vibration is the same; the sound just spreads out.
- C. Sound cannot travel more than a few steps  
  _Rationale:_ Sound travels far, just getting quieter.
- D. Distance changes the pitch to silence  
  _Rationale:_ Distance affects loudness, not pitch to silence.
