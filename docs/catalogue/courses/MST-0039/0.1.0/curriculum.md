## Learning outcomes

1. Interpret a strategic-level case scenario and integrate strategy, risk and financial-strategy knowledge
2. Apply analysis and decision techniques to strategic-case requirements
3. Plan and construct answers to strategic-case tasks under timed conditions

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: This course assesses knowledge and applied reasoning via single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only. The official examination is an integrated written case study responding to an unseen scenario, which this MCQ/MR format does not reproduce; see the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0039-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) At strategic level, a board-facing case answer is strongest when it:

- A. Integrates strategy, risk and financial-strategy issues into clear recommendations **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: strategic cases reward integrated, decision-focused advice to the board.
- B. Lists strategic models without applying them  
  _Rationale:_ Unapplied models earn little; application to the scenario earns marks.
- C. Focuses only on detailed bookkeeping entries  
  _Rationale:_ Strategic level is about direction and decisions, not bookkeeping detail.
- D. Avoids making any recommendation  
  _Rationale:_ A board expects a reasoned recommendation.

**MST-0039-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A strategic case presents an acquisition opportunity with strategic fit but financing strain. The best structured response is to:

- A. Weigh the strategic benefits against the financing risk and give a conditional recommendation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: balancing benefit and financing risk with conditions is the strongest board-level answer.
- B. Recommend the acquisition regardless of financing  
  _Rationale:_ Ignoring financing strain gives unbalanced advice.
- C. Reject it purely because financing is tight  
  _Rationale:_ Rejecting on financing alone ignores the strategic fit and alternatives.
- D. Leave the decision entirely to shareholders without analysis  
  _Rationale:_ The case requires the finance leader's reasoned analysis.

**MST-0039-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO features of an effective strategic-level case response under time pressure.

- A. Prioritising tasks by allocated marks **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: aligning effort with marks maximises the score.
- B. Grounding recommendations in the scenario's financial and strategic data **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: scenario-grounded reasoning earns application marks.
- C. Reproducing the pre-seen material verbatim  
  _Rationale:_ Copying pre-seen material adds no analysis.
- D. Ignoring ethical and governance dimensions  
  _Rationale:_ Ethics and governance are usually examined.
- E. Leaving the highest-weighted task unanswered  
  _Rationale:_ Skipping high-mark tasks loses the most marks.
