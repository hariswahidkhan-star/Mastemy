## Module and lesson plan

### M01 — Foundations of critical thinking  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 26%)_

- M01L01 — What critical thinking is; claims, reasons and conclusions (120 min)
- M01L02 — Arguments versus assertions and the role of standards (120 min)
- Worked applications: Break a short argument into its claim, reasons and conclusion; Distinguish an argument from an unsupported assertion
- Common misconception addressed: Treating a confidently stated opinion as a proven fact

### M02 — Evidence and reasoning  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 26%)_

- M02L01 — Evaluating sources, relevance and sufficiency of evidence (120 min)
- M02L02 — Deductive and inductive reasoning; correlation versus causation (120 min)
- Worked applications: Assess whether cited evidence actually supports the conclusion; Explain why a correlation in data need not imply causation
- Common misconception addressed: Assuming that because two things move together one causes the other

### M03 — Assumptions, bias and fallacies  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 24%)_

- M03L01 — Surfacing hidden assumptions and cognitive biases (120 min)
- M03L02 — Common logical fallacies and how to spot them (120 min)
- Worked applications: Identify an unstated assumption a recommendation depends on; Name the fallacy in a persuasive but flawed argument
- Common misconception addressed: Believing that being smart makes someone immune to bias

### M04 — Evidence-based decisions  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 24%)_

- M04L01 — Structured evaluation and weighing options (120 min)
- M04L02 — Communicating a reasoned, defensible recommendation (120 min)
- Worked applications: Weigh options against explicit criteria and evidence; Write a recommendation that states its reasoning and its limitations
- Common misconception addressed: Deciding first and then selecting only the evidence that fits

## Integrative case

Evaluate a business proposal critically: clarify the claim and its reasoning, test the quality and relevance of the evidence, surface assumptions and biases, spot fallacies, and reach a defensible, evidence-based recommendation.
