## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0001-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A sole trader has unlimited liability for the debts of the business. What does this mean?

- A. The owner's personal assets can be used to settle business debts **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: with unlimited liability there is no legal separation, so personal assets are exposed.
- B. The business can borrow any amount it wishes  
  _Rationale:_ Liability concerns responsibility for debts, not borrowing capacity.
- C. Liability is capped at the amount of capital introduced  
  _Rationale:_ That describes limited liability, not unlimited liability.
- D. Creditors can only claim against business bank accounts  
  _Rationale:_ Creditors are not restricted to business accounts under unlimited liability.

**MST-0001-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which of the following is a primary purpose of an organisation's internal control system?

- A. To safeguard assets and ensure the reliability of records **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: safeguarding assets and reliable records are core objectives of internal control.
- B. To guarantee the business makes a profit  
  _Rationale:_ No control system can guarantee profitability.
- C. To replace the need for external audit  
  _Rationale:_ Internal control supports, but does not replace, external audit.
- D. To set the organisation's dividend policy  
  _Rationale:_ Dividend policy is a financing decision, not an internal-control objective.

**MST-0001-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO features that distinguish a limited company from a sole tradership.

- A. Separate legal personality **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a company is a separate legal person distinct from its owners.
- B. Limited liability for shareholders **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: shareholders' liability is limited to the amount unpaid on their shares.
- C. No requirement to keep accounting records  
  _Rationale:_ Companies must keep accounting records; this is not a distinguishing benefit.
- D. Profits are automatically exempt from tax  
  _Rationale:_ Company profits are subject to tax; they are not exempt.
- E. Owners cannot be employees of the business  
  _Rationale:_ Owners can also be employees/directors of a company.
