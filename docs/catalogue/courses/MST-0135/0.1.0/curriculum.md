## Learning outcomes

1. Describe programme management principles, governance themes and the transformational flow
2. Apply vision, blueprint and benefits concepts to a programme
3. Evaluate governance themes such as risk, quality and stakeholder engagement
4. Analyse how tranches deliver capability and realise benefits

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0135-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A programme delivers a new system (an output) but usage stays low and expected savings do not appear. This illustrates the difference between:

- A. Outputs delivered and benefits realised **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: delivering outputs does not guarantee the outcomes and benefits.
- B. Risk and issue  
  _Rationale:_ This scenario is about benefits, not risk vs issue.
- C. Vision and mission  
  _Rationale:_ The gap here is outputs vs benefits, not vision vs mission.
- D. Cost and schedule  
  _Rationale:_ The point is realisation, not cost/schedule.

**MST-0135-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) What best distinguishes a programme from a large project?

- A. A programme coordinates related projects and change to realise strategic benefits **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: programmes align projects and business change to benefits and strategy.
- B. A programme is simply a project with more people  
  _Rationale:_ Scale alone does not make a programme.
- C. A programme never has governance  
  _Rationale:_ Programmes have strong governance.
- D. A programme only delivers documents  
  _Rationale:_ Programmes deliver capability and benefits.

**MST-0135-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO artefacts most directly define where a programme is going and how it will know it got there? (Select TWO)

- A. The vision statement **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the vision describes the desired future state.
- B. The benefits map/profiles **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: benefits define measurable success.
- C. The office seating plan  
  _Rationale:_ Seating is irrelevant to programme direction.
- D. The staff holiday calendar  
  _Rationale:_ The holiday calendar does not define direction.
- E. The cafeteria menu  
  _Rationale:_ Irrelevant to programme outcomes.
