## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the role of cost estimating and quantity surveying across a project
2. Take off quantities from drawings using a consistent method
3. Build up unit rates from labour, plant, material and overhead
4. Prepare and interpret a bill of quantities and a cost plan
5. Apply contingency, inflation and risk allowances honestly
6. Value work done, manage variations and report cost against budget

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2203-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An early concept-stage estimate is presented as a single exact figure with no range. What is the main problem?

- A. It overstates certainty; early estimates carry wide ranges and should be communicated as such **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: concept estimates are approximate and should show a range.
- B. Estimates should never use numbers  
  _Rationale:_ Estimates do use numbers; the issue is implied false precision.
- C. Single figures are illegal  
  _Rationale:_ There is no such legal rule; the issue is honesty about uncertainty.
- D. Ranges are only for final accounts  
  _Rationale:_ Ranges are most important early, when uncertainty is highest.

**MST-2203-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO components are typically built into a unit rate? (Select TWO.)

- A. Labour cost for the task **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: labour is a core component of a unit rate.
- B. Material cost for the task **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: materials are a core component of a unit rate.
- C. The client's personal salary  
  _Rationale:_ The client's salary is not part of a construction unit rate.
- D. The architect's holiday plans  
  _Rationale:_ This is irrelevant to rate build-up.

**MST-2203-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) What does an interim valuation determine during construction?

- A. The value of work properly done to date, as a basis for an interim payment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: interim valuations value completed work for payment.
- B. The final selling price of the finished building  
  _Rationale:_ That is a market value, not an interim valuation.
- C. The contractor's total future profit  
  _Rationale:_ It values work to date, not total future profit.
- D. The weather forecast for the next pour  
  _Rationale:_ Valuations are financial, not meteorological.
