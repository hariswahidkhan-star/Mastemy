## Learning outcomes

1. Describe capital markets, participants and the economic factors that affect them
2. Compare securities products (equity, debt, packaged, options and others) and their risks
3. Explain trading mechanics, customer accounts and prohibited activities
4. Describe the regulatory framework, SRO rules and registration requirements

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0064-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An investor is most concerned about interest-rate risk. Which bond is MOST exposed to it, all else equal?

- A. A 2-year Treasury note  
  _Rationale:_ Shorter maturities have lower interest-rate (duration) risk.
- B. A 30-year Treasury bond **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: longer maturity means higher duration and the greatest price sensitivity to rate changes.
- C. A 90-day Treasury bill  
  _Rationale:_ Very short maturities have minimal interest-rate risk.
- D. A money market instrument  
  _Rationale:_ Money market instruments have negligible interest-rate risk.

**MST-0064-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) For a regular-way equity trade, settlement currently occurs on which day relative to the trade date?

- A. Trade date (T+0)  
  _Rationale:_ Regular-way equities do not settle same day.
- B. One business day after trade (T+1) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: regular-way equity trades settle one business day after the trade date under the current standard.
- C. Two business days after trade (T+2)  
  _Rationale:_ T+2 was the prior standard before the move to T+1.
- D. Three business days after trade (T+3)  
  _Rationale:_ T+3 is an outdated settlement cycle.

**MST-0064-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO activities are prohibited practices under securities rules? (Select TWO)

- A. Churning a customer account to generate commissions **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: excessive trading to generate commissions (churning) is prohibited.
- B. Front-running a large customer order **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: trading ahead of a customer order (front-running) is prohibited.
- C. Recommending a diversified portfolio  
  _Rationale:_ Suitable diversification is a legitimate recommendation.
- D. Disclosing material risks to a client  
  _Rationale:_ Disclosure is required, not prohibited.
- E. Executing a client's unsolicited order  
  _Rationale:_ Executing an unsolicited order is permitted.
