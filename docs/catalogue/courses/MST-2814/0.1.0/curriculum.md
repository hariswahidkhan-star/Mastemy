## Learning outcomes

1. Understand multiplication as repeated addition and equal groups
2. Recall multiplication facts for common tables
3. Understand division as sharing and as grouping
4. Use the link between multiplication and division fact families
5. Solve word problems involving multiplication and division
6. Interpret and handle remainders in simple problems

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2814-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is 6 x 4?

- A. 24 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 6 groups of 4 is 24, the same as 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4.
- B. 10  
  _Rationale:_ That is 6 + 4; multiplication means repeated addition of equal groups.
- C. 18  
  _Rationale:_ That is 6 x 3; 6 x 4 is 24.
- D. 28  
  _Rationale:_ That is 7 x 4; 6 x 4 is 24.

**MST-2814-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO facts that belong to the same fact family as 3 x 8 = 24. (Select TWO.)

- A. 24 / 3 = 8 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: dividing the product by one factor gives the other factor.
- B. 8 x 3 = 24 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: multiplication can be done in either order for the same family.
- C. 3 x 7 = 21  
  _Rationale:_ This uses 7, not 8, so it is a different family.
- D. 24 / 6 = 4  
  _Rationale:_ 6 and 4 are not the factors in this family.

**MST-2814-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) 36 pencils are shared equally among 4 children. How many does each child get?

- A. 9 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 36 divided by 4 is 9, because 4 x 9 = 36.
- B. 8  
  _Rationale:_ 4 x 8 = 32, which is too few; the answer is 9.
- C. 40  
  _Rationale:_ That adds instead of sharing; sharing makes each part smaller.
- D. 32  
  _Rationale:_ 32 is 4 x 8; dividing 36 by 4 gives 9.
