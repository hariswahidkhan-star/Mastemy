## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the principles, ethics and history behind Good Clinical Practice
2. Describe roles, responsibilities and oversight in clinical trials
3. Apply requirements for consent, protocol compliance and data integrity
4. Describe safety reporting, monitoring and quality in trials

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every course outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Hands-on operation, the quality of tools and live outputs, and professional judgement are taught by video demonstration and model-answer analysis; the certificate assessment uses single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only and never requires uploads, recorded work, submitted code or peer review.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1778-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under GCP, informed consent is best described as:

- A. An ongoing process of giving information and confirming voluntary agreement, documented by signature **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: consent is a continuing process, with the signed form documenting it, not the whole of it.
- B. Simply obtaining the participant's signature on a form  
  _Rationale:_ The signature documents consent but is not the whole process, which must be informed and ongoing.
- C. A verbal agreement that need not be documented  
  _Rationale:_ GCP requires the consent process to be documented.
- D. The sponsor's decision to enrol a participant  
  _Rationale:_ Consent is the participant's voluntary decision, not the sponsor's.**MST-1778-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A trial participant is hospitalised due to an unexpected reaction to the study drug. This is most appropriately classified as a:

- A. Serious adverse event **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an event resulting in hospitalisation meets the seriousness criteria and is a serious adverse event.
- B. Protocol deviation  
  _Rationale:_ A protocol deviation is a departure from the protocol, not a medical event like hospitalisation.
- C. Routine finding that need not be recorded  
  _Rationale:_ Adverse events must be recorded; a serious one has specific reporting duties.
- D. Monitoring visit  
  _Rationale:_ A monitoring visit is an oversight activity, not a participant safety event.**MST-1778-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are among the ALCOA principles for trial data quality? (Select TWO)

- A. Attributable **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: data must be attributable to who recorded them and when.
- B. Legible **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: data must be legible and permanent.
- C. Anonymous  
  _Rationale:_ ALCOA requires data to be attributable, which is the opposite of anonymous recording.
- D. Approximate  
  _Rationale:_ ALCOA requires accuracy, not approximation.
