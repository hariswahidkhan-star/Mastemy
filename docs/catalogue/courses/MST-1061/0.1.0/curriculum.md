## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1061-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the core shift when moving from individual contributor to manager?

- A. Results now come largely through other people rather than your own output **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a manager's impact is achieved through the team, not solo delivery.
- B. You stop being accountable for any results  
  _Rationale:_ Managers remain accountable for team outcomes.
- C. Technical skill becomes completely irrelevant  
  _Rationale:_ Technical judgment still helps, even if it is no longer the main job.
- D. You should personally do all the hardest tasks yourself  
  _Rationale:_ Hoarding the hard work is a classic new-manager trap.

**MST-1061-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) You delegate a task but find yourself making every decision on it. This most likely means you:

- A. Delegated the task without delegating enough authority **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: effective delegation passes appropriate decision authority, not just the work.
- B. Chose the wrong person and should take it back  
  _Rationale:_ The issue is the authority given, not necessarily the person.
- C. Should stop delegating entirely  
  _Rationale:_ The fix is clearer delegation, not avoiding it.
- D. Need to add more approval steps  
  _Rationale:_ More approvals increase, not reduce, the micromanagement.

**MST-1061-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO practices make feedback more effective? (Select TWO)

- A. Giving it promptly and tied to specific behaviour **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: timely, specific feedback is easier to act on.
- B. Focusing on the behaviour and its impact rather than the person's character **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: behaviour-and-impact feedback is constructive and less defensive.
- C. Storing all feedback for a single annual review  
  _Rationale:_ Delayed, batched feedback loses relevance and impact.
- D. Keeping it vague so the person is not discouraged  
  _Rationale:_ Vague feedback gives nothing concrete to change.
