## Learning outcomes

1. Explain global HR strategy and the role of HR across multiple countries
2. Describe global talent acquisition, mobility and workforce management
3. Explain global total rewards, benefits and compliance differences
4. Describe global employee relations, culture and risk management

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0296-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which best describes a sound approach to global HR policy?

- A. Standardize where it adds value and localize where law or culture requires **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: effective global HR balances consistency with local adaptation.
- B. Apply the headquarters country's rules everywhere without change  
  _Rationale:_ Ignoring local law and culture creates legal and engagement risk.
- C. Let every site invent fully independent policies  
  _Rationale:_ No alignment loses the benefits of a global framework.
- D. Avoid written policy to stay flexible  
  _Rationale:_ Lack of policy increases inconsistency and risk.

**MST-0296-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) An employee is sent on a two-year assignment abroad. Which element is essential in the package?

- A. Mobility, tax and compliance support, not only a converted salary **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: international assignments require mobility and compliance support.
- B. Only a currency conversion of base pay  
  _Rationale:_ Salary conversion ignores assignment and compliance costs.
- C. A promise to ignore the host country's employment law  
  _Rationale:_ Host-country law still applies and cannot be ignored.
- D. No documentation to keep it flexible  
  _Rationale:_ Assignments need clear, documented terms.

**MST-0296-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO obligations that typically differ by country and must be checked locally.

- A. Statutory benefits and leave entitlements **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: statutory benefits vary widely by jurisdiction.
- B. Employee data-privacy requirements **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: data-privacy rules differ significantly across countries.
- C. The company's internal mission statement  
  _Rationale:_ A mission statement is internal and not jurisdiction-set.
- D. The brand color palette  
  _Rationale:_ Branding is unrelated to legal obligations.
- E. The global org chart layout  
  _Rationale:_ Chart layout is not a statutory obligation.
