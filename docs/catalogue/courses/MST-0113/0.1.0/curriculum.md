## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the healthcare regulatory environment and the elements of a healthcare compliance program
2. Apply fraud, waste and abuse and billing-integrity rules to operations
3. Evaluate privacy and security obligations for protected health information
4. Analyse auditing, investigation and corrective-action processes in a healthcare setting

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0113-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A claims audit shows visits billed at a higher-complexity code than the documentation supports. What is the primary compliance concern?

- A. Upcoding: billing a level not supported by documentation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: billing a higher level than documentation supports is upcoding, a core billing-integrity violation.
- B. Nothing, as long as the service was clinically appropriate  
  _Rationale:_ Clinical appropriateness does not justify billing an unsupported level.
- C. Undercoding that benefits the payer  
  _Rationale:_ The scenario is higher-than-supported coding, i.e., upcoding.
- D. A privacy violation  
  _Rationale:_ This is a billing-integrity issue, not primarily privacy.

**MST-0113-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A laptop containing protected health information is lost. Before deciding whether to report, what should the compliance team do first?

- A. Perform a breach-risk assessment to determine the probability that PHI was compromised **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a risk assessment determines whether the incident is a reportable breach.
- B. Immediately conclude it is not a breach because it was accidental  
  _Rationale:_ Intent does not resolve breach status; assessment is required.
- C. Report to every patient without any assessment  
  _Rationale:_ Notification follows the assessment and its conclusions.
- D. Delete the device remotely and record nothing  
  _Rationale:_ Failing to document and assess is itself non-compliant.

**MST-0113-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) A referral arrangement pays a physician above fair market value for patients sent to the hospital's imaging centre. Which TWO legal concepts are most directly implicated? (Select TWO)

- A. Anti-kickback concerns over remuneration for referrals **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: paying for referrals implicates anti-kickback principles.
- B. Physician self-referral (Stark-type) restrictions on financial relationships **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a financial relationship affecting referrals implicates self-referral rules.
- C. Trademark infringement  
  _Rationale:_ Trademark law is unrelated to the arrangement.
- D. Securities registration  
  _Rationale:_ No securities offering is involved.
- E. Export control  
  _Rationale:_ Export control is irrelevant to physician referrals.
