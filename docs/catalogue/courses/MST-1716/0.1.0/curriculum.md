## Learning outcomes

1. What fintech is
2. Payments and money movement
3. Lending and open banking
4. Emerging tech
5. Risk, regulation and inclusion

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses conceptual knowledge through selection items; does not assess building or operating a real fintech product or a regulatory licensing determination.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1716-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Open banking primarily enables:

- A. Authorised third parties to access account data via secure APIs with customer consent **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: consented API access to account data is the core of open banking.
- B. Banks to refuse all data sharing  
  _Rationale:_ Open banking is about enabling consented sharing, not refusing it.
- C. Anonymous access to anyone's account  
  _Rationale:_ Access requires customer consent and authorisation.
- D. The removal of all financial regulation  
  _Rationale:_ Open banking operates within regulation.

**MST-1716-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select ALL that apply) Which two are genuine risks that fintech products must manage? (Select TWO) (Select TWO)

- A. Cybersecurity and fraud **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: digital products face cyber and fraud risk.
- B. Data privacy and misuse of personal data **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: handling personal data creates privacy risk.
- C. Being immune to regulation  
  _Rationale:_ Fintechs are not immune to regulation.
- D. Guaranteed profitability  
  _Rationale:_ No product is guaranteed profitable.

**MST-1716-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Using non-traditional 'alternative data' (such as utility payments) in lending is mainly intended to:

- A. Assess borrowers who lack a traditional credit history **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: alternative data helps score thin-file borrowers.
- B. Guarantee every applicant is approved  
  _Rationale:_ It informs assessment, not automatic approval.
- C. Remove the need for any credit assessment  
  _Rationale:_ It is part of assessment, not a replacement for it.
- D. Eliminate all lending bias automatically  
  _Rationale:_ More data can introduce bias and does not guarantee fairness.
