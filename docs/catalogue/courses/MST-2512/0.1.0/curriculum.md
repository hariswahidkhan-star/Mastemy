## Learning outcomes

1. Classify financial assets using the business model and cash-flow characteristics tests
2. Account for financial liabilities and the treatment of own credit risk
3. Apply the expected credit loss impairment model at a conceptual level
4. Explain initial and subsequent measurement for the main instrument categories
5. Describe the conditions and mechanics of hedge accounting
6. Account for derecognition and common reclassifications

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2512-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under IFRS 9, which two tests determine the classification of a financial asset?

- A. The entity's business model for managing the assets and the asset's contractual cash-flow characteristics **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: classification uses the business model test and the SPPI cash-flow test.
- B. The asset's market price and the entity's tax rate  
  _Rationale:_ Neither market price nor tax rate drives classification.
- C. The maturity date and the issuer's credit rating only  
  _Rationale:_ Rating and maturity are not the classification tests.
- D. Management's profit target for the year  
  _Rationale:_ Profit targets do not determine classification.

**MST-2512-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO statements about the IFRS 9 expected credit loss (ECL) model are correct? (Select TWO.)

- A. Credit losses are recognised before a default occurs, based on expected losses **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ECL is a forward-looking model, not an incurred-loss model.
- B. A significant increase in credit risk moves an instrument from 12-month to lifetime ECL measurement **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: stage 2 uses lifetime ECL after a significant increase in credit risk.
- C. Losses are recognised only once a counterparty has actually defaulted  
  _Rationale:_ That describes the superseded incurred-loss approach.
- D. ECL applies only to equity investments  
  _Rationale:_ ECL applies to debt instruments, not equity investments.

**MST-2512-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A company designates a financial liability at fair value through profit or loss and its own credit standing deteriorates, increasing the gain. Where is the own-credit portion of the fair-value change generally presented?

- A. In other comprehensive income, not in profit or loss **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the own-credit change is generally presented in OCI to avoid counter-intuitive P&L gains.
- B. In profit or loss as a financing gain  
  _Rationale:_ Presenting the own-credit gain in P&L is what the rule is designed to avoid.
- C. As a direct increase to share capital  
  _Rationale:_ It is not an equity-capital movement.
- D. Netted against revenue  
  _Rationale:_ It is not a revenue item.
