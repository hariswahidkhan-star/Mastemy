## Learning outcomes

1. Identify where AI helps and harms journalistic work
2. Verify AI-generated claims and sources
3. Detect manipulated images, audio and video
4. Use AI for research and drafting responsibly
5. Apply editorial and ethical standards to AI use

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1398-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An AI tool provides a quote with a named source. What must a journalist do before publishing it?

- A. Independently verify that the quote and source are real and accurate **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI can fabricate quotes and citations, so verification is essential.
- B. Publish it because the AI named a source  
  _Rationale:_ AI-named sources are often fabricated.
- C. Trust it if the wording sounds plausible  
  _Rationale:_ Plausibility is not verification.
- D. Assume the source consented to be quoted  
  _Rationale:_ Consent and existence must both be confirmed.

**MST-1398-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO steps help a newsroom use AI responsibly? (Select TWO.)

- A. Disclose to readers when AI materially assisted a story **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: transparency sustains reader trust.
- B. Fact-check AI outputs before publication **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: verification guards accuracy.
- C. Publish AI drafts directly to save time  
  _Rationale:_ Unchecked drafts risk errors and fabrications.
- D. Treat AI as a neutral, bias-free tool  
  _Rationale:_ AI can reflect and amplify bias.

**MST-1398-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why is detecting manipulated media increasingly important in journalism?

- A. Synthetic images, audio and video can look real and mislead audiences **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: realistic fakes can spread false information quickly.
- B. Manipulated media is always obvious  
  _Rationale:_ Modern fakes are often hard to detect by eye.
- C. Only text can be faked by AI  
  _Rationale:_ Images, audio and video can all be synthesised.
- D. Verification is unnecessary for visual content  
  _Rationale:_ Visual content needs verification too.
