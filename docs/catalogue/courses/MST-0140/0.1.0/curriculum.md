## Learning outcomes

1. Describe advanced product ownership, strategy and value measurement
2. Apply product strategy, vision and goals to complex stakeholder landscapes
3. Evaluate evidence-based management and validated learning for products
4. Analyse portfolio, pricing and go-to-market decisions as a Product Owner

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0140-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A Product Owner presents a list of dated features and calls it the product strategy. The most accurate critique is that:

- A. A feature list is not a strategy; strategy defines outcomes and how to achieve them **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: strategy is about outcomes and choices, not a dated feature list.
- B. Feature lists are illegal  
  _Rationale:_ They are not illegal, just insufficient as strategy.
- C. Strategy must never mention features  
  _Rationale:_ Features can appear, but strategy is about outcomes.
- D. Dates make any list a strategy  
  _Rationale:_ Adding dates does not create strategy.

**MST-0140-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under evidence-based management, 'unrealised value' helps a Product Owner understand:

- A. The potential additional value if the product better met customer needs **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: unrealised value gauges the opportunity still available.
- B. How many hours the team worked  
  _Rationale:_ Hours worked is an effort measure, not unrealised value.
- C. The number of Jira tickets closed  
  _Rationale:_ Ticket counts are output, not unrealised value.
- D. The team's seating arrangement  
  _Rationale:_ Irrelevant to value measurement.

**MST-0140-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO practices reflect a genuinely evidence-based Product Owner? (Select TWO)

- A. Forming hypotheses and validating them with real data **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: validated learning is central to evidence-based ownership.
- B. Measuring value and outcomes, not just output **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: evidence-based management focuses on value.
- C. Deciding solely on the HiPPO's opinion  
  _Rationale:_ Deferring to the highest-paid person's opinion is not evidence-based.
- D. Shipping features without measuring impact  
  _Rationale:_ Unmeasured shipping is not evidence-based.
- E. Hiding negative results  
  _Rationale:_ Suppressing data undermines learning.
