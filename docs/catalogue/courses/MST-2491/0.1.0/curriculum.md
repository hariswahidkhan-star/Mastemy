## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the time value of money and why it matters for investment decisions
2. Compute and interpret payback, NPV and IRR for a project
3. Identify relevant cash flows and exclude sunk and non-incremental costs
4. Choose and apply an appropriate hurdle rate
5. Compare and prioritise projects under capital constraints
6. Recognise the risks, biases and limits of appraisal numbers

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2491-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why is net present value generally preferred to simple payback?

- A. NPV accounts for the time value of money and all project cash flows, not just the time to recover outlay **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: payback ignores timing beyond recovery and later cash flows.
- B. NPV is always easier to calculate  
  _Rationale:_ NPV is usually more, not less, involved.
- C. Payback cannot be computed for most projects  
  _Rationale:_ Payback is easy to compute.
- D. NPV ignores cash flows entirely  
  _Rationale:_ NPV is built from cash flows.

**MST-2491-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO cash flows are relevant to a project investment decision? (Select TWO.)

- A. Incremental revenue the project generates **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: incremental inflows are relevant.
- B. An opportunity cost of using space the project occupies **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: forgone use is a relevant cost.
- C. Market research already paid for last year  
  _Rationale:_ That is a sunk cost and irrelevant.
- D. An unchanged head-office cost reallocated to the project  
  _Rationale:_ Non-incremental allocations are irrelevant.

**MST-2491-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A project has a positive NPV but a payback longer than the company's informal rule of thumb. The best response is to:

- A. Weigh the positive NPV and strategic value against liquidity needs rather than reject on payback alone **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: payback is a crude screen; NPV and context should lead.
- B. Reject it automatically because of payback  
  _Rationale:_ A rigid payback rule can destroy value.
- C. Accept it without any further thought  
  _Rationale:_ Liquidity and risk still warrant judgement.
- D. Recompute until payback looks shorter  
  _Rationale:_ Manipulating the number is not appraisal.
