## Learning outcomes

1. Describe end-to-end supply chain design and strategy concepts
2. Explain planning and execution of supply, demand and inventory
3. Analyse supplier and customer relationship and logistics management
4. Evaluate supply chain risk, compliance and continuous improvement

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: official item formats not reproducible as MCQ/MR (for example performance, speaking, essay, hands-on or simulation tasks) are not reproduced here; see the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0304-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Sales and operations planning (S&OP) primarily aims to do which of the following?

- A. Reconcile demand and supply plans into one agreed operating plan **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: S&OP balances demand and supply into a single, cross-functional plan.
- B. Schedule individual machines on the shop floor each hour  
  _Rationale:_ That is detailed scheduling/execution, below the S&OP level.
- C. Negotiate the price of a single purchase order  
  _Rationale:_ S&OP is a planning process, not transactional procurement negotiation.
- D. Design the physical layout of a warehouse  
  _Rationale:_ Warehouse layout is a facility-design task, not S&OP.

**MST-0304-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A critical component comes from one supplier in one country. Which action most directly reduces supply risk?

- A. Qualifying a second source in a different region **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a qualified alternate source in another region reduces single-source and geographic risk.
- B. Increasing the sales forecast for the finished product  
  _Rationale:_ Changing the forecast does not reduce supply-side concentration risk.
- C. Extending customer payment terms  
  _Rationale:_ Payment terms affect cash flow, not supply risk.
- D. Switching to a just-in-time replenishment with the same supplier  
  _Rationale:_ JIT with the same single source can increase exposure to that supplier's disruption.

**MST-0304-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO metrics that are appropriate for measuring supply chain delivery performance.

- A. On-time in-full (OTIF) delivery rate **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: OTIF is a standard measure of delivery reliability.
- B. Order cycle time **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: order cycle time measures responsiveness of the delivery process.
- C. Employee headcount in the finance team  
  _Rationale:_ Finance headcount is unrelated to delivery performance.
- D. Number of marketing campaigns launched  
  _Rationale:_ Marketing campaign counts do not measure delivery performance.
- E. Average office temperature  
  _Rationale:_ This is irrelevant to supply chain delivery.
