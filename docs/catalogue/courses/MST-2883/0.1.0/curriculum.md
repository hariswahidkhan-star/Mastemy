## Learning outcomes

1. Identify theme, character and plot in fiction
2. Recognise common figurative language such as simile and metaphor
3. Explain how poets use rhythm, rhyme and imagery
4. Interpret mood and tone in a text
5. Compare how two texts treat a similar idea
6. Support interpretations with evidence from the text

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2883-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which of these is a simile?

- A. Her smile was like sunshine. **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a simile compares using 'like' or 'as'.
- B. Her smile was sunshine.  
  _Rationale:_ That is a metaphor; it says one thing is another.
- C. She smiled.  
  _Rationale:_ This is a plain statement with no comparison.
- D. The smile.  
  _Rationale:_ This is just a noun phrase, not a comparison.

**MST-2883-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) What does the 'mood' of a text mean?

- A. The feeling the text creates in the reader **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: mood is the atmosphere or feeling evoked.
- B. The number of pages it has  
  _Rationale:_ Page count is not mood.
- C. The author's full name  
  _Rationale:_ The author's name is not the mood.
- D. The font the words are printed in  
  _Rationale:_ Font choice is not the text's mood.

**MST-2883-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO features would you look for when analysing a poem? (Select TWO.)

- A. Imagery that creates pictures in the mind **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: imagery is a key poetic feature.
- B. Rhythm created by stressed and unstressed syllables **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: rhythm shapes how a poem sounds.
- C. The price printed on the back cover  
  _Rationale:_ Price is not a feature of the poem.
- D. The weight of the book in grams  
  _Rationale:_ A book's weight is irrelevant to poetry analysis.
