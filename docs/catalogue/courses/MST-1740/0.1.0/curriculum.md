## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the key concepts of Direct, Plan and Improve, including scope, governance and decision making
2. Describe how to use the ITIL guiding principles to direct, plan and improve
3. Explain the role of risk, controls and compliance in planning and improving
4. Describe how to plan and manage value streams and improve organisational capability

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1740-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) How does governance differ from management in ITIL 4?

- A. Governance directs and oversees; management plans and runs day-to-day activities **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: governance sets direction and evaluates/monitors; management executes within that direction.
- B. Governance and management are the same thing  
  _Rationale:_ They are distinct: one directs and oversees, the other executes.
- C. Governance only handles incidents  
  _Rationale:_ Incidents are an operational, management-level concern.
- D. Management sets strategy and governance ignores it  
  _Rationale:_ This reverses the roles; governance sets direction.

**MST-1740-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) An organisation accepts a low-impact, low-likelihood risk without further action. Which risk response is this?

- A. Tolerate (accept) the risk **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: accepting a risk without further action is the tolerate response, appropriate for low exposure.
- B. Transfer the risk  
  _Rationale:_ Transfer moves the risk to a third party, e.g. via insurance.
- C. Treat the risk  
  _Rationale:_ Treat applies controls to reduce the risk.
- D. Terminate the risk  
  _Rationale:_ Terminate removes the activity causing the risk.

**MST-1740-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are steps of the ITIL continual improvement model?

- A. What is the vision? **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'What is the vision?' is the first step of the model.
- B. Where are we now? **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'Where are we now?' establishes the baseline.
- C. Who approves the budget?  
  _Rationale:_ This is not a step of the continual improvement model.
- D. What is the exam weighting?  
  _Rationale:_ Not a model step; the model asks vision, baseline, target, plan, action, check, momentum.
- E. When does the contract end?  
  _Rationale:_ Not a step of the continual improvement model.
