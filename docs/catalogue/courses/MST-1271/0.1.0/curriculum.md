## Learning outcomes

1. Analyse environmental, social and economic factors affecting a site
2. Apply codes and regulations to programming decisions
3. Conduct site analysis and evaluation
4. Conduct building and program analysis to define project requirements

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1271-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Zoning ordinances primarily regulate:

- A. Permitted land use, density and setbacks **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: zoning governs land use, density, height and setbacks.
- B. Fire-rated assembly construction details  
  _Rationale:_ Those are building-code, not zoning, matters.
- C. The architect's fee structure  
  _Rationale:_ Fees are a business matter, not zoning.
- D. Interior furniture selection  
  _Rationale:_ Furniture selection is not regulated by zoning.

**MST-1271-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Applying a net-to-gross efficiency factor to a space program produces the:

- A. Net assignable area only  
  _Rationale:_ Net area excludes circulation and support; the factor adds those.
- B. Estimated gross building area **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the efficiency factor scales net area up to gross building area.
- C. Site area  
  _Rationale:_ Site area is land, not building area.
- D. Construction cost  
  _Rationale:_ Cost derives from area and rates, not directly from this factor.

**MST-1271-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO items belong in a site analysis?

- A. Access, circulation and utility connections **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: access and utilities are core site-analysis content.
- B. Topography and drainage constraints **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: topography and drainage are key site factors.
- C. The client's favourite paint colour  
  _Rationale:_ Finish preferences are not site analysis.
- D. The contractor's payroll records  
  _Rationale:_ Payroll is unrelated to site analysis.
