## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0156-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A stakeholder asks 'why did sales drop last quarter?' Which type of analytics most directly answers this?

- A. Diagnostic analytics **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: diagnostic analytics explains why something happened by examining causes and relationships.
- B. Descriptive analytics  
  _Rationale:_ Descriptive analytics reports what happened, not why.
- C. Prescriptive analytics  
  _Rationale:_ Prescriptive analytics recommends actions; it does not primarily explain the cause.
- D. Predictive analytics  
  _Rationale:_ Predictive analytics forecasts the future rather than explaining a past drop.

**MST-0156-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Before analysis, a dataset has duplicate records and inconsistent date formats. Which step addresses this?

- A. Data preparation/cleansing **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: preparation and cleansing resolve duplicates and formatting before analysis.
- B. Model deployment  
  _Rationale:_ Deployment happens after a validated model, not for cleaning raw data.
- C. Stakeholder sign-off  
  _Rationale:_ Sign-off is a governance step, not data cleansing.
- D. Dashboard theming  
  _Rationale:_ Visual styling does not fix underlying data quality.

**MST-0156-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO practices improve how analytics insights drive business decisions? (Select TWO)

- A. Tailoring the message and visuals to the decision-maker's needs **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: fitting communication to the audience improves uptake of insights.
- B. Stating assumptions and the confidence/limitations of results **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: disclosing assumptions and limitations supports sound decisions.
- C. Presenting every raw table with no interpretation  
  _Rationale:_ Raw tables without interpretation rarely drive decisions.
- D. Hiding results that contradict the sponsor's view  
  _Rationale:_ Suppressing inconvenient findings is unethical and undermines decisions.
- E. Choosing visuals purely for decoration  
  _Rationale:_ Decorative visuals that do not aid understanding add noise, not value.
