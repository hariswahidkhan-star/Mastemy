## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1645-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) How should enterprise risk best be communicated to a board of directors?

- A. Framed as business impact with likelihood, owners and treatment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: boards need risk in business terms, not raw technical counts.
- B. As a raw list of CVE identifiers  
  _Rationale:_ CVE lists are technical detail unsuited to a board audience.
- C. By withholding residual risk to avoid alarm  
  _Rationale:_ Hiding residual risk undermines informed governance.
- D. As the number of firewall rules changed  
  _Rationale:_ That is an activity metric, not business risk.

**MST-1645-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A service must lose no more than 15 minutes of data in a disaster. Which objective does that define?

- A. Recovery point objective (RPO) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: RPO is the maximum tolerable data loss measured in time.
- B. Recovery time objective (RTO)  
  _Rationale:_ RTO is how fast service is restored, not how much data is lost.
- C. Mean time between failures (MTBF)  
  _Rationale:_ MTBF measures reliability, not tolerable data loss.
- D. Service level agreement (SLA)  
  _Rationale:_ An SLA is a broader commitment, not this specific data-loss measure.

**MST-1645-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO actions belong in the early (preparation and identification) phases of incident management? (Select TWO.)

- A. Define an incident classification and escalation scheme in advance **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: classification/escalation is prepared before incidents occur.
- B. Establish and test detection and alerting capability **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: detection must be in place to identify incidents.
- C. Permanently delete all logs after any alert  
  _Rationale:_ Deleting logs destroys evidence and detection history.
- D. Pay any ransom immediately without analysis  
  _Rationale:_ That is not a sound preparation/identification action.
- E. Skip the post-incident review  
  _Rationale:_ Reviews belong to the closing phase and must not be skipped.
