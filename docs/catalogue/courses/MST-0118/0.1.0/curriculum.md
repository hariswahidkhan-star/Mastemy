## Learning outcomes

1. Describe business continuity concepts, terminology and the programme lifecycle
2. Apply risk assessment and business impact analysis to prioritise recovery
3. Evaluate continuity and recovery strategies against impact and resources
4. Analyse plan development, exercising and programme maintenance

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0118-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A business impact analysis shows a claims-processing outage becomes severe after four hours. This four-hour figure most directly informs the:

- A. Recovery time objective (RTO) for that process **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the point at which impact becomes unacceptable informs the RTO.
- B. Marketing budget  
  _Rationale:_ The marketing budget is unrelated to recovery objectives.
- C. Office paint colour  
  _Rationale:_ This is irrelevant to continuity planning.
- D. Employee dress code  
  _Rationale:_ Dress code has no bearing on RTO.

**MST-0118-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which statement best distinguishes a risk assessment from a business impact analysis?

- A. Risk assessment examines threats and likelihood; BIA examines consequences of disruption over time **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the two analyses answer different questions and complement each other.
- B. They are identical and interchangeable  
  _Rationale:_ They address different questions and are not interchangeable.
- C. A BIA only lists IT servers  
  _Rationale:_ A BIA covers business processes, not just servers.
- D. A risk assessment sets the dress code  
  _Rationale:_ This is not a function of a risk assessment.

**MST-0118-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are essential to make a continuity plan credible rather than shelfware? (Select TWO)

- A. Regular exercising of the plan **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: exercising validates that the plan works.
- B. Scheduled maintenance and updates **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: plans must be kept current to stay credible.
- C. Locking the plan away untested  
  _Rationale:_ An untested plan is not proven.
- D. Writing it once and never revisiting it  
  _Rationale:_ Stale plans lose credibility.
- E. Keeping objectives secret from responders  
  _Rationale:_ Responders must know the objectives.
