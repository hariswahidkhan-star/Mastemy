## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1850-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Choose the correct measure word: "一 ___ 书" (one ___ book).

- A. 本 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 本 (ben) is the standard measure word for books.
- B. 只  
  _Rationale:_ 只 (zhi) is used for certain animals, not books.
- C. 张  
  _Rationale:_ 张 (zhang) is for flat objects like paper, not bound books.
- D. 杯  
  _Rationale:_ 杯 (bei) is for cups of liquid, not books.

**MST-1850-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A gap-fill reading item is best answered by:

- A. Choosing the word whose meaning fits the whole sentence **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the correct word is the one that makes the sentence meaningful and grammatical.
- B. Choosing any character of the right shape  
  _Rationale:_ Shape does not determine meaning or fit.
- C. Choosing the longest word  
  _Rationale:_ Length is irrelevant to the correct choice.
- D. Leaving it blank  
  _Rationale:_ A choice must be made to score.

**MST-1850-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO strategies help on an HSK 3 listening true/false item?

- A. Listening for the overall meaning, not just single words **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: meaning, not isolated words, decides the match.
- B. Reading the statement before the audio plays **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: knowing the statement focuses listening.
- C. Assuming the statement is true if any word matches  
  _Rationale:_ A single matching word does not make a statement true.
- D. Ignoring the statement entirely  
  _Rationale:_ The statement must be understood to judge it.
- E. Choosing true whenever the audio is long  
  _Rationale:_ Audio length is irrelevant to truth value.
