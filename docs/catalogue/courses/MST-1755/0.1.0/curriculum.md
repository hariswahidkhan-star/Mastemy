## Integrative case

A company has thirty competing initiatives and limited funding. The learner must organise related projects into programmes, build a scoring model to prioritise the portfolio, define governance decision rights and a PMO role, and link delivery to strategic benefits, then recommend which initiatives to fund, defer or stop.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1755-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A programme is best defined as:

- A. A group of related projects managed together to achieve benefits not available individually **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: programmes coordinate related projects for collective benefit.
- B. Any project that costs a lot of money  
  _Rationale:_ Cost does not make a project a programme.
- C. A single large project with many tasks  
  _Rationale:_ Size alone does not make a programme.
- D. The company's entire set of initiatives  
  _Rationale:_ That describes a portfolio.

**MST-1755-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO factors belong in a portfolio prioritisation score? (Select TWO.)

- A. Strategic alignment with organisational objectives **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: alignment is a core portfolio selection criterion.
- B. Expected benefit relative to cost and risk **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: value for money and risk weigh in prioritisation.
- C. The seniority of the sponsor's job title  
  _Rationale:_ Prioritisation should rest on value, not politics.
- D. How early in the year the idea was proposed  
  _Rationale:_ Submission date is not a value criterion.

**MST-1755-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A supportive (as opposed to directive) PMO primarily:

- A. Provides templates, training and expertise while teams retain control **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a supportive PMO enables rather than controls delivery.
- B. Takes over and runs every project directly  
  _Rationale:_ That describes a directive PMO.
- C. Only files status reports with no other role  
  _Rationale:_ That understates even a supportive PMO's value.
- D. Sets staff salaries across the organisation  
  _Rationale:_ Pay-setting is not a PMO function.
