## Learning outcomes

1. Identify nouns, verbs, adjectives and adverbs in sentences
2. Write complete sentences with a subject and a verb
3. Use capital letters and full stops correctly
4. Punctuate questions and exclamations accurately
5. Use commas in lists and apostrophes for contractions
6. Edit a short piece of writing to fix grammar and punctuation

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2878-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which word is the verb in 'The dog barks loudly'?

- A. barks **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'barks' is the action word (verb).
- B. dog  
  _Rationale:_ 'dog' is a noun, the thing doing the action.
- C. loudly  
  _Rationale:_ 'loudly' is an adverb describing how it barks.
- D. The  
  _Rationale:_ 'The' is an article, not a verb.

**MST-2878-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which sentence is punctuated correctly?

- A. Where are you going? **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a question ends with a question mark and starts with a capital.
- B. where are you going.  
  _Rationale:_ It needs a capital letter and a question mark.
- C. Where are you going  
  _Rationale:_ A question needs an end punctuation mark.
- D. Where are you going!  
  _Rationale:_ This is a question, so it needs a question mark, not an exclamation mark.

**MST-2878-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO sentences use commas or apostrophes correctly? (Select TWO.)

- A. I bought apples, pears and bread. **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: commas separate items in a list.
- B. We don't have any milk. **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the apostrophe marks the contraction of 'do not'.
- C. I like apples pears and bread.  
  _Rationale:_ This list is missing its separating commas.
- D. The cat's are sleeping.  
  _Rationale:_ 'cats' is a plural here and should not take an apostrophe.
