## Learning outcomes

1. Explain exercise science, anatomy, physiology and nutrition fundamentals
2. Apply the ACE Integrated Fitness Training (IFT) model and client assessment
3. Design and progress exercise programs using the ACE approach
4. Apply the ACE Mover Method, rapport, behaviour change and professional responsibility

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1280-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) The ACE Mover Method emphasises that the trainer should primarily:

- A. Collaborate with the client using questions, asking permission and partnering on solutions **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the ACE Mover Method is a client-centred, collaborative approach built on asking, breaking down barriers and partnering.
- B. Dictate a fixed program with no client input  
  _Rationale:_ This contradicts the collaborative, client-centred philosophy.
- C. Avoid discussing goals with the client  
  _Rationale:_ Goals are central and explored collaboratively.
- D. Diagnose medical conditions  
  _Rationale:_ Diagnosis is outside scope and not part of the method.

**MST-1280-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which is an appropriate first step with a new, deconditioned client?

- A. Establish rapport and assess health history/readiness before strenuous testing **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: building rapport and screening readiness comes before demanding assessments or training.
- B. Perform a maximal strength test immediately  
  _Rationale:_ Maximal testing is inappropriate before screening and rapport.
- C. Prescribe a therapeutic diet  
  _Rationale:_ That is outside scope and premature.
- D. Skip assessment and begin high-intensity intervals  
  _Rationale:_ High intensity without screening is unsafe for a deconditioned client.

**MST-1280-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are consistent with a trainer's professional responsibility and scope?

- A. Referring a client with chest pain to a medical professional **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: recognising red flags and referring out is a core responsibility.
- B. Providing general healthy-eating guidance **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: general, non-medical nutrition guidance is within scope.
- C. Diagnosing a musculoskeletal disorder  
  _Rationale:_ Diagnosis is outside a trainer's scope.
- D. Prescribing rehabilitation for a torn ligament  
  _Rationale:_ Rehabilitation prescription belongs to licensed clinicians.
- E. Guaranteeing a specific weight-loss amount by a date  
  _Rationale:_ Guaranteeing outcomes is unethical and unrealistic.
