## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0385-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) For steady one-dimensional heat conduction through a plane wall, Fourier's law says the heat-transfer rate is:

- A. Proportional to the temperature difference and inversely proportional to the wall thickness **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: q = kA(dT)/L, rising with dT and falling with thickness.
- B. Independent of the temperature difference  
  _Rationale:_ Conduction is driven by the temperature difference.
- C. Proportional to the wall thickness  
  _Rationale:_ Thicker walls reduce, not increase, conduction for a given dT.
- D. Zero whenever the two surfaces differ in temperature  
  _Rationale:_ A temperature difference drives a nonzero conduction rate.

**MST-0385-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) In a steady-flow control volume with one inlet and one outlet and no heat or work interactions, the first law reduces to a balance in which:

- A. The energy entering with the inlet stream equals the energy leaving with the outlet stream **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: with no heat/work and steady state, energy in equals energy out.
- B. Energy accumulates without limit in the control volume  
  _Rationale:_ Steady state means no accumulation.
- C. Mass is created to balance the energy  
  _Rationale:_ Mass is conserved; it is not created.
- D. The outlet energy is always double the inlet energy  
  _Rationale:_ Nothing doubles the energy in this balance.

**MST-0385-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO statements about the modes of heat transfer are correct?

- A. Conduction transfers heat through a material without bulk motion of the material **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: conduction is diffusion of energy through a medium.
- B. Radiation can transfer heat across a vacuum **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: thermal radiation needs no medium.
- C. Convection requires no fluid motion at all  
  _Rationale:_ Convection depends on fluid motion.
- D. Conduction requires a vacuum to occur  
  _Rationale:_ Conduction occurs within matter, not in a vacuum.
- E. Radiation only occurs in liquids  
  _Rationale:_ Radiation occurs for any body above absolute zero, including in vacuum.
