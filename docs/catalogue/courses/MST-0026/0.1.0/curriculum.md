## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0026-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under the accruals concept, revenue and the costs of earning it should be:

- A. Recognised in the period in which they are earned and incurred, not when cash moves **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: accruals accounting matches income and expense to the period they relate to, independent of cash flow.
- B. Recognised only when cash is received or paid  
  _Rationale:_ That describes cash accounting, not the accruals concept.
- C. Recognised only at the end of the entity's life  
  _Rationale:_ Periodic accounts are prepared regularly, not only at the end.
- D. Never recognised if estimated  
  _Rationale:_ Estimates are permitted and often required under accruals accounting.

**MST-0026-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A business buys goods for resale on credit. What is the correct double entry?

- A. Debit purchases, credit trade payables **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the expense (purchases) is debited and the liability (payables) is credited.
- B. Debit trade payables, credit purchases  
  _Rationale:_ This reverses the entry; payables increase with a credit.
- C. Debit cash, credit purchases  
  _Rationale:_ No cash moves on a credit purchase.
- D. Debit sales, credit cash  
  _Rationale:_ This records a cash sale, not a credit purchase.

**MST-0026-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO statements that are part of a complete set of financial statements for a single entity.

- A. Statement of profit or loss **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it reports income and expenses for the period.
- B. Statement of financial position **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it reports assets, liabilities and equity at a date.
- C. A marketing plan  
  _Rationale:_ A marketing plan is not a financial statement.
- D. The managing director's employment contract  
  _Rationale:_ A contract is not a financial statement.
- E. A list of competitor prices  
  _Rationale:_ Competitor pricing is not a financial statement.
