## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0036-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Porter's five forces framework is primarily used to analyse:

- A. The attractiveness and competitive structure of an industry **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the five forces assess industry competitive structure and profitability potential.
- B. An organisation's internal cost structure  
  _Rationale:_ Internal costs are analysed by value-chain or costing tools, not five forces.
- C. A project's net present value  
  _Rationale:_ NPV is a capital-appraisal technique, unrelated to five forces.
- D. The liquidity of a company's balance sheet  
  _Rationale:_ Liquidity is a financial-ratio matter, not an industry-structure framework.

**MST-0036-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A company adopts a balanced scorecard. Which statement best describes its purpose?

- A. To measure performance across financial and non-financial perspectives linked to strategy **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the balanced scorecard links strategy to financial, customer, process and learning measures.
- B. To replace all financial reporting with customer surveys  
  _Rationale:_ It complements, not replaces, financial measures.
- C. To calculate the weighted average cost of capital  
  _Rationale:_ WACC is a financing calculation, not a scorecard purpose.
- D. To set statutory audit requirements  
  _Rationale:_ Statutory audit is a compliance matter, not a scorecard function.

**MST-0036-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO principles commonly associated with good corporate governance.

- A. Separation of the roles of chair and chief executive **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: separating the two roles avoids excessive concentration of power.
- B. An effective independent non-executive presence on the board **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: independent non-executives provide objective oversight.
- C. Allowing executives to set their own pay without review  
  _Rationale:_ Unchecked self-set pay is a governance weakness.
- D. Concealing risks from shareholders  
  _Rationale:_ Transparency, not concealment, is a governance principle.
- E. Avoiding any external audit  
  _Rationale:_ Independent audit supports, rather than undermines, governance.
