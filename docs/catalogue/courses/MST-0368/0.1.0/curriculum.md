## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0368-Q0001** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) If ultrasound frequency increases, which generally happens to axial resolution and penetration?

- A. Resolution improves and penetration decreases **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: higher frequency gives better resolution but less depth.
- B. Both improve  
  _Rationale:_ Penetration decreases as frequency rises.
- C. Both decrease  
  _Rationale:_ Resolution improves, not decreases.
- D. Neither changes  
  _Rationale:_ Both are affected by frequency.

**MST-0368-Q0002** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) Colour Doppler aliasing is best reduced by:

- A. Increasing the pulse repetition frequency (scale) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: raising the scale/PRF raises the Nyquist limit and reduces aliasing.
- B. Decreasing the scale  
  _Rationale:_ Lowering the scale worsens aliasing.
- C. Increasing overall gain  
  _Rationale:_ Gain affects brightness, not the Nyquist limit.
- D. Switching to a higher-frequency transducer  
  _Rationale:_ Higher frequency can worsen aliasing for a given flow.

**MST-0368-Q0003** (multiple-answer-selection, Select TWO) Which TWO reflect the ALARA principle in ultrasound?

- A. Keeping output power as low as reasonably achievable **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ALARA minimises acoustic exposure.
- B. Limiting scanning time to what is needed **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reducing dwell time reduces exposure.
- C. Maximising output for a faster exam  
  _Rationale:_ Maximising output contradicts ALARA.
- D. Ignoring thermal and mechanical indices  
  _Rationale:_ These indices guide safe output.
- E. Scanning longer for sharper images regardless of exposure  
  _Rationale:_ Unnecessary exposure violates ALARA.
