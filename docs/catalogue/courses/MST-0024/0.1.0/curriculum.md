## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0024-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) If a central bank raises interest rates to reduce inflation, what is the most likely short-term effect on aggregate demand?

- A. Aggregate demand falls as borrowing and spending become more expensive **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: higher rates raise the cost of borrowing and saving's reward, tending to reduce consumption and investment.
- B. Aggregate demand rises because saving becomes less attractive  
  _Rationale:_ Higher rates make saving more attractive, not less, reducing current spending.
- C. Aggregate demand is unaffected by interest rates  
  _Rationale:_ Interest rates are a key channel through which monetary policy affects demand.
- D. Aggregate demand rises because investment becomes cheaper  
  _Rationale:_ Higher rates make investment more expensive, not cheaper.

**MST-0024-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) In a market, a decrease in the price of a good, other things equal, will normally cause:

- A. An increase in the quantity demanded (a movement along the demand curve) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a price fall causes a movement along the demand curve, increasing quantity demanded.
- B. A shift of the whole demand curve to the right  
  _Rationale:_ A price change moves along the curve; it does not shift the curve.
- C. A decrease in quantity demanded  
  _Rationale:_ Lower price normally raises, not lowers, quantity demanded.
- D. No change in quantity demanded  
  _Rationale:_ For a normal good, quantity demanded responds to price.

**MST-0024-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO functions performed by the financial system of an economy.

- A. Channelling funds from savers to borrowers **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: intermediation moves funds from surplus to deficit units.
- B. Providing a mechanism for payments and settlement **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the financial system provides payment and settlement services.
- C. Setting the national minimum wage  
  _Rationale:_ Minimum wages are set by government/legislation, not the financial system.
- D. Producing physical goods for export  
  _Rationale:_ Producing goods is the real economy, not the financial system.
- E. Issuing passports to citizens  
  _Rationale:_ This is a government administrative function, unrelated to the financial system.
