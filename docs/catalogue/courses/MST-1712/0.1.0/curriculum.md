## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the purpose of credit analysis and the lending decision
2. Analyse a borrower's financial statements for credit quality
3. Assess qualitative factors, business and industry risk
4. Apply ratio and cash-flow analysis to repayment capacity
5. Structure a credit recommendation with terms, covenants and monitoring

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

A relationship manager receives a working-capital facility request from a mid-size distributor. ## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1712-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A borrower is highly profitable but has negative operating cash flow for two years. For a credit decision this most signals:

- A. Possible working-capital or earnings-quality problems to investigate **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: profit without cash flow warrants scrutiny of receivables, inventory and accruals.
- B. That the loan is low risk because profit is high  
  _Rationale:_ Repayment depends on cash, not reported profit alone.
- C. That ratios are irrelevant for this borrower  
  _Rationale:_ Ratios and cash flow together are essential here.
- D. That collateral is unnecessary  
  _Rationale:_ Nothing here reduces the need for security.

**MST-1712-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO ratios most directly measure a borrower's ability to service debt? (Select TWO.)

- A. Interest coverage ratio **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it compares earnings to interest obligations.
- B. Debt-service coverage ratio **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it compares cash flow to total debt service.
- C. Gross profit margin  
  _Rationale:_ It measures profitability, not debt-servicing capacity directly.
- D. Inventory turnover  
  _Rationale:_ It measures efficiency, not repayment capacity.

**MST-1712-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why do lenders include financial covenants in a loan agreement?

- A. To trigger early action if the borrower's condition deteriorates **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: covenants give early warning and a right to act.
- B. To guarantee the loan will be repaid  
  _Rationale:_ Covenants reduce, not remove, risk.
- C. To increase the interest rate automatically each year  
  _Rationale:_ That is pricing, not the purpose of covenants.
- D. To replace the need for any monitoring  
  _Rationale:_ Covenants are monitored, not a substitute for it.
