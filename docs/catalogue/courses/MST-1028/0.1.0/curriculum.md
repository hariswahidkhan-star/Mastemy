## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1028-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) IFRS 18 primarily standardises which aspect of financial reporting?

- A. The presentation and structure of the primary financial statements **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: IFRS 18 focuses on presentation and disclosure, not measurement.
- B. How revenue is measured  
  _Rationale:_ Revenue measurement is addressed by a different standard.
- C. How deferred tax is calculated  
  _Rationale:_ Deferred tax calculation is outside IFRS 18's focus.
- D. The valuation of intangible assets  
  _Rationale:_ Intangible measurement is governed by other standards.

**MST-1028-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO requirements apply when an entity presents a management-defined performance measure (MPM)? (Select TWO)

- A. Reconcile the MPM to the most comparable IFRS-defined subtotal **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: IFRS 18 requires a reconciliation to the nearest IFRS subtotal.
- B. Explain why the measure provides useful information **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the entity must explain the usefulness of the MPM.
- C. Present the MPM instead of the IFRS subtotals  
  _Rationale:_ MPMs supplement, not replace, IFRS subtotals.
- D. Keep the MPM undisclosed in the notes  
  _Rationale:_ MPMs must be disclosed and reconciled, not hidden.

**MST-1028-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Interest expense on borrowings is classified in which IFRS 18 income-statement category?

- A. Financing **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: interest on borrowings falls in the financing category.
- B. Operating  
  _Rationale:_ Financing costs are not operating items under IFRS 18.
- C. Investing  
  _Rationale:_ Investing covers returns on investments, not borrowing costs.
- D. It is excluded from the income statement  
  _Rationale:_ Interest expense is presented, within financing.
