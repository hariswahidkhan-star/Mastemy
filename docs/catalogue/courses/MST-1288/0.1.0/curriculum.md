## Learning outcomes

1. Describe cancer biology, prevention, screening and early detection (knowledge practice)
2. Explain treatment modalities and nursing management of side effects
3. Apply oncologic emergency recognition and symptom management
4. Analyse survivorship, palliative care, and professional/ethical practice in oncology

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1288-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A neutropenic patient on chemotherapy develops a fever of 38.5C. How should the nurse interpret this?

- A. A minor issue to recheck next day  
  _Rationale:_ Fever in neutropenia is urgent, not routine.
- B. A potential oncologic emergency (febrile neutropenia) needing prompt action **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: fever with neutropenia is a medical emergency requiring prompt evaluation and treatment.
- C. Expected and requiring no response  
  _Rationale:_ Febrile neutropenia always warrants prompt action.
- D. A sign the cancer is cured  
  _Rationale:_ Fever is unrelated to cure and signals possible infection.

**MST-1288-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the best characterisation of palliative care?

- A. Care provided only in the final days of life  
  _Rationale:_ That describes a narrow end-of-life phase, not palliative care overall.
- B. Symptom- and quality-of-life-focused care that can accompany active treatment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: palliative care focuses on symptoms and quality of life and may run alongside curative treatment.
- C. Care that stops all symptom management  
  _Rationale:_ Palliative care centres on symptom management.
- D. A type of chemotherapy  
  _Rationale:_ Palliative care is a care approach, not a chemotherapy regimen.

**MST-1288-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are recognised oncologic emergencies the nurse must act on quickly?

- A. Spinal cord compression with new neurologic deficits **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: spinal cord compression is a true oncologic emergency.
- B. Tumor lysis syndrome with metabolic derangement **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: tumor lysis syndrome is a recognised oncologic emergency.
- C. Mild transient fatigue after a clinic visit  
  _Rationale:_ Mild fatigue is common and not an emergency.
- D. A healed surgical scar  
  _Rationale:_ A healed scar is not an emergency.
