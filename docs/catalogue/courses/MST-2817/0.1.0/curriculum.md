## Learning outcomes

1. Measure length, mass and capacity using standard units
2. Convert between simple related units such as cm and m
3. Read scales and estimate measurements sensibly
4. Collect and record data in tables and tally charts
5. Draw and read bar charts and pictograms
6. Answer questions by interpreting a chart or table

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2817-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) How many centimetres are there in 2 metres?

- A. 200 cm **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: there are 100 cm in 1 metre, so 2 metres is 200 cm.
- B. 20 cm  
  _Rationale:_ That would be only part of one metre; 2 m is 200 cm.
- C. 2 cm  
  _Rationale:_ 2 cm is much smaller than 2 metres.
- D. 2000 cm  
  _Rationale:_ 2000 cm would be 20 metres, not 2 metres.

**MST-2817-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO things a bar chart helps you do. (Select TWO.)

- A. Compare how popular different choices are **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: bar heights let you compare categories at a glance.
- B. See which category has the most **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the tallest bar shows the largest value.
- C. Measure the mass of an object  
  _Rationale:_ Mass is measured with scales, not read from a bar chart.
- D. Convert centimetres to metres  
  _Rationale:_ Unit conversion is arithmetic, not something a bar chart shows.

**MST-2817-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A tally chart shows four marks crossed by a fifth for 'red'. How many chose red?

- A. 5 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a group of four marks crossed by a fifth stands for 5.
- B. 4  
  _Rationale:_ The crossing mark is the fifth, making 5 in total.
- C. 1  
  _Rationale:_ A single crossed group counts all five marks together.
- D. 10  
  _Rationale:_ One crossed group of tallies is 5, not 10.
