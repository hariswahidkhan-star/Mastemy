## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1105-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) To improve the chance that an answer engine cites your page, you should:

- A. Hide the key facts deep in long, meandering prose  
  _Rationale:_ Buried facts are hard for answer engines to extract and cite.
- B. Answer the likely question clearly and early, with citable facts **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: answer-first, fact-rich content is easier to extract and attribute.
- C. Remove all headings and structure  
  _Rationale:_ Removing structure makes extraction harder, not easier.
- D. Fill the page with unrelated keywords  
  _Rationale:_ Keyword stuffing does not help extraction or citability.

**MST-1105-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why is classic keyword rank tracking insufficient for measuring AI-search visibility?

- A. AI answers never mention brands  
  _Rationale:_ AI answers do mention and cite brands and sources.
- B. Visibility now depends on whether you are cited or mentioned in generated answers, not just a ranked position **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: generative answers require tracking citations and mentions, not only rank.
- C. Keywords no longer exist at all  
  _Rationale:_ Keywords still matter; the point is that rank alone no longer captures visibility.
- D. Rank tracking is always perfectly accurate  
  _Rationale:_ Accuracy is not the issue; coverage of a new surface is.

**MST-1105-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are real trade-offs when deciding whether to allow AI crawler access? (Select TWO)

- A. Allowing access can increase the chance of being cited in AI answers **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: access is often a precondition for inclusion and citation.
- B. Blocking access protects content but may reduce AI-answer visibility **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: blocking trades visibility for control over content use.
- C. Blocking AI crawlers guarantees higher classic rankings  
  _Rationale:_ AI crawler policy does not control classic organic rankings.
- D. Allowing access removes the need for any structured data  
  _Rationale:_ Structured data still aids extraction regardless of access policy.
