## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1089-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In a RACI chart, the 'Accountable' role is the person who:

- A. Does the hands-on work  
  _Rationale:_ That is the Responsible role, not Accountable.
- B. Is ultimately answerable and owns the decision, and should be a single person **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: accountability should rest with one clearly named owner.
- C. Is asked for input before a decision  
  _Rationale:_ That is the Consulted role.
- D. Is told after the decision is made  
  _Rationale:_ That is the Informed role.

**MST-1089-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) The principle that 'structure follows strategy' implies that a redesign should start from:

- A. The preferences of the most senior leaders  
  _Rationale:_ Personal preference is not a design basis.
- B. What the organisation is trying to achieve **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the strategy defines what the structure must enable.
- C. The current org chart  
  _Rationale:_ Starting from today's chart tends to preserve its flaws.
- D. The available office space  
  _Rationale:_ Facilities do not determine organisational structure.

**MST-1089-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are likely causes of chronic handoff failures between teams? (Select TWO)

- A. No one clearly owns the interface between the teams **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: unowned interfaces drop work between the teams.
- B. Responsibilities overlap so each assumes the other will act **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: overlapping duties create gaps when both wait.
- C. Each team has a clear single point of contact  
  _Rationale:_ A clear contact reduces, not causes, handoff failure.
- D. The process steps are documented and agreed  
  _Rationale:_ Agreed steps help handoffs rather than break them.
