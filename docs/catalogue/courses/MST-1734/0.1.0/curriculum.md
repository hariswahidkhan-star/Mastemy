## Learning outcomes

1. Purpose of management reporting
2. Designing KPIs
3. Variance and performance analysis
4. Dashboards and visualisation
5. Narrative and decisions

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess building a live reporting system or a specific organisation's chosen KPI set.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1734-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A 'leading' KPI differs from a 'lagging' KPI in that a leading KPI:

- A. Signals likely future performance before results appear **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: leading indicators are predictive of future outcomes.
- B. Only reports what already happened  
  _Rationale:_ That describes a lagging indicator.
- C. Is always a financial figure  
  _Rationale:_ Leading KPIs are often operational, not financial.
- D. Cannot be measured  
  _Rationale:_ Leading KPIs are measurable, e.g. pipeline or bookings.

**MST-1734-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select ALL that apply) Which two features make a management report genuinely useful? (Select TWO) (Select TWO)

- A. It supports a specific decision or action **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: useful reports drive decisions.
- B. It explains variances, not just lists numbers **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: explanation turns data into insight.
- C. It includes every available metric  
  _Rationale:_ Overloading with metrics obscures the message.
- D. It uses as many charts as possible for decoration  
  _Rationale:_ Decorative clutter reduces clarity.

**MST-1734-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Actual spend is 120 against a budget of 100. The variance is best reported as:

- A. 20 unfavourable (over budget), with an explanation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: spending 20 over budget is an unfavourable variance needing explanation.
- B. 20 favourable  
  _Rationale:_ Spending more than budget is unfavourable, not favourable.
- C. No variance  
  _Rationale:_ There is a 20 difference.
- D. A 20% profit  
  _Rationale:_ A spending overrun is not a profit figure.
