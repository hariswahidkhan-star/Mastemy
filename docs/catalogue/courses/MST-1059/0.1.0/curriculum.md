## Learning outcomes

1. Explain how nonprofit finance differs from for-profit finance
2. Account for restricted and unrestricted funds
3. Prepare nonprofit budgets and monitor grant spending
4. Report to funders, boards and regulators transparently
5. Apply financial controls and demonstrate accountability

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1059-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Restricted funds in a nonprofit are:

- A. Resources that may only be used for the purpose specified by the donor or funder **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: restricted funds carry donor-imposed limits on use.
- B. Funds the organisation can spend on anything  
  _Rationale:_ That describes unrestricted funds.
- C. Always cash held in a separate bank account  
  _Rationale:_ Restriction is about permitted use, not necessarily a separate account.
- D. Profit distributed to owners  
  _Rationale:_ Nonprofits have no owners receiving profit.

**MST-1059-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO practices support financial accountability in a nonprofit? (Select TWO.)

- A. Transparent reporting of both program and administrative costs **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: honest cost reporting builds funder and public trust.
- B. Segregating duties over donation handling and recording **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: segregation protects against misuse of funds.
- C. Spending restricted grants on unrelated running costs  
  _Rationale:_ This breaches donor restrictions and trust.
- D. Avoiding any external reporting  
  _Rationale:_ Nonprofits owe accountability to funders and regulators.

**MST-1059-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Overstating the program-expense ratio by hiding real administrative costs is problematic because it:

- A. Misleads donors and regulators about how money is used **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: disguising admin costs misrepresents the organisation's finances.
- B. Is required by nonprofit accounting  
  _Rationale:_ Honest classification, not disguise, is required.
- C. Increases the organisation's actual impact  
  _Rationale:_ It changes presentation, not real impact.
- D. Has no accountability consequence  
  _Rationale:_ It undermines accountability and can breach regulation.
