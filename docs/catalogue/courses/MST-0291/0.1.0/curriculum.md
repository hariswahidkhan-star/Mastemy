## Learning outcomes

1. Apply HR behavioural competencies to workplace situational-judgement scenarios
2. Analyse people and organisation HR knowledge to recommend talent and rewards decisions
3. Evaluate workplace, risk and strategy knowledge including US employment-law constraints
4. Integrate HR knowledge and competencies into a coherent HR plan

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0291-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A department has 30% annual turnover concentrated in the first 90 days of employment. Which intervention most directly targets that pattern?

- A. A structured onboarding and 30/60/90-day check-in programme **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: early-tenure turnover is most directly reduced by structured onboarding and early check-ins.
- B. An annual engagement survey  
  _Rationale:_ A yearly survey is too infrequent to affect first-90-day attrition.
- C. A market pay study for senior roles  
  _Rationale:_ Senior-role pay does not address new-hire early departures.
- D. A new performance-rating scale  
  _Rationale:_ Rating scales act on existing staff, not the onboarding window.

**MST-0291-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which US law most directly governs advance-notice obligations for certain large layoffs?

- A. The WARN Act **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the WARN Act sets advance-notice duties for qualifying mass layoffs and plant closings.
- B. The FLSA  
  _Rationale:_ The FLSA covers wages and hours, not layoff notice.
- C. The ADA  
  _Rationale:_ The ADA addresses disability accommodation, not layoff notice.
- D. ERISA  
  _Rationale:_ ERISA governs benefit plans, not layoff notice.

**MST-0291-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) An HR business partner wants to strengthen the Ethical Practice competency on their team. Select TWO actions that most directly support that competency.

- A. Establish a confidential channel for reporting misconduct **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a safe reporting channel is a core ethical-practice control.
- B. Publish and model a clear conflict-of-interest policy **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: transparent conflict-of-interest handling is central to ethical practice.
- C. Increase the annual training budget for technical HRIS skills  
  _Rationale:_ HRIS training builds technology skills, not the ethical-practice competency.
- D. Shorten the recruiting cycle time  
  _Rationale:_ Faster hiring is an efficiency goal unrelated to ethical practice.
- E. Add more questions to the engagement survey  
  _Rationale:_ Survey length does not build ethical practice.
