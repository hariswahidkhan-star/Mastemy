## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0030-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under the accruals concept, when should revenue from a credit sale be recognised?

- A. When the performance obligation is satisfied and control passes to the customer **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: revenue is recognised when control of the goods or service transfers, not when cash is received.
- B. When the cash is received from the customer  
  _Rationale:_ That is the cash basis, not accruals; recognition is tied to transfer of control, not receipt.
- C. When the sales order is placed by the customer  
  _Rationale:_ An order alone creates no performance yet; nothing has been delivered or controlled.
- D. At the end of the reporting period regardless of delivery  
  _Rationale:_ Period end does not trigger recognition; satisfaction of the obligation does.

**MST-0030-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A company's current ratio is 0.8. Which action would most directly improve its short-term liquidity position?

- A. Negotiating longer payment terms with suppliers **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: extending payables delays cash outflows, improving the short-term liquidity and the current ratio's denominator timing.
- B. Purchasing additional inventory on cash terms  
  _Rationale:_ Paying cash for inventory reduces cash, a more liquid current asset, without improving liquidity.
- C. Repaying a long-term loan early using cash  
  _Rationale:_ Using cash to repay debt reduces current assets and worsens short-term liquidity.
- D. Revaluing non-current assets upwards  
  _Rationale:_ Revaluing non-current assets does not affect current assets or current liabilities.

**MST-0030-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO items that are presented within the statement of cash flows under operating activities (indirect method).

- A. Depreciation added back to profit **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: depreciation is a non-cash expense added back to reconcile profit to operating cash flow.
- B. Changes in trade receivables and payables **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: movements in working-capital items are operating adjustments under the indirect method.
- C. Proceeds from issuing share capital  
  _Rationale:_ Share issues are financing activities, not operating.
- D. Purchase of property, plant and equipment  
  _Rationale:_ Capital expenditure is an investing activity.
- E. Dividends paid to shareholders  
  _Rationale:_ Dividends paid are classified as financing (or separately), not operating adjustments.
