## Learning outcomes

1. Identify appropriate uses of AI in research and academic writing
2. Use AI to support literature review and drafting responsibly
3. Verify sources and avoid fabricated citations
4. Uphold research integrity, authorship and disclosure norms
5. Recognise bias, privacy and reproducibility risks with AI

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A graduate researcher wants to use AI to speed up a literature review and manuscript drafting. Decide where AI can assist, verify every source and claim, uphold authorship and disclosure norms, and avoid fabricated citations or undisclosed AI use.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1383-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An AI tool provides a perfectly formatted citation for a claim. What must the researcher do before using it?

- A. Verify the reference actually exists and supports the claim **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI commonly fabricates plausible-looking citations.
- B. Use it because the formatting is correct  
  _Rationale:_ Formatting does not prove the source exists.
- C. Assume AI never invents references  
  _Rationale:_ AI frequently invents references.
- D. Cite it without reading the source  
  _Rationale:_ Citing unverified, unread sources breaches integrity.

**MST-1383-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO practices uphold research integrity when using AI? (Select TWO.)

- A. Disclose how AI was used, per journal and institutional policy **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: transparency about AI use is a core integrity norm.
- B. Keep a human author accountable for all content **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: accountability cannot be delegated to a tool.
- C. List the AI tool as a co-author  
  _Rationale:_ AI tools generally cannot meet authorship criteria.
- D. Use AI to draft results without running the study  
  _Rationale:_ Fabricating results is research misconduct.

**MST-1383-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why is relying only on an AI summary of a key paper risky in a literature review?

- A. The summary may omit or distort findings the review depends on **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI summaries can be incomplete or inaccurate.
- B. Summaries are always complete and accurate  
  _Rationale:_ AI summaries can miss or misstate key points.
- C. Reading the paper is never necessary  
  _Rationale:_ Verifying against the source is essential.
- D. AI understands every methodology perfectly  
  _Rationale:_ AI can misread methods and results.
