## Learning outcomes

1. Manage the internal audit activity and plan risk-based engagements
2. Plan individual assurance and consulting engagements
3. Perform the engagement and gather evidence
4. Communicate results and monitor remediation

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0092-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An auditor selects 25 items judgmentally from a population of 10,000 invoices. What is the key limitation?

- A. Results cannot be statistically projected to the population **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a judgmental sample does not support statistical projection.
- B. The sample is too large to evaluate  
  _Rationale:_ 25 items is small, not too large.
- C. It guarantees no errors exist  
  _Rationale:_ No sample can guarantee the absence of errors.
- D. It must always be 10% of the population  
  _Rationale:_ There is no fixed 10% rule.

**MST-0092-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) When should engagement objectives be finalized?

- A. After a preliminary risk assessment of the area **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: objectives follow understanding the process and its risks.
- B. Before learning anything about the process  
  _Rationale:_ Setting objectives before understanding risk is premature.
- C. Only after the report is written  
  _Rationale:_ Objectives guide fieldwork and must precede reporting.
- D. They are never documented  
  _Rationale:_ Objectives must be documented.

**MST-0092-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) A well-structured audit finding typically includes which TWO of the following elements? (Select TWO)

- A. Condition (what is) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: condition is a core element of a finding.
- B. Criteria (what should be) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: criteria is a core element of a finding.
- C. The auditor’s salary  
  _Rationale:_ Irrelevant to a finding.
- D. A list of unrelated compliments  
  _Rationale:_ Not an element of a finding.
