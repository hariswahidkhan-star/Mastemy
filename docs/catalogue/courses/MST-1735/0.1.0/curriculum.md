## Learning outcomes

1. Capital budgeting basics
2. Payback and accounting methods
3. Net present value
4. IRR and alternatives
5. Risk and the decision

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; this is general education, not investment advice, and it does not assess a real capital-investment decision for a specific firm.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1735-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under the NPV rule, a project should normally be accepted when its NPV is:

- A. Positive **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a positive NPV adds value and should be accepted.
- B. Negative  
  _Rationale:_ A negative NPV destroys value and should be rejected.
- C. Exactly zero always  
  _Rationale:_ Zero NPV is the breakeven, not a clear accept signal.
- D. The largest in absolute size regardless of sign  
  _Rationale:_ Size does not override a negative sign.

**MST-1735-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select ALL that apply) Which two are weaknesses of the simple payback method? (Select TWO) (Select TWO)

- A. It ignores cash flows after the payback point **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: payback disregards later cash flows.
- B. It ignores the time value of money **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: simple payback does not discount.
- C. It always agrees with NPV  
  _Rationale:_ Payback can conflict with NPV.
- D. It requires a discount rate to compute  
  _Rationale:_ Simple payback needs no discount rate, which is part of the problem.

**MST-1735-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A cost already incurred that cannot be recovered should be treated in a project appraisal as:

- A. Irrelevant (a sunk cost) and excluded from the decision **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: sunk costs do not change with the decision.
- B. A relevant cash outflow to include  
  _Rationale:_ Sunk costs are not relevant to the decision.
- C. A guaranteed future benefit  
  _Rationale:_ A past cost is not a future benefit.
- D. The discount rate  
  _Rationale:_ It is unrelated to the discount rate.
