## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0150-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Two features have equal job size. Feature X has far higher cost of delay than Feature Y. Using WSJF, which should be sequenced first?

- A. Feature X, because WSJF favours higher cost of delay for equal job size **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: WSJF = cost of delay / job size; with equal job size the higher cost of delay yields a higher WSJF.
- B. Feature Y, because smaller cost of delay is safer  
  _Rationale:_ WSJF sequences the higher value-per-size first, not the lower cost of delay.
- C. Whichever was requested first  
  _Rationale:_ Request order is not a WSJF input.
- D. Both must wait until the next PI  
  _Rationale:_ Nothing in WSJF requires deferring both features.

**MST-0150-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the primary purpose of PI objectives written by a team during PI Planning?

- A. To summarize the business outcomes the team commits to for the PI **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: PI objectives express intended business outcomes and align the team with ART goals.
- B. To list every user story in the plan  
  _Rationale:_ PI objectives summarize outcomes, not an exhaustive story list.
- C. To replace the iteration backlog  
  _Rationale:_ They do not replace iteration backlogs; they describe outcomes.
- D. To set individual performance targets  
  _Rationale:_ They are team outcomes, not individual targets.

**MST-0150-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO activities are core Product Owner responsibilities during iteration execution? (Select TWO)

- A. Accepting completed stories against acceptance criteria **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the PO confirms stories meet acceptance criteria.
- B. Maintaining and refining the team backlog **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ongoing backlog refinement keeps upcoming work ready.
- C. Writing the ART's architectural runway  
  _Rationale:_ Architectural runway is driven by System Architect/Engineering, not the PO.
- D. Approving the organization's annual budget  
  _Rationale:_ Budgeting is a portfolio/Lean Portfolio Management concern, not a PO task.
- E. Managing individual team member appraisals  
  _Rationale:_ People management is not a PO responsibility in SAFe.
