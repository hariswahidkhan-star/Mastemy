## Learning outcomes

1. Identify where AI adds value across the procurement and sourcing cycle
2. Use AI to assist spend analysis and supplier research with verification
3. Apply AI to contract and document review while keeping a human in the loop
4. Recognise bias, data-confidentiality and vendor-lock risks in procurement AI
5. Keep controls, auditability and fair treatment of suppliers when using AI

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Hands-on operation of the tools and workflows taught, the quality of live outputs, and professional judgement on the job are taught by video demonstration and model-answer analysis; the certificate assessment uses single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only and never requires uploads, recorded work, graded prompts or peer review.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1193-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which procurement task is the best fit for AI assistance with human review?

- A. Summarising and categorising a large spend dataset for analyst review **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: high-volume summarisation with human verification suits AI well.
- B. Making the final supplier award decision autonomously  
  _Rationale:_ The award decision requires accountable human judgement.
- C. Signing the contract on behalf of the company  
  _Rationale:_ Contract execution is an authority that AI cannot hold.
- D. Setting procurement policy without oversight  
  _Rationale:_ Policy requires human governance, not autonomous AI.

**MST-1193-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) AI returns a claim that a supplier is in financial distress. Which TWO actions are appropriate before acting? (Select TWO.)

- A. Verify the claim against a credible, current source **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI claims must be corroborated before they drive decisions.
- B. Check whether the information is current and specific to that supplier **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: outdated or mismatched data would mislead the decision.
- C. Immediately disqualify the supplier on the AI claim alone  
  _Rationale:_ Acting on an unverified claim is unfair and risky.
- D. Share the unverified claim with competing suppliers  
  _Rationale:_ Spreading unverified claims is both unfair and a confidentiality risk.

**MST-1193-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) You want AI help on a live tender but the bids are confidential. What is the safest approach?

- A. Use a controlled tool with data-protection terms and avoid entering identifying bid detail **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a controlled tool plus data minimisation protects confidentiality.
- B. Paste all bids into a free public chatbot  
  _Rationale:_ Public tools can expose confidential competitive data.
- C. Email the bids to the tool vendor for analysis  
  _Rationale:_ This breaches bidder confidentiality.
- D. Share bids with one supplier to benchmark  
  _Rationale:_ Disclosing bids to a competitor is a serious breach.
