## Learning outcomes

1. Name the main outside parts of the body
2. Say what the eyes, ears, nose, tongue and skin help us do
3. Describe how the body moves and rests
4. Explain simple ways to keep the body healthy
5. Tell why food, water and sleep matter
6. Show how to keep the body clean and safe

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2860-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which body part do we use to hear sounds?

- A. Ears **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ears let us hear sounds around us.
- B. Eyes  
  _Rationale:_ Eyes are for seeing, not hearing.
- C. Feet  
  _Rationale:_ Feet help us walk and stand.
- D. Elbow  
  _Rationale:_ The elbow helps the arm bend.

**MST-2860-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why do we need to sleep at night?

- A. So the body can rest and grow **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: sleep helps the body rest, heal and grow.
- B. So we can eat more sweets  
  _Rationale:_ Sleep is not about eating sweets.
- C. So our hair turns blue  
  _Rationale:_ Sleep does not change hair colour.
- D. So we never feel hungry again  
  _Rationale:_ We still get hungry after sleeping.

**MST-2860-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO habits keep the body healthy? (Select TWO.)

- A. Drinking water **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: water keeps the body working well.
- B. Brushing teeth **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: brushing keeps teeth clean and strong.
- C. Skipping sleep  
  _Rationale:_ Skipping sleep is not healthy.
- D. Never washing hands  
  _Rationale:_ Not washing hands spreads germs.
