## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1040-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A project's net present value is positive at the firm's cost of capital. The basic decision rule says to:

- A. Accept the project **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a positive NPV means the project adds value at the required return.
- B. Reject the project  
  _Rationale:_ Rejection applies when NPV is negative.
- C. Accept only if payback is over ten years  
  _Rationale:_ Payback length is not the NPV decision rule.
- D. Defer regardless of NPV  
  _Rationale:_ A positive NPV argues for acceptance, not automatic deferral.

**MST-1040-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) In a discounted cash flow valuation, the terminal value represents:

- A. The value of cash flows beyond the explicit forecast period **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: terminal value captures the ongoing value after the modeled years.
- B. The cash held in the bank today  
  _Rationale:_ That is current cash, not terminal value.
- C. The total of all historical profits  
  _Rationale:_ Valuation looks forward, not at accumulated past profit.
- D. The purchase price paid for comparables  
  _Rationale:_ That relates to relative valuation, not the DCF terminal value.

**MST-1040-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO statements about NPV and IRR are correct? (Select TWO)

- A. NPV is expressed as a money amount of value added at the discount rate **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: NPV measures value creation in currency terms.
- B. IRR can be misleading when comparing mutually exclusive projects of different scale **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: IRR ignores scale and can conflict with NPV rankings.
- C. A positive NPV always means a negative IRR  
  _Rationale:_ A positive NPV typically coincides with an IRR above the discount rate.
- D. Payback period accounts fully for the time value of money  
  _Rationale:_ Simple payback ignores discounting and cash flows after payback.
