## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0149-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A team repeatedly misses its iteration goals because work is pulled in mid-iteration by a manager outside the team. What is the Scrum Master's most appropriate first action?

- A. Coach the stakeholder and team on protecting iteration scope and using the backlog for new requests **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a servant-leader Scrum Master addresses the systemic impediment by coaching stakeholders and reinforcing WIP/scope agreements.
- B. Tell the team to work overtime to absorb the extra work  
  _Rationale:_ This hides the impediment and harms sustainable pace; it does not address the cause.
- C. Add the new work silently to the current iteration  
  _Rationale:_ Accepting unmanaged scope change undermines predictability and the team's commitment.
- D. Escalate immediately to the RTE before speaking with anyone  
  _Rationale:_ Escalation may follow, but the Scrum Master should first coach locally; jumping straight to escalation skips the servant-leader role.

**MST-0149-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) During PI Planning, two teams discover a dependency that cannot be resolved within the planned iterations. Where is this best captured and made visible?

- A. On the program board as a dependency with a planned resolution **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the program (ART planning) board visualizes cross-team dependencies and milestones so they can be managed.
- B. In a private note kept by one Scrum Master  
  _Rationale:_ A private note is not transparent and will not be managed across the ART.
- C. Only in the team's iteration backlog  
  _Rationale:_ The iteration backlog does not make the cross-team nature of the dependency visible.
- D. Deferred to the next PI with no record  
  _Rationale:_ Deferring without a record loses the dependency and risks the PI objectives.

**MST-0149-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO behaviours are consistent with a Scrum Master acting as a servant leader on the ART? (Select TWO)

- A. Removing impediments the team cannot resolve on its own **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: unblocking the team is a core servant-leader responsibility.
- B. Facilitating team events so the team can self-organize **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: facilitation that builds the team's own capability is servant leadership.
- C. Assigning tasks to each team member each morning  
  _Rationale:_ Directive task assignment undermines self-organization.
- D. Approving all design decisions before work begins  
  _Rationale:_ Centralizing decisions removes team ownership and is not servant leadership.
- E. Reporting individual productivity metrics to management  
  _Rationale:_ Individual surveillance metrics damage trust and are not a servant-leader practice.
