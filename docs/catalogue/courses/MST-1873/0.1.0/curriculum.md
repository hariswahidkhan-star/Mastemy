## Learning outcomes

1. Explain story structure and narrative fundamentals
2. Develop character, conflict and theme
3. Adapt storytelling to formats and platforms

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A creator must shape a brand's story for video and short-form social. They must apply a clear structure, develop character, conflict and theme, and adapt the story to each platform's format, defending the hook-and-stakes approach over front-loaded exposition.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1873-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A strong story opening (hook) should quickly establish:

- A. A reason to care and what is at stake **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: hooks create interest through stakes.
- B. The full backstory in detail first  
  _Rationale:_ Front-loaded exposition loses the audience.
- C. The credits and legal notices  
  _Rationale:_ Not a narrative hook.
- D. The export file format  
  _Rationale:_ Irrelevant to storytelling.

**MST-1873-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO drive compelling conflict? (Select TWO.)

- A. A character with a clear goal **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: goals create the desire that conflict obstructs.
- B. An obstacle that stands between character and goal **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the obstacle is the conflict.
- C. Conflict invented with no character motivation  
  _Rationale:_ Unmotivated conflict feels hollow.
- D. Removing all stakes from the story  
  _Rationale:_ No stakes means no tension.

**MST-1873-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Adapting a story across platforms means:

- A. Reshaping form and pacing to suit each platform **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: formats differ, so the telling adapts.
- B. Posting the identical cut everywhere  
  _Rationale:_ Ignores platform differences.
- C. Changing the theme on every platform  
  _Rationale:_ The core theme can stay consistent.
- D. Removing the character entirely  
  _Rationale:_ Character still anchors the story.
