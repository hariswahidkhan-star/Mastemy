## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1702-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under COSO, a monthly bank reconciliation that catches errors after they occur is best described as which control type?

- A. Detective **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it detects errors after the fact rather than preventing them.
- B. Preventive  
  _Rationale:_ Preventive controls stop errors before they happen.
- C. Corrective  
  _Rationale:_ Corrective controls fix an identified problem; reconciliation detects it.
- D. Entity-level  
  _Rationale:_ This is a process-level activity, not an entity-level control.

**MST-1702-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select ALL that apply) Which two conditions together make a control deficiency a material weakness? (Select TWO) (Select TWO)

- A. A reasonable possibility of a misstatement occurring **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reasonable possibility of occurrence is one criterion.
- B. The potential misstatement is material **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a material magnitude is the other criterion.
- C. The control is manual rather than automated  
  _Rationale:_ Manual controls are not automatically material weaknesses.
- D. The deficiency was found by internal audit  
  _Rationale:_ Who finds it does not determine its severity.

**MST-1702-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which SOX section requires the CEO and CFO to certify the accuracy of financial reports each period?

- A. Section 302 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: Section 302 covers executive certification of reports.
- B. Section 404  
  _Rationale:_ Section 404 covers management's assessment of ICFR.
- C. Section 101  
  _Rationale:_ Section 101 established the PCAOB, not certification.
- D. Section 906  
  _Rationale:_ Section 906 addresses criminal certification, not the periodic report accuracy certification tested here.
