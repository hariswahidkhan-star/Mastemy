## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the FP&A business-partnering operating model and stakeholder map
2. Build driver-based plans and rolling forecasts
3. Design budgets and targets that are realistic and owned
4. Analyse variances and write decision-useful commentary
5. Support decisions with business cases and scenarios
6. Communicate insight with influence and integrity

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A business-unit head is missing its margin target and blames 'the market'. As the FP&A partner, build a driver-based forecast and a price/volume/mix bridge, design a scenario for a recovery plan, and present a one-page recommendation that challenges the sponsor's optimism while keeping the relationship.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1038-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An FP&A partner is asked only to 'explain the variance'. What makes the commentary decision-useful?

- A. Identify the business cause and recommend an action, not just the number **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: decision-useful commentary links the variance to a cause and an action.
- B. Restate the variance amount in words  
  _Rationale:_ That repeats the number without insight.
- C. List every line item that moved  
  _Rationale:_ Volume is not insight; it buries the cause.
- D. Blame the market without evidence  
  _Rationale:_ Attribution without evidence is not analysis.

**MST-1038-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO inputs most improve a driver-based forecast over a line-item budget? (Select TWO.)

- A. Operational drivers such as units, price and conversion rates **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: drivers connect the model to the business.
- B. A rolling forecast horizon updated with actuals **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: rolling updates keep the forecast current.
- C. Simply copying last year and adding a percentage  
  _Rationale:_ That ignores the drivers of change.
- D. Hiding assumptions so the number looks firm  
  _Rationale:_ Opaque assumptions reduce trust and usefulness.

**MST-1038-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A sponsor's business case shows only an optimistic base case. What should the FP&A partner add?

- A. Scenario and sensitivity analysis showing the downside **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: scenarios expose the risk in a single-point estimate.
- B. A larger font for the NPV  
  _Rationale:_ Presentation does not address the risk.
- C. Removal of the assumptions page  
  _Rationale:_ Hiding assumptions is the opposite of good practice.
- D. A higher growth rate to improve the NPV  
  _Rationale:_ Inflating inputs is not objective analysis.
