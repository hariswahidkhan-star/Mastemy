## Learning outcomes

1. Identify everyday office tasks AI can speed up
2. Use AI to draft, summarise and organise work responsibly
3. Write effective prompts for common workplace tasks
4. Verify AI output and protect confidential information
5. Adopt AI habits that save time without creating risk

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1381-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An employee wants to summarise a confidential contract using a free public AI tool. What is the main concern?

- A. Confidential data may be stored or reused outside the organisation's control **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: pasting sensitive data into an unapproved tool risks exposure.
- B. The summary will be too short  
  _Rationale:_ Length is not the key risk here.
- C. There is no risk with contracts  
  _Rationale:_ Contracts often contain confidential information.
- D. AI tools cannot read contracts  
  _Rationale:_ The concern is data handling, not capability.

**MST-1381-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO habits make AI help at work more reliable? (Select TWO.)

- A. Read and fact-check AI output before sending it **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: review catches errors and fabrications.
- B. Verify any specific facts or figures AI provides **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI can invent plausible but wrong details.
- C. Forward AI drafts without reading them  
  _Rationale:_ Unread drafts can contain errors or wrong tone.
- D. Assume AI never makes mistakes  
  _Rationale:_ AI regularly produces confident errors.

**MST-1381-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A worker gets a vague AI answer to 'write a reply'. What most improves the result?

- A. Adding context, audience, tone and the key points to include **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: specific context and constraints guide better output.
- B. Repeating the same vague prompt louder  
  _Rationale:_ Repetition without detail does not help.
- C. Removing all context to keep it short  
  _Rationale:_ Less context usually gives a weaker result.
- D. Asking the AI to guess what is needed  
  _Rationale:_ Guessing yields generic, less useful output.
