## Learning outcomes

1. Explain why change efforts succeed or fail and pick a model
2. Analyse stakeholders and assess organisational readiness
3. Craft a change vision and secure effective sponsorship
4. Plan communication and engage people through resistance
5. Enable adoption and measure behaviour change
6. Sustain change responsibly and ethically

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A company is rolling out a new operating system that changes how 400 people work. Diagnose readiness, map stakeholders, craft a vision and sponsorship plan, design communication and enablement, define adoption metrics, and plan reinforcement so the change sticks and is handled fairly.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1077-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A leader announces a change once in a town hall and expects adoption. Why is this likely to fail?

- A. Change requires sustained communication, sponsorship and enablement, not a single announcement **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: adoption is built over time, not declared once.
- B. Town halls are never useful  
  _Rationale:_ They can help; the problem is relying on one event.
- C. People always resist any change equally  
  _Rationale:_ Resistance varies and can be managed.
- D. Announcements guarantee behaviour change  
  _Rationale:_ An announcement is not behaviour change.

**MST-1077-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO signs suggest low change readiness? (Select TWO.)

- A. Past change initiatives stalled and were not followed through **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a poor track record lowers readiness.
- B. Key stakeholders are silent and disengaged **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: silence often masks resistance, not agreement.
- C. Sponsors actively and visibly back the change  
  _Rationale:_ Active sponsorship raises readiness.
- D. Teams ask informed questions about the plan  
  _Rationale:_ Engaged questions are a positive sign.

**MST-1077-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) After go-live, what best indicates the change has actually succeeded?

- A. Sustained new behaviour and adoption metrics over time **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: success is sustained behaviour, not the go-live date.
- B. The project closed on schedule  
  _Rationale:_ On-time delivery is not the same as adoption.
- C. The launch communication was sent  
  _Rationale:_ Sending a message is not behaviour change.
- D. The old system was switched off  
  _Rationale:_ Switching off the old system does not prove adoption.
