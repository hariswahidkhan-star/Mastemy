## Learning outcomes

1. Understand the three-part Speaking format and timing
2. Apply fluency and coherence strategies in extended responses
3. Build lexical resource and pronunciation awareness
4. Evaluate model responses against the Speaking band descriptors (knowledge-level)

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0395-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In Speaking Part 2, the candidate has one minute to prepare and must speak for up to two minutes. What is the best use of the preparation minute?

- A. Note key points and a logical order to cover the cue-card prompts **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: brief structured notes support a coherent long turn.
- B. Write a full word-for-word script to read aloud  
  _Rationale:_ Reading a script harms fluency and natural coherence and is not allowed.
- C. Memorise an unrelated prepared answer  
  _Rationale:_ An off-topic memorised answer fails task relevance.
- D. Do nothing and improvise with no plan  
  _Rationale:_ No planning risks a disorganised, short turn.

**MST-0395-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which behaviour most improves the 'Fluency and Coherence' band?

- A. Speaking at a natural pace with logical linking and minimal breakdown **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: natural flow and coherent linking define this criterion.
- B. Speaking as fast as possible  
  _Rationale:_ Speed is not fluency; it can reduce clarity and coherence.
- C. Pausing frequently to search for rare words  
  _Rationale:_ Frequent hesitation lowers fluency.
- D. Giving only one-word answers  
  _Rationale:_ Minimal responses cannot demonstrate coherence.

**MST-0395-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO improve 'Lexical Resource' in Speaking?

- A. Using accurate topic-specific collocations **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: precise collocation shows lexical range and control.
- B. Paraphrasing when the exact word is unavailable **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: effective paraphrase is explicitly rewarded.
- C. Inserting rare idioms regardless of fit  
  _Rationale:_ Misused idioms reduce accuracy and clarity.
- D. Repeating the same basic words throughout  
  _Rationale:_ Repetition shows limited range.
- E. Spelling words aloud to show vocabulary  
  _Rationale:_ Spelling is irrelevant to spoken lexical resource.
