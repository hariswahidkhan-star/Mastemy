## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0097-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) IT governance is primarily the responsibility of:

- A. The board and executive leadership, who set direction and oversight **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: governance is a board/executive accountability for direction and oversight.
- B. Individual help-desk technicians  
  _Rationale:_ Front-line staff execute operations, they do not set governance.
- C. Only external auditors  
  _Rationale:_ Auditors provide assurance, not governance direction.
- D. Software vendors  
  _Rationale:_ Vendors supply products; they do not govern the enterprise.

**MST-0097-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) The core aim of benefits realization in enterprise IT governance is to:

- A. Ensure IT investments deliver their intended business value **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: benefits realization tracks that investments achieve expected value.
- B. Maximize the number of projects started  
  _Rationale:_ Starting projects is not the same as realizing benefits.
- C. Minimize all IT spending to zero  
  _Rationale:_ Governance balances value and cost, not zero spend.
- D. Replace strategy with technology choices  
  _Rationale:_ Technology serves strategy; it does not replace it.

**MST-0097-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO components of an effective IT risk optimization approach.

- A. Identifying and assessing IT-related risks against risk appetite **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: risk optimization starts with identifying and assessing risk vs appetite.
- B. Defining risk responses and monitoring residual risk **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: responses and ongoing monitoring are essential to optimization.
- C. Ignoring risk because controls are expensive  
  _Rationale:_ Ignoring risk is the opposite of optimization.
- D. Transferring every risk regardless of cost-benefit  
  _Rationale:_ Blanket transfer ignores cost-benefit and appetite.
- E. Documenting risks but never acting on them  
  _Rationale:_ Documentation without response is not optimization.
