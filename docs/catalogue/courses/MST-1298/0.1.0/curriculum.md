## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the federal acquisition framework and the contract management body of knowledge (knowledge practice)
2. Apply pre-award processes: planning, solicitation and source selection under federal rules
3. Describe award, administration and contract changes in the federal context
4. Analyse closeout, compliance and ethics in federal contracting

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1298-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Who generally has the authority to obligate the federal government under a contract?

- A. Any project team member  
  _Rationale:_ Team members generally cannot bind the government.
- B. A warranted contracting officer acting within their authority **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: only a warranted contracting officer, within the limits of the warrant, can bind the government.
- C. The contractor's sales representative  
  _Rationale:_ The contractor cannot obligate the government.
- D. Any agency employee who attends the meeting  
  _Rationale:_ Attendance confers no contracting authority.

**MST-1298-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A contractor performs extra work on the verbal say-so of a staffer with no contracting authority. What is this best called?

- A. A properly executed modification  
  _Rationale:_ A proper modification requires authorised action, absent here.
- B. An unauthorised commitment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: direction by someone without authority creating an obligation is an unauthorised commitment.
- C. A standard option exercise  
  _Rationale:_ Options are exercised formally by the contracting officer.
- D. A closeout activity  
  _Rationale:_ This concerns performance direction, not closeout.

**MST-1298-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are proper steps in federal contract closeout?

- A. Verifying that all deliverables and services were accepted **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: confirming completion/acceptance is part of closeout.
- B. Ensuring final payment and records are settled and retained **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: final financial settlement and record retention are closeout steps.
- C. Deleting the contract file immediately after final delivery  
  _Rationale:_ Records must be retained, not destroyed, at closeout.
- D. Ignoring any outstanding disputes  
  _Rationale:_ Outstanding disputes must be resolved, not ignored.
