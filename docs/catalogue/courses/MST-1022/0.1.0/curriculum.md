## Integrative case

Advise a manufacturer deciding whether to accept a one-off discounted order: classify the relevant costs, compute the contribution and break-even impact, decide using only incremental cash flows, and present a recommendation that separates relevant from sunk and fixed costs.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1022-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which cost is NOT relevant to a decision about a future special order?

- A. A sunk cost already incurred **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: sunk costs are past and unavoidable, so they are irrelevant to future decisions.
- B. The incremental material cost of the order  
  _Rationale:_ Incremental material cost changes with the decision, so it is relevant.
- C. Extra labour needed for the order  
  _Rationale:_ Additional labour is an incremental cost and is relevant.
- D. Avoidable packaging cost  
  _Rationale:_ Avoidable costs differ between options and are relevant.

**MST-1022-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO inputs are required to compute the break-even point in units? (Select TWO)

- A. Total fixed costs **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: fixed costs must be covered to break even.
- B. Contribution margin per unit **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: break-even units equal fixed costs divided by contribution margin per unit.
- C. The income tax rate  
  _Rationale:_ Tax is not needed for a pre-tax break-even calculation.
- D. The dividend payout ratio  
  _Rationale:_ Dividend policy is unrelated to break-even.

**MST-1022-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the contribution margin per unit?

- A. Selling price per unit minus variable cost per unit **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: contribution margin is price less variable cost, the amount contributing to fixed costs and profit.
- B. Selling price minus total cost per unit  
  _Rationale:_ That is profit per unit, not contribution margin.
- C. Fixed cost divided by units  
  _Rationale:_ That is fixed cost per unit, not contribution margin.
- D. Revenue minus cost of goods sold  
  _Rationale:_ That is gross profit, not contribution margin.
