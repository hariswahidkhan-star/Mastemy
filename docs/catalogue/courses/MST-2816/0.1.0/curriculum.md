## Learning outcomes

1. Classify 2D shapes by their sides and angles
2. Identify right angles and compare angles as larger or smaller
3. Recognise lines of symmetry in simple shapes
4. Measure and calculate the perimeter of rectilinear shapes
5. Find the area of rectangles by counting and by multiplying
6. Distinguish between perimeter and area in problems

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2816-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the perimeter of a rectangle with sides 5 cm and 3 cm?

- A. 16 cm **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: perimeter is 5 + 3 + 5 + 3 = 16 cm, the distance all the way around.
- B. 15 cm  
  _Rationale:_ 15 is the area (5 x 3), not the perimeter.
- C. 8 cm  
  _Rationale:_ 8 is only one length plus one width; the shape has two of each.
- D. 10 cm  
  _Rationale:_ That counts only the two length sides, not the widths.

**MST-2816-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO statements that are true about a square. (Select TWO.)

- A. It has four equal sides **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: all four sides of a square are the same length.
- B. It has four right angles **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: every corner of a square is a right angle.
- C. It has only two sides the same  
  _Rationale:_ All four sides are equal, not just two.
- D. It has no lines of symmetry  
  _Rationale:_ A square has four lines of symmetry.

**MST-2816-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A rectangle is 6 cm long and 4 cm wide. What is its area?

- A. 24 square cm **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: area of a rectangle is length x width, 6 x 4 = 24.
- B. 20 square cm  
  _Rationale:_ 20 is the perimeter (6 + 4 + 6 + 4), not the area.
- C. 10 square cm  
  _Rationale:_ That adds the length and width; area multiplies them.
- D. 12 square cm  
  _Rationale:_ 12 is 6 x 2; area here is 6 x 4 = 24.
