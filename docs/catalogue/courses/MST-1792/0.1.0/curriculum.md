## Learning outcomes

1. Quality concepts
2. Quality planning
3. Quality control
4. Quality assurance and systems
5. Continuous improvement

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Selection items cannot demonstrate performing a live audit or inspection; hands-on practice belongs on site.

## Integrative case

A supplier keeps shipping defective parts and inspection is not catching them all. The learner must map quality costs, turn customer needs into specifications, select control tools, distinguish QA from QC, and run PDCA with root-cause analysis, then recommend a quality management approach.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1792-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Prevention costs are incurred to:

- A. Stop defects from occurring in the first place **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: prevention costs (training, design) avoid defects, lowering failure cost.
- B. Inspect finished products for defects  
  _Rationale:_ That is an appraisal cost.
- C. Rework products that failed  
  _Rationale:_ That is an internal failure cost.
- D. Handle customer complaints after delivery  
  _Rationale:_ That is an external failure cost.

**MST-1792-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are quality assurance (not control) activities? (Select TWO.)

- A. Defining and auditing the process that makes the product **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: QA focuses on the process to prevent defects.
- B. Training staff and standardising procedures **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: building capability is a process-focused QA activity.
- C. Measuring a finished part against its tolerance  
  _Rationale:_ That is quality control.
- D. Sorting good units from defective units  
  _Rationale:_ That is quality control inspection.

**MST-1792-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A Pareto chart is used to:

- A. Show which few causes account for most of the problems **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: Pareto highlights the vital few causes to tackle first.
- B. Plot a process variable over time  
  _Rationale:_ That is a run or control chart.
- C. Show the steps in a process  
  _Rationale:_ That is a flowchart.
- D. Display the spread of a measurement  
  _Rationale:_ That is a histogram.
