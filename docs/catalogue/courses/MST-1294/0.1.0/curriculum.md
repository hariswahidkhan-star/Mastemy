## Learning outcomes

1. Explain integrative green-building concepts and the structure of LEED rating systems
2. Describe LEED credit categories (Location/Transportation, Sustainable Sites, Water, Energy, Materials, Indoor Environmental Quality)
3. Explain the LEED certification process, prerequisites, credits and points
4. Describe synergies between systems and the role of integrative design

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1294-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In LEED, what is the difference between a prerequisite and a credit?

- A. Prerequisites are mandatory with no points; credits are optional and earn points **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: prerequisites must be met to certify but award no points, while credits are optional and contribute points.
- B. Prerequisites earn the most points; credits earn none  
  _Rationale:_ Prerequisites award no points; this is reversed.
- C. They are the same thing  
  _Rationale:_ They differ: one is mandatory, the other optional.
- D. Credits are mandatory and prerequisites are optional  
  _Rationale:_ This reverses their definitions.

**MST-1294-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which strategy most directly supports the Location and Transportation category goals?

- A. Selecting a site near public transit and amenities to reduce car trips **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: Location and Transportation rewards reduced car dependence via transit access and density.
- B. Installing low-flow toilets  
  _Rationale:_ That is a Water Efficiency strategy.
- C. Specifying low-VOC paints  
  _Rationale:_ That is an Indoor Environmental Quality strategy.
- D. Adding roof insulation  
  _Rationale:_ That is primarily an Energy and Atmosphere strategy.

**MST-1294-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are LEED certification levels?

- A. Gold **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: Gold is a LEED certification level.
- B. Platinum **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: Platinum is the highest LEED certification level.
- C. Diamond  
  _Rationale:_ Diamond is not a LEED level.
- D. Bronze  
  _Rationale:_ Bronze is not a LEED level; the levels are Certified, Silver, Gold, Platinum.
- E. Titanium  
  _Rationale:_ Titanium is not a LEED level.
