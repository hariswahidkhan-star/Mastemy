## Learning outcomes

1. Define the role and evaluation criteria
2. Create consistent, job-relevant interview questions
3. Reduce bias and make a defensible decision
4. Apply confidentiality, accuracy and human-review controls to the workflow

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every course outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Hands-on operation of ChatGPT, the quality of live AI outputs, and professional judgement on accepting AI suggestions are taught by video demonstration and model-answer analysis; the certificate assessment uses single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only and never requires uploads, recorded work, graded prompts or peer review.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0483-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why use a structured interview with a fixed question set?

- A. It makes candidates comparable and reduces bias **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: consistent, job-relevant questions improve fairness and comparability.
- B. It guarantees the best hire every time  
  _Rationale:_ Structure improves fairness but cannot guarantee outcomes.
- C. It removes the need for human judgement  
  _Rationale:_ Humans still make and own the decision.
- D. It is faster to skip criteria  
  _Rationale:_ Skipping criteria undermines the purpose of structure.

**MST-0483-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) An AI screening tool is described as 'unbiased'. What is the correct stance?

- A. Review it for bias; AI can reflect biased data **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI can inherit and amplify bias, so human review and fairness checks are required.
- B. Trust the claim and skip review  
  _Rationale:_ Accepting an unverified fairness claim is risky.
- C. Use it only for protected groups  
  _Rationale:_ That would itself be discriminatory and improper.
- D. Let it make the final decision  
  _Rationale:_ Hiring decisions require human accountability.

**MST-0483-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO support a fair, defensible hiring process? (Select TWO)

- A. Scoring against a predefined scorecard **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: predefined criteria make decisions evidence-based.
- B. Documenting the evidence behind each rating **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: documentation makes the decision defensible.
- C. Relying on overall gut feel  
  _Rationale:_ Unstructured impressions invite bias.
- D. Changing questions per candidate  
  _Rationale:_ Inconsistent questions remove comparability.
