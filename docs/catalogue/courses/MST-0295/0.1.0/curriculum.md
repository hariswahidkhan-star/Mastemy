## Learning outcomes

1. Lead HR strategy and align people programs with organizational direction
2. Direct workforce planning, talent management and total rewards at an enterprise level
3. Oversee employee relations, engagement and organizational effectiveness
4. Govern HR risk, compliance, ethics and HR technology at a senior level

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0295-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A senior HR leader is asked how HR adds strategic value. Which statement best reflects a strategic HR role?

- A. Aligning workforce capability and programs with the organization's business goals **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: strategic HR links people decisions to business direction and outcomes.
- B. Processing payroll and maintaining attendance records  
  _Rationale:_ These are operational tasks, not strategic leadership.
- C. Filing regulatory paperwork only when audited  
  _Rationale:_ Reactive compliance is not strategy and invites risk.
- D. Keeping HR activity separate from business planning  
  _Rationale:_ Isolating HR from planning is the opposite of a strategic role.

**MST-0295-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Two critical roles will become vacant in 12 months. What is the most appropriate senior-HR response?

- A. Activate a succession plan that develops internal candidates and tracks readiness **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: succession planning prepares pipelines ahead of predictable vacancies.
- B. Wait until the roles are empty, then post them externally  
  _Rationale:_ Reactive hiring risks long gaps in critical roles.
- C. Promote by seniority regardless of readiness  
  _Rationale:_ Seniority alone does not ensure capability for the role.
- D. Freeze the roles to save budget  
  _Rationale:_ Freezing critical roles harms operations and is not planning.

**MST-0295-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO practices that strengthen a defensible total-rewards decision.

- A. Benchmarking pay against relevant external market data **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: market data supports fair, competitive pay decisions.
- B. Documenting the rationale and internal equity analysis **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: documentation and equity review make decisions defensible.
- C. Setting pay based on a manager's personal preference  
  _Rationale:_ Personal preference introduces bias and risk.
- D. Hiding the pay structure from all employees permanently  
  _Rationale:_ Secrecy alone is not a sound rewards practice.
- E. Changing criteria for each individual case  
  _Rationale:_ Inconsistent criteria undermine equity and defensibility.
