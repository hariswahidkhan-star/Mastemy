## Learning outcomes

1. Apply inventory cost-flow methods (FIFO, weighted average) under IAS 2
2. Measure inventory at the lower of cost and net realisable value
3. Identify and account for slow-moving and obsolete stock
4. Design inventory provisioning and write-down policies
5. Analyse the financial-statement impact of inventory decisions

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1047-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under IAS 2, inventory is measured at:

- A. The lower of cost and net realisable value **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: IAS 2 requires the lower of cost and NRV.
- B. Always historical cost, never written down  
  _Rationale:_ Write-downs to NRV are required when NRV falls below cost.
- C. Current replacement cost in all cases  
  _Rationale:_ Replacement cost is not the IAS 2 measurement base for finished goods.
- D. Expected selling price with no deductions  
  _Rationale:_ NRV deducts costs to complete and sell.

**MST-1047-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO signals most strongly suggest inventory should be written down? (Select TWO.)

- A. Selling price has fallen below cost plus costs to sell **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: NRV below cost triggers a write-down.
- B. A product line has not sold for 18 months with no future demand **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: obsolete stock with no realisable value needs writing down.
- C. Inventory turnover improved this quarter  
  _Rationale:_ Faster turnover is positive and does not imply a write-down.
- D. Purchase prices from suppliers rose  
  _Rationale:_ Higher input cost alone does not require a write-down.

**MST-1047-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) In a period of rising prices, FIFO compared with weighted average will generally report:

- A. Higher closing inventory and higher profit **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: FIFO leaves newer, higher-cost items in stock, raising reported profit.
- B. Lower closing inventory and lower profit  
  _Rationale:_ That is the opposite of FIFO's effect in rising prices.
- C. Identical results to weighted average  
  _Rationale:_ The methods diverge when prices change.
- D. No inventory on the balance sheet  
  _Rationale:_ Both methods still report closing inventory.
