## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0006-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) The UK personal allowance is reduced for individuals with high income. The reduction is:

- A. £1 for every £2 of adjusted net income above the income threshold **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the allowance is tapered by £1 for every £2 above the threshold.
- B. £1 for every £1 above the threshold  
  _Rationale:_ The taper is £1 for every £2, not £1 for £1.
- C. A flat 20% reduction  
  _Rationale:_ The restriction is a taper, not a flat percentage.
- D. Only applied to savings income  
  _Rationale:_ The taper applies to the personal allowance generally.

**MST-0006-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which of the following is generally an exempt supply for UK VAT purposes?

- A. Insurance services **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: insurance is generally an exempt supply.
- B. Standard-rated retail goods  
  _Rationale:_ These are taxable at the standard rate.
- C. Consultancy services to a UK business  
  _Rationale:_ These are generally standard-rated.
- D. Sale of office stationery  
  _Rationale:_ Stationery is standard-rated.

**MST-0006-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO sources of income that are exempt from UK income tax.

- A. Interest from an ISA (Individual Savings Account) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ISA interest is exempt from income tax.
- B. Premium Bond prizes **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: Premium Bond prizes are exempt.
- C. Employment salary  
  _Rationale:_ Salary is taxable employment income.
- D. Rental income from a buy-to-let property  
  _Rationale:_ Property income is taxable.
- E. Dividends above the dividend allowance  
  _Rationale:_ Dividends above the allowance are taxable.
