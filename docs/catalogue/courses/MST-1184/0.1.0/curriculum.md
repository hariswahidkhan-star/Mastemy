## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the treasury workflow and where AI adds value
2. Apply AI to cash-flow forecasting
3. Use AI for liquidity and working-capital insight
4. Apply AI to FX and interest-rate risk monitoring
5. Use AI for fraud and payment-anomaly detection
6. Govern AI use for control, audit and model risk

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

The analyst must interpret AI forecasts with their error bands, run stress scenarios, set up anomaly triage with approval controls, and address model-risk and data governance, then defend the design to the treasurer and internal audit.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1184-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An AI cash-flow forecast should be used by treasury as:

- A. A probabilistic estimate with an error band, validated against actuals **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: forecasts carry uncertainty and must be backtested.
- B. A guaranteed figure that needs no checking  
  _Rationale:_ Forecasts are uncertain and require validation.
- C. A reason to stop monitoring cash  
  _Rationale:_ Monitoring remains essential.
- D. A replacement for all treasury judgement  
  _Rationale:_ Human judgement remains central.

**MST-1184-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO controls should surround AI-based payment-anomaly detection? (Select TWO.)

- A. Human approval before any flagged payment is released or blocked **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a human control preserves segregation of duties.
- B. An audit trail of flags and decisions **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: traceability supports audit and accountability.
- C. Letting the model release payments autonomously  
  _Rationale:_ That removes essential controls.
- D. Disabling logging to speed processing  
  _Rationale:_ Removing logs destroys the audit trail.

**MST-1184-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Managing cash to a single AI forecast without scenarios is risky because it:

- A. Ignores the range of plausible outcomes and leaves no buffer plan **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: scenarios and stress views protect against forecast error.
- B. Is always more accurate than scenario analysis  
  _Rationale:_ A single point estimate is not more reliable than a scenario range.
- C. Eliminates the need for a liquidity buffer  
  _Rationale:_ Buffers are still needed given uncertainty.
- D. Guarantees the forecast will be met  
  _Rationale:_ No forecast is guaranteed.
