## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1093-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In market sizing, SOM (serviceable obtainable market) represents the share that:

- A. Everyone in the world could theoretically buy  
  _Rationale:_ That describes TAM, the total addressable market.
- B. You can realistically win in a defined period **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: SOM is the reachable, winnable portion in practice.
- C. Your segment could buy if you reached all of it  
  _Rationale:_ That is SAM, the serviceable available market.
- D. Competitors already control entirely  
  _Rationale:_ SOM is about what you can obtain, not what rivals hold.

**MST-1093-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A 'beachhead' market-entry approach means first targeting:

- A. As many segments as possible at once  
  _Rationale:_ That is the opposite of a focused beachhead.
- B. One narrow segment you can win and build from **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a beachhead concentrates resources on a winnable first segment.
- C. Only the largest customers available  
  _Rationale:_ Size alone does not define a beachhead.
- D. Whichever region has the lowest taxes  
  _Rationale:_ Tax is one factor, not the beachhead logic.

**MST-1093-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are genuine barriers to entering a new market? (Select TWO)

- A. Strong incumbent brands with loyal customers **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: entrenched brands make it costly to win customers.
- B. High regulatory or licensing requirements **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: regulatory hurdles raise the cost and time to enter.
- C. A large and growing addressable market  
  _Rationale:_ A growing market is an attraction, not a barrier.
- D. Readily available distribution channels  
  _Rationale:_ Easy distribution lowers, not raises, the barrier.
