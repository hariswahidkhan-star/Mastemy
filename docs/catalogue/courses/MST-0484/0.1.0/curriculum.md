## Learning outcomes

1. Understand the account and buyer
2. Tailor the message to the buyer
3. Draft an accurate, persuasive proposal
4. Apply confidentiality, accuracy and human-review controls to the workflow

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every course outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Hands-on operation of ChatGPT, the quality of live AI outputs, and professional judgement on accepting AI suggestions are taught by video demonstration and model-answer analysis; the certificate assessment uses single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only and never requires uploads, recorded work, graded prompts or peer review.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0484-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What should come before drafting a tailored value proposition?

- A. Understanding the buyer's goals and priorities **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: value is defined relative to the buyer's outcomes, so their needs come first.
- B. Listing every product feature  
  _Rationale:_ Features without buyer context do not establish value.
- C. Choosing the deck theme  
  _Rationale:_ Styling is not the basis of the value proposition.
- D. Setting the discount  
  _Rationale:_ Discounting is a later, tactical step.

**MST-0484-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) ChatGPT drafts a proposal that states a specific price and delivery date. Before sending:

- A. Verify the price and commitments against approved terms **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the model cannot know current approved pricing, so terms must be confirmed.
- B. Send it because it looks professional  
  _Rationale:_ Appearance does not confirm the terms are correct.
- C. Increase the price for margin  
  _Rationale:_ Arbitrary changes are not verification.
- D. Remove the scope section  
  _Rationale:_ Removing scope weakens the proposal and does not verify terms.

**MST-0484-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO make an AI-assisted proposal stronger? (Select TWO)

- A. Framing capabilities as business value for the buyer **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: value framing resonates with decision-makers.
- B. Verifying all prices and commitments before sending **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: accuracy on terms protects the deal and the company.
- C. Padding it with every feature  
  _Rationale:_ Feature dumping dilutes the message.
- D. Stating terms the model cannot confirm  
  _Rationale:_ Unverifiable terms create risk.
