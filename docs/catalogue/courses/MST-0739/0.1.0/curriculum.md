## Learning outcomes

1. Create notebooks and add sources
2. Ask grounded questions and read citations
3. Generate summaries and study aids
4. Organise notes and findings
5. Use audio overviews and sharing
6. Apply source-grounding limits responsibly

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Selection items assess knowledge and applied reasoning only; they do not assess hands-on performance in the product, which is taught through instructor-built walkthroughs.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0739-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What does it mean that NotebookLM is 'source-grounded'?

- A. Its answers are based on the sources you add to the notebook **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: responses draw on the added sources with citations.
- B. It answers from the open web by default  
  _Rationale:_ It grounds in your added sources, not the open web by default.
- C. It never needs citations  
  _Rationale:_ Citations are central to its grounding.
- D. It trains a model on your documents permanently  
  _Rationale:_ Grounding is retrieval, not permanent training.

**MST-0739-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why should you open the citation behind a NotebookLM answer?

- A. To confirm the answer matches the source passage **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: citations let you verify the answer against the source.
- B. To train the model faster  
  _Rationale:_ Citations do not affect training.
- C. To add a new source automatically  
  _Rationale:_ Opening a citation does not add sources.
- D. To remove the answer from history  
  _Rationale:_ Citations do not delete history.

**MST-0739-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are appropriate uses of NotebookLM? (Select TWO.)

- A. Summarising and querying a set of documents you added **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it is built for querying your sources.
- B. Generating study aids grounded in your sources **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: briefings and FAQs are grounded study aids.
- C. Answering questions about topics with no added source  
  _Rationale:_ Without a source it cannot ground the answer.
- D. Guaranteeing facts without any verification  
  _Rationale:_ Verification is still needed.
