## Learning outcomes

1. Order and calculate with positive and negative integers
2. Add, subtract, multiply and divide directed numbers
3. Use squares, cubes and simple powers
4. Find factors, multiples and prime numbers
5. Find the highest common factor and lowest common multiple
6. Use index notation for products of the same number

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2823-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is -4 + 9?

- A. 5 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: starting at -4 and moving up 9 on the number line reaches 5.
- B. -13  
  _Rationale:_ That treats the 9 as negative; moving up from -4 gives 5.
- C. -5  
  _Rationale:_ Moving up 9 from -4 passes zero and reaches positive 5.
- D. 13  
  _Rationale:_ That ignores the negative start; the answer is 5.

**MST-2823-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO numbers that are prime. (Select TWO.)

- A. 7 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 7 has exactly two factors, 1 and 7.
- B. 11 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 11 has exactly two factors, 1 and 11.
- C. 9  
  _Rationale:_ 9 has factors 1, 3 and 9, so it is not prime.
- D. 1  
  _Rationale:_ 1 has only one factor, so it is not counted as prime.

**MST-2823-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is 2 cubed (2 raised to the power 3)?

- A. 8 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 2 cubed means 2 x 2 x 2, which is 8.
- B. 6  
  _Rationale:_ 6 is 2 x 3; a cube multiplies the number by itself three times.
- C. 9  
  _Rationale:_ 9 is 3 squared, not 2 cubed.
- D. 4  
  _Rationale:_ 4 is 2 squared; cubing uses three factors of 2.
