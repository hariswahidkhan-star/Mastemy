## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the levels of structural organisation and homeostatic control in the human body
2. Explain the structure and function of the musculoskeletal and nervous systems
3. Explain the structure and function of the cardiovascular, respiratory and renal systems
4. Explain the structure and function of the digestive, endocrine and reproductive systems

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every course outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Hands-on operation, the quality of tools and live outputs, and professional judgement are taught by video demonstration and model-answer analysis; the certificate assessment uses single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only and never requires uploads, recorded work, submitted code or peer review.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1764-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A hormone raises blood calcium when levels fall, and its secretion stops once calcium returns to normal. Which type of control does this describe?

- A. Negative feedback **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the response opposes and corrects the original change, then switches off.
- B. Positive feedback  
  _Rationale:_ Positive feedback amplifies a change, as in blood clotting or labour, rather than correcting it.
- C. Feedforward anticipation  
  _Rationale:_ Feedforward acts before a change occurs; here the gland responds to a change already detected.
- D. Open-loop control  
  _Rationale:_ Open-loop control has no sensing of the output; here calcium is sensed and the response adjusts.**MST-1764-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Blood returning to the heart from the body tissues first enters which chamber?

- A. Right atrium **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: deoxygenated systemic blood returns via the venae cavae into the right atrium.
- B. Left atrium  
  _Rationale:_ The left atrium receives oxygenated blood from the lungs, not from the body tissues.
- C. Left ventricle  
  _Rationale:_ The left ventricle pumps oxygenated blood out to the body; it does not receive venous return.
- D. Right ventricle  
  _Rationale:_ Blood reaches the right ventricle only after passing through the right atrium.**MST-1764-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO structures are part of the nephron, the functional unit of the kidney? (Select TWO)

- A. Glomerulus **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the glomerulus is the filtering capillary tuft of the nephron.
- B. Proximal convoluted tubule **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the proximal convoluted tubule reabsorbs much of the filtrate and is part of the nephron.
- C. Alveolus  
  _Rationale:_ The alveolus is the gas-exchange sac of the lung, not part of the nephron.
- D. Sarcomere  
  _Rationale:_ The sarcomere is the contractile unit of muscle, unrelated to the kidney.
