## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the signs that a chemical reaction has happened
2. Distinguish chemical changes from physical changes
3. Explain that new substances with new properties form in a reaction
4. Recognise reactants and products in simple word equations
5. Give safe everyday examples such as rusting, burning and baking
6. Follow strict safety rules and let an adult handle any heat or chemicals

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance. Any experiment ideas are optional and require adult supervision; they are not assessed.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2853-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which observation is the strongest sign that a chemical reaction has occurred?

- A. A new substance with different properties has formed **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: forming a new substance is the key sign of a chemical reaction.
- B. The material simply changed shape  
  _Rationale:_ Changing shape is a physical change, not a reaction.
- C. The material got colder in the fridge  
  _Rationale:_ Cooling alone is usually a physical change.
- D. The material was poured into a new cup  
  _Rationale:_ Pouring changes location, not the substance.

**MST-2853-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are examples of chemical reactions? (Select TWO.)

- A. Iron rusting in damp air **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: rusting forms a new substance, iron oxide.
- B. Wood burning into ash and smoke **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: burning forms new substances and is a chemical reaction.
- C. Ice melting into water  
  _Rationale:_ Melting is a physical change; it is still water.
- D. Sugar dissolving in tea  
  _Rationale:_ Dissolving is a physical change; the sugar is unchanged.

**MST-2853-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) In the reaction 'iron + oxygen -> rust', which is the product?

- A. Rust **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: rust is the new substance formed, so it is the product.
- B. Iron  
  _Rationale:_ Iron is a reactant, a starting material.
- C. Oxygen  
  _Rationale:_ Oxygen is a reactant, a starting material.
- D. The air temperature  
  _Rationale:_ Temperature is not a substance in the equation.
