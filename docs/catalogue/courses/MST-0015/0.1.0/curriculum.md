## Learning outcomes

1. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Regulatory environment' to exam-style problems
2. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Professional and ethical considerations' to exam-style problems
3. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Quality management and practice management' to exam-style problems
4. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Planning and conducting an audit of historical financial information' to exam-style problems
5. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Completion, review and reporting' to exam-style problems
6. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Other assignments and current issues' to exam-style problems

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Official ACCA exams use constructed-response and computational item formats (written answers, calculations, OT case scenarios) that this platform's MCQ/MR forms do not reproduce; see the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0015-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) When a predecessor auditor is contacted by a proposed incoming auditor, the predecessor should:

- A. Respond only after obtaining the client's permission to disclose information **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: client permission is required before the predecessor discloses information.
- B. Refuse to communicate under any circumstances  
  _Rationale:_ Communication is appropriate once permission is obtained.
- C. Disclose all information without the client's consent  
  _Rationale:_ Disclosure without consent breaches confidentiality.
- D. Charge the incoming auditor a fee for information  
  _Rationale:_ Charging a fee is not the governing principle here.

**MST-0015-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A modified audit opinion that is 'qualified' is issued when misstatements are:

- A. Material but not pervasive to the financial statements **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: material but not pervasive misstatement gives a qualified ('except for') opinion.
- B. Immaterial to the financial statements  
  _Rationale:_ Immaterial matters do not warrant modification.
- C. Both material and pervasive  
  _Rationale:_ Material and pervasive misstatement gives an adverse opinion.
- D. Entirely resolved before the report date  
  _Rationale:_ Resolved matters need no modification.

**MST-0015-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO threats to auditor independence identified in the ethical framework.

- A. Self-interest threat **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: self-interest is a recognised threat to independence.
- B. Familiarity threat **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: familiarity is a recognised threat to independence.
- C. Inflation threat  
  _Rationale:_ Inflation is not an independence threat in the ethical framework.
- D. Liquidity threat  
  _Rationale:_ Liquidity is a financial risk, not an ethics threat.
- E. Competition threat  
  _Rationale:_ Competition is not a listed independence threat.
