## Learning outcomes

1. Apply hazard-recognition and safety-management-system fundamentals
2. Analyse hazard-control and industrial-hygiene decisions using the hierarchy of controls
3. Evaluate incident investigation, emergency planning and safety metrics
4. Integrate safety knowledge into a workplace safety programme

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0327-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) According to the hierarchy of controls, which option is most effective at reducing a hazard?

- A. Elimination of the hazard **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: elimination is the most effective control because it removes the hazard entirely.
- B. Personal protective equipment  
  _Rationale:_ PPE is the least effective control, used last.
- C. Administrative controls such as rotation  
  _Rationale:_ Administrative controls rank below engineering controls.
- D. Warning signs  
  _Rationale:_ Warnings are administrative controls, low on the hierarchy.

**MST-0327-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which of the following is a leading safety indicator?

- A. Number of completed safety inspections **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: completed inspections are a proactive, leading indicator.
- B. Total recordable incident rate  
  _Rationale:_ TRIR is a lagging indicator, measured after incidents occur.
- C. Lost-time injury count  
  _Rationale:_ Lost-time injuries are lagging outcomes.
- D. Workers' compensation cost  
  _Rationale:_ Comp cost is a lagging financial outcome.

**MST-0327-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) A risk assessment rates risk from severity and likelihood. Select TWO actions that correctly lower the assessed risk level.

- A. Install engineering controls that reduce the severity of a release **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reducing severity lowers the risk score.
- B. Reduce exposure frequency to lower likelihood **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reducing likelihood lowers the risk score.
- C. Rename the hazard category in the register  
  _Rationale:_ Relabelling does not change actual risk.
- D. Increase the number of warning signs only  
  _Rationale:_ Signs are weak controls and change risk little.
- E. Remove the hazard from the register without action  
  _Rationale:_ Deleting a record hides risk rather than reducing it.
