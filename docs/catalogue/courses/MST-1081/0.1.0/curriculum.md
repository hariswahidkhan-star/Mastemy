## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1081-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In the STAR method, the 'R' stands for the part that candidates most often omit, namely the:

- A. Situation that set the scene  
  _Rationale:_ That is the 'S'; it is usually included, sometimes at too much length.
- B. Result and its measurable impact **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the Result is the payoff interviewers want and the part most often left out.
- C. Role title you held  
  _Rationale:_ STAR's R is Result, not role.
- D. Reason you left the job  
  _Rationale:_ STAR's R is Result, not reason for leaving.

**MST-1081-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) When a behavioural interviewer asks 'Tell me about a time you handled conflict', the best answer is:

- A. A hypothetical description of what you would do  
  _Rationale:_ Behavioural questions ask for a real past example, not a hypothetical.
- B. A specific real example structured as situation, action and result **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a concrete past example with a clear outcome is what the question seeks.
- C. A statement that you never have conflicts  
  _Rationale:_ Claiming no conflict reads as evasive or self-unaware.
- D. A complaint about the other person involved  
  _Rationale:_ Blame-focused answers signal poor self-awareness and teamwork.

**MST-1081-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are good questions to ask an interviewer at the close? (Select TWO)

- A. What would success in this role look like in the first six months? **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it shows outcome focus and helps you understand expectations.
- B. How does the team handle feedback and development? **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it signals growth mindset and helps assess fit.
- C. Can I have the job now?  
  _Rationale:_ It is presumptuous and does not gather useful information.
- D. Do you check social media during work hours?  
  _Rationale:_ It is irrelevant and signals the wrong priorities.
