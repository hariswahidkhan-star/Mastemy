## Learning outcomes

1. Describe auditing concepts applied to an integrated GRC capability
2. Apply risk-based audit planning to GRC objectives and controls
3. Evaluate evidence, testing and findings for GRC processes
4. Analyse reporting, remediation follow-up and audit quality

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0117-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An auditor performs only a walkthrough of a control and concludes it is operating effectively all year. The main weakness is that:

- A. A walkthrough confirms design, not operating effectiveness over the period **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: operating effectiveness needs testing across the period, not a single walkthrough.
- B. Walkthroughs are never useful  
  _Rationale:_ Walkthroughs are useful for understanding design.
- C. The control owner approved it  
  _Rationale:_ Owner approval does not establish effectiveness.
- D. Auditors should own the control  
  _Rationale:_ Auditors must remain independent of the controls.

**MST-0117-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A GRC audit finding describes repeated late access reviews. The most valuable addition to the finding is:

- A. The root cause of why reviews are late **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: identifying root cause enables effective remediation.
- B. A longer list of the same symptoms  
  _Rationale:_ More symptoms without root cause add little value.
- C. The auditor's personal opinion of staff  
  _Rationale:_ Personal opinions are not appropriate audit content.
- D. A decision to hide the finding  
  _Rationale:_ Concealing findings breaches audit ethics.

**MST-0117-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) To preserve audit independence while reviewing a GRC capability, which TWO practices apply? (Select TWO)

- A. The auditor does not design or operate the controls being tested **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: independence requires separation from the controls audited.
- B. Findings are reported without management altering the conclusions **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: management cannot override audit conclusions.
- C. The auditor accepts a bonus tied to a clean report  
  _Rationale:_ A bonus for a clean result impairs independence.
- D. Management writes the auditor's conclusions  
  _Rationale:_ Management writing conclusions destroys independence.
- E. The auditor skips testing to save time  
  _Rationale:_ Skipping testing undermines the audit, not independence.
