## Learning outcomes

1. Explain supply chain transformation drivers and strategy
2. Describe technology, digitalization and data in supply chain transformation
3. Apply change management and capability building for transformation
4. Describe measuring, sustaining and scaling supply chain transformation

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0306-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Supply chain transformation is best understood as:

- A. An ongoing capability combining strategy, technology, data and change **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: transformation is more than a single technology project.
- B. Buying one software package  
  _Rationale:_ Software alone is not transformation.
- C. A one-time cost-cutting exercise  
  _Rationale:_ Transformation is ongoing, not a single cut.
- D. Reorganizing the org chart only  
  _Rationale:_ Structure alone does not transform the supply chain.

**MST-0306-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A pilot process improvement succeeded in one site. To scale it, the team should first:

- A. Plan change management and capability building for the other sites **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: scaling needs deliberate change and capability work.
- B. Assume every site will adopt it automatically  
  _Rationale:_ Adoption is not automatic.
- C. Stop measuring once the pilot ends  
  _Rationale:_ Measurement must continue to sustain value.
- D. Hide the results from other sites  
  _Rationale:_ Sharing results supports scaling.

**MST-0306-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO factors essential to delivering transformation value.

- A. Clear metrics that link change to business outcomes **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: metrics prove value was delivered.
- B. Active change management to drive adoption **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: adoption is needed for value to appear.
- C. Relying on technology purchase alone  
  _Rationale:_ Technology alone does not deliver value.
- D. Ignoring stakeholder concerns  
  _Rationale:_ Unaddressed concerns block adoption.
- E. Avoiding any measurement  
  _Rationale:_ Without measurement value cannot be shown.
