## Learning outcomes

1. Apply the Code of Ethics and Standards of Professional Conduct to analyst scenarios
2. Value equity, fixed income and derivative instruments using item-set reasoning
3. Analyze financial statements and corporate issuer decisions
4. Evaluate alternative investments and portfolio management at Level II depth

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.
- Format gap: The official CFA Level II exam uses item-set (vignette) questions tied to shared cases; Mastemy practice uses standalone MCQ/MR and models vignette reasoning only approximately. No constructed-response component is assessed.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0052-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In a multistage dividend discount model, the terminal value is most sensitive to which assumption?

- A. The long-run (terminal) growth rate relative to the discount rate **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: terminal value hinges on the gap between the discount rate and terminal growth.
- B. The dividend paid in year one only  
  _Rationale:_ A single near-term dividend has small effect on terminal value.
- C. The number of decimal places used  
  _Rationale:_ Rounding precision is immaterial to sensitivity.
- D. The company ticker symbol  
  _Rationale:_ The ticker has no bearing on valuation.

**MST-0052-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) An analyst accepts a lavish gift from a client that could affect objectivity. Under the Standards, the best action is to:

- A. Disclose and obtain employer consent before accepting **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the Standards require disclosure and consent for gifts that could impair objectivity.
- B. Accept silently because it is customary  
  _Rationale:_ Non-disclosure of a benefit that could impair objectivity breaches the Standards.
- C. Accept and offset it with a donation  
  _Rationale:_ A donation does not cure the independence/objectivity concern.
- D. Refuse all client contact permanently  
  _Rationale:_ An extreme refusal is not what the Standards require.

**MST-0052-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO statements about a callable bond are correct? (Select TWO)

- A. Its value is lower than an otherwise identical option-free bond **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the embedded call benefits the issuer, reducing value to the holder.
- B. Effective duration captures its rate sensitivity better than modified duration **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: with an embedded option, effective duration is appropriate.
- C. It can never be redeemed before maturity  
  _Rationale:_ A call feature lets the issuer redeem early, which is the point of the option.
- D. Its yield is always lower than a comparable option-free bond  
  _Rationale:_ Investors demand a higher yield to bear call risk, not lower.
