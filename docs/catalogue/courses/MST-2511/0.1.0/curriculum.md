## Learning outcomes

1. Determine whether a contract is, or contains, a lease
2. Measure a lessee's right-of-use asset and lease liability at commencement
3. Account for subsequent measurement, interest and depreciation for a lessee
4. Apply the recognition exemptions for short-term and low-value leases
5. Classify and account for leases from a lessor's perspective
6. Account for reassessments, modifications and sale-and-leaseback transactions

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2511-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) At the commencement of a lease, how does a lessee generally measure the lease liability?

- A. At the present value of the lease payments not yet paid, discounted at the rate implicit in the lease or the incremental borrowing rate **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the liability is the discounted outstanding lease payments.
- B. At the undiscounted total of all lease payments over the term  
  _Rationale:_ The liability is discounted, not the undiscounted total.
- C. At the fair value of the underlying asset  
  _Rationale:_ The liability is based on the lease payments, not the asset's fair value.
- D. At zero until the first payment is made  
  _Rationale:_ The liability is recognised at commencement.

**MST-2511-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO leases may a lessee elect not to recognise on the balance sheet under the recognition exemptions? (Select TWO.)

- A. A short-term lease of 12 months or less with no purchase option **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: short-term leases qualify for the exemption.
- B. A lease of a low-value underlying asset **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: low-value asset leases qualify for the exemption.
- C. A 10-year lease of a core distribution centre  
  _Rationale:_ A long-term lease of a significant asset does not qualify.
- D. A lease with a bargain purchase option the lessee is reasonably certain to exercise  
  _Rationale:_ Such a lease is not short-term and does not qualify.

**MST-2511-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A lessee argues that because it depreciates the right-of-use asset on a straight-line basis and the liability unwinds using the effective-interest method, the asset and liability will stay equal each year. Why is this wrong?

- A. The two balances follow different patterns, so the right-of-use asset and the lease liability diverge over the lease term **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: straight-line depreciation and effective-interest unwinding produce different carrying amounts.
- B. The right-of-use asset is never depreciated  
  _Rationale:_ The right-of-use asset is depreciated.
- C. The lease liability is remeasured to fair value each year  
  _Rationale:_ The liability is measured at amortised cost, not fair value.
- D. Both are always written down to zero immediately  
  _Rationale:_ Neither is written off at commencement.
