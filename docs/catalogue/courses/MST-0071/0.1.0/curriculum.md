## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the investment banking representative role across capital raising and advisory engagements
2. Apply due-diligence, valuation and financial-analysis methods to a transaction
3. Evaluate securities-offering registration, disclosure and communication rules
4. Analyse conflicts, information-barrier and conduct obligations in banking transactions

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0071-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) When normalising a target's EBITDA for a valuation, which adjustment is MOST appropriate?

- A. Adding back a one-time, non-recurring legal settlement expense **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: genuinely non-recurring items are added back so EBITDA reflects ongoing earning power.
- B. Removing ordinary recurring salaries of production staff  
  _Rationale:_ Recurring operating costs are not normalisation add-backs.
- C. Capitalising routine monthly rent as a one-time item  
  _Rationale:_ Routine rent is a recurring operating expense, not a one-time item.
- D. Adding projected synergies not yet realised  
  _Rationale:_ Unrealised buyer synergies do not belong in the target's standalone normalised EBITDA.

**MST-0071-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A banker on a registered IPO receives a reporter's request for the issuer's growth outlook during the quiet period. What is the appropriate action?

- A. Decline to make statements that could condition the market outside the prospectus **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: offering communications are restricted; information should be confined to permitted channels such as the prospectus.
- B. Share the internal projection model to be transparent  
  _Rationale:_ Disseminating non-public projections during the offering is improper gun-jumping.
- C. Forward the request to research for a quick rating  
  _Rationale:_ This risks improper research involvement in the offering.
- D. Post the outlook on the banker's social media  
  _Rationale:_ This is an unauthorised offering communication.

**MST-0071-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) A deal team wants to bring a research analyst 'over the wall' to help with a potential transaction. Which TWO controls are central to doing this appropriately? (Select TWO)

- A. Documenting the wall-crossing and restricting the analyst's related activity while in possession of material non-public information **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: wall-crossings are logged and the recipient is restricted appropriately.
- B. Obtaining compliance involvement before sharing material non-public information **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: information-barrier procedures require compliance oversight of wall-crossings.
- C. Letting the analyst immediately publish a favourable report to support the deal  
  _Rationale:_ Publishing to support a deal is a conflict and misuse of the barrier.
- D. Sharing the information firm-wide to ensure consistency  
  _Rationale:_ Broad dissemination defeats the information barrier.
- E. Allowing the analyst to trade the issuer's stock personally  
  _Rationale:_ Personal trading on MNPI is prohibited.
