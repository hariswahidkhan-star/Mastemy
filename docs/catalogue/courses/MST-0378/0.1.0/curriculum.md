## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0378-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) On a CPM schedule, the critical path is best described as the:

- A. Longest-duration path with zero total float **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the critical path is the longest path through the network and has zero total float.
- B. Shortest path through the network  
  _Rationale:_ The critical path is the longest, not shortest, path.
- C. Path with the most activities  
  _Rationale:_ Number of activities does not define criticality; duration and float do.
- D. Path with the greatest total float  
  _Rationale:_ The critical path has the least (zero) float, not the greatest.

**MST-0378-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) For workers exposed to an unprotected edge at height, the most appropriate primary control is:

- A. A fall-protection system such as guardrails or personal fall arrest **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a physical fall-protection system is the appropriate primary control for exposure to a fall hazard.
- B. A verbal reminder at the morning briefing  
  _Rationale:_ Administrative reminders do not substitute for physical fall protection.
- C. Posting a sign near the edge  
  _Rationale:_ Signage alone does not control a fall hazard.
- D. Adding the hazard to next month's agenda  
  _Rationale:_ Deferral leaves the hazard uncontrolled.

**MST-0378-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO statements about temporary works such as formwork and shoring are correct?

- A. They require engineering design for the loads they carry **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: temporary works are engineered for their loading, like permanent structures.
- B. They must account for construction loads during placement **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: construction and placement loads govern many temporary-works designs.
- C. They never need to be checked once installed  
  _Rationale:_ Temporary works require inspection and may need rechecking.
- D. They carry no load because they are temporary  
  _Rationale:_ Temporary works carry significant loads during construction.
- E. They are exempt from any safety requirement  
  _Rationale:_ Safety requirements apply to temporary works.
