## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the leader's role in building and supporting agile teams
2. Apply leadership behaviours that enable self-management and empiricism
3. Evaluate organisational structures, metrics and culture for agility
4. Analyse leading change and removing systemic impediments

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0142-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Leadership rewards individuals for being 100% utilised. In an agile context this metric most likely:

- A. Discourages collaboration and slack needed for flow and improvement **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: utilisation targets can harm flow, quality and collaboration.
- B. Guarantees faster value delivery  
  _Rationale:_ High utilisation often slows value delivery.
- C. Is the best measure of agility  
  _Rationale:_ Utilisation is not a measure of agility or value.
- D. Improves customer outcomes directly  
  _Rationale:_ It does not directly improve outcomes.

**MST-0142-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) An agile leader best supports a self-managing team by:

- A. Setting clear goals and removing impediments, then trusting the team to decide how **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: agile leaders enable and support rather than dictate the how.
- B. Assigning every task personally  
  _Rationale:_ Micromanagement undermines self-management.
- C. Approving each technical decision  
  _Rationale:_ That removes the team's ownership.
- D. Withholding goals to keep control  
  _Rationale:_ Teams need clear goals to self-organise.

**MST-0142-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO leadership actions most help an organisation become more agile? (Select TWO)

- A. Removing systemic impediments that span teams **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: leaders can remove organisation-level blockers.
- B. Aligning metrics and incentives with value and outcomes **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: incentives shape behaviour toward agility.
- C. Mandating agility by decree without support  
  _Rationale:_ Mandates without support rarely change culture.
- D. Rewarding local utilisation over outcomes  
  _Rationale:_ This drives the wrong behaviour.
- E. Centralising all decisions with executives  
  _Rationale:_ Centralisation blocks self-management.
