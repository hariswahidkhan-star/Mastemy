## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the stages of a life cycle from birth to adult
2. Compare the life cycles of a butterfly, a frog and a bird
3. Explain what metamorphosis means with examples
4. Identify how young animals change as they grow
5. Relate an animal's life cycle to its habitat and needs
6. Use correct words such as larva, pupa and tadpole

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2862-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) During which stage does a caterpillar turn into a butterfly?

- A. The pupa (chrysalis) stage **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: inside the pupa the caterpillar changes into a butterfly.
- B. The egg stage  
  _Rationale:_ The egg is the very first stage, before the caterpillar.
- C. The adult stage  
  _Rationale:_ The adult is the butterfly that has already formed.
- D. The tadpole stage  
  _Rationale:_ Tadpoles are part of a frog's life cycle, not a butterfly's.

**MST-2862-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A young frog that lives in water and has a tail is called a:

- A. Tadpole **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a tadpole is the young, water-living stage of a frog.
- B. Larva  
  _Rationale:_ Larva is used for insects such as caterpillars.
- C. Chick  
  _Rationale:_ A chick is a baby bird.
- D. Pupa  
  _Rationale:_ A pupa is the resting stage of some insects.

**MST-2862-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO animals go through metamorphosis, changing body form as they grow? (Select TWO.)

- A. A frog **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a frog changes from tadpole to adult frog.
- B. A butterfly **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a butterfly changes from caterpillar to adult.
- C. A horse  
  _Rationale:_ A foal looks like a small adult horse; no metamorphosis.
- D. An elephant  
  _Rationale:_ A baby elephant resembles an adult; no metamorphosis.
