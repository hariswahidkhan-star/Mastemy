## Learning outcomes

1. Apply sourcing, spend-analysis and total-cost-of-ownership methods
2. Analyse negotiation, contracting and purchasing-law scenarios
3. Evaluate supplier performance, risk and continuity decisions
4. Integrate supply-management knowledge across the source-to-contract cycle

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0307-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Total cost of ownership for a purchased part should include the unit price plus:

- A. Freight, quality, inventory-holding and end-of-life costs **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: TCO adds all acquisition, use and disposal costs to the unit price.
- B. Only the supplier's list price  
  _Rationale:_ List price alone is exactly what TCO is designed to go beyond.
- C. The buyer's annual salary  
  _Rationale:_ General staff salary is not a part-level TCO element.
- D. Corporate income tax  
  _Rationale:_ Income tax is not a procurement TCO component.

**MST-0307-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A buyer's BATNA in a negotiation is:

- A. The best alternative to a negotiated agreement **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: BATNA is the best option if no deal is reached, and it sets the walk-away point.
- B. The budget authority the buyer holds  
  _Rationale:_ That is spend authority, not BATNA.
- C. The supplier's target margin  
  _Rationale:_ The supplier's margin is not the buyer's BATNA.
- D. The agreed payment terms  
  _Rationale:_ Payment terms are a deal term, not the alternative to a deal.

**MST-0307-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) A critical part is single-sourced overseas. Select TWO actions that most reduce continuity-of-supply risk.

- A. Qualify a second source in a different region **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a qualified alternate source directly reduces single-source risk.
- B. Hold a strategic safety stock of the critical part **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: buffer stock bridges a supply interruption while alternatives activate.
- C. Switch the contract from fixed-price to cost-plus  
  _Rationale:_ Contract type changes cost risk, not supply continuity.
- D. Extend standard payment terms to 90 days  
  _Rationale:_ Longer payment terms do not protect supply continuity.
- E. Consolidate all spend with the single supplier for a discount  
  _Rationale:_ Consolidating increases dependence on the single source.
