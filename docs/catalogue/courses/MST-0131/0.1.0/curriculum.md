## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the PRINCE2 principles and their application
2. Describe the PRINCE2 themes and management products
3. Describe the PRINCE2 processes and tailoring

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0131-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which PRINCE2 principle requires that a project remain desirable, viable and achievable throughout?

- A. Manage by stages  
  _Rationale:_ That principle concerns dividing the project into stages.
- B. Continued business justification **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: continued business justification keeps the project worthwhile throughout.
- C. Focus on products  
  _Rationale:_ That principle concerns defining and delivering products.
- D. Tailor to suit the project  
  _Rationale:_ That principle concerns adapting PRINCE2 to context.

**MST-0131-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) The business case, risk and quality are examples of PRINCE2:

- A. Themes **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: business case, risk and quality are among the seven themes.
- B. Processes  
  _Rationale:_ Processes describe the flow of activity, not these aspects.
- C. Principles  
  _Rationale:_ Principles are the guiding obligations, not themes.
- D. Management products  
  _Rationale:_ Management products are specific documents or records.

**MST-0131-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO of the seven PRINCE2 principles.

- A. Learn from experience **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: learn from experience is a PRINCE2 principle.
- B. Maximise utilisation of all resources  
  _Rationale:_ That is not one of the seven principles.
- C. Defined roles and responsibilities **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: defined roles and responsibilities is a principle.
- D. Eliminate all project documentation  
  _Rationale:_ PRINCE2 does not call for eliminating documentation.
- E. Fix scope before any planning  
  _Rationale:_ That is not a PRINCE2 principle.
