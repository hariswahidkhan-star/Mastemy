## Learning outcomes

1. Integrate the geosphere, hydrosphere, atmosphere and biosphere as interacting systems
2. Explain plate tectonics as the unifying theory of Earth science
3. Describe the rock cycle and the deep-time context of geological change
4. Interpret topographic and geological maps and basic cross-sections
5. Explain how Earth's internal and surface processes shape landscapes
6. Evaluate how Earth resources form and are used sustainably

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A regional survey must assess a valley for a new reservoir: students analyse interactions among the Earth spheres, use tectonic and rock-cycle evidence and a geological map to judge stability, and evaluate the sustainable use of local water and mineral resources before advising on the project.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2840-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Plate tectonics is described as a 'unifying theory' of Earth science because it:

- A. Explains earthquakes, volcanoes, mountain building and the distribution of many rocks within one framework **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it links many phenomena under one explanatory theory.
- B. Only explains the ages of fossils  
  _Rationale:_ It explains far more than fossil ages.
- C. Describes the weather over a single week  
  _Rationale:_ That is meteorology, not plate tectonics.
- D. Applies only to the ocean floor and nothing else  
  _Rationale:_ It applies to continents and oceans alike.

**MST-2840-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A geologist finds an undisturbed sequence of sedimentary layers. By the principle of superposition, which layer is oldest? (Select ONE)

- A. The layer at the bottom of the sequence **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: in undisturbed strata, lower layers were deposited first and are oldest.
- B. The layer at the very top  
  _Rationale:_ Top layers are the youngest in undisturbed strata.
- C. The thickest layer regardless of position  
  _Rationale:_ Thickness does not determine age.
- D. The layer with the brightest colour  
  _Rationale:_ Colour does not indicate relative age.

**MST-2840-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO interactions between Earth's spheres. (Select TWO)

- A. Rivers (hydrosphere) erode rock (geosphere) to shape valleys **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: this is a hydrosphere-geosphere interaction.
- B. Plants (biosphere) remove carbon dioxide from the air (atmosphere) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: this is a biosphere-atmosphere interaction.
- C. The spheres are sealed off and never exchange matter  
  _Rationale:_ The spheres continually exchange matter and energy.
- D. Geological maps cause volcanoes to form  
  _Rationale:_ Maps are representations; they do not cause processes.
