## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the domains of talent development capability at a professional level
2. Design, develop and deliver learning solutions aligned to business needs
3. Apply evaluation, measurement and data to improve talent development
4. Explain organizational development, change and talent management

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0300-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) At a professional level, learning solutions should be driven primarily by:

- A. A clear link to business needs and performance outcomes **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: solutions align to business and performance needs.
- B. Whatever technology is newest  
  _Rationale:_ Novelty is not a design driver.
- C. The trainer's personal preferences  
  _Rationale:_ Preference is not a sound basis for design.
- D. The largest possible content volume  
  _Rationale:_ Volume does not equal effectiveness.

**MST-0300-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which measure best demonstrates a program's organizational impact?

- A. A business metric that improved after the program **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: business-level measures show organizational impact.
- B. Number of modules created  
  _Rationale:_ Output count does not show impact.
- C. Learner satisfaction only  
  _Rationale:_ Satisfaction alone is not impact.
- D. Hours of video produced  
  _Rationale:_ Production volume is not an outcome.

**MST-0300-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO elements of an effective change-management plan for a rollout.

- A. Stakeholder communication and engagement **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: communication and engagement support adoption.
- B. Reinforcement and support after go-live **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: post-launch support sustains change.
- C. Keeping the change secret until launch day  
  _Rationale:_ Surprise undermines adoption.
- D. Ignoring resistance entirely  
  _Rationale:_ Unaddressed resistance derails change.
- E. Removing all training to save time  
  _Rationale:_ Training supports successful change.
