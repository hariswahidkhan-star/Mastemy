## Learning outcomes

1. Use A1 greetings, numbers and everyday vocabulary in context
2. Apply present-tense regular verbs, gender and noun-adjective agreement
3. Understand simple written and spoken French on familiar topics

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning (vocabulary, rules, comprehension) through selection items (MCQ/MR) only; does not assess live speaking, extended writing production or any hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A learner reads a short A1 self-introduction in French and answers about it, then plans (in writing) a six-sentence self-introduction choosing correct verb forms, articles and adjective agreement, explaining each grammar choice.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1862-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which is the most appropriate formal greeting for a stranger during the day in French?

- A. Bonjour **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'Bonjour' is the standard polite daytime greeting.
- B. Salut  
  _Rationale:_ 'Salut' is informal and used with friends, not a stranger formally.
- C. Bonne nuit  
  _Rationale:_ 'Bonne nuit' means 'good night' when going to bed, not a greeting.
- D. Au revoir  
  _Rationale:_ 'Au revoir' means 'goodbye', not a greeting on meeting.

**MST-1862-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO phrases show correct noun-article gender agreement in French? (Select TWO.)

- A. la table (the table) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'table' is feminine, so 'la' is correct.
- B. le livre (the book) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'livre' is masculine, so 'le' is correct.
- C. le table  
  _Rationale:_ Incorrect: 'table' is feminine and takes 'la', not 'le'.
- D. la livre (meaning book)  
  _Rationale:_ Incorrect: 'livre' meaning book is masculine and takes 'le'.

**MST-1862-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Complete with the correctly agreeing adjective: 'La voiture est ____.' (The car is green.)

- A. verte **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'voiture' is feminine singular, so 'verte' agrees.
- B. vert  
  _Rationale:_ 'vert' is masculine singular and does not agree with a feminine noun.
- C. verts  
  _Rationale:_ 'verts' is masculine plural; the noun is feminine singular.
- D. vertes  
  _Rationale:_ 'vertes' is feminine plural; the noun is singular.
