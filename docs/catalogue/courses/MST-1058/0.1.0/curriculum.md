## Learning outcomes

1. Build and interpret core startup financial metrics
2. Analyse unit economics including CAC, LTV and contribution margin
3. Model runway, burn rate and cash management
4. Prepare a startup for fundraising and due diligence
5. Understand cap tables, dilution and funding instruments

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1058-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A startup with strong revenue growth but a customer lifetime value below its customer acquisition cost is:

- A. Losing money on each customer and likely not yet sustainable **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: LTV below CAC means each customer destroys value despite growth.
- B. Definitely profitable because revenue is growing  
  _Rationale:_ Growth with LTV<CAC worsens losses.
- C. Guaranteed to attract investment  
  _Rationale:_ Poor unit economics are a red flag to investors.
- D. Not affected by unit economics  
  _Rationale:_ Unit economics are central to sustainability.

**MST-1058-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO figures do you need to estimate a startup's cash runway? (Select TWO.)

- A. Current cash balance **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: runway starts from available cash.
- B. Net monthly burn rate **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: runway is cash divided by net monthly burn.
- C. The founder's personal credit score  
  _Rationale:_ This is not used to compute company runway.
- D. The number of office plants  
  _Rationale:_ Irrelevant to runway.

**MST-1058-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) When a startup raises a priced equity round, existing shareholders typically experience:

- A. Dilution of their ownership percentage **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: issuing new shares dilutes existing holders' percentage.
- B. An automatic increase in their percentage ownership  
  _Rationale:_ New shares reduce, not increase, existing percentages.
- C. No change to the cap table  
  _Rationale:_ A priced round changes the cap table.
- D. A guaranteed cash payout  
  _Rationale:_ A primary round funds the company, not existing holders.
