## Learning outcomes

1. Tell living things apart from non-living things with examples
2. List simple needs of living things: food, water, air and space
3. Recognise that living things grow, move and make young
4. Sort common objects into living, once-living and never-living
5. Describe how we care for a plant or animal's needs
6. Observe a living thing and say one way it changes over time

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A classroom sets up a nature corner with a potted bean, a pet fish and a collection of pebbles and toys: the children decide which items are living, plan how to meet the living things' needs each day, and record how the bean changes over two weeks.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2830-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which of these is a living thing?

- A. A growing sunflower **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a sunflower grows, needs water and makes seeds, so it is living.
- B. A plastic ball  
  _Rationale:_ A ball never grows or needs food, so it is non-living.
- C. A metal spoon  
  _Rationale:_ A spoon is non-living.
- D. A rubber boot  
  _Rationale:_ A boot is non-living.

**MST-2830-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) What do all living things need to stay alive? (Select ONE)

- A. Water, food and air **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: these are basic needs shared by living things.
- B. Only sunshine  
  _Rationale:_ Sunshine alone is not enough for animals.
- C. Only a toy to play with  
  _Rationale:_ Toys are not a need of living things.
- D. Nothing at all  
  _Rationale:_ Living things do have needs.

**MST-2830-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO things that show a kitten is a living thing. (Select TWO)

- A. It grows bigger over time **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: growth is a sign of living things.
- B. It needs food and water **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: needing food and water is a sign of life.
- C. It is made of metal  
  _Rationale:_ Being metal is a feature of non-living objects.
- D. It stays exactly the same forever  
  _Rationale:_ Living things change and grow; they do not stay the same forever.
