## Learning outcomes

1. Explain where AI helps in the accounting workflow
2. Apply AI to transaction coding and reconciliations
3. Use AI to accelerate the month-end close
4. Apply AI to anomaly detection and controls
5. Draft financial narratives and variance commentary with AI
6. Govern AI use for accuracy, audit and ethics

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A finance team wants to cut two days off its month-end close using AI. The accountant must map which close tasks AI can assist, design human review and audit-trail controls, use AI to draft accruals and variance commentary, and address data-privacy and accountability concerns, then present the controlled workflow to the controller.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1182-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) When AI proposes account codings for a batch of transactions, the accountant should:

- A. Review and verify the suggestions before posting **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI output requires human verification for accuracy and control.
- B. Post all suggestions automatically to save time  
  _Rationale:_ Unreviewed auto-posting undermines controls.
- C. Assume the AI is always right  
  _Rationale:_ AI can misclassify; verification is required.
- D. Delete the ledger and rebuild it from AI output  
  _Rationale:_ Reckless and uncontrolled.

**MST-1182-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are sound governance controls for AI in the close? (Select TWO.)

- A. Maintain an audit trail of AI-assisted steps and human sign-off **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: traceability supports audit and accountability.
- B. Avoid entering confidential data into uncontrolled tools **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: data privacy must be protected.
- C. Let AI approve its own journal entries  
  _Rationale:_ That removes segregation of duties.
- D. Hide AI use from the auditors  
  _Rationale:_ Concealment undermines the audit trail.

**MST-1182-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) An AI tool flags 40 transactions as anomalies. The appropriate response is to:

- A. Triage and investigate the flags by risk, treating them as leads not conclusions **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: flags are signals to investigate, not confirmed errors.
- B. Reverse all 40 transactions immediately  
  _Rationale:_ Flags are not confirmed errors.
- C. Ignore them all as false positives  
  _Rationale:_ Dismissing without review defeats the control.
- D. Delete the anomaly report  
  _Rationale:_ That discards useful control information.
