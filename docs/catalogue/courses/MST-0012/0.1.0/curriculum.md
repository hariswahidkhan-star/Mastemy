- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Official ACCA exams use constructed-response and computational item formats (written answers, calculations, OT case scenarios) that this platform's MCQ/MR forms do not reproduce; see the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0012-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) The weighted average cost of capital (WACC) is most appropriate as a discount rate when:

- A. The project has the same business and financial risk as the company's existing operations **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: WACC is valid when risk profile is unchanged.
- B. The project changes the company's gearing significantly  
  _Rationale:_ A material gearing change invalidates the existing WACC.
- C. The project is in a completely different industry  
  _Rationale:_ Different business risk requires a project-specific rate.
- D. The company is about to be liquidated  
  _Rationale:_ Liquidation is not the basis for using WACC.

**MST-0012-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) In a management buy-out, the key feature is that:

- A. The existing management team acquires the business it runs **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an MBO is an acquisition by the incumbent management team.
- B. An external competitor acquires the business  
  _Rationale:_ That is a trade sale, not an MBO.
- C. The company lists its shares for the first time  
  _Rationale:_ That is an IPO.
- D. The company repays all its debt early  
  _Rationale:_ Debt repayment is not the defining feature of an MBO.

**MST-0012-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO techniques used to hedge foreign currency transaction risk.

- A. Forward exchange contracts **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: forwards fix a future exchange rate to hedge transaction risk.
- B. Currency options **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: options hedge while retaining upside.
- C. Issuing a bonus share issue  
  _Rationale:_ A bonus issue does not hedge currency risk.
- D. Declaring a scrip dividend  
  _Rationale:_ A scrip dividend does not hedge currency risk.
- E. Revaluing non-current assets  
  _Rationale:_ Revaluation does not hedge transaction risk.
