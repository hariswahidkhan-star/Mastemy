## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the inbound methodology (attract, engage, delight) and the flywheel
2. Create content and SEO strategy that attracts the right audience
3. Convert and nurture leads with landing pages, forms, email and marketing automation
4. Align marketing and sales and measure performance to improve continuously

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1820-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What are the three stages of the inbound methodology?

- A. Attract, engage, delight **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the inbound methodology is attract, engage and delight, powering the flywheel.
- B. Plan, build, ship  
  _Rationale:_ That is a generic delivery sequence, not the inbound stages.
- C. Awareness, consideration, decision  
  _Rationale:_ Those are the buyer's-journey stages, not the inbound methodology stages.
- D. Deter, detect, delay  
  _Rationale:_ That is a security model, unrelated to inbound.

**MST-1820-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A topic-cluster SEO model organises content around:

- A. A pillar page linked to related cluster content on subtopics **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a pillar page covers a broad topic and links to cluster pages on specific subtopics, strengthening topical authority.
- B. A single keyword repeated across unrelated pages  
  _Rationale:_ Keyword stuffing across unrelated pages is not the topic-cluster model.
- C. Random blog posts with no internal links  
  _Rationale:_ Clusters depend on deliberate internal linking to a pillar.
- D. Only paid search ads  
  _Rationale:_ Topic clusters are an organic/SEO structure, not paid ads.

**MST-1820-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are effective ways to convert website visitors into leads?

- A. A clear call-to-action leading to a landing page with a form **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: CTAs and landing-page forms capture visitor information in exchange for an offer.
- B. A gated content offer that collects contact details **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: gating valuable content behind a form is a standard lead-capture tactic.
- C. Hiding all contact options from visitors  
  _Rationale:_ Hiding conversion paths reduces, not increases, leads.
- D. Removing every form from the site  
  _Rationale:_ Without forms there is no lead capture.
- E. Blocking search engines from the site  
  _Rationale:_ Blocking search engines reduces traffic and conversions.
