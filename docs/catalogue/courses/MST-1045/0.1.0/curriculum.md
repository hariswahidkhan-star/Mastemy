## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1045-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Revenue leakage in a subscription business most accurately refers to:

- A. Revenue the company was entitled to but did not bill or collect **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: leakage is earned-but-unbilled or uncollected revenue, not only fraud.
- B. Only revenue lost to deliberate customer fraud  
  _Rationale:_ Fraud is one cause; most leakage is from process and configuration errors.
- C. Discounts that marketing approved  
  _Rationale:_ Approved discounts are not leakage.
- D. Taxes remitted to the government  
  _Rationale:_ Tax remittance is not revenue leakage.

**MST-1045-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are examples of a detective control over billing? (Select TWO)

- A. An exception report flagging usage with no matching charge **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an exception report detects anomalies after processing.
- B. A usage-to-billing reconciliation run each period **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a reconciliation detects mismatches after billing.
- C. A mandatory approval before a price plan can be activated  
  _Rationale:_ That is a preventive control, acting before the event.
- D. A field validation that blocks an invalid tariff at entry  
  _Rationale:_ Input validation is preventive, not detective.

**MST-1045-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A leakage finding is reported as '1,200 affected accounts' with no monetary figure. What is missing?

- A. Quantification of the financial impact of the leakage **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: findings must be sized financially to be actionable.
- B. The names of the affected customers  
  _Rationale:_ Customer names are not what makes the finding actionable.
- C. The billing system's version number  
  _Rationale:_ The system version is not the missing element.
- D. A marketing plan  
  _Rationale:_ A marketing plan is unrelated to sizing leakage.
