## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the healthcare privacy legal framework and key definitions
2. Apply privacy rules to uses, disclosures and patient rights
3. Evaluate safeguards, breach response and vendor management for health data
4. Analyse privacy-programme governance, auditing and enforcement

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0114-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A clinic uses patient records to bill an insurer and coordinate follow-up care. Under the privacy framework, this use most likely:

- A. Is permitted as treatment, payment and operations without separate authorisation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: TPO uses are generally permitted without separate authorisation.
- B. Requires written authorisation for each record  
  _Rationale:_ Routine TPO uses do not each require authorisation.
- C. Is always prohibited  
  _Rationale:_ Such core uses are permitted, not prohibited.
- D. Requires public posting of the records  
  _Rationale:_ No rule requires public posting; that would breach privacy.

**MST-0114-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Staff email unencrypted PHI to the wrong external recipient. The first privacy-compliance step is to:

- A. Perform a breach risk assessment to determine notification duties **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a risk assessment determines whether the incident is a reportable breach.
- B. Ignore it because email is informal  
  _Rationale:_ Misdirected PHI must be assessed, not ignored.
- C. Delete all logs to avoid liability  
  _Rationale:_ Destroying evidence is improper and may be illegal.
- D. Immediately tell the press  
  _Rationale:_ Media notice is not the first or routine step.

**MST-0114-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) A new business associate will process PHI on the covered entity's behalf. Which TWO controls are most essential before data sharing begins? (Select TWO)

- A. A signed business associate agreement defining permitted uses **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a BAA is required before a business associate handles PHI.
- B. Documented safeguards and breach-notification obligations **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the vendor must commit to safeguards and breach notice.
- C. The vendor's marketing brochure  
  _Rationale:_ Marketing materials are not a privacy control.
- D. A verbal promise to be careful  
  _Rationale:_ An informal promise does not meet the requirement.
- E. The vendor CEO's personal phone number  
  _Rationale:_ Contact trivia is not a required control.
