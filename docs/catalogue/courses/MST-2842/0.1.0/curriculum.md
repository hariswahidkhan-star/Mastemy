## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the scale and structure of the Solar System, galaxy and universe
2. Explain stellar life cycles and how elements are formed
3. Use gravity and orbital motion to explain planetary and satellite paths
4. Explain the evidence for an expanding universe and the Big Bang
5. Describe how telescopes and spectra reveal information about distant objects
6. Reason about cosmic distances, light-time and measurement

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A student observatory team tracks a variable star and a distant galaxy: they estimate distances using light-time, explain the star's likely life stage, use gravity to model orbits in the field of view, and interpret the galaxy's redshift as evidence for an expanding universe.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2842-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why do astronomers often express distances in light-years?

- A. Because cosmic distances are so vast that light takes years to cross them, making kilometres unwieldy **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a light-year is the distance light travels in a year, convenient for huge distances.
- B. Because a light-year measures time, not distance  
  _Rationale:_ A light-year is a distance, despite the name.
- C. Because light slows down to one year in space  
  _Rationale:_ Light speed is constant; this is a misconception.
- D. Because planets are only a light-year apart  
  _Rationale:_ Planets are far closer than a light-year apart.

**MST-2842-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) The light from distant galaxies is shifted toward longer (redder) wavelengths. What does this provide evidence for? (Select ONE)

- A. The universe is expanding, carrying galaxies apart **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: cosmological redshift is key evidence for expansion.
- B. The galaxies are cooling down and turning red  
  _Rationale:_ Redshift here is about expansion, not surface colour.
- C. The galaxies are made only of red stars  
  _Rationale:_ Composition is not what redshift of this kind indicates.
- D. Light is being created inside our telescopes  
  _Rationale:_ Redshift is a property of the incoming light, not instrument-made.

**MST-2842-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO statements that correctly describe gravity and orbits. (Select TWO)

- A. Gravity provides the centripetal force that keeps a planet in orbit **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: gravity continually pulls the planet toward the Sun, curving its path.
- B. A satellite in orbit is continually 'falling' toward Earth while moving forward **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: orbit is free-fall combined with sideways motion.
- C. There is no gravity anywhere in space  
  _Rationale:_ Gravity acts throughout space; orbits depend on it.
- D. Planets move in perfectly straight lines forever  
  _Rationale:_ Gravity bends their paths into orbits, not straight lines.
