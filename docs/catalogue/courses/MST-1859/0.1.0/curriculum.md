## Learning outcomes

1. Apply skimming and scanning to locate gist and specific information
2. Infer meaning, purpose and tone from textual evidence
3. Analyse text structure, cohesion and the writer's argument

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning (vocabulary, rules, comprehension) through selection items (MCQ/MR) only; does not assess live speaking, extended writing production or any hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1859-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) You need to find only the publication year in a long article as fast as possible. Which reading strategy is most appropriate?

- A. Scanning **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: scanning moves the eyes quickly to locate a specific piece of information.
- B. Intensive reading  
  _Rationale:_ Intensive reading is slow and detailed, unnecessary for one fact.
- C. Skimming  
  _Rationale:_ Skimming finds the gist, not a single precise detail.
- D. Re-reading aloud  
  _Rationale:_ Reading aloud is slow and not targeted at locating a fact.

**MST-1859-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO practices support a valid inference about a text? (Select TWO.)

- A. Citing specific words or details from the text **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: valid inferences are grounded in textual evidence.
- B. Combining stated details with general knowledge logically **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: inference links what is stated to reasonable background knowledge.
- C. Choosing the reading you personally prefer  
  _Rationale:_ Incorrect: personal preference is not evidence.
- D. Ignoring the surrounding context of a sentence  
  _Rationale:_ Incorrect: context is essential to sound inference.

**MST-1859-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) In 'Solar power is clean. However, storage remains costly.', the word 'However' signals that the next idea is:

- A. a contrast with the previous idea **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'however' signals a contrast or concession.
- B. an example of the previous idea  
  _Rationale:_ Examples are signalled by words like 'for instance'.
- C. a cause of the previous idea  
  _Rationale:_ Cause is signalled by words like 'because'.
- D. a restatement of the previous idea  
  _Rationale:_ Restatement is signalled by words like 'in other words'.
