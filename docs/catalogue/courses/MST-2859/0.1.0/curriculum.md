## Learning outcomes

1. Name common animals and say where each one lives
2. Match an animal to the home that keeps it safe and warm
3. Describe how a home gives an animal food, water and shelter
4. Compare homes on land, in water and in the air
5. Explain why different animals need different homes
6. Spot how people can help protect animal homes

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2859-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A rabbit digs a home underground. What is this home called?

- A. A burrow **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: rabbits dig burrows underground to stay safe.
- B. A nest  
  _Rationale:_ Nests are usually built by birds, often up in trees.
- C. A hive  
  _Rationale:_ A hive is the home bees make.
- D. A pond  
  _Rationale:_ A pond is water where fish and frogs live.

**MST-2859-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which home belongs to a bird?

- A. A nest in a tree **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: many birds build nests in trees for their eggs.
- B. A web  
  _Rationale:_ A web is made by a spider, not a bird.
- C. A shell on its back  
  _Rationale:_ A snail carries a shell; a bird does not.
- D. A hole in the sea floor  
  _Rationale:_ That could be a home for a sea creature, not a bird.

**MST-2859-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO things does an animal get from its home? (Select TWO.)

- A. Shelter to stay safe **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a home keeps an animal safe from weather and danger.
- B. A place to rest **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: animals rest and sleep in their homes.
- C. New fur colours  
  _Rationale:_ A home does not change an animal's fur colour.
- D. The ability to fly  
  _Rationale:_ A home does not give an animal the power to fly.
