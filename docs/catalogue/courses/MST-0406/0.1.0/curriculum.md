## Learning outcomes

1. Apply C2-level reading comprehension and use-of-English strategies to complex texts
2. Apply C2-level listening comprehension strategies across registers and genres
3. Plan sophisticated extended writing to a C2 standard (self-checked via models)
4. Evaluate nuanced spoken interaction strategies at C2 level (self-checked via models)

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess:
  - Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.
  - Productive skills (sophisticated extended writing and extended speaking interaction) are practised and self-checked against model responses only; they are NOT machine-scored or certified by this course and do not count toward the completion threshold.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0406-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In a C2 key-word transformation using 'no sooner', which structure is required?

- A. Inversion: 'No sooner had he arrived than...' **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'No sooner' at the front triggers subject-auxiliary inversion with 'than'.
- B. 'No sooner he arrived when...'  
  _Rationale:_ No inversion and wrong linker; this is ungrammatical.
- C. 'No sooner than he arrived...'  
  _Rationale:_ Misplaces 'than' and omits the inversion.
- D. 'He no sooner arrive...'  
  _Rationale:_ Wrong verb form and no inversion.

**MST-0406-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A C2 listening item tests implied attitude. The best strategy is to:

- A. Infer stance from tone, lexis and what is left unsaid **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: C2 listening rewards inference beyond literal content.
- B. Select the option that repeats the speaker's words  
  _Rationale:_ Literal word-matching ignores the implied meaning being tested.
- C. Assume the first opinion stated is the answer  
  _Rationale:_ Order is not a reliable cue for implied attitude.
- D. Ignore tone and focus only on facts  
  _Rationale:_ Tone is central to attitude items.

**MST-0406-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO mark out a strong C2 Proficiency written response?

- A. Sophisticated yet clear control of language **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: range with clarity is assessed at C2.
- B. Well-organised, cohesive development of ideas **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: organisation and cohesion are criteria.
- C. Deliberate obscurity to appear advanced  
  _Rationale:_ Obscurity harms communicative effect and is penalised.
- D. Ignoring task register requirements  
  _Rationale:_ Register must match the task.
- E. Omitting a clear overall position  
  _Rationale:_ A clear stance is expected in argumentative tasks.
