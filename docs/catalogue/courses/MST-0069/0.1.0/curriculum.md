## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the scope of the general securities principal role and the supervisory framework FINRA rules establish
2. Apply supervision and written-supervisory-procedure requirements to branch operations and personnel
3. Evaluate communications with the public, new-account and trading activity against conduct and recordkeeping rules
4. Analyse underwriting, financial-responsibility and reporting obligations a principal oversees

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0069-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A firm designates a principal to approve retail communications. Under FINRA's communications framework, which category generally requires principal approval BEFORE first use?

- A. Retail communication distributed to more than 25 retail investors within any 30-day period **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: retail communications to more than 25 retail investors in 30 days generally require principal approval before first use.
- B. A one-to-one email to a single existing customer  
  _Rationale:_ A single personalised email is correspondence, reviewed under correspondence procedures rather than pre-use principal approval.
- C. An internal memo to registered representatives  
  _Rationale:_ Internal communications are not communications with the public.
- D. An oral statement made on a phone call  
  _Rationale:_ Oral statements are not written or electronic communications subject to the pre-use approval rule.

**MST-0069-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A branch manager wants to reduce how often transactions are reviewed to save time. As principal, what is the governing consideration for setting review frequency in the firm's written supervisory procedures?

- A. The firm's size, business mix and risk, so reviews are reasonably designed to achieve compliance **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: WSPs must be reasonably designed for the firm's business and risk; frequency is risk-based, not arbitrary.
- B. Whatever interval minimises staff workload  
  _Rationale:_ Convenience is not a permissible basis for supervisory review frequency.
- C. A fixed annual review for all activity regardless of risk  
  _Rationale:_ A single fixed interval ignores the risk-based design standard.
- D. Only reviews triggered by a customer complaint  
  _Rationale:_ Supervision must be proactive, not solely complaint-driven.

**MST-0069-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) A principal reviews a new municipal-securities underwriting the firm is joining. Which TWO oversight points fall squarely within the principal's supervisory responsibility here? (Select TWO)

- A. Confirming communications about the new issue meet content and approval standards **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: review of offering communications is a principal supervisory duty.
- B. Checking the allocation against free-riding and withholding restrictions **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: policing allocation of a hot issue is within supervisory responsibility.
- C. Personally setting the coupon rate offered to issuers  
  _Rationale:_ Pricing negotiation is a business function, not the principal's supervisory review duty.
- D. Guaranteeing the bonds will not decline in value  
  _Rationale:_ No one can guarantee market value; this is not a supervisory obligation.
- E. Selecting which retail customer receives the largest social-media following  
  _Rationale:_ This is irrelevant to supervisory duties over an underwriting.
