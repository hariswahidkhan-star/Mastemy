## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0376-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A process yields a profit of $100 with probability 0.6 and a loss of $50 with probability 0.4. The expected monetary value per run is:

- A. $40 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 0.6(100) + 0.4(-50) = 60 - 20 = 40.
- B. $50  
  _Rationale:_ This ignores the loss outcome's contribution.
- C. $25  
  _Rationale:_ This is not the probability-weighted average of the outcomes.
- D. $60  
  _Rationale:_ This counts only the gain and omits the expected loss.

**MST-0376-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Two future cash flows are compared using present worth at interest rate i. Doubling i will generally:

- A. Reduce the present worth of future cash flows **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a higher discount rate lowers the present worth of future amounts.
- B. Increase the present worth of future cash flows  
  _Rationale:_ Higher discounting decreases, not increases, present worth.
- C. Leave present worth unchanged  
  _Rationale:_ Present worth is sensitive to the interest rate.
- D. Only affect the first year's cash flow  
  _Rationale:_ All future cash flows are discounted more heavily, not just the first.

**MST-0376-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO statements about a standard linear program are correct?

- A. The objective function is linear in the decision variables **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: linearity of the objective is a defining property.
- B. The constraints are linear equalities or inequalities **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: LP constraints must be linear.
- C. The decision variables must be integers  
  _Rationale:_ Integrality defines integer programming, not a standard LP.
- D. The feasible region is always a single point  
  _Rationale:_ The feasible region is generally a polyhedron, not a point.
- E. A maximization LP cannot be converted to a minimization LP  
  _Rationale:_ Any max LP can be rewritten as a min of the negated objective.
