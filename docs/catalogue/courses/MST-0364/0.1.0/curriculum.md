## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0364-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) During a treatment session a patient asks the physical therapist assistant (PTA) to add an intervention not in the plan of care. The PTA should:

- A. Contact the supervising physical therapist before adding it **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the PTA works under the plan of care and must involve the supervising PT to change it.
- B. Add the intervention because the patient requested it  
  _Rationale:_ Patient request does not authorise working outside the plan of care.
- C. Discharge the patient  
  _Rationale:_ Discharge is not indicated and is not the PTA's independent decision.
- D. Refuse all further treatment  
  _Rationale:_ The PTA can continue authorised interventions; refusal is not warranted.

**MST-0364-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A patient's heart rate and blood pressure rise sharply and the patient reports dizziness during exercise. The most appropriate immediate action is to:

- A. Stop the activity and monitor the patient **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an abnormal physiological response with symptoms warrants stopping and monitoring.
- B. Increase the exercise intensity  
  _Rationale:_ Increasing intensity would worsen the abnormal response.
- C. Ignore it as a normal training effect  
  _Rationale:_ Dizziness with a sharp haemodynamic change is not a normal response to continue through.
- D. Document it at the end of the week  
  _Rationale:_ The response requires immediate action, not delayed documentation.

**MST-0364-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO actions are consistent with safe therapeutic-exercise progression under a plan of care?

- A. Advancing load only when the current level is tolerated without adverse response **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: progression follows tolerance and stays within the plan of care.
- B. Reassessing the patient's response before progressing **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reassessment guides safe progression.
- C. Doubling resistance each visit regardless of response  
  _Rationale:_ Fixed aggressive increases ignore tolerance and are unsafe.
- D. Changing the plan of care goals independently  
  _Rationale:_ Goal changes are the supervising PT's responsibility.
- E. Skipping documentation of the session  
  _Rationale:_ Documentation is required, not optional.
