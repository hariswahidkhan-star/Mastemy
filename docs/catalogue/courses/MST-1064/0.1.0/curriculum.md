## Integrative case

Coach a capable team member who keeps asking you for answers: choose coaching over mentoring where appropriate, use listening and powerful questions, structure the conversation with GROW, agree accountable next steps, and set up an ongoing relationship with clear boundaries; then explain where you coached versus mentored and why.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1064-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What most clearly distinguishes coaching from mentoring?

- A. Coaching draws answers out of the person; mentoring shares the mentor's own experience **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: coaching is non-directive and client-led; mentoring shares the mentor's expertise.
- B. Coaching and mentoring are identical in practice  
  _Rationale:_ They differ in direction and source of answers.
- C. Mentoring never involves listening  
  _Rationale:_ Listening matters in both.
- D. Coaching always means giving direct instructions  
  _Rationale:_ Instruction is directive, the opposite of coaching.

**MST-1064-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are core coaching skills? (Select TWO)

- A. Active listening to understand rather than to reply **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: deep listening is foundational to coaching.
- B. Asking open, non-leading questions **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: powerful open questions expand the coachee's thinking.
- C. Supplying the answer as quickly as possible  
  _Rationale:_ That removes ownership and is not coaching.
- D. Steering the coachee toward your preferred option with leading questions  
  _Rationale:_ Leading questions undermine the coachee's own thinking.

**MST-1064-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) In the GROW model, what should be clarified before exploring options?

- A. The goal and the current reality **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: GROW establishes Goal and Reality before Options and Will.
- B. The final decision and who to blame  
  _Rationale:_ GROW is developmental, not blame-focused.
- C. Only the will or next step  
  _Rationale:_ Will comes last, after goal, reality and options.
- D. The mentor's preferred solution  
  _Rationale:_ GROW is coachee-led, not solution-imposed.
