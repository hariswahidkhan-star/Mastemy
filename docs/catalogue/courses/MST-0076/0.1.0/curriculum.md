## Learning outcomes

1. Describe economic factors and investment vehicles relevant to advising and securities agents
2. Apply suitability and strategy concepts for clients of advisers and broker-dealers
3. Evaluate state and federal law governing advisers, agents and broker-dealers
4. Analyse ethical duties, conflicts and disclosure across combined-law scenarios

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0076-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A firm advises for a fee and also executes commission trades for the same client. When giving ongoing advice for the fee, which standard applies?

- A. The fiduciary standard owed by an investment adviser **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: advisory activity for a fee is held to the fiduciary standard.
- B. No standard, because the client signed a waiver  
  _Rationale:_ Fiduciary duties generally cannot be waived away.
- C. Only the broker-dealer suitability rule  
  _Rationale:_ Advisory activity is held to the higher fiduciary standard, not suitability alone.
- D. Whatever standard the firm prefers  
  _Rationale:_ The applicable standard is set by law, not firm preference.

**MST-0076-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A commission account shows frequent trades that mostly generate fees with little client benefit. This most likely indicates:

- A. Churning, a prohibited practice **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: excessive trading for commissions is churning and is prohibited.
- B. A model fiduciary process  
  _Rationale:_ Churning is the opposite of acting in the client's interest.
- C. Required diversification  
  _Rationale:_ Diversification does not require excessive fee-generating trades.
- D. A tax-loss harvesting rule  
  _Rationale:_ Tax-loss harvesting does not explain fee-driven overtrading.

**MST-0076-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) An adviser representative manages accounts for clients in two states. Which TWO points most directly determine state registration obligations? (Select TWO)

- A. Whether the adviser has a place of business in the state **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a place of business is a key registration trigger.
- B. The number and type of clients in the state **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: client counts and de minimis rules affect state registration.
- C. The adviser's favourite asset class  
  _Rationale:_ Personal preferences do not affect registration.
- D. The colour of the firm's logo  
  _Rationale:_ Branding is irrelevant to registration.
- E. The adviser's commuting distance  
  _Rationale:_ Commuting distance is not a registration criterion.
