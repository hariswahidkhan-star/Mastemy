## Learning outcomes

1. Apply knowledge of biological and biochemical foundations to passage-based items
2. Apply chemical and physical foundations to living-systems problems
3. Apply psychological, social and biological foundations of behavior
4. Analyse and reason about scientific passages and research data under timing

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0415-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A passage reports that an enzyme's rate plateaus at high substrate concentration. This indicates:

- A. Enzyme active sites are saturated (approaching Vmax) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a plateau at high substrate reflects saturation near Vmax.
- B. The enzyme has denatured  
  _Rationale:_ Denaturation would decrease rate, not produce a stable plateau.
- C. Substrate concentration is zero  
  _Rationale:_ The plateau occurs at high, not zero, substrate.
- D. The reaction is non-enzymatic  
  _Rationale:_ The described saturation behavior is characteristic of enzymes.

**MST-0415-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A research passage reports a correlation between two variables. The best conclusion is that:

- A. A relationship exists but causation is not established **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: correlation alone does not demonstrate causation.
- B. One variable definitely causes the other  
  _Rationale:_ Causation requires more than correlation.
- C. The variables are unrelated  
  _Rationale:_ A reported correlation indicates some relationship.
- D. The study must be invalid  
  _Rationale:_ Correlation data can be valid without proving cause.

**MST-0415-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO strategies suit MCAT passage-based science items?

- A. Use the passage data even when it differs from memorised facts **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the passage governs the answer.
- B. Identify what each question is actually asking before answering **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: targeting the question avoids distractor traps.
- C. Ignore figures and read only the prose  
  _Rationale:_ Figures often carry the needed data.
- D. Assume the longest option is correct  
  _Rationale:_ Length is not a reliability cue.
- E. Answer only from memory, skipping the passage  
  _Rationale:_ Passage-based items require reading the passage.
