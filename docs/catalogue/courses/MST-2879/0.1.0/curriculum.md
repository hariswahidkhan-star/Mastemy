## Learning outcomes

1. Apply common spelling patterns and rules
2. Spell words with prefixes and suffixes correctly
3. Use a dictionary to check spellings and meanings
4. Learn and use new vocabulary accurately
5. Group words by meaning as synonyms and antonyms
6. Proofread writing to find and fix spelling mistakes

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2879-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) How do you usually add '-ing' to the verb 'make'?

- A. Drop the 'e' and add -ing to get 'making' **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: drop the silent 'e' before adding -ing.
- B. Keep the 'e' to get 'makeing'  
  _Rationale:_ The silent 'e' is dropped first.
- C. Double the 'k' to get 'makking'  
  _Rationale:_ No consonant is doubled here.
- D. Add nothing and leave it as 'make'  
  _Rationale:_ The ending -ing must be added.

**MST-2879-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) What does the prefix 'un-' do in the word 'unhappy'?

- A. It makes the word mean the opposite: not happy **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'un-' reverses the meaning.
- B. It makes the word mean 'very happy'  
  _Rationale:_ 'un-' means not, it does not intensify.
- C. It changes the word to a past tense  
  _Rationale:_ 'un-' is not a tense marker.
- D. It has no effect on the meaning  
  _Rationale:_ 'un-' clearly changes the meaning to the opposite.

**MST-2879-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO words are synonyms (words with similar meanings)? (Select TWO.)

- A. big **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'big' and 'large' mean nearly the same.
- B. large **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'large' is a synonym of 'big'.
- C. tiny  
  _Rationale:_ 'tiny' is an antonym of 'big', not a synonym.
- D. blue  
  _Rationale:_ 'blue' is a colour, unrelated in meaning to size.
