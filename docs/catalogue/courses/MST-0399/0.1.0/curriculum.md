## Learning outcomes

1. Apply reading and listening comprehension strategies for academic English
2. Produce spoken responses for independent and integrated tasks (self-checked via model responses)
3. Produce written responses for integrated and discussion tasks
4. Integrate the four skills under test-like timing

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0399-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Passage: 'Although the theory was popular, later evidence undermined its central claim.' What does the passage imply about the theory?

- A. Its main claim was weakened by later evidence **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'undermined its central claim' means the main claim was weakened.
- B. It was never popular  
  _Rationale:_ The passage says it was popular.
- C. The later evidence supported it  
  _Rationale:_ 'Undermined' is the opposite of supported.
- D. The theory had no central claim  
  _Rationale:_ The passage refers to its central claim.

**MST-0399-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) In the TOEFL integrated writing task, a strong response mainly:

- A. Relates the lecture points to the reading points accurately **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the integrated task is scored on accurately relating the lecture to the reading.
- B. States the writer's personal opinion at length  
  _Rationale:_ Personal opinion is for the independent/discussion task, not integrated.
- C. Reproduces the reading word for word  
  _Rationale:_ Copying is penalised; paraphrase and relate instead.
- D. Ignores the lecture and summarises the reading only  
  _Rationale:_ Both sources must be integrated.

**MST-0399-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) To raise a TOEFL integrated speaking score, select TWO practices that most directly help.

- A. Take concise notes capturing the key points from both sources **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: good source notes let you include the required content.
- B. Organise the answer clearly and finish within the time limit **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: coherence and completing within time are scored delivery features.
- C. Use as many rare idioms as possible  
  _Rationale:_ Idiom count is not a scoring criterion and can hurt clarity.
- D. Speak as fast as possible to add content  
  _Rationale:_ Rushed, unclear speech lowers the delivery score.
- E. Memorise one answer and reuse it for any prompt  
  _Rationale:_ A canned answer that ignores the prompt is penalised.
