## Learning outcomes

1. Explain how to plan and build a service value stream to create, deliver and support services
2. Describe the organisation, people, information and technology aspects of service management
3. Apply the relevant ITIL practices (e.g. service desk, incident, problem, change enablement, service configuration) to the value stream
4. Explain how to coordinate, prioritise and structure work to deliver and support services

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1737-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What best describes an ITIL 4 'value stream'?

- A. A series of steps an organisation uses to create and deliver products and services to a consumer **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a value stream is a combination of activities an organisation performs to deliver value to a consumer.
- B. A single ITIL practice executed end to end  
  _Rationale:_ A value stream draws on several practices; it is not one practice.
- C. A fixed hierarchy of management approvals  
  _Rationale:_ A value stream is about the flow of work to value, not an approval hierarchy.
- D. A catalogue of all services offered  
  _Rationale:_ That is closer to a service catalogue, not a value stream.

**MST-1737-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A recurring issue has caused several incidents. Which practice is primarily responsible for finding and removing its underlying cause?

- A. Problem management **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: problem management identifies causes of incidents and manages workarounds and known errors.
- B. Incident management  
  _Rationale:_ Incident management restores service quickly; it does not focus on root-cause removal.
- C. Change enablement  
  _Rationale:_ Change enablement controls changes; it may implement a fix but does not diagnose the cause.
- D. Service desk  
  _Rationale:_ The service desk is the point of contact; it logs and coordinates but does not own cause analysis.

**MST-1737-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are among the four dimensions of service management in ITIL 4?

- A. Organisations and people **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: this is one of the four dimensions.
- B. Information and technology **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: this is one of the four dimensions.
- C. Incidents and problems  
  _Rationale:_ These are practices/work items, not one of the four dimensions.
- D. Strengths and weaknesses  
  _Rationale:_ This is not an ITIL dimension; the four are organisations/people, information/technology, partners/suppliers, value streams/processes.
- E. Risks and issues  
  _Rationale:_ These are management concepts, not one of the four dimensions.
