## Learning outcomes

1. Foundations
2. Types of cryptoassets
3. Wallets, keys and custody

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses conceptual knowledge through selection items; this is general education, not investment advice, and it does not assess trading, custody of real assets, or a regulatory classification for a specific token.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1725-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) If a holder loses the only private key to a self-custodied cryptoasset, they typically:

- A. Lose access to the asset permanently, with no reset available **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: without the key, self-custodied assets are unrecoverable.
- B. Can call support to reset the key like a password  
  _Rationale:_ Self-custody has no central reset mechanism.
- C. Automatically receive a replacement asset  
  _Rationale:_ No replacement is issued.
- D. Keep full access via their email account  
  _Rationale:_ Access depends on the key, not an email login.

**MST-1725-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select ALL that apply) Which two statements about stablecoins are correct? (Select TWO) (Select TWO)

- A. They aim to maintain a stable value, often pegged to a currency **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: stability against a reference is their design goal.
- B. Their ability to hold the peg depends on their backing and design **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: backing quality affects peg reliability.
- C. They are guaranteed by governments to never lose value  
  _Rationale:_ No such universal government guarantee exists.
- D. They are all identical in risk and structure  
  _Rationale:_ Stablecoins differ widely in backing and risk.

**MST-1725-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A message promising guaranteed high daily returns if you send crypto to a wallet now is most likely:

- A. A scam exploiting fear of missing out **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: guaranteed high returns with urgency is a classic scam pattern.
- B. A standard regulated investment product  
  _Rationale:_ Guaranteed high returns are a red flag, not a regulated norm.
- C. A smart contract audit  
  _Rationale:_ That is unrelated to this solicitation.
- D. A central bank digital currency  
  _Rationale:_ A CBDC is a state-issued currency, not this scheme.
