## Learning outcomes

1. Distinguish current tax from deferred tax
2. Account for current tax assets and liabilities for the period
3. Explain the temporary-difference approach to deferred tax
4. Identify common taxable and deductible temporary differences
5. Measure deferred tax using appropriate rates and recognise deferred tax assets
6. Present and disclose income taxes, including the tax reconciliation

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2520-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What approach does IAS 12 use to measure deferred tax?

- A. The temporary-difference (balance-sheet) approach based on differences between carrying amounts and tax bases **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: deferred tax is based on temporary differences between carrying amount and tax base.
- B. A timing-difference approach based only on the income statement  
  _Rationale:_ IAS 12 uses the balance-sheet temporary-difference approach.
- C. Discounting all future tax payments to present value  
  _Rationale:_ Deferred tax is not discounted.
- D. A flat percentage of accounting profit  
  _Rationale:_ Deferred tax is not a flat percentage of profit.

**MST-2520-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO conditions are relevant to recognising a deferred tax asset for unused tax losses? (Select TWO.)

- A. It is probable that future taxable profit will be available against which the losses can be used **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: recoverability against future taxable profits is required.
- B. The losses relate to temporary differences or carry-forwards that are deductible in the future **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the asset must represent a future deductible benefit.
- C. The entity has paid a dividend in the period  
  _Rationale:_ Dividend payment is irrelevant to recognising a deferred tax asset.
- D. The directors wish to improve reported profit  
  _Rationale:_ Management preference is not a recognition condition.

**MST-2520-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A company measures its deferred tax liability by discounting the expected future tax payments to present value. Why is this inappropriate under IAS 12?

- A. IAS 12 prohibits discounting deferred tax assets and liabilities **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: deferred tax balances are not discounted.
- B. Deferred tax must always be discounted at the risk-free rate  
  _Rationale:_ The opposite is true: discounting is prohibited.
- C. Deferred tax liabilities are never recognised  
  _Rationale:_ Deferred tax liabilities are recognised; they are just not discounted.
- D. Discounting is only allowed for current tax  
  _Rationale:_ Neither current nor deferred tax is discounted in this way.
