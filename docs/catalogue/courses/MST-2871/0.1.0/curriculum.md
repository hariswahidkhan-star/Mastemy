## Learning outcomes

1. Define ecosystems and their biotic and abiotic parts
2. Explain energy flow and trophic levels
3. Describe nutrient cycles such as the carbon cycle
4. Explain interdependence and population dynamics
5. Analyse human impacts on ecosystems
6. Evaluate ways to conserve and restore ecosystems

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2871-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why does usable energy decrease at each higher trophic level?

- A. Energy is lost as heat and through life processes **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: energy is lost as heat and in respiration and movement at each level.
- B. Energy is created by predators  
  _Rationale:_ Energy is not created; it is transferred and lost.
- C. Higher levels do not use any energy  
  _Rationale:_ All organisms use energy to live.
- D. Producers take all the energy back  
  _Rationale:_ Producers capture light energy but do not reclaim it from consumers.

**MST-2871-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which of the following is an abiotic factor in an ecosystem?

- A. Temperature **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: temperature is a non-living (abiotic) factor.
- B. A fox  
  _Rationale:_ A fox is a living, biotic factor.
- C. Grass  
  _Rationale:_ Grass is a living, biotic factor.
- D. Earthworms  
  _Rationale:_ Earthworms are living, biotic factors.

**MST-2871-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are human activities that can reduce biodiversity? (Select TWO.)

- A. Clearing forests for farmland **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: deforestation destroys habitats and reduces biodiversity.
- B. Polluting rivers with waste **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: water pollution harms species and lowers biodiversity.
- C. Creating protected nature reserves  
  _Rationale:_ Reserves help protect, not reduce, biodiversity.
- D. Replanting native trees  
  _Rationale:_ Replanting supports biodiversity.
