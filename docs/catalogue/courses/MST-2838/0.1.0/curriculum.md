## Learning outcomes

1. Distinguish weather from climate and name factors shaping climate
2. Explain the natural greenhouse effect and the carbon cycle
3. Describe how human activity increases greenhouse gases
4. Relate rising greenhouse gases to observed climate change
5. Evaluate simple mitigation and adaptation actions
6. Interpret climate data such as temperature and CO2 graphs

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2838-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the difference between weather and climate?

- A. Weather is the day-to-day state of the atmosphere; climate is the long-term average over many years **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: climate is the long-term pattern, weather is short-term.
- B. They mean exactly the same thing  
  _Rationale:_ They are related but not the same.
- C. Weather is the long-term average and climate is today's conditions  
  _Rationale:_ This reverses the two definitions.
- D. Climate only describes the weather on a single day  
  _Rationale:_ Climate spans many years, not one day.

**MST-2838-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) How does the natural greenhouse effect keep Earth warm enough for life? (Select ONE)

- A. Greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit heat, slowing its escape to space **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: greenhouse gases trap outgoing heat, warming the surface.
- B. Greenhouse gases block all sunlight from reaching Earth  
  _Rationale:_ They mainly affect outgoing heat, not incoming light.
- C. They cool the Earth by reflecting heat away  
  _Rationale:_ They trap heat rather than mainly cooling.
- D. They create new energy inside the atmosphere  
  _Rationale:_ They redistribute heat; they do not create energy.

**MST-2838-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO human activities that add extra greenhouse gases to the atmosphere. (Select TWO)

- A. Burning coal, oil and gas for energy **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: burning fossil fuels releases carbon dioxide.
- B. Large-scale clearing of forests **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: deforestation reduces carbon uptake and releases carbon.
- C. Planting and growing more trees  
  _Rationale:_ Growing trees removes carbon dioxide rather than adding it.
- D. Using electricity generated by wind turbines  
  _Rationale:_ Wind power adds little to no greenhouse gas during use.
