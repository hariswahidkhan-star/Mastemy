## Learning outcomes

1. Explain end-to-end supply chain and strategic trade-offs
2. Plan demand and measure forecast quality
3. Set inventory policies that balance cost and service
4. Source and manage suppliers strategically
5. Plan production around capacity and constraints
6. Design logistics and distribution for cost and service
7. Run S&OP to align demand, supply and finance
8. Build resilient, sustainable supply chains

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1161-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why can optimising a single function harm the whole supply chain?

- A. Local optimisation can shift cost or risk elsewhere and worsen end-to-end performance **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the chain is a system; local wins can cause system losses.
- B. Functions never affect each other  
  _Rationale:_ They are interdependent.
- C. The cheapest function is always best for the chain  
  _Rationale:_ Lowest local cost can raise total cost.
- D. End-to-end thinking is only for finance  
  _Rationale:_ It applies across the whole chain.

**MST-1161-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO factors drive the right level of safety stock? (Select TWO.)

- A. Demand and supply variability **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: more variability needs more safety stock for a given service level.
- B. The target service level **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a higher service target requires more safety stock.
- C. The colour of the packaging  
  _Rationale:_ Irrelevant to safety stock.
- D. The CEO's personal preference  
  _Rationale:_ Safety stock is set by variability and service, not preference.

**MST-1161-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the main purpose of the S&OP process?

- A. To align demand, supply and finance on one agreed plan **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: S&OP balances and aligns the plan across functions.
- B. To produce a more detailed demand forecast only  
  _Rationale:_ S&OP is broader than forecasting.
- C. To replace the finance budget  
  _Rationale:_ S&OP aligns with finance; it does not replace the budget.
- D. To schedule individual machines  
  _Rationale:_ That is detailed scheduling, not S&OP.
