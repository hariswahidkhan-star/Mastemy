## Learning outcomes

1. Classify the major groups of microorganisms relevant to human health
2. Describe bacterial structure and the basis of Gram staining
3. Explain concepts of pathogenicity, virulence and the chain of infection
4. Summarise innate and adaptive immune responses and their key cells
5. Explain the principles of vaccination and immunological memory
6. Describe the educational basis of antimicrobial action and resistance

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2132-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Gram-positive bacteria appear purple under Gram staining primarily because they have:

- A. A thick peptidoglycan cell wall that retains the crystal violet dye **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the thick peptidoglycan layer traps the crystal violet stain.
- B. No cell wall at all  
  _Rationale:_ Gram-positive bacteria have a prominent cell wall.
- C. An outer lipopolysaccharide membrane that retains dye  
  _Rationale:_ That outer membrane is a feature of Gram-negative bacteria, which lose the dye.
- D. A nucleus that stains purple  
  _Rationale:_ Bacteria are prokaryotes and lack a membrane-bound nucleus.

**MST-2132-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO features belong to adaptive, rather than innate, immunity? (Select TWO.)

- A. Immunological memory **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: memory is a hallmark of adaptive immunity.
- B. Antigen-specific recognition **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: adaptive immunity targets specific antigens.
- C. Immediate, non-specific skin barrier  
  _Rationale:_ Barriers are part of innate immunity.
- D. General phagocytosis by neutrophils  
  _Rationale:_ Non-specific phagocytosis is an innate response.
- E. Rapid fever response within minutes  
  _Rationale:_ Fever is an innate, non-specific response.

**MST-2132-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A vaccine most directly protects by:

- A. Generating immunological memory without causing the disease **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: vaccines prime memory B and T cells so a later exposure is met quickly.
- B. Killing microbes already circulating with an antibiotic  
  _Rationale:_ Vaccines are not antimicrobials and act before exposure.
- C. Replacing the need for an immune system  
  _Rationale:_ Vaccines work through the immune system, not in place of it.
- D. Providing lifelong passive antibodies from a donor  
  _Rationale:_ That describes passive immunity, not vaccination.
