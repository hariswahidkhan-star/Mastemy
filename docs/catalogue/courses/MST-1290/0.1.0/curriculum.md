## Learning outcomes

1. Explain security principles and risk management across physical, personnel and information security
2. Describe business principles and practices relevant to security management
3. Explain investigations, legal and regulatory considerations for security operations
4. Describe crisis management, business continuity and the protection of people and assets

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1290-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A layered physical-security design is often described by which set of functions?

- A. Deter, detect, delay and respond **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: defence-in-depth physical security aims to deter, detect, delay and enable response.
- B. Encrypt, hash, sign and audit  
  _Rationale:_ These are information-security cryptographic functions, not the physical layering model.
- C. Plan, do, check, act  
  _Rationale:_ That is a continual-improvement cycle, not the physical-security functions.
- D. Identify, protect, detect, recover  
  _Rationale:_ That resembles a cyber framework, not the classic physical deter/detect/delay/respond model.

**MST-1290-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) In risk management, risk is most commonly expressed as a function of:

- A. Threat, vulnerability and impact (consequence) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: risk considers the threat, the vulnerability it can exploit, and the impact if it occurs.
- B. Budget and headcount only  
  _Rationale:_ Budget and headcount are resources, not the definition of risk.
- C. The number of cameras installed  
  _Rationale:_ Camera count is a control, not a measure of risk.
- D. The age of the building  
  _Rationale:_ Building age alone does not define risk.

**MST-1290-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are valid risk-treatment options?

- A. Mitigate the risk by adding controls **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: mitigation reduces likelihood or impact through controls.
- B. Transfer the risk (e.g. via insurance or contract) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: transfer shifts financial impact to a third party.
- C. Ignore the risk and never document it  
  _Rationale:_ Ignoring without documentation is not a recognised treatment; acceptance must be informed and recorded.
- D. Guarantee the risk cannot occur  
  _Rationale:_ No control can guarantee a risk will never occur.
- E. Delete all records of the asset  
  _Rationale:_ Destroying records is not a risk-treatment option.
