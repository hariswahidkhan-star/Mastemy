## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0055-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A key risk when valuing private equity holdings relative to public equities is that:

- A. Reported valuations are stale and may understate true volatility **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: infrequent, appraisal-based pricing smooths returns and understates measured volatility.
- B. They are marked to market every second  
  _Rationale:_ Private holdings are not continuously marked to market; that is the point.
- C. They have no manager fees  
  _Rationale:_ Private equity typically carries substantial management and performance fees.
- D. They are fully liquid at all times  
  _Rationale:_ Private equity is characteristically illiquid.

**MST-0055-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Compared with a long-only equity fund, a market-neutral hedge-fund strategy primarily aims to:

- A. Generate returns largely independent of overall market direction **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: market-neutral strategies seek return from relative value while minimising net market exposure.
- B. Maximise beta to the equity market  
  _Rationale:_ Maximising beta is the opposite of market neutrality.
- C. Guarantee positive returns in every period  
  _Rationale:_ No strategy guarantees positive returns each period.
- D. Avoid the use of any derivatives  
  _Rationale:_ Market-neutral strategies often use derivatives and short positions.

**MST-0055-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO elements that are central to operational due diligence on an alternative-investment manager.

- A. Independent verification of the fund's valuation and administration **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: verifying valuation policy and third-party administration is core operational due diligence.
- B. Assessment of segregation of duties and internal controls **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reviewing controls and segregation of duties is central to operational due diligence.
- C. Guaranteeing the fund's future returns  
  _Rationale:_ Future returns cannot be guaranteed and are not part of due diligence.
- D. Relying solely on the manager's marketing brochure  
  _Rationale:_ Marketing material alone is not adequate due diligence.
- E. Ignoring the fund's service providers  
  _Rationale:_ Service providers are a key focus of operational due diligence.
