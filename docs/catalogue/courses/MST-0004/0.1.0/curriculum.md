## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0004-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) For a contract to be legally binding, which of the following is an essential element?

- A. Offer, acceptance, consideration and intention to create legal relations **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: these are the essential elements of a valid simple contract.
- B. Writing signed by both parties in every case  
  _Rationale:_ Most contracts need not be in writing.
- C. Approval by a court  
  _Rationale:_ Court approval is not required to form a contract.
- D. Payment of a deposit  
  _Rationale:_ A deposit is not a general requirement for contract formation.

**MST-0004-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which document sets out the internal rules governing the relationship between a company and its members?

- A. The articles of association **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the articles of association regulate the company's internal affairs.
- B. The statement of capital  
  _Rationale:_ This records share capital, not internal governance rules.
- C. The register of charges  
  _Rationale:_ This lists charges over company assets.
- D. The annual confirmation statement  
  _Rationale:_ This confirms registered information at a point in time.

**MST-0004-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO consequences that generally follow from a company having separate legal personality.

- A. The company can own property in its own name **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a company can hold property distinct from its members.
- B. The company can sue and be sued in its own name **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a company has its own legal standing to litigate.
- C. Members are automatically liable for all company debts  
  _Rationale:_ Separate personality means members are not automatically liable.
- D. The company ceases to exist if a member dies  
  _Rationale:_ A company has perpetual succession.
- E. Directors own the company's assets personally  
  _Rationale:_ Company assets belong to the company, not directors personally.
