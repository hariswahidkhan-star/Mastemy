## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0375-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) At steady state in a completely mixed reactor operating with no reaction, the mass rate of a conservative substance leaving the reactor equals:

- A. The mass rate entering the reactor **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: at steady state with no reaction or accumulation, mass in equals mass out.
- B. Zero, because the reactor stores all mass  
  _Rationale:_ At steady state there is no accumulation, so storage is not increasing.
- C. Twice the mass rate entering  
  _Rationale:_ Nothing in a conservative steady-state balance doubles the throughput.
- D. The reactor volume times the flow rate  
  _Rationale:_ That product has the wrong units and is not a mass rate.

**MST-0375-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Two future cash flows are compared using present worth at interest rate i. Doubling i will generally:

- A. Reduce the present worth of future cash flows **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a higher discount rate lowers the present worth of future amounts.
- B. Increase the present worth of future cash flows  
  _Rationale:_ Higher discounting decreases, not increases, present worth.
- C. Leave present worth unchanged  
  _Rationale:_ Present worth is sensitive to the interest rate.
- D. Only affect the first year's cash flow  
  _Rationale:_ All future cash flows are discounted more heavily, not just the first.

**MST-0375-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO statements about a hazard quotient (HQ) used in risk screening are correct?

- A. It is computed as exposure divided by a reference dose (or concentration) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: HQ = exposure / reference value.
- B. An HQ greater than 1 indicates a potential for non-cancer concern **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: HQ > 1 flags that exposure exceeds the reference value.
- C. An HQ directly gives the probability of developing cancer  
  _Rationale:_ HQ addresses non-cancer screening, not cancer probability.
- D. HQ is independent of the magnitude of exposure  
  _Rationale:_ HQ scales directly with exposure, so it is not independent of it.
- E. HQ can be computed without any toxicity reference value  
  _Rationale:_ A reference dose or concentration is required to compute HQ.
