## Learning outcomes

1. Describe investment vehicles, economic factors and quantitative methods relevant to advising
2. Apply client-profiling, suitability and portfolio concepts to recommendations
3. Evaluate laws, regulations and the fiduciary duties of investment advisers
4. Analyse ethical conflicts and disclosure obligations in an advisory relationship

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0075-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An investment adviser receives a commission for recommending one of two equally suitable funds. Under the fiduciary standard, what must the adviser do?

- A. Place the client's interest first and fully disclose the conflict **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the fiduciary standard requires putting the client first and disclosing material conflicts.
- B. Always choose the higher-commission fund  
  _Rationale:_ Choosing for the adviser's benefit breaches the fiduciary duty.
- C. Avoid telling the client about the commission  
  _Rationale:_ Concealing a conflict violates disclosure obligations.
- D. Refuse to recommend either fund  
  _Rationale:_ A suitable recommendation can still be made with proper disclosure.

**MST-0075-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A client aged 68 with low risk tolerance holds 80% of assets in one volatile stock. The most suitable first step is to:

- A. Discuss reducing the concentration to match risk tolerance and horizon **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reducing an unsuitable concentration aligns the portfolio with the client's profile.
- B. Add leverage to amplify returns  
  _Rationale:_ Leverage increases risk, contrary to low risk tolerance.
- C. Move everything into a single new stock  
  _Rationale:_ Replacing one concentration with another does not fix the problem.
- D. Do nothing because past returns were strong  
  _Rationale:_ Past returns do not justify an unsuitable concentration.

**MST-0075-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO facts are most essential to document when profiling a client before making recommendations? (Select TWO)

- A. The client's time horizon **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: time horizon is fundamental to suitability.
- B. The client's risk tolerance and objectives **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: risk tolerance and objectives drive suitable recommendations.
- C. The adviser's personal portfolio returns  
  _Rationale:_ The adviser's own returns are not part of the client profile.
- D. The brokerage's quarterly sales target  
  _Rationale:_ Sales targets are irrelevant to client suitability.
- E. The name of the client's childhood school  
  _Rationale:_ This is not material to a suitability determination.
