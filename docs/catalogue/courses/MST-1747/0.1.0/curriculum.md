## Learning outcomes

1. Explain why stakeholder management matters to project and change success
2. Identify and analyse stakeholders and their interests
3. Map stakeholders by power, interest and attitude
4. Design and deliver fit-for-purpose engagement and communication
5. Manage expectations, conflict and stakeholder relationships over time

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A project manager is rolling out a new expense system opposed by several department heads. Identify the stakeholders, map them by power and interest, diagnose the sources of resistance, and build an engagement and communication plan that moves the key blockers from opposed to supportive.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1747-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) On a power-interest grid, how should you treat a stakeholder with high power but low interest?

- A. Keep them satisfied with concise, relevant updates **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: high-power, low-interest stakeholders should be kept satisfied without overload.
- B. Ignore them until the project ends  
  _Rationale:_ High power means they can still derail the work.
- C. Involve them in every detailed decision  
  _Rationale:_ That over-engages a low-interest stakeholder.
- D. Treat them the same as low-power, low-interest  
  _Rationale:_ Their power demands more attention than minimal monitoring.

**MST-1747-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) A department head opposes a change. Which TWO actions best address the resistance? (Select TWO.)

- A. Uncover the underlying concern behind the opposition **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: understanding the real driver lets you address it.
- B. Involve them early in shaping the rollout **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: involvement builds ownership and reduces resistance.
- C. Escalate immediately to have them overruled  
  _Rationale:_ Forcing compliance usually deepens resistance.
- D. Stop communicating with them to avoid conflict  
  _Rationale:_ Silence erodes trust and leaves the concern unresolved.

**MST-1747-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why should a stakeholder map be revisited during a project rather than made only at the start?

- A. Stakeholders, their influence and attitudes change over time **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the map must track shifting power, interest and attitude.
- B. The first map is always wrong  
  _Rationale:_ The point is change over time, not initial error.
- C. Mapping is only a documentation requirement  
  _Rationale:_ It is an active management tool, not paperwork.
- D. To increase the number of stakeholders listed  
  _Rationale:_ Re-assessment is about accuracy, not length.
