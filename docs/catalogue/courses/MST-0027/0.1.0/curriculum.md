## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0027-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An accountant is offered a gift by a client that could influence their judgement. Which fundamental ethical principle is most directly threatened?

- A. Objectivity **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a gift creating bias threatens objectivity, the principle of not allowing undue influence to override professional judgement.
- B. Confidentiality  
  _Rationale:_ Confidentiality concerns disclosure of information, not gifts influencing judgement.
- C. Professional competence  
  _Rationale:_ Competence concerns skill and knowledge, not bias from gifts.
- D. Timeliness  
  _Rationale:_ Timeliness is not one of the fundamental ethical principles.

**MST-0027-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) For a simple contract to be legally binding, which of the following is normally required?

- A. Offer, acceptance, consideration and intention to create legal relations **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: these are the essential elements of a valid simple contract.
- B. Only a verbal promise with no other element  
  _Rationale:_ A bare promise without consideration or intention is not generally enforceable.
- C. Registration with a government court  
  _Rationale:_ Most simple contracts need no registration.
- D. A payment of at least the market value  
  _Rationale:_ Consideration need not be adequate, only sufficient; market value is not required.

**MST-0027-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO features commonly associated with good corporate governance.

- A. A balance of executive and independent non-executive directors on the board **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: board balance and independence are governance features.
- B. An effective audit committee overseeing financial reporting and controls **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an audit committee strengthens oversight and accountability.
- C. Concentrating all power in a single unaccountable individual  
  _Rationale:_ Concentrating unchecked power is contrary to good governance.
- D. Hiding risks from shareholders  
  _Rationale:_ Transparency to shareholders is a governance aim, not concealment.
- E. Paying no attention to internal control  
  _Rationale:_ Sound internal control is central to good governance.
