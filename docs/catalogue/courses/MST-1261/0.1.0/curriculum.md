## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the added value that procurement and supply can deliver to an organisation
2. Describe the key stages of the sourcing and procurement cycle
3. Explain the organisational infrastructure, stakeholders and systems that shape procurement
4. Describe the need for compliance with legal, regulatory and governance requirements

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1261-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A buyer evaluates a bid on price, quality, delivery, quantity and place. Which procurement concept is being applied?

- A. The five rights of procurement **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the five rights (right quality, quantity, place, time, price) frame how procurement delivers value.
- B. The Kraljic matrix  
  _Rationale:_ Kraljic positions items by risk and spend, not these five criteria.
- C. Total cost of ownership  
  _Rationale:_ TCO sums lifetime costs; it is not this five-part framework.
- D. The procurement cycle  
  _Rationale:_ The cycle is a sequence of stages, not these evaluation criteria.

**MST-1261-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) At which stage of the sourcing cycle is a detailed specification normally finalised?

- A. After contract award  
  _Rationale:_ Specifications must be set before tenders are sought, not after award.
- B. Before inviting tenders **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a clear specification is prepared before suppliers are invited to bid.
- C. During contract management  
  _Rationale:_ Contract management monitors performance against an agreed specification.
- D. During supplier payment  
  _Rationale:_ Payment is a post-delivery activity unrelated to specifying need.

**MST-1261-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO activities are part of the post-contract-award phase of the procurement cycle?

- A. Managing supplier performance against KPIs **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: performance management is a post-award, contract-management activity.
- B. Drafting the invitation to tender  
  _Rationale:_ Drafting the ITT happens before award.
- C. Conducting supplier appraisal of shortlisted bidders  
  _Rationale:_ Appraisal of bidders happens pre-award during selection.
- D. Reviewing and closing the contract at end of term **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: contract review and closure is a post-award activity.
