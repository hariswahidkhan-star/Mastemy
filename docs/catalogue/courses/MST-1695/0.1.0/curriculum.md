## Learning outcomes

1. The accounting framework
2. Recording transactions
3. From trial balance to adjustments
4. The financial statements

- Does not assess: Selection items cannot demonstrate full ledger preparation; recording and statement preparation are taught through instructor-built worked examples.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1695-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A business buys equipment for cash. How does this affect the accounting equation?

- A. One asset (equipment) rises and another asset (cash) falls; totals are unchanged **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: swapping one asset for another leaves total assets and the equation balanced.
- B. Assets rise and liabilities rise  
  _Rationale:_ No liability is involved in a cash purchase.
- C. Equity falls because cash left the business  
  _Rationale:_ Paying cash for an asset does not reduce equity; it is an asset swap.
- D. The equation no longer balances  
  _Rationale:_ Double-entry keeps the equation in balance after every transaction.

**MST-1695-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select ALL that apply) Which statements about debits and credits are correct? (Select TWO)

- A. A debit increases an asset account **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: assets increase on the debit side.
- B. A credit increases a liability account **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: liabilities increase on the credit side.
- C. Every transaction affects only one account  
  _Rationale:_ Double-entry means each transaction touches at least two accounts.
- D. Debits must always be larger than credits in an entry  
  _Rationale:_ Total debits must equal total credits, not exceed them.

**MST-1695-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) At period end, a company has earned revenue it has not yet invoiced. Which adjusting entry is needed?

- A. Accrue the revenue: increase a receivable and recognise the income **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: accrual accounting records earned revenue before cash is received.
- B. Do nothing until the invoice is sent  
  _Rationale:_ Accrual accounting recognises revenue when earned, not when invoiced.
- C. Record it as a prepayment  
  _Rationale:_ A prepayment is cash paid in advance, which is not this situation.
- D. Reduce equity for the unbilled amount  
  _Rationale:_ Earned revenue increases income and equity, it does not reduce equity.
