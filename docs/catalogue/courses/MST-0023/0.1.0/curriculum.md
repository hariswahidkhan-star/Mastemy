## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0023-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A company reports a current ratio of 2.5 and a quick (acid-test) ratio of 0.8. What does the gap between these two ratios most strongly indicate?

- A. A large proportion of current assets is tied up in inventory **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the quick ratio excludes inventory, so a current ratio far above the quick ratio means inventory is a large part of current assets.
- B. The company has no current liabilities  
  _Rationale:_ Both ratios use current liabilities as the denominator; the gap is driven by the numerator, not by having no current liabilities.
- C. Receivables are being collected faster than average  
  _Rationale:_ Collection speed is measured by receivables days, not by the gap between current and quick ratios.
- D. The company is technically insolvent  
  _Rationale:_ A quick ratio below 1 is a liquidity concern but does not by itself establish insolvency.

**MST-0023-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A firm can raise debt at an after-tax cost of 6% and equity at a cost of 12%. Its target capital structure is 40% debt and 60% equity. What is its weighted average cost of capital (WACC)?

- A. 9.6% **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: WACC = 0.40 x 6% + 0.60 x 12% = 2.4% + 7.2% = 9.6%.
- B. 9.0%  
  _Rationale:_ This is the simple average of 6% and 12%, which ignores the 40/60 weights.
- C. 18.0%  
  _Rationale:_ This adds the two costs; WACC is a weighted average, not a sum.
- D. 7.2%  
  _Rationale:_ This is only the equity component (0.60 x 12%); the debt component must be added.

**MST-0023-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO costs that are relevant to a short-term decision on whether to accept a one-off special order.

- A. Incremental variable costs of producing the order **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: additional variable costs caused by the order are relevant.
- B. The contribution forgone on displaced regular sales **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: opportunity cost of lost regular sales is a relevant cost.
- C. Depreciation on existing machinery  
  _Rationale:_ Depreciation on assets already owned is a sunk, non-cash allocation and is not relevant.
- D. Last year's apportioned head-office overhead  
  _Rationale:_ Apportioned fixed overhead that will not change is not relevant to the decision.
- E. The original purchase price of raw material already in stock  
  _Rationale:_ Historic purchase cost is sunk; the relevant measure is the replacement or opportunity cost.
