## Learning outcomes

1. Describe major periods and movements in art and their defining characteristics
2. Analyse an artwork using formal elements and principles of design
3. Place artworks in their historical, cultural and technological context
4. Compare how different movements responded to earlier traditions
5. Use appropriate vocabulary to discuss medium, style and iconography
6. Form and justify an interpretation of an artwork with visual evidence

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A learner selects two works from different movements and writes a comparative analysis: formal description, historical context, iconography, and a justified interpretation of how each reflects the concerns of its era.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2426-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Formal analysis of an artwork primarily focuses on:

- A. How visual elements such as line, colour, shape and composition are organised **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: formal analysis examines the visual organisation of the work.
- B. The artist's biography above all else  
  _Rationale:_ That is biographical, not formal, analysis.
- C. Only the monetary value of the work  
  _Rationale:_ Market value is not formal analysis.
- D. The viewer's unexplained emotional reaction alone  
  _Rationale:_ Reaction is not structured formal analysis.

**MST-2426-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO features most distinguish Baroque painting from High Renaissance painting? (Select TWO.)

- A. Dramatic, directional light and strong contrast (chiaroscuro/tenebrism) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: heightened dramatic light is characteristic of the Baroque.
- B. Dynamic diagonal movement and theatrical energy **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: Baroque favours movement and drama over calm balance.
- C. A preference for flat, symbolic, non-illusionistic space  
  _Rationale:_ That is not characteristic Baroque.
- D. Complete absence of religious subject matter  
  _Rationale:_ Baroque includes much religious work.

**MST-2426-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Iconography, as a method, is the study of:

- A. The subjects, symbols and conventional meanings depicted in images **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: iconography interprets subject matter and symbolism.
- B. The chemical composition of pigments  
  _Rationale:_ That is materials analysis.
- C. The frame and hanging height  
  _Rationale:_ That is display, not iconography.
- D. Only the artist's signature  
  _Rationale:_ A signature is not iconography.
