## Learning outcomes

1. Write accessible narration scripts
2. Generate and refine AI narration
3. Build accessible slides
4. Combine narration with slides
5. Publish accessibly on YouTube
6. Operate a repeatable accessible-production workflow

## Integrative case

Produce one accessible lesson: write a spoken-style script, generate AI narration with pronunciation fixes, build high-contrast slides with alt text, sync and export a narrated video, then publish on YouTube with an accurate transcript, chapters and a descriptive summary.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)


**MST-0819-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why mark pauses and emphasis for a text-to-speech engine?

- A. To make synthetic narration clearer and better paced **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: pacing and emphasis cues improve spoken clarity.
- B. To raise the video resolution  
  _Rationale:_ TTS cues do not affect resolution.
- C. To add captions automatically  
  _Rationale:_ Captions are produced separately.
- D. To change the slide order  
  _Rationale:_ Narration cues do not reorder slides.

**MST-0819-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO practices improve slide accessibility? (Select TWO.)

- A. Add alt text to meaningful images **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: alt text conveys image meaning to screen-reader users.
- B. Ensure sufficient colour contrast **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: adequate contrast keeps text legible.
- C. Convey key information by colour alone  
  _Rationale:_ Colour alone excludes colour-blind users.
- D. Place all text inside a single flattened image  
  _Rationale:_ Text in an image is not readable by assistive tech.

**MST-0819-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Auto-captions alone are usually insufficient for accessibility because...

- A. they contain errors in terms, names and timing **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: automatic captions need human correction.
- B. they are always too short  
  _Rationale:_ Length is not the issue; accuracy is.
- C. they disable chapters  
  _Rationale:_ Captions and chapters are independent.
- D. they lower the audio quality  
  _Rationale:_ Captions do not alter audio.
