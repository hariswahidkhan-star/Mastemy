## Learning outcomes

1. How tax systems work
2. Income and the tax calculation
3. Business and transaction taxes
4. Compliance and administration
5. Tax planning vs avoidance vs evasion

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses conceptual knowledge through selection items; this is general tax education, not tax advice, and it does not assess preparation of a real tax return or a jurisdiction-specific filing position.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1703-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A taxpayer's marginal rate is 40% but they pay 28% of total income in tax. The 28% figure is the:

- A. Effective (average) tax rate **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: tax paid divided by total income is the effective rate.
- B. Marginal tax rate  
  _Rationale:_ The marginal rate applies only to the top slice of income.
- C. Statutory headline rate  
  _Rationale:_ The headline rate is a published band rate, not the average paid.
- D. Withholding rate  
  _Rationale:_ Withholding is a collection mechanism, not this average measure.

**MST-1703-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select ALL that apply) Which two statements about VAT/GST are conceptually correct? (Select TWO) (Select TWO)

- A. Registered businesses generally collect it on behalf of the government **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: VAT is collected by businesses and remitted onward.
- B. The final consumer typically bears the economic cost **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: VAT is designed to fall on final consumption.
- C. It is a direct tax on company profits  
  _Rationale:_ VAT is an indirect transaction tax, not a profits tax.
- D. Exempt and zero-rated supplies are identical in effect  
  _Rationale:_ They differ: zero-rated allows input recovery, exempt generally does not.

**MST-1703-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Deliberately under-reporting income to reduce tax owed is best classified as:

- A. Tax evasion (illegal) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: concealing income to pay less tax is illegal evasion.
- B. Tax planning  
  _Rationale:_ Planning uses lawful means; concealment is not lawful.
- C. Tax avoidance  
  _Rationale:_ Avoidance exploits lawful rules; under-reporting is outright illegal.
- D. Tax deferral  
  _Rationale:_ Deferral delays legitimate tax; this hides a liability entirely.
