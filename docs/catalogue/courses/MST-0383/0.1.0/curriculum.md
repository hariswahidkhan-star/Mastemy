## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0383-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In a vapour-compression refrigeration cycle, the component that absorbs heat from the conditioned space is the:

- A. Evaporator **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the evaporator absorbs heat from the space as the refrigerant vaporises.
- B. Condenser  
  _Rationale:_ The condenser rejects heat; it does not absorb it from the space.
- C. Compressor  
  _Rationale:_ The compressor raises refrigerant pressure; it does not absorb space heat.
- D. Expansion valve  
  _Rationale:_ The expansion valve drops pressure; it is not the heat-absorbing component.

**MST-0383-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A cooling load calculation that includes only sensible heat and omits moisture removal will most likely:

- A. Underestimate the total cooling load **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ignoring latent (moisture) load understates the total cooling requirement.
- B. Overestimate the total cooling load  
  _Rationale:_ Omitting a real load cannot overestimate the total.
- C. Have no effect on the total  
  _Rationale:_ Latent load is a real component, so omitting it changes the total.
- D. Only affect the heating load  
  _Rationale:_ Latent load affects the cooling, not heating, calculation here.

**MST-0383-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO properties can be read directly from a point plotted on a psychrometric chart?

- A. Humidity ratio **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: humidity ratio is a chart axis/readable property.
- B. Dry-bulb temperature **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: dry-bulb temperature is a chart axis.
- C. Pipe friction factor  
  _Rationale:_ Friction factor is unrelated to the psychrometric chart.
- D. Electrical resistance  
  _Rationale:_ Electrical resistance is not a psychrometric property.
- E. Soil bearing capacity  
  _Rationale:_ Soil properties are unrelated to the psychrometric chart.
