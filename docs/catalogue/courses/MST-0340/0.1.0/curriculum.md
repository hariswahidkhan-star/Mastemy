## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0340-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A facility manager must decide between run-to-failure and preventive maintenance for a critical air-handling unit. Which factor most justifies preventive maintenance?

- A. The unit's failure would cause high consequence and costly unplanned downtime **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: high failure consequence and downtime cost justify planned preventive maintenance.
- B. The unit is inexpensive and easy to replace  
  _Rationale:_ Low consequence and easy replacement can justify run-to-failure, not preventive maintenance.
- C. Spare parts are always in stock  
  _Rationale:_ Parts availability alone does not determine the maintenance strategy.
- D. The unit is located near the manager's office  
  _Rationale:_ Physical location is irrelevant to the maintenance strategy decision.

**MST-0340-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) When evaluating a facility sustainability initiative, which measure best reflects operational energy performance for benchmarking?

- A. Energy use intensity (energy per unit floor area over time) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: energy use intensity normalizes consumption by area and time for benchmarking.
- B. The total purchase price of the building  
  _Rationale:_ Purchase price is a capital figure, not an operational energy measure.
- C. The number of occupants on the busiest day  
  _Rationale:_ Peak occupancy alone does not measure energy performance.
- D. The age of the HVAC manufacturer's brand  
  _Rationale:_ Brand age is unrelated to measured energy performance.

**MST-0340-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO elements that belong in a facility emergency preparedness plan.

- A. Defined evacuation routes and assembly points **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: evacuation routes and assembly points are core emergency-plan elements.
- B. Assigned roles and a communication protocol for incidents **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: role assignment and communication protocols are essential to response.
- C. A guarantee that no emergency will ever occur  
  _Rationale:_ No plan can guarantee the absence of emergencies.
- D. A rule to delay all notifications until after the event is over  
  _Rationale:_ Delaying notification undermines emergency response.
- E. A policy to remove all exit signage to reduce clutter  
  _Rationale:_ Removing exit signage would degrade, not improve, safety.
