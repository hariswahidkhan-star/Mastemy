## Integrative case

A sales team bought a CRM system but reps still track deals in personal spreadsheets, data is a mess, and no one trusts the forecast. Apply CRM fundamentals: treat CRM as strategy and process, improve data quality, structure the pipeline, strengthen service and retention, and address the adoption and privacy issues that make or break the system.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1834-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A manager says 'we bought a CRM, so our customer management is sorted.' What is the flaw?

- A. CRM is a strategy and set of processes, not just a software purchase **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: without aligned processes and adoption, the software alone changes little.
- B. Buying software always fixes customer management  
  _Rationale:_ Tools without process and adoption rarely deliver value.
- C. CRM has nothing to do with processes  
  _Rationale:_ Process and strategy are central to CRM.
- D. Customer management cannot be improved  
  _Rationale:_ It can, through strategy, process and good data.

**MST-1834-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which practices improve the value of CRM data? (Select TWO)

- A. Setting standards for how data is captured **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: consistent capture keeps data usable and trustworthy.
- B. Regularly maintaining and de-duplicating records **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: maintenance preserves data quality over time.
- C. Collecting as much data as possible regardless of quality  
  _Rationale:_ Volume without quality degrades usefulness.
- D. Letting every user invent their own fields freely  
  _Rationale:_ Uncontrolled fields fragment and corrupt the data.

**MST-1834-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Reps refuse to use a new CRM and keep private spreadsheets. What should a manager address first?

- A. Adoption: why the process and tool are not fitting users' work **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the core issue is adoption and process fit, not the software itself.
- B. Buy a different CRM immediately  
  _Rationale:_ Switching tools rarely solves an adoption problem.
- C. Add more mandatory data fields  
  _Rationale:_ More friction worsens adoption.
- D. Ignore it, as spreadsheets are equivalent  
  _Rationale:_ Fragmented data undermines forecasting and service.
