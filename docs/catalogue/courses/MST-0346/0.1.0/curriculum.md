## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0346-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A rise in arterial blood pressure is detected by the baroreceptors. Which immediate autonomic response restores pressure toward baseline?

- A. Increased parasympathetic tone, lowering heart rate **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: baroreceptor firing raises vagal (parasympathetic) tone and reduces heart rate to lower cardiac output.
- B. Increased sympathetic tone, raising heart rate  
  _Rationale:_ This would raise pressure further, worsening the disturbance.
- C. Release of renin to retain sodium  
  _Rationale:_ Renin acts over hours to days, not as the immediate reflex.
- D. Reduced venous capacitance  
  _Rationale:_ Venoconstriction would raise, not lower, pressure.

**MST-0346-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A drug's hepatic clearance falls while dose and dosing interval stay the same. At steady state the plasma concentration will:

- A. Increase **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: at a fixed dose rate, steady-state concentration is inversely related to clearance, so lower clearance raises it.
- B. Decrease  
  _Rationale:_ Lower clearance cannot reduce steady-state concentration at a fixed dose.
- C. Stay the same  
  _Rationale:_ Steady-state concentration depends on clearance, so it changes.
- D. Fall to zero  
  _Rationale:_ The drug is still administered; concentration does not fall to zero.

**MST-0346-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO features most directly distinguish a malignant neoplasm from a benign one?

- A. Invasion of surrounding tissue **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: local invasion is a defining feature of malignancy.
- B. Capacity to metastasise **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: metastatic potential is characteristic of malignant tumours.
- C. Presence of a well-formed capsule  
  _Rationale:_ A capsule is typical of benign, not malignant, tumours.
- D. Slow, expansile growth  
  _Rationale:_ Slow expansile growth is more typical of benign lesions.
- E. Cells resembling the tissue of origin  
  _Rationale:_ Close resemblance (differentiation) is more typical of benign tumours.
