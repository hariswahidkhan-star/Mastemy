## Learning outcomes

1. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Foundations of internal auditing, the IPPF and professional ethics' to exam-style problems
2. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Managing the internal audit function and engagement planning' to exam-style problems
3. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Performing the engagement: fieldwork, evidence and communicating results' to exam-style problems

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Official The IIA examinations may use item formats (for example scenario, performance-based or constructed-response tasks) that this platform's MCQ/MR forms do not reproduce; see the exam-version record. Domain structure here is a design assumption pending official confirmation.
