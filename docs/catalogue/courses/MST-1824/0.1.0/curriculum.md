## Learning outcomes

1. Explain what marketing is and the role it plays in an organisation
2. Analyse customers, segments and target markets
3. Develop positioning and the marketing mix
4. Plan channels, content and campaigns across the funnel
5. Measure marketing performance and optimise spend

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1824-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the main purpose of market segmentation?

- A. To group customers with similar needs so you can target and serve them better **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: segmentation enables focused targeting and relevant messaging.
- B. To increase the total number of products  
  _Rationale:_ Segmentation is about customers, not product count.
- C. To lower the price for everyone  
  _Rationale:_ It informs strategy, not a blanket price cut.
- D. To avoid any advertising spend  
  _Rationale:_ Segmentation guides, not eliminates, spend.

**MST-1824-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) A brand's positioning is 'premium, hand-crafted quality'. Which TWO marketing-mix choices are consistent with it? (Select TWO.)

- A. A price at the higher end of the category **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: premium pricing reinforces the quality positioning.
- B. Selective distribution through curated retailers **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: selective placement signals exclusivity and quality.
- C. Deep permanent discounting in bulk outlets  
  _Rationale:_ Heavy discounting undermines a premium position.
- D. Messaging focused only on being the cheapest  
  _Rationale:_ A cheapest-price message contradicts premium positioning.

**MST-1824-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A campaign reports millions of impressions but no change in sales or sign-ups. What is the main issue?

- A. Measuring a vanity metric rather than an outcome tied to business goals **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: impressions alone do not show business impact.
- B. The campaign reached too few people  
  _Rationale:_ Reach was high; impact was not measured meaningfully.
- C. Positioning is irrelevant to measurement  
  _Rationale:_ Positioning matters, but the issue here is the metric chosen.
- D. The funnel has too many stages  
  _Rationale:_ Funnel length is not the problem described.
