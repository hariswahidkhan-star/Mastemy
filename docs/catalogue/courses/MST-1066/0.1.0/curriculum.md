## Module and lesson plan

### M01 — Foundations of emotional intelligence  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 26%)_

- M01L01 — What emotional intelligence is and why it matters at work (120 min)
- M01L02 — The self-awareness and self-management domains (120 min)
- Worked applications: Name the emotion behind a strong reaction in a recent work moment; Choose a regulation technique before responding to a provocative email
- Common misconception addressed: Believing emotional intelligence means suppressing or hiding all emotion

### M02 — Self-awareness and regulation  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 26%)_

- M02L01 — Recognising triggers, patterns and physical cues (120 min)
- M02L02 — Regulation strategies and the pause between stimulus and response (120 min)
- Worked applications: Map personal triggers and early warning signs; Practise a reframing technique to lower reactivity
- Common misconception addressed: Treating emotions as facts about reality rather than signals to examine

### M03 — Empathy and social awareness  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 24%)_

- M03L01 — Empathy, perspective-taking and reading the room (120 min)
- M03L02 — Active listening and reading non-verbal cues (120 min)
- Worked applications: Reflect back a colleague's concern to confirm understanding; Distinguish what a person feels from what you assume they feel
- Common misconception addressed: Confusing sympathy or agreement with empathy

### M04 — Relationship management  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 24%)_

- M04L01 — Building trust and giving considerate feedback (120 min)
- M04L02 — Repairing and sustaining working relationships (120 min)
- Worked applications: Give specific feedback using a behaviour-and-impact structure; Open a conversation to repair a strained relationship
- Common misconception addressed: Assuming strong technical skill makes relationship skills unnecessary

## Integrative case

Apply emotional intelligence across a tense project: notice and label your own reactions under pressure, regulate before responding, read a colleague's perspective accurately, and hold a conversation that repairs a strained working relationship.
