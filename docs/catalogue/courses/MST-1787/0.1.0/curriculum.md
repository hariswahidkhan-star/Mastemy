## Learning outcomes

1. Procurement foundations
2. Sourcing and supplier selection

- Does not assess: Selection items cannot demonstrate running a live negotiation; practice comes through instructor-led simulations.

## Integrative case

A manufacturer is over-reliant on one supplier and overpaying. The learner must build sourcing criteria, evaluate alternative suppliers, choose a contract type and negotiation position, design a supplier scorecard and risk plan, and apply ethics rules, then recommend a sourcing decision and defend the value delivered.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1787-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A BATNA in negotiation is:

- A. Your best alternative to a negotiated agreement **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: BATNA is your fallback if no deal is reached, setting your walk-away point.
- B. The first price the supplier quotes  
  _Rationale:_ That is an opening offer, not your alternative.
- C. A type of legal contract clause  
  _Rationale:_ BATNA is a negotiation concept, not a clause.
- D. The budget approved by finance  
  _Rationale:_ Budget is a constraint, not your alternative to a deal.

**MST-1787-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO belong in weighted supplier selection? (Select TWO.)

- A. Quality and capability to meet requirements **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: fitness for purpose is a core criterion.
- B. Total cost, not just unit price **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: total cost of ownership reflects true value.
- C. The supplier's office decor  
  _Rationale:_ Decor is irrelevant to selection.
- D. Whichever supplier replied first  
  _Rationale:_ Response order is not a selection criterion.

**MST-1787-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Cost avoidance differs from cost savings because it:

- A. Prevents a future cost increase rather than reducing current spend **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: avoidance stops costs rising; savings cut the actual spend.
- B. Always appears directly in the budget  
  _Rationale:_ Avoidance often does not reduce the visible budget.
- C. Is the same thing as cost savings  
  _Rationale:_ They are distinct and should not be conflated.
- D. Means buying nothing at all  
  _Rationale:_ Avoidance is not the absence of purchasing.
