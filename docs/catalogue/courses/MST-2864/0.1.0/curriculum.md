## Learning outcomes

1. Name major bones of the human skeleton
2. Explain how the skeleton supports and protects the body
3. Describe how muscles move bones at joints
4. Identify different types of joints
5. Explain why muscles work in pairs
6. Describe simple ways to keep bones and muscles strong

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2864-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the main job of the rib cage?

- A. To protect organs like the heart and lungs **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the ribs form a cage that protects the heart and lungs.
- B. To help us taste food  
  _Rationale:_ Tasting is done by the tongue, not ribs.
- C. To store water  
  _Rationale:_ Ribs do not store water.
- D. To grow hair  
  _Rationale:_ Ribs have nothing to do with growing hair.

**MST-2864-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which type of joint lets your arm swing in many directions at the shoulder?

- A. Ball-and-socket joint **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the shoulder is a ball-and-socket joint that moves many ways.
- B. Hinge joint  
  _Rationale:_ A hinge joint, like the elbow, moves mainly one way.
- C. Fixed joint  
  _Rationale:_ Fixed joints, like in the skull, barely move.
- D. No joint  
  _Rationale:_ There is a joint at the shoulder; it is not jointless.

**MST-2864-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO things help keep bones and muscles strong? (Select TWO.)

- A. Regular exercise **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: moving and exercising builds strong bones and muscles.
- B. Eating calcium-rich foods **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: foods with calcium help build strong bones.
- C. Sitting still all day  
  _Rationale:_ Sitting still does not strengthen muscles.
- D. Never using your legs  
  _Rationale:_ Unused muscles grow weaker, not stronger.
