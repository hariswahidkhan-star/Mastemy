## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0050-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which qualitative characteristic of useful financial information requires that it faithfully represents the substance of transactions?

- A. Faithful representation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: information must faithfully represent what it purports to, ideally complete, neutral and free from error.
- B. Comparability  
  _Rationale:_ Comparability lets users identify similarities and differences across periods or entities.
- C. Timeliness  
  _Rationale:_ Timeliness is about having information available in time to influence decisions.
- D. Verifiability  
  _Rationale:_ Verifiability is about independent observers reaching consensus; it is not the same as faithful representation.

**MST-0050-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) The accounting equation is best expressed as:

- A. Assets = Capital + Liabilities **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the resources of a business (assets) equal the claims against them (capital plus liabilities).
- B. Assets = Capital - Liabilities  
  _Rationale:_ Liabilities are added, not subtracted, in the accounting equation.
- C. Assets + Liabilities = Capital  
  _Rationale:_ This mis-states the equation; liabilities belong on the claims side.
- D. Capital = Assets + Liabilities  
  _Rationale:_ Capital equals assets minus liabilities, not assets plus liabilities.

**MST-0050-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO items that appear in a statement of profit or loss (rather than the statement of financial position).

- A. Sales revenue **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: revenue is an income item in profit or loss.
- B. Cost of sales **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: cost of sales is an expense in profit or loss.
- C. Trade receivables  
  _Rationale:_ Receivables are a current asset in the statement of financial position.
- D. Share capital  
  _Rationale:_ Share capital is equity in the statement of financial position.
- E. Property, plant and equipment  
  _Rationale:_ Non-current assets appear in the statement of financial position.
