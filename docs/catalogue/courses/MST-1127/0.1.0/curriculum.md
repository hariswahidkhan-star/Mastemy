## Learning outcomes

1. Set up an audio recording signal chain
2. Record clean voiceover and spoken audio
3. Edit and clean up recorded audio
4. Mix levels, EQ and dynamics
5. Master and meet loudness standards
6. Export and deliver audio for target platforms

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

An audio producer must deliver a narrated episode for a podcast and a companion video. They must build the recording chain, capture clean voiceover, edit and de-noise, mix with EQ and compression, master to the correct loudness, and deliver the right formats, defending the loudness and format choices per platform.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1127-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Gain staging is set correctly when the recording:

- A. Has a healthy level with headroom and no clipping **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: good gain staging avoids clipping while keeping level.
- B. Clips loudly on the peaks  
  _Rationale:_ Clipping is distortion to avoid.
- C. Is recorded as quietly as possible  
  _Rationale:_ Too low adds noise when boosted.
- D. Uses no microphone at all  
  _Rationale:_ Nonsensical for recording.

**MST-1127-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are good voice-editing practices? (Select TWO.)

- A. Comp the best phrases from multiple takes **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: comping builds the strongest performance.
- B. Apply noise reduction gently to avoid artefacts **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: light cleanup preserves natural tone.
- C. Push noise reduction to maximum on every clip  
  _Rationale:_ Heavy NR makes the voice robotic.
- D. Leave all plosives and clicks in the final cut  
  _Rationale:_ They distract the listener.

**MST-1127-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) LUFS is a measure used in audio mastering to:

- A. Quantify perceived loudness to meet platform standards **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: LUFS targets ensure consistent loudness.
- B. Set the microphone polar pattern  
  _Rationale:_ Unrelated to LUFS.
- C. Choose the room colour  
  _Rationale:_ Irrelevant to loudness.
- D. Measure video resolution  
  _Rationale:_ LUFS is an audio measure.
