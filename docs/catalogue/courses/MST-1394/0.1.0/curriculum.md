## Learning outcomes

1. Explain core AI concepts and capabilities for decision-making
2. Identify valuable and risky AI use cases in a team
3. Lead responsible and ethical AI adoption
4. Manage people, change and ROI in AI initiatives

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)


**MST-1394-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A manager says 'AI can do anything a human can.' What is the best correction?

- A. AI is already fully general and needs no oversight  
  _Rationale:_ Overstates capability; oversight is still required.
- B. AI excels at specific tasks but has limits and needs human oversight **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: capability is task-specific and bounded.
- C. AI cannot do anything useful  
  _Rationale:_ Understates real, useful capability.
- D. AI and human judgement are identical  
  _Rationale:_ They differ; AI lacks human judgement and accountability.

**MST-1394-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO signals suggest a high-value AI use case for a team? (Select TWO.)

- A. A frequent, time-consuming task that costs the team **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: frequency and cost drive value.
- B. A flashy demo unrelated to the team's work  
  _Rationale:_ Novelty without fit is not value.
- C. Relevant data is available and usable **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: data readiness is a feasibility signal.
- D. No one has asked for it and it solves no problem  
  _Rationale:_ No problem means no value.

**MST-1394-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A team buys an AI tool but usage stays near zero. What is the most likely gap?

- A. Missing change management, training and workflow fit **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: tools alone do not drive adoption.
- B. The tool is too cheap to be useful  
  _Rationale:_ Price does not explain low usage.
- C. Staff are incapable of learning anything new  
  _Rationale:_ Unsupported and dismissive of the real cause.
- D. AI tools never get used  
  _Rationale:_ Many AI tools are adopted when rolled out well.
