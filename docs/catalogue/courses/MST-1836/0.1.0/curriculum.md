## Integrative case

A growing company has no real HR practices: hiring is ad hoc, feedback happens once a year, and a grievance has just been raised informally. Apply HR fundamentals: position HR strategically, structure the employee lifecycle, ensure fair and documented practice, build ongoing performance and development, and handle the grievance properly.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1836-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why is it risky to make hiring and promotion decisions on 'gut feel' with no documentation?

- A. It invites unfairness and gives no defensible record of the decision **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: documentation supports fairness, consistency and due process.
- B. Documentation is only needed for firing  
  _Rationale:_ Fair, recorded process matters across people decisions.
- C. Gut feel is the most reliable basis for decisions  
  _Rationale:_ Unstructured judgement is prone to bias.
- D. Records make decisions less fair  
  _Rationale:_ Records support, rather than reduce, fairness.

**MST-1836-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which practices strengthen performance management? (Select TWO)

- A. Giving timely, specific feedback throughout the year **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: regular feedback is more effective than a single annual event.
- B. Linking individual goals to team or business objectives **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: alignment makes goals meaningful and measurable.
- C. Saving all feedback for one annual review  
  _Rationale:_ Delayed feedback loses relevance and impact.
- D. Keeping goals vague so they are easy to meet  
  _Rationale:_ Vague goals cannot be assessed or developed against.

**MST-1836-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) An employee raises a grievance informally. What is a fair first step for HR?

- A. Follow a consistent, documented process to understand and address it **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a fair, recorded process protects everyone and ensures due process.
- B. Ignore it unless it is put in writing with witnesses  
  _Rationale:_ Dismissing a concern undermines fairness and trust.
- C. Immediately discipline the person complained about  
  _Rationale:_ Acting before investigation denies due process.
- D. Tell the employee to resolve it entirely alone  
  _Rationale:_ HR has a role in ensuring fair handling.
