## Learning outcomes

1. Navigate YouTube Studio Analytics tabs and metrics
2. Interpret reach: impressions, CTR and traffic sources
3. Interpret engagement: watch time and average view duration
4. Read audience-retention curves to improve videos
5. Understand the audience: returning viewers and demographics
6. Track subscribers and monetization metrics

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Selection items assess knowledge and applied reasoning only; they do not assess hands-on performance in the product, which is taught through instructor-built walkthroughs.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1468-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A video has many impressions but a very low click-through rate. What does this most likely indicate?

- A. The thumbnail/title are not compelling enough to earn clicks **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: high impressions with low CTR points to a weak thumbnail or title.
- B. The video has no audio  
  _Rationale:_ CTR is about clicks on the thumbnail, not audio.
- C. The channel is demonetized  
  _Rationale:_ Monetization status does not determine CTR.
- D. Watch time is automatically high  
  _Rationale:_ CTR says nothing about watch time.

**MST-1468-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO metrics best reflect how well a video holds viewers? (Select TWO.)

- A. Average view duration **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: average view duration shows how long viewers watch.
- B. Audience retention percentage **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the retention curve shows how well the video keeps viewers.
- C. Number of impressions  
  _Rationale:_ Impressions measure how often the thumbnail was shown, not retention.
- D. Channel creation date  
  _Rationale:_ The channel's age does not measure a video's retention.

**MST-1468-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) What does the audience-retention curve help a creator diagnose?

- A. Where viewers drop off or re-watch within the video **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the curve reveals drop-offs and spikes so creators can improve pacing.
- B. The channel's total lifetime revenue  
  _Rationale:_ Revenue is a separate monetization metric.
- C. The viewer's exact home address  
  _Rationale:_ Analytics does not expose individual addresses.
- D. The upload resolution of the video  
  _Rationale:_ Resolution is a file property, not a retention metric.
