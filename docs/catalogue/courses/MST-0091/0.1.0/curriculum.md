## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the foundations, independence and objectivity of internal auditing
2. Apply proficiency, due professional care and quality assurance requirements
3. Analyze governance, risk management and control concepts
4. Identify fraud risks and controls

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0091-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which of the following is part of the mandatory guidance in the IIA's International Professional Practices Framework?

- A. The Standards **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the Standards are mandatory guidance in the IPPF.
- B. Practice Guides  
  _Rationale:_ Practice Guides are recommended, not mandatory.
- C. Position Papers  
  _Rationale:_ Position Papers are recommended guidance.
- D. Internal audit blog posts  
  _Rationale:_ Informal content is not part of the IPPF.

**MST-0091-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) The chief audit executive reports functionally to management and administratively to the board. What is the main concern?

- A. Organizational independence is weakened **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: functional reporting should be to the board to protect independence.
- B. The audit plan will be too large  
  _Rationale:_ Reporting lines do not dictate plan size.
- C. CPE requirements are unmet  
  _Rationale:_ This is unrelated to proficiency/CPE.
- D. The charter becomes optional  
  _Rationale:_ A charter remains required regardless.

**MST-0091-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are appropriate internal audit responses to identified fraud red flags? (Select TWO)

- A. Expand procedures and preserve evidence **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: expanding scope and preserving evidence are appropriate responses.
- B. Communicate concerns to appropriate parties **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: escalation to the right parties is appropriate.
- C. Publicly accuse the suspected employee  
  _Rationale:_ Public accusation is improper and risks legal exposure.
- D. Ignore the red flags to avoid conflict  
  _Rationale:_ Ignoring red flags breaches due professional care.
