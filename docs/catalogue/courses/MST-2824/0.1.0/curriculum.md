## Learning outcomes

1. Solve linear equations including those with brackets and fractions
2. Rearrange formulae to change the subject
3. Solve and represent linear inequalities
4. Solve simultaneous linear equations
5. Factorise and solve simple quadratic equations
6. Model real situations with equations and inequalities

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2824-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Solve 2(x + 3) = 14.

- A. x = 4 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: expand to 2x + 6 = 14, subtract 6 to get 2x = 8, so x = 4.
- B. x = 10  
  _Rationale:_ That subtracts 6 but forgets to divide by 2.
- C. x = 7  
  _Rationale:_ That divides 14 by 2 before dealing with the bracket.
- D. x = 5.5  
  _Rationale:_ That divides 14 by 2 and ignores the +3 inside the bracket.

**MST-2824-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO values of x that satisfy the inequality x > 5. (Select TWO.)

- A. 6 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 6 is greater than 5.
- B. 9 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 9 is greater than 5.
- C. 5  
  _Rationale:_ x must be strictly greater than 5, so 5 is not included.
- D. 2  
  _Rationale:_ 2 is less than 5, so it does not satisfy x > 5.

**MST-2824-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) What are the solutions of x squared - 5x + 6 = 0?

- A. x = 2 and x = 3 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it factorises to (x - 2)(x - 3) = 0, giving x = 2 or x = 3.
- B. x = -2 and x = -3  
  _Rationale:_ Those would factorise to (x + 2)(x + 3); the signs are wrong.
- C. x = 5 and x = 6  
  _Rationale:_ 5 and 6 are the coefficients, not the roots.
- D. x = 1 and x = 6  
  _Rationale:_ 1 and 6 do not multiply to 6 and add to -5 as required.
