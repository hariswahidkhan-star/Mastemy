## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0160-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why is a documented basis of estimate (BOE) essential to a professional estimate?

- A. It records scope, assumptions, methods and exclusions so the estimate can be understood and validated **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the BOE captures the assumptions, methods, scope and exclusions, making the estimate transparent and defensible.
- B. It replaces the need for any cost data  
  _Rationale:_ A BOE documents the estimate; it does not replace underlying cost data.
- C. It guarantees the project will be on budget  
  _Rationale:_ No document guarantees budget outcomes; the BOE supports understanding and validation.
- D. It is only needed after the project finishes  
  _Rationale:_ The BOE is prepared with the estimate, not after project completion.

**MST-0160-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A parametric estimate uses a cost-per-square-metre factor from historical projects. What must be checked before applying it to a new project?

- A. That the historical data is normalized and comparable in scope, time and location **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: parametric factors must be normalized for scope, escalation and location to be valid for the new project.
- B. That the new project has exactly the same client  
  _Rationale:_ Client identity does not validate a parametric factor.
- C. Nothing; historical factors always transfer directly  
  _Rationale:_ Factors require normalization; they do not transfer unadjusted.
- D. Only that the currency symbol matches  
  _Rationale:_ Currency symbol alone ignores escalation, scope and location differences.

**MST-0160-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are legitimate purposes of an estimate review before issue? (Select TWO)

- A. Checking that scope, quantities and pricing are consistent and complete **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: review verifies completeness and consistency of scope, quantities and pricing.
- B. Confirming assumptions, exclusions and contingency are documented and reasonable **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reviewers confirm assumptions/exclusions and the reasonableness of contingency.
- C. Lowering the total to match a desired number regardless of basis  
  _Rationale:_ Changing figures to hit a target without basis is unethical and invalid.
- D. Removing the basis of estimate to simplify the package  
  _Rationale:_ The BOE must remain; removing it undermines defensibility.
- E. Hiding known risks from the approver  
  _Rationale:_ Concealing risks falsifies the estimate's risk picture.
