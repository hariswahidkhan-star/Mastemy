## Learning outcomes

1. Purpose and principles of auditing
2. The audit process and risk
3. Evidence and procedures
4. Controls and sampling
5. Reporting and conclusions

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess performance of a live audit engagement or professional judgement under an engagement partner.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1701-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An auditor expresses an opinion that financial statements are free from material misstatement. What level of assurance is this?

- A. Reasonable assurance, not absolute **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an audit gives reasonable (high but not absolute) assurance.
- B. Absolute assurance of no error  
  _Rationale:_ No audit can guarantee the absence of all error or fraud.
- C. No assurance at all  
  _Rationale:_ An audit opinion does convey assurance; this describes a compilation.
- D. A guarantee of future solvency  
  _Rationale:_ An audit opinion is on the statements, not a guarantee of future performance.

**MST-1701-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select ALL that apply) Which sources of audit evidence are generally considered more reliable? (Select TWO) (Select TWO)

- A. A confirmation received directly from the client's bank **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: external evidence obtained directly by the auditor is more reliable.
- B. The auditor's own recalculation of a schedule **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: evidence obtained directly by the auditor is highly reliable.
- C. A photocopy of an internal memo  
  _Rationale:_ Internal, easily altered documents are weaker evidence.
- D. An oral assurance from management  
  _Rationale:_ Management representations alone are weak and must be corroborated.

**MST-1701-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) The auditor cannot obtain sufficient evidence over a material inventory balance and the effect could be pervasive. Which opinion fits?

- A. Disclaimer of opinion **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a pervasive scope limitation leads to a disclaimer.
- B. Unqualified opinion  
  _Rationale:_ An unqualified opinion is inappropriate when evidence is missing.
- C. Qualified 'except for' opinion  
  _Rationale:_ A qualified opinion fits a material but not pervasive issue.
- D. Adverse opinion  
  _Rationale:_ Adverse fits pervasive misstatement, not a lack of evidence.
