## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1027-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) At commencement of a lease, how is the right-of-use asset initially measured (in the typical case)?

- A. At the initial lease liability plus prepayments, initial direct costs and estimated dismantling costs **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the ROU asset starts from the liability plus those additions.
- B. At the fair value of the underlying asset  
  _Rationale:_ IFRS 16 does not measure the ROU asset at the asset's fair value.
- C. At the total of all undiscounted lease payments  
  _Rationale:_ The liability is the present value, not the undiscounted total.
- D. At zero until the first payment is made  
  _Rationale:_ The asset is recognised at commencement, not deferred.

**MST-1027-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO recognition exemptions does IFRS 16 permit a lessee to apply? (Select TWO)

- A. Leases with a term of 12 months or less (short-term) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: short-term leases may be exempted from on-balance-sheet recognition.
- B. Leases of low-value underlying assets **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: low-value asset leases may be exempted.
- C. All leases of property regardless of value  
  _Rationale:_ There is no blanket property exemption.
- D. Any lease the company prefers to keep off balance sheet  
  _Rationale:_ Exemptions are defined by criteria, not preference.

**MST-1027-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Over what period is the right-of-use asset normally depreciated when the lease does not transfer ownership?

- A. The lease term (or shorter asset useful life if applicable) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: without ownership transfer, depreciation is over the lease term.
- B. The underlying asset's full economic life regardless of lease term  
  _Rationale:_ Full economic life applies only when ownership transfers.
- C. A fixed five years for all leases  
  _Rationale:_ There is no fixed statutory period.
- D. It is not depreciated  
  _Rationale:_ The ROU asset is depreciated.
