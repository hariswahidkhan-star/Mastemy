## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0380-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A simply supported beam carries a single central point load. The maximum bending moment occurs:

- A. At mid-span, under the load **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: for a central point load on a simple beam the maximum moment is at mid-span.
- B. At the supports  
  _Rationale:_ Bending moment is zero at simple supports.
- C. Uniformly along the beam  
  _Rationale:_ The moment varies along the span; it is not uniform.
- D. At the quarter points only  
  _Rationale:_ The maximum for this case is at mid-span, not the quarter points.

**MST-0380-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) In load and resistance factor design, the design strength of a member is:

- A. The nominal strength multiplied by a resistance factor less than one **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: design strength = phi x nominal strength, with phi <= 1.
- B. The nominal strength multiplied by a load factor greater than one  
  _Rationale:_ Load factors amplify loads, not the member's resistance.
- C. Equal to the service load with no factors  
  _Rationale:_ LRFD applies factors to both demand and resistance.
- D. The nominal strength divided by the modulus of elasticity  
  _Rationale:_ Modulus of elasticity is a stiffness property, not a strength factor.

**MST-0380-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO statements comparing steel and reinforced concrete members are generally correct?

- A. Steel has high tensile strength and is used for tension members **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: steel resists tension well.
- B. Concrete is strong in compression but weak in tension, so steel reinforcement resists tension **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reinforcement carries the tension concrete cannot.
- C. Plain concrete is as strong in tension as in compression  
  _Rationale:_ Concrete is far weaker in tension than in compression.
- D. Steel reinforcement is placed to carry compression only  
  _Rationale:_ Reinforcement is placed chiefly where tension develops.
- E. Load factors are unnecessary for either material  
  _Rationale:_ Factored loads are used in modern design of both.
