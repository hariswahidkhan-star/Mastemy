## Integrative case

A company has a DEI statement on its website but hiring panels are informal, meetings are dominated by a few voices, and a recent microaggression went unaddressed. Apply DEI fundamentals: clarify the concepts, surface bias and barriers, build inclusive everyday behaviour, make processes fairer, and turn statements into measured action.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1837-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the key difference between equality and equity?

- A. Equality gives everyone the same; equity adjusts support to needs and barriers **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: equity accounts for different starting points and barriers.
- B. They mean exactly the same thing  
  _Rationale:_ They are related but distinct concepts.
- C. Equity means treating everyone identically  
  _Rationale:_ That describes equality, not equity.
- D. Equality means ignoring fairness entirely  
  _Rationale:_ Equality is one notion of fairness, not its absence.

**MST-1837-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which actions help reduce bias in a hiring process? (Select TWO)

- A. Using structured, consistent interview questions for all candidates **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: structure reduces the influence of unconscious bias.
- B. Defining job-relevant criteria before reviewing candidates **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: pre-set criteria guard against post-hoc rationalisation.
- C. Relying on an informal 'culture fit' gut feeling  
  _Rationale:_ Vague 'fit' judgements often encode bias.
- D. Assuming a neutral-seeming process needs no examination  
  _Rationale:_ Processes can embed systemic barriers despite appearing neutral.

**MST-1837-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A company publishes a strong DEI statement but changes none of its practices. What does this illustrate?

- A. A statement is not the same as measured, sustained action **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: real change requires action and honest measurement, not words alone.
- B. Publishing a statement guarantees inclusion  
  _Rationale:_ Statements without action do not change outcomes.
- C. DEI cannot be measured  
  _Rationale:_ Progress can be tracked with meaningful measures.
- D. Everyday behaviour is irrelevant to DEI  
  _Rationale:_ Inclusive behaviour is central to real change.
