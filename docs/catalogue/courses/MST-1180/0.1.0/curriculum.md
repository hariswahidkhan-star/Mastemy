- Does not assess: Selection items cannot demonstrate operating plant or walking a site inspection; hands-on practice belongs on site.

## Integrative case

A facilities manager takes over a mixed-use building with an ageing HVAC system, under-used floors, overdue statutory inspections and a rising energy bill. The learner must prioritise planned maintenance, improve space utilisation, restore compliance, and set SLAs and KPIs for an outsourced contract, then present an improvement plan.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1180-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which is an example of a 'hard' FM service?

- A. HVAC maintenance **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: hard FM covers the physical building fabric and engineering systems such as HVAC.
- B. Cleaning  
  _Rationale:_ Cleaning is a soft service.
- C. Catering  
  _Rationale:_ Catering is a soft service.
- D. Reception staffing  
  _Rationale:_ Reception is a soft service.

**MST-1180-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO statements about SLAs and KPIs are correct? (Select TWO.)

- A. An SLA states the service level that is promised **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the SLA defines the committed standard.
- B. A KPI measures whether the SLA is being met **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: KPIs quantify performance against the promise.
- C. An SLA and a KPI are the same document  
  _Rationale:_ They are distinct: one is the promise, one is the measure.
- D. KPIs should be set after the contract ends  
  _Rationale:_ KPIs must be defined up front to be useful.

**MST-1180-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Space utilisation is most accurately measured by:

- A. How often space is actually occupied over time **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: utilisation reflects real occupancy, not just allocation.
- B. How many desks are assigned to staff  
  _Rationale:_ Assignment is capacity, not utilisation.
- C. The total floor area of the building  
  _Rationale:_ Area is a denominator, not utilisation itself.
- D. The number of meeting rooms booked once  
  _Rationale:_ A single booking does not measure sustained use.
