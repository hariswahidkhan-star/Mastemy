## Learning outcomes

1. Identify where AI supports HR and recruiting workflows
2. Use AI to assist sourcing, screening and communication responsibly
3. Recognise bias and fairness risks in AI hiring tools
4. Apply privacy and candidate-rights practices to AI in HR
5. Keep human judgement and accountability in people decisions

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A recruiter wants to use AI to speed up a high-volume hiring round. Decide where AI can assist, where it could introduce bias or legal risk, and design a process that keeps fairness, candidate privacy and human decision-making intact.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1376-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An AI screening tool is trained on a company's past hires. Why might this introduce bias?

- A. It can learn and repeat historical patterns of unfair selection **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: models trained on biased history can reproduce that bias.
- B. Past data is always perfectly fair  
  _Rationale:_ Historical hiring data can embed existing bias.
- C. Training on history guarantees fairness  
  _Rationale:_ History can carry the very bias we want to avoid.
- D. Bias is impossible once data is digital  
  _Rationale:_ Digitising data does not remove bias.

**MST-1376-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO practices keep AI-assisted hiring fair and accountable? (Select TWO.)

- A. Require human review before any rejection decision **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: human review catches unfair or erroneous outcomes.
- B. Document how AI influenced each decision **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: documentation supports accountability and appeals.
- C. Let the tool auto-reject candidates with no review  
  _Rationale:_ Unreviewed auto-rejection risks unfair, unlawful outcomes.
- D. Hide the use of AI from all candidates  
  _Rationale:_ Concealment undermines transparency and trust.

**MST-1376-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A recruiter wants to feed candidate CVs into a public AI tool. What is the key concern?

- A. Candidate personal data may be exposed or reused without consent **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: pasting personal data into an uncontrolled tool risks privacy breaches.
- B. The tool will improve the candidate's skills  
  _Rationale:_ That is not the relevant risk.
- C. There is no privacy issue with CVs  
  _Rationale:_ CVs contain personal data needing protection.
- D. Consent is never required in recruiting  
  _Rationale:_ Candidate data use often requires a lawful basis.
