## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the structure and function of major eukaryotic and prokaryotic cell components
2. Explain how biological membranes control transport and compartmentalisation
3. Trace the flow of energy through cellular respiration and photosynthesis
4. Explain the cell cycle, mitosis and the checkpoints that regulate division
5. Relate cytoskeletal and trafficking systems to cell shape, movement and secretion
6. Interpret basic microscopy and cell-biology evidence to reason about cell state

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1974-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A cell is placed in a solution with a much higher solute concentration than its cytoplasm. What happens to the cell?

- A. Water leaves the cell by osmosis and the cell shrinks **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: water moves toward the higher-solute (hypertonic) side, so the cell loses water.
- B. Water enters the cell and it swells and may burst  
  _Rationale:_ That describes hypotonic surroundings, the opposite case.
- C. Nothing, because membranes block all water movement  
  _Rationale:_ Water crosses membranes readily via the bilayer and aquaporins.
- D. Solute is actively pumped in until concentrations match  
  _Rationale:_ The immediate passive response is water loss by osmosis.

**MST-1974-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO organelles are directly involved in producing and packaging proteins for secretion? (Select TWO.)

- A. Rough endoplasmic reticulum **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ribosomes on the rough ER synthesise secretory and membrane proteins.
- B. Golgi apparatus **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the Golgi modifies, sorts and packages proteins for secretion.
- C. Peroxisome  
  _Rationale:_ Peroxisomes handle oxidative reactions, not secretory protein packaging.
- D. Smooth endoplasmic reticulum  
  _Rationale:_ Smooth ER handles lipid synthesis and detoxification, not protein packaging.

**MST-1974-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) During which process is the majority of ATP generated in aerobic respiration?

- A. Oxidative phosphorylation at the inner mitochondrial membrane **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the electron transport chain and ATP synthase produce most ATP.
- B. Glycolysis in the cytosol  
  _Rationale:_ Glycolysis yields only a small net amount of ATP.
- C. The citric acid cycle directly  
  _Rationale:_ The cycle mainly supplies electron carriers, not bulk ATP directly.
- D. Fermentation  
  _Rationale:_ Fermentation regenerates NAD+ but makes little ATP.
