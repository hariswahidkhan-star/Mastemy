## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0088-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Statutory accounting (SAP) in US insurance regulation emphasizes, relative to GAAP:

- A. Conservatism and solvency/policyholder protection **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: SAP is conservative and focused on solvency and policyholder protection.
- B. Maximizing reported earnings  
  _Rationale:_ That is more an aim of some GAAP presentations, not SAP conservatism.
- C. Ignoring the ability to pay claims  
  _Rationale:_ SAP is specifically concerned with claims-paying ability.
- D. Eliminating all reserves  
  _Rationale:_ SAP requires conservative reserves.

**MST-0088-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A Statement of Actuarial Opinion on loss reserves primarily addresses whether the reserves are:

- A. Reasonable and make adequate provision for unpaid claims **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the opinion speaks to reasonableness and adequacy of reserves.
- B. The lowest legally allowed number  
  _Rationale:_ The opinion is about reasonableness, not minimization.
- C. Equal to premium income  
  _Rationale:_ Reserves are not set equal to premium income.
- D. Irrelevant to solvency  
  _Rationale:_ Reserve adequacy is central to solvency.

**MST-0088-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO mechanisms that support policyholder protection when a US insurer becomes insolvent.

- A. State guaranty funds/associations **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: guaranty funds cover certain claims of insolvent insurers.
- B. Risk-based capital (RBC) requirements and regulatory action levels **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: RBC triggers regulatory intervention before insolvency worsens.
- C. Letting the insurer set its own solvency rules with no oversight  
  _Rationale:_ Self-regulation without oversight does not protect policyholders.
- D. Deleting the insurer’s reserves from the balance sheet  
  _Rationale:_ Removing reserves would harm, not protect, policyholders.
- E. Paying dividends ahead of claims in insolvency  
  _Rationale:_ Policyholder claims rank ahead of shareholder dividends.
