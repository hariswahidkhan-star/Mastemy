## Learning outcomes

1. Explain and apply the in-scope objectives of 'The Technical Test Analyst's tasks in risk-based testing' as published by ISTQB for the stated exam version.
2. Explain and apply the in-scope objectives of 'White-box test techniques' as published by ISTQB for the stated exam version.
3. Explain and apply the in-scope objectives of 'Static and dynamic analysis' as published by ISTQB for the stated exam version.
4. Explain and apply the in-scope objectives of 'Quality characteristics for technical testing' as published by ISTQB for the stated exam version.
5. Explain and apply the in-scope objectives of 'Reviews' as published by ISTQB for the stated exam version.
6. Explain and apply the in-scope objectives of 'Test tools and automation' as published by ISTQB for the stated exam version.
