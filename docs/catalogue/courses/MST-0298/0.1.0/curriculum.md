## Learning outcomes

1. Lead HR strategy aligned with organizational direction in an international setting
2. Direct workforce planning, talent management and total rewards at senior level
3. Oversee employee relations, engagement and organizational effectiveness internationally
4. Govern HR risk, compliance, ethics and HR technology at a senior level

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0298-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which statement best reflects a strategic senior-HR role in a multinational?

- A. Aligning global workforce capability and programs with business goals **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: strategic HR links people decisions to business direction.
- B. Only processing payroll across countries  
  _Rationale:_ Payroll processing is operational, not strategic.
- C. Filing paperwork only when audited  
  _Rationale:_ Reactive compliance is not strategy.
- D. Keeping HR separate from business planning  
  _Rationale:_ Isolating HR is the opposite of strategy.

**MST-0298-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Critical cross-border roles will be vacant within a year. The best senior-HR response is to:

- A. Activate succession planning that develops and tracks internal readiness **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: succession planning prepares pipelines ahead of vacancies.
- B. Wait until the roles are empty to act  
  _Rationale:_ Reactive hiring risks long critical gaps.
- C. Promote strictly by seniority  
  _Rationale:_ Seniority alone does not ensure readiness.
- D. Freeze the roles to save money  
  _Rationale:_ Freezing critical roles harms operations.

**MST-0298-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO practices that make a cross-border total-rewards decision defensible.

- A. Benchmarking against relevant local market data **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: local market data supports fair pay.
- B. Documenting rationale and internal equity analysis **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: documentation makes decisions defensible.
- C. Setting pay on personal preference  
  _Rationale:_ Preference introduces bias and risk.
- D. Ignoring local statutory requirements  
  _Rationale:_ Local law cannot be ignored.
- E. Changing criteria case by case  
  _Rationale:_ Inconsistency undermines equity.
