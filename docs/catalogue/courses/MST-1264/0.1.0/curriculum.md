## Learning outcomes

1. Explain ethical, environmental, social and economic dimensions of sustainability
2. Describe corporate social responsibility and ethical sourcing practices
3. Explain how to source ethically and manage supply-chain risk
4. Describe relevant standards, legislation and assurance mechanisms

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1264-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) The 'triple bottom line' evaluates performance against which three dimensions?

- A. People, planet and profit **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the triple bottom line covers social (people), environmental (planet) and economic (profit) dimensions.
- B. Price, place and promotion  
  _Rationale:_ These are marketing mix elements, not sustainability pillars.
- C. Plan, do, check  
  _Rationale:_ This is a continuous-improvement cycle, not the triple bottom line.
- D. Cost, quality and time  
  _Rationale:_ These are project constraints, not sustainability pillars.

**MST-1264-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Making an unsubstantiated environmental claim to appear more sustainable than is true is known as:

- A. Greenwashing **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: greenwashing is making misleading or unsubstantiated sustainability claims.
- B. Due diligence  
  _Rationale:_ Due diligence is investigation to verify, the opposite of this.
- C. Life-cycle analysis  
  _Rationale:_ LCA measures real impacts across a product's life.
- D. Carbon offsetting  
  _Rationale:_ Offsetting compensates for emissions; it is not a false claim by definition.

**MST-1264-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO actions strengthen ethical due diligence beyond tier-1 suppliers?

- A. Mapping sub-tier suppliers and their locations **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: visibility into lower tiers is essential for genuine due diligence.
- B. Requiring cascaded codes of conduct down the chain **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: cascading standards to sub-tiers extends ethical control.
- C. Checking only the tier-1 supplier's annual accounts  
  _Rationale:_ Accounts alone do not reveal sub-tier labour risks.
- D. Relying on the lowest quoted price as a proxy for compliance  
  _Rationale:_ Low price is unrelated to ethical compliance and may increase risk.
