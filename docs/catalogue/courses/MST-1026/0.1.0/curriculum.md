## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1026-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the core principle of IFRS 15?

- A. Recognise revenue to depict the transfer of goods or services for the consideration expected **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: revenue reflects the transfer of promised goods/services at the amount expected in exchange.
- B. Recognise revenue when cash is collected  
  _Rationale:_ IFRS 15 is not cash-based.
- C. Recognise revenue evenly across the financial year  
  _Rationale:_ Timing follows transfer of control, not the calendar.
- D. Recognise revenue only at year end  
  _Rationale:_ Recognition follows satisfaction of obligations throughout the period.

**MST-1026-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO criteria indicate revenue should be recognised over time? (Select TWO)

- A. The customer simultaneously receives and consumes the benefits as the entity performs **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: this is one of the over-time criteria in IFRS 15.
- B. The entity's performance creates an asset with no alternative use and there is an enforceable right to payment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: this combination also triggers over-time recognition.
- C. The customer pays a deposit up front  
  _Rationale:_ A deposit does not by itself determine timing.
- D. The contract spans more than one accounting period  
  _Rationale:_ Duration alone does not mandate over-time recognition.

**MST-1026-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) When a contract has variable consideration, how is it treated in the transaction price?

- A. Estimated and included, subject to the constraint that a significant reversal is not probable **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: variable consideration is estimated and constrained to avoid probable significant reversals.
- B. Excluded entirely until fully certain  
  _Rationale:_ IFRS 15 requires estimation, not deferral until certainty.
- C. Always recognised at the maximum possible amount  
  _Rationale:_ The constraint limits over-optimistic estimates.
- D. Recognised only after the contract ends  
  _Rationale:_ It is estimated during the contract, subject to the constraint.
