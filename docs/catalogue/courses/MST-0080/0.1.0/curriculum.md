## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0080-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) If claim counts follow a Poisson distribution, which statement is true?

- A. The mean equals the variance **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: for a Poisson distribution the mean and variance are both equal to the rate parameter.
- B. The variance is always zero  
  _Rationale:_ A zero variance would mean a constant, not a Poisson count.
- C. It can take negative values  
  _Rationale:_ Count distributions are non-negative integers.
- D. It has no mean  
  _Rationale:_ The Poisson distribution has a finite mean equal to its rate.

**MST-0080-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) An aggregate loss model S = X1 + ... + XN with N the claim count and X the severities assumes, in the collective risk model, that:

- A. N and the severities are independent and severities are identically distributed **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the collective risk model assumes independence of frequency and severity and i.i.d. severities.
- B. Severities are always equal to the mean claim  
  _Rationale:_ Severities are random, not fixed at the mean.
- C. The number of claims is fixed in advance  
  _Rationale:_ That is the individual risk model, not the collective model.
- D. Losses can be negative  
  _Rationale:_ Severities are non-negative in standard loss models.

**MST-0080-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO quantities that increase as a deductible on a coverage increases, all else equal.

- A. The insurer’s expected payment per loss decreases, so the retained loss to the policyholder increases **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a higher deductible shifts more loss to the policyholder.
- B. The expected number of payments the insurer makes (payments above the deductible) decreases  
  _Rationale:_ This decreases, not increases, so it is not a correct choice.
- C. The policyholder’s expected retained loss **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the retained loss below the deductible rises with the deductible.
- D. The ground-up expected loss before any deductible  
  _Rationale:_ Ground-up loss does not depend on the deductible.
- E. The insurer’s expected payment per loss  
  _Rationale:_ This decreases with a higher deductible.
