## Module and lesson plan

### M01 — Understanding conflict  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 26%)_

- M01L01 — Sources and types of workplace conflict (120 min)
- M01L02 — Conflict styles and when each fits (120 min)
- Worked applications: Diagnose the source of a described team conflict; Identify your default conflict style and its trade-offs
- Common misconception addressed: Seeing all conflict as destructive rather than sometimes productive

### M02 — Preparing for difficult conversations  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 26%)_

- M02L01 — Separating the issue, emotions and identity (120 min)
- M02L02 — Setting intent, goals and a safe setting (120 min)
- Worked applications: Plan the opening of a difficult conversation with a clear purpose; Anticipate the other person's perspective before the meeting
- Common misconception addressed: Walking into a hard conversation with no preparation and winging it

### M03 — Interests and problem solving  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 24%)_

- M03L01 — Positions versus interests (120 min)
- M03L02 — Generating options and objective criteria (120 min)
- Worked applications: Uncover the interest behind a fixed position; Brainstorm options that could satisfy both parties' interests
- Common misconception addressed: Negotiating positions directly instead of exploring underlying interests

### M04 — Reaching and sustaining agreement  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 24%)_

- M04L01 — Agreement, commitments and follow-through (120 min)
- M04L02 — Repairing relationships and escalation when needed (120 min)
- Worked applications: Turn a resolution into specific, owned commitments; Decide when to escalate a conflict appropriately
- Common misconception addressed: Assuming a verbal truce without clear commitments will hold

## Integrative case

Resolve a live workplace dispute: diagnose the source and style of the conflict, prepare for a difficult conversation, separate positions from underlying interests, and reach a durable agreement while keeping the relationship intact.
