## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the layers of the Earth and their properties
2. Explain plate tectonics and the evidence for moving plates
3. Relate plate boundaries to earthquakes and volcanoes
4. Describe how different volcano types form and erupt
5. Explain how magma, lava and igneous rock are linked
6. Interpret a simple hazard map for a volcanic region

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2837-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which layer of the Earth is the thin, solid, outermost layer we live on?

- A. The crust **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the crust is the thin, solid outer layer.
- B. The inner core  
  _Rationale:_ The inner core is the deepest, solid metal centre.
- C. The mantle  
  _Rationale:_ The mantle lies below the crust and is mostly solid but can flow slowly.
- D. The outer core  
  _Rationale:_ The outer core is a deep liquid metal layer.

**MST-2837-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Earthquakes and volcanoes mostly occur in the same belts. What best explains this? (Select ONE)

- A. Both are concentrated at the boundaries between tectonic plates **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: plate boundaries are where most quakes and volcanoes occur.
- B. They happen randomly with no pattern  
  _Rationale:_ They follow plate-boundary patterns.
- C. They only occur in the middle of flat plates  
  _Rationale:_ Most occur at boundaries, not plate interiors.
- D. Volcanoes cause the plates to first appear  
  _Rationale:_ Plates are not created by volcanoes in this way.

**MST-2837-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO features typical of a composite (stratovolcano) eruption. (Select TWO)

- A. Explosive eruptions with ash clouds **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: composite volcanoes often erupt explosively with ash.
- B. Steep sides built from layers of ash and lava **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: alternating layers build their steep cones.
- C. Very gentle, runny lava forming a broad flat shield  
  _Rationale:_ That describes shield volcanoes, not composite ones.
- D. No connection to any plate boundary  
  _Rationale:_ Composite volcanoes are typically at convergent boundaries.
