## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the purpose and key metrics of a help desk and service desk
2. Apply active-listening and questioning techniques to understand user needs
3. Handle difficult and escalated conversations professionally
4. Use ticketing and knowledge systems to track and resolve requests
5. Describe service-level agreements and how they shape support behaviour

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A frustrated customer calls because their account has been locked for two days and a previous ticket was closed without a fix. De-escalate the conversation, confirm the real need, check the SLA, log and route the ticket correctly, and set clear expectations about what happens next.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1656-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A customer is shouting and repeating themselves. What is the most effective first response?

- A. Acknowledge their frustration and confirm you will help **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: acknowledgement lowers tension before problem-solving.
- B. Interrupt to explain the policy  
  _Rationale:_ Interrupting during high emotion usually escalates the call.
- C. Place them on hold immediately  
  _Rationale:_ An abrupt hold can feel dismissive and increase anger.
- D. Tell them to calm down  
  _Rationale:_ Telling someone to calm down commonly has the opposite effect.

**MST-1656-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO practices improve first-contact resolution? (Select TWO.)

- A. Having an accurate, searchable knowledge base **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: good knowledge lets agents resolve more on first contact.
- B. Asking clarifying questions early in the call **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: understanding the real need avoids repeat contacts.
- C. Closing tickets quickly before confirming a fix  
  _Rationale:_ Premature closure causes re-opens and lowers true resolution.
- D. Transferring every request to tier 2  
  _Rationale:_ Unnecessary transfers reduce first-contact resolution.

**MST-1656-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) What does an SLA response target primarily commit the support team to?

- A. A maximum time to acknowledge or begin work on a ticket **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: response targets set the time to respond, not necessarily to resolve.
- B. A guarantee the issue is always the user's fault  
  _Rationale:_ SLAs do not assign blame.
- C. A promise never to escalate tickets  
  _Rationale:_ Escalation is normal and compatible with SLAs.
- D. A rule that tickets can never be closed  
  _Rationale:_ SLAs govern timing, not whether tickets close.
