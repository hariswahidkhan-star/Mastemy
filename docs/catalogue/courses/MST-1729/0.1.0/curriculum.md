## Learning outcomes

1. Why regulate finance
2. Who regulates
3. Prudential regulation
4. Conduct and consumer protection
5. Compliance in practice

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses conceptual knowledge through selection items; this is a general overview, not legal or compliance advice, and it does not assess a jurisdiction-specific regulatory determination.

## Integrative case

Brief a new finance graduate on the regulatory landscape: explain why finance is regulated, map which regulators do what, show how capital and conduct rules protect the system and consumers, and describe how a firm's compliance function and culture keep it on side.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1729-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Prudential regulation of banks is mainly concerned with:

- A. The safety and soundness of firms and the system **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: prudential rules target solvency and stability.
- B. How politely staff answer the phone  
  _Rationale:_ That is a service matter, not prudential regulation.
- C. Setting the prices firms may charge for coffee  
  _Rationale:_ Pricing of unrelated goods is not prudential regulation.
- D. Advertising creative standards  
  _Rationale:_ Advertising content is a conduct matter, not prudential.

**MST-1729-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select ALL that apply) Which two are core goals of financial regulation? (Select TWO) (Select TWO)

- A. Protecting consumers **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: consumer protection is a core goal.
- B. Maintaining financial stability **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: systemic stability is a core goal.
- C. Guaranteeing every firm makes a profit  
  _Rationale:_ Regulation does not guarantee profits.
- D. Eliminating all competition  
  _Rationale:_ Regulation generally supports fair competition, not its elimination.

**MST-1729-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Requiring firms to disclose key product information to customers mainly addresses:

- A. Information asymmetry between firm and consumer **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: disclosure reduces the information gap.
- B. The firm's internal capital ratio  
  _Rationale:_ That is a prudential, not a disclosure, matter.
- C. The central bank's interest-rate decision  
  _Rationale:_ Disclosure rules are unrelated to rate-setting.
- D. Employee payroll processing  
  _Rationale:_ Payroll is unrelated to consumer disclosure.
