## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Official ACCA exams use constructed-response and computational item formats (written answers, calculations, OT case scenarios) that this platform's MCQ/MR forms do not reproduce; see the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0010-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An organisation's governance structure in which the roles of chair and chief executive are held by different people primarily supports:

- A. A separation of power and improved board accountability **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: splitting the roles avoids concentration of power and strengthens accountability.
- B. Faster unilateral decision-making by one leader  
  _Rationale:_ Separation deliberately avoids concentrating decision power.
- C. Elimination of the need for non-executive directors  
  _Rationale:_ Non-executives remain important for oversight.
- D. Avoidance of all financial reporting  
  _Rationale:_ Reporting obligations are unaffected.

**MST-0010-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) In Mendelow's stakeholder matrix, stakeholders with high power and high interest should be:

- A. Managed closely (key players) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: high power and high interest stakeholders are key players to manage closely.
- B. Kept informed only  
  _Rationale:_ That strategy is for low power, high interest stakeholders.
- C. Kept satisfied only  
  _Rationale:_ That strategy is for high power, low interest stakeholders.
- D. Given minimal effort  
  _Rationale:_ Minimal effort is for low power, low interest stakeholders.

**MST-0010-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO elements commonly included in the COSO enterprise risk management framework.

- A. Risk assessment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: risk assessment is a core element of ERM.
- B. Control activities **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: control activities are part of the framework.
- C. Dividend declaration  
  _Rationale:_ Dividend declaration is a financing decision, not an ERM element.
- D. Share buy-backs  
  _Rationale:_ Share buy-backs are not an ERM component.
- E. Product pricing  
  _Rationale:_ Pricing is an operational decision, not an ERM element.
