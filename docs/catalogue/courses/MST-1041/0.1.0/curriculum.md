## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1041-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) The debt service cover ratio (DSCR) for a period is best described as:

- A. Cash flow available for debt service divided by debt service due **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: DSCR compares cash available for debt service to the debt service due.
- B. Total debt divided by total equity  
  _Rationale:_ That describes gearing, not DSCR.
- C. Revenue divided by operating costs  
  _Rationale:_ That is an operating margin, not a cover ratio.
- D. Net profit divided by interest only  
  _Rationale:_ DSCR covers principal and interest from cash flow, not just interest from profit.

**MST-1041-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO features are characteristic of a project-finance (limited recourse) structure? (Select TWO)

- A. Lenders rely primarily on the project's own cash flows for repayment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: repayment comes from project cash flows, not the sponsors' balance sheets.
- B. The project is held in a dedicated special-purpose vehicle **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an SPV ring-fences the project's assets and liabilities.
- C. Sponsors guarantee all debt with full recourse in all cases  
  _Rationale:_ Full recourse is the opposite of limited-recourse project finance.
- D. There is no separate legal entity for the project  
  _Rationale:_ A separate SPV is a defining feature.

**MST-1041-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why are debt repayments often 'sculpted' in a project-finance model?

- A. To keep the cover ratio stable as cash flow varies over time **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: sculpting shapes repayments to the cash profile to hold cover steady.
- B. To make the model easier to build  
  _Rationale:_ Sculpting adds complexity; it is done for cover stability.
- C. To avoid paying any interest  
  _Rationale:_ Sculpting does not eliminate interest.
- D. Because regulators require straight-line repayment  
  _Rationale:_ There is no such straight-line requirement; sculpting is the point.
