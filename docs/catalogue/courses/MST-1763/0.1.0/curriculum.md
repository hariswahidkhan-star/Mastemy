## Learning outcomes

1. Break medical terms into roots, prefixes and suffixes to decode meaning
2. Apply correct combining forms and plural rules when building terms
3. Use anatomical position, planes and directional terms accurately
4. Interpret terminology for the major body systems
5. Read common diagnostic, procedural and pharmacology terms
6. Expand clinical abbreviations safely and flag error-prone ones

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1763-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) The term 'hepatomegaly' is best decoded as:

- A. Enlargement of the liver **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: hepat/o = liver, -megaly = enlargement.
- B. Inflammation of the liver  
  _Rationale:_ Inflammation is -itis, not -megaly.
- C. Surgical removal of the liver  
  _Rationale:_ Removal is -ectomy.
- D. Study of the liver  
  _Rationale:_ Study of is -logy.

**MST-1763-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO suffixes indicate a surgical procedure? (Select TWO.)

- A. -ectomy (excision) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: -ectomy is surgical removal.
- B. -ostomy (creating an opening) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: -ostomy is a surgical opening.
- C. -algia (pain)  
  _Rationale:_ -algia denotes pain, not surgery.
- D. -megaly (enlargement)  
  _Rationale:_ -megaly describes a finding, not a procedure.

**MST-1763-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A note says a lesion is on the 'distal' forearm. This means it is:

- A. Farther from the trunk than a proximal point **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: distal = farther from the point of attachment.
- B. Nearer to the trunk  
  _Rationale:_ Nearer is proximal.
- C. Toward the midline  
  _Rationale:_ Toward the midline is medial.
- D. On the back surface  
  _Rationale:_ The back surface is posterior/dorsal.
