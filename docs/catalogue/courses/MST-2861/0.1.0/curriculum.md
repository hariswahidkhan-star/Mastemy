## Learning outcomes

1. Name the main parts of a plant
2. Say what a seed needs to grow
3. Describe how a flower looks and smells
4. Explain why plants need sunlight and water
5. Compare different kinds of plants and flowers
6. Show kind ways to care for a growing plant

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2861-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which part of a plant holds it in the ground and drinks water?

- A. Roots **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: roots hold the plant and take in water.
- B. Flower  
  _Rationale:_ The flower is the pretty, often smelly part.
- C. Leaf  
  _Rationale:_ Leaves catch sunlight.
- D. Petal  
  _Rationale:_ Petals are parts of the flower.

**MST-2861-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) What does a seed need to start growing?

- A. Water and warmth **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: seeds need water and warmth to sprout.
- B. A loud song  
  _Rationale:_ Singing does not make a seed grow.
- C. A new toy  
  _Rationale:_ Toys do not help seeds grow.
- D. Cold ice only  
  _Rationale:_ Ice is too cold for most seeds to sprout.

**MST-2861-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO things do most plants need to stay alive? (Select TWO.)

- A. Sunlight **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: plants use sunlight to make their food.
- B. Water **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: plants need water to live and grow.
- C. Chocolate  
  _Rationale:_ Plants do not need chocolate.
- D. Darkness all day  
  _Rationale:_ Most plants need light, not constant darkness.
