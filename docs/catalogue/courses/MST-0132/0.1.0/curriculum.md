## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the PRINCE2 principles, themes and process model
2. Apply the themes to a project's organisation, plans, risk, quality and change
3. Evaluate the PRINCE2 processes across the project lifecycle
4. Analyse tailoring PRINCE2 to a project's context and an exception situation

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0132-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A project manager forecasts that the current stage will exceed its agreed cost tolerance. According to PRINCE2's manage-by-exception principle, what should happen?

- A. Raise an exception to the project board, which decides how to proceed **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a forecast breach of tolerance is escalated via an exception to the next level of authority.
- B. The project manager quietly absorbs the overrun  
  _Rationale:_ Concealing a tolerance breach violates manage-by-exception.
- C. Immediately close the project  
  _Rationale:_ Closure is not the automatic response to a tolerance forecast.
- D. Ignore it until the stage ends  
  _Rationale:_ A forecast breach must be escalated, not ignored.

**MST-0132-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which statement best reflects the 'continued business justification' principle?

- A. The business case is checked throughout and the project stops if justification disappears **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: justification must remain valid throughout, not just at the start.
- B. Justification is confirmed once at the start and never revisited  
  _Rationale:_ That contradicts 'continued' justification.
- C. A project should finish regardless of whether it still makes sense  
  _Rationale:_ Loss of justification is grounds to stop.
- D. Only the project manager judges justification  
  _Rationale:_ Direction and justification sit with the board, not solely the PM.

**MST-0132-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) A team wants to tailor PRINCE2 for a small, low-risk project. Which TWO are legitimate tailoring actions? (Select TWO)

- A. Combining management products into simpler, fit-for-purpose documents **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: products can be combined or simplified while preserving their purpose.
- B. Scaling the formality of processes to the project's scale and risk **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: tailoring adjusts formality to context.
- C. Dropping the principles because the project is small  
  _Rationale:_ The principles are mandatory and cannot be dropped.
- D. Abandoning the business case entirely  
  _Rationale:_ Continued justification is a principle and cannot be removed.
- E. Removing all board oversight  
  _Rationale:_ Governance roles remain; tailoring is not abandonment of the method.
