## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the regulatory framework governing research analysts and their reports
2. Apply disclosure and conflict-of-interest rules to research publications
3. Evaluate communications, quiet periods and interactions with banking and issuers
4. Analyse supervisory, recordkeeping and certification obligations for research

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0073-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An analyst's firm acted as lead underwriter for an issuer three weeks ago. The analyst wants to publish an initiation now. What does the research rule framework most directly require the analyst to consider?

- A. Whether a quiet period restricts publishing research around the offering **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: offering-related quiet periods restrict the timing of research after a firm participates in an offering.
- B. Whether the issuer approves the rating first  
  _Rationale:_ Issuers cannot approve research ratings; that would breach independence.
- C. Whether banking will meet its revenue target  
  _Rationale:_ Banking revenue is irrelevant to the analyst's regulatory obligations.
- D. Nothing, because initiations are always permitted  
  _Rationale:_ Initiations are not unconditionally permitted; timing restrictions can apply.

**MST-0073-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which item must appear in a research report when the analyst's firm has an investment-banking relationship with the covered issuer?

- A. A disclosure of the banking relationship and any resulting conflict **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: material conflicts such as banking relationships must be disclosed in the report.
- B. The analyst's home address  
  _Rationale:_ Personal contact details are not a required conflict disclosure.
- C. A guaranteed price target  
  _Rationale:_ No rule requires a guaranteed target; that would be misleading.
- D. The issuer's internal board minutes  
  _Rationale:_ Confidential issuer governance documents are not a report disclosure item.

**MST-0073-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Banking and the covered issuer both pressure an analyst to upgrade a cautious rating before an offering. Which TWO actions are consistent with research-analyst independence rules? (Select TWO)

- A. Keep the rating supported by the analysis and document the basis **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ratings must reflect the analyst's genuine, evidence-based view.
- B. Ensure all required conflict disclosures appear in the report **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: disclosure of conflicts is central to compliance.
- C. Let banking approve the rating before publication  
  _Rationale:_ Banking cannot control or pre-approve research ratings.
- D. Raise the rating to protect the banking fee  
  _Rationale:_ Changing a view for banking revenue violates independence rules.
- E. Promise the issuer favourable coverage for continued access  
  _Rationale:_ Trading coverage for access is prohibited.
