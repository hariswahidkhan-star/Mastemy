## Learning outcomes

1. Identify financial transaction and fraud schemes
2. Apply the legal elements relevant to fraud examination
3. Conduct investigations and support fraud prevention and deterrence

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0101-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) The 'fraud triangle' comprises which three elements?

- A. Motive, means, mens rea  
  _Rationale:_ These are legal, not fraud-triangle, elements.
- B. Detection, deterrence, prosecution  
  _Rationale:_ These are responses, not the triangle.
- C. Pressure, opportunity, rationalisation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the fraud triangle is pressure, opportunity and rationalisation.
- D. Assets, liabilities, equity  
  _Rationale:_ These are accounting elements.

**MST-0101-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) In an admission-seeking interview, when should the primary suspect normally be interviewed?

- A. First, before any other witness  
  _Rationale:_ Interviewing the suspect first forfeits the advantage of corroborating evidence.
- B. Last, after evidence and neutral witnesses are addressed **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the suspect is interviewed last, once evidence is marshalled.
- C. Never  
  _Rationale:_ The suspect interview is a core step.
- D. Only in writing  
  _Rationale:_ Admission-seeking interviews are conducted in person.

**MST-0101-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO examples of asset misappropriation schemes.

- A. Billing scheme using a shell company **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: shell-company billing is an asset-misappropriation scheme.
- B. Overstating revenue in financial statements  
  _Rationale:_ That is financial-statement fraud, not misappropriation.
- C. Payroll ghost-employee scheme **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a ghost-employee payroll scheme misappropriates assets.
- D. Bribing a government official  
  _Rationale:_ That is corruption, a separate category.
- E. Misstating a disclosure note  
  _Rationale:_ That is financial-statement fraud.
