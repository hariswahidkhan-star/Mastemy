## Learning outcomes

1. Apply Circular 230 practice rules, duties and sanctions for practice before the IRS
2. Represent taxpayers in examinations, appeals, collection and penalty matters
3. Handle specific representation situations including powers of attorney and third-party authorizations
4. Complete the filing process with required e-file, recordkeeping and preparer obligations

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0042-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under Circular 230, what must a practitioner do upon learning a client has not complied with the revenue laws?

- A. Immediately report the client to the IRS  
  _Rationale:_ Circular 230 does not require self-initiated reporting of the client to the IRS.
- B. Advise the client promptly of the noncompliance and its consequences **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the practitioner must inform the client of the noncompliance, error or omission and the consequences of not correcting it.
- C. Resign from all engagements with the client  
  _Rationale:_ Resignation is not the required step; advising the client is.
- D. Amend the return without telling the client  
  _Rationale:_ The practitioner cannot act without the client; the duty is to advise.

**MST-0042-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which form grants an Enrolled Agent authority to represent a taxpayer before the IRS?

- A. Form 8821, Tax Information Authorization  
  _Rationale:_ Form 8821 only authorizes access to information; it does not grant representation.
- B. Form 2848, Power of Attorney and Declaration of Representative **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: Form 2848 grants representation authority to an eligible representative such as an EA.
- C. Form 1040-X, Amended Return  
  _Rationale:_ That is an amended return, not an authorization.
- D. Form W-9  
  _Rationale:_ Form W-9 requests a taxpayer identification number.

**MST-0042-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are collection alternatives an EA may pursue for a client who cannot pay in full? (Select TWO)

- A. Installment agreement **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an installment agreement lets the taxpayer pay over time.
- B. Offer in compromise **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an offer in compromise can settle a liability for less than the full amount when criteria are met.
- C. Automatic penalty waiver for all taxpayers  
  _Rationale:_ No automatic blanket waiver exists.
- D. Permanent cancellation of the tax debt on request  
  _Rationale:_ Debt is not cancelled on request.
- E. Conversion of the debt to a credit  
  _Rationale:_ This is not a collection alternative.
