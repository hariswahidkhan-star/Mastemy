## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1080-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which goal is written in SMART form?

- A. Get better at data analysis someday  
  _Rationale:_ It lacks a measure, a deadline and specificity.
- B. Complete a certified SQL course and analyse two real datasets by 30 June **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it is specific, measurable, time-bound and achievable.
- C. Be the best analyst in the company  
  _Rationale:_ It is not specific or measurable and has no timeframe.
- D. Do more analysis work  
  _Rationale:_ Vague and unmeasurable, with no deadline.

**MST-1080-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) The strongest portfolio piece for a target role primarily shows:

- A. A list of your job responsibilities  
  _Rationale:_ Duties describe activity, not the value you created.
- B. A result you produced and the impact it had **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: evidence of outcomes and impact persuades far more than duties.
- C. The number of years you have worked  
  _Rationale:_ Tenure alone does not demonstrate capability.
- D. A full list of every tool you have touched  
  _Rationale:_ A tool list without results proves little about your value.

**MST-1080-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO habits build a durable professional network? (Select TWO)

- A. Offer help and stay in touch before you need anything **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reciprocity built over time creates genuine relationships.
- B. Keep relationships warm with occasional, relevant contact **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: light, consistent contact keeps a network alive.
- C. Reach out to contacts only when you are job hunting  
  _Rationale:_ Transactional, needs-only contact weakens relationships.
- D. Collect as many connections as possible with no follow-up  
  _Rationale:_ Volume without relationship adds little real value.
