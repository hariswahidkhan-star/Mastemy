## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0397-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A Yes/No/Not Given question asks about the writer's opinion. The passage reports others' opinions but the writer states no view on the point. The claim 'The writer believes X' is:

- A. Not Given **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the writer expresses no view on the point, so their belief is not given.
- B. Yes  
  _Rationale:_ The writer states no such belief, so Yes is unsupported.
- C. No  
  _Rationale:_ No requires the writer to express the opposite view; they are silent.
- D. True  
  _Rationale:_ Yes/No/Not Given, not True/False, applies to opinion questions.

**MST-0397-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A summary-completion answer must come from the passage. The candidate knows a perfect synonym not used in the text. What should they do?

- A. Use the exact word from the passage as required **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: summary completion from the text requires the passage's own words within the limit.
- B. Use their own synonym  
  _Rationale:_ A synonym not in the text is marked wrong for from-the-text tasks.
- C. Leave it blank to be safe  
  _Rationale:_ A blank scores nothing; the correct word is retrievable from the text.
- D. Write two alternatives  
  _Rationale:_ Two answers in one box are marked wrong.

**MST-0397-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO strategies best manage a three-passage Academic Reading paper in 60 minutes?

- A. Budgeting roughly equal time and moving on from stuck questions **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: pacing and not over-investing in one item protect overall score.
- B. Transferring answers carefully within the exam time **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: Academic Reading has no extra transfer time, so accurate in-time transfer matters.
- C. Reading every passage fully word-by-word first  
  _Rationale:_ Full close reading of all passages wastes limited time.
- D. Leaving the hardest passage until after time runs out  
  _Rationale:_ There is no time after the clock stops; all must be done within 60 minutes.
- E. Guessing nothing to avoid wrong answers  
  _Rationale:_ There is no penalty for wrong answers, so unanswered items waste marks.
