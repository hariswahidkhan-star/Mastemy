## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1043-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An EPC contractor transfers control of its work to the customer continuously as construction proceeds. How is revenue recognised?

- A. Over time, using a measure of progress toward completion **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: when control transfers over time, revenue is recognised over time by progress.
- B. Only at the final handover of the asset  
  _Rationale:_ Point-in-time recognition does not apply when over-time criteria are met.
- C. When each invoice is paid in cash  
  _Rationale:_ Revenue follows performance, not cash receipt.
- D. Equally in each calendar month regardless of progress  
  _Rationale:_ Recognition follows measured progress, not a flat monthly split.

**MST-1043-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO figures are needed to forecast an EPC contract's final outcome under a cost-based progress method? (Select TWO)

- A. Costs incurred to date **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: costs to date are part of measuring progress and forecasting.
- B. Estimated cost to complete **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: cost-to-complete is required to forecast total cost and margin.
- C. The CEO's bonus target  
  _Rationale:_ A bonus target is irrelevant to the contract forecast.
- D. The marketing budget  
  _Rationale:_ Marketing spend does not enter the contract forecast.

**MST-1043-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Revenue from an unapproved, disputed claim should generally be:

- A. Constrained and recognised only to the extent it is highly probable it will not reverse **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: variable consideration from claims is constrained until highly probable.
- B. Recognised in full immediately as billed  
  _Rationale:_ Disputed claim revenue is uncertain and must be constrained.
- C. Ignored permanently even once approved  
  _Rationale:_ Once approved it is recognised; it is not ignored permanently.
- D. Recorded as a fixed asset  
  _Rationale:_ Claim revenue is not a fixed asset.
