## Learning outcomes

1. Determine the tax treatment of business entities (sole proprietorships, partnerships, corporations, S corporations and farmers)
2. Compute business income, deductions, basis, depreciation and the QBI deduction
3. Analyze business financial records to prepare accurate returns
4. Handle specialized business returns, trusts, exempt organizations and retirement plans

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0041-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An LLC with two members elects to be taxed as an S corporation. How is ordinary business income generally taxed?

- A. At the entity level at corporate rates  
  _Rationale:_ S corporations are generally pass-through; they do not pay corporate income tax on ordinary income.
- B. Passed through to shareholders and taxed on their individual returns **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: S-corporation ordinary income passes through to shareholders in proportion to stock ownership and is taxed on their 1040s.
- C. Deferred until distributed as dividends  
  _Rationale:_ Pass-through income is taxed when earned, not when distributed.
- D. Exempt from federal income tax  
  _Rationale:_ The income is taxable to the shareholders.

**MST-0041-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A business buys qualifying equipment and wants the largest first-year write-off. Which provision allows immediate expensing up to an annual limit?

- A. Section 179 expensing **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: Section 179 lets a business elect to expense qualifying property immediately, subject to annual dollar and income limits.
- B. Straight-line depreciation  
  _Rationale:_ Straight-line spreads the cost evenly over the recovery period.
- C. Amortization  
  _Rationale:_ Amortization applies to intangibles, not equipment.
- D. Percentage depletion  
  _Rationale:_ Depletion applies to natural resources.

**MST-0041-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are separately stated items that pass through to partners rather than being included in ordinary business income? (Select TWO)

- A. Charitable contributions **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: charitable contributions are separately stated so partners apply their own limits.
- B. Section 1231 gains **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: Section 1231 gains and losses are separately stated for character at the partner level.
- C. Cost of goods sold  
  _Rationale:_ COGS is part of ordinary business income.
- D. Employee wages  
  _Rationale:_ Wages are an ordinary deduction, not separately stated.
- E. Rent paid on business premises  
  _Rationale:_ Ordinary business rent is part of ordinary income.
