(General legal literacy, not legal advice.)

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1830-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which elements are generally required to form a simple valid contract?

- A. Offer, acceptance and consideration **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: these are core elements of contract formation in common-law systems.
- B. A notarised document only  
  _Rationale:_ Many valid contracts need no notarisation.
- C. A government licence for every agreement  
  _Rationale:_ Most everyday contracts require no licence.
- D. Payment in cash exclusively  
  _Rationale:_ Consideration can take many forms, not only cash.

**MST-1830-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which statements about limited liability are correct? (Select TWO)

- A. It generally limits owners' losses to what they invested **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: shareholders are typically not liable beyond their investment.
- B. It does not protect against personal fraud or wrongful acts **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: courts can look past the company for personal misconduct.
- C. It makes owners immune from all legal consequences  
  _Rationale:_ Personal wrongdoing and guarantees can still create liability.
- D. It applies automatically to a sole trader  
  _Rationale:_ A sole trader has unlimited personal liability.

**MST-1830-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A manager reuses photographs found online in a paid advertisement without permission. What is the main legal risk?

- A. Copyright infringement of the images **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: images are usually protected by copyright regardless of being online.
- B. No risk, because online content is public domain  
  _Rationale:_ Being online does not place work in the public domain.
- C. Only a risk if the images are watermarked  
  _Rationale:_ Protection does not depend on a visible watermark.
- D. A trademark violation unrelated to the images  
  _Rationale:_ The core issue is copyright, not trademark.
