## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the integrated structure and foundational concepts assessed by the NextGen format
2. Apply foundational lawyering skills within integrated question sets (knowledge practice)
3. Analyse integrated fact scenarios spanning multiple doctrinal areas
4. Plan preparation strategy for integrated knowledge-and-skills tasks

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1278-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) How does the integrated NextGen format differ most from a legacy single-subject question?

- A. It tests only one narrow doctrine at a time  
  _Rationale:_ That describes the legacy format, not the integrated one.
- B. It combines foundational lawyering skills with legal knowledge in realistic scenarios **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the integrated format blends doctrinal knowledge with applied lawyering skills.
- C. It removes all legal knowledge requirements  
  _Rationale:_ Knowledge is still assessed, integrated with skills.
- D. It is scored purely on spelling  
  _Rationale:_ Scoring targets legal analysis and skills, not spelling.

**MST-1278-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) In a client-counselling style task, what is generally the best next step after identifying the client's goal?

- A. Immediately guarantee a specific outcome  
  _Rationale:_ Guaranteeing outcomes is improper and unrealistic.
- B. Explain realistic options and their legal consequences to the client **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: counselling requires presenting options and consequences so the client can decide.
- C. Refuse to discuss any risks  
  _Rationale:_ Hiding risks fails the counselling duty.
- D. Decide for the client without input  
  _Rationale:_ The client, not the lawyer, decides the objective.

**MST-1278-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO preparation habits best fit an integrated knowledge-and-skills exam?

- A. Practising applied skills tasks, not just recall **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an integrated exam rewards practising skills in context.
- B. Reviewing doctrine in realistic scenarios **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: studying doctrine through scenarios mirrors the integrated format.
- C. Memorising answer keys word for word  
  _Rationale:_ Rote memorisation of keys does not build transferable skill.
- D. Avoiding timed practice entirely  
  _Rationale:_ Skipping timed practice leaves the candidate unprepared for conditions.
