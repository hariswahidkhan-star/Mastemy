## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0028-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which activity best illustrates the finance function acting as a business partner rather than a scorekeeper?

- A. Advising operational managers on the financial implications of strategic options **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: partnering means providing forward-looking decision support to the business.
- B. Only recording historic transactions in the ledger  
  _Rationale:_ Recording history is the scorekeeping role, not partnering.
- C. Filing statutory returns after the year end  
  _Rationale:_ Compliance filing is a stewardship task, not business partnering.
- D. Archiving old invoices  
  _Rationale:_ Archiving is administrative, not partnering.

**MST-0028-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which technology most directly enables an organisation to analyse very large and varied datasets for decision making?

- A. Big data analytics **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: big data analytics processes large, varied and fast-moving datasets for insight.
- B. A paper filing cabinet  
  _Rationale:_ A filing cabinet cannot analyse large datasets.
- C. A single desktop calculator  
  _Rationale:_ A calculator cannot handle large, varied datasets.
- D. A fax machine  
  _Rationale:_ A fax transmits documents and does not analyse data.

**MST-0028-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO characteristics that make information useful for management decision making.

- A. It is relevant to the decision being taken **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: relevance is essential for decision-useful information.
- B. It is sufficiently accurate and available in time **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: accuracy and timeliness make information fit for decisions.
- C. It is as voluminous as possible regardless of need  
  _Rationale:_ Excess volume reduces, not improves, usefulness.
- D. It is kept secret from the decision maker  
  _Rationale:_ Information withheld from the decision maker cannot be useful to them.
- E. It is always expressed only in non-financial terms  
  _Rationale:_ Useful information may be financial or non-financial; restricting it is not a quality.
