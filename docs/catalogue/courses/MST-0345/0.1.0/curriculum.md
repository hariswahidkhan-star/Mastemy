## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0345-Q0001** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) An observational cohort finds that coffee drinkers have more lung cancer. Smoking is more common among coffee drinkers. Smoking here is best described as a:

- A. Confounder **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: smoking is associated with both the exposure and the outcome and distorts the apparent relationship.
- B. Mediator  
  _Rationale:_ A mediator lies on the causal pathway from exposure to outcome, which is not the case here.
- C. Random error  
  _Rationale:_ This is a systematic bias, not random variation.
- D. Effect modifier  
  _Rationale:_ Effect modification changes the effect size across strata; the scenario describes confounding.

**MST-0345-Q0002** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) For a stable patient with a well-controlled chronic condition who reports worsening symptoms, the best first step is to:

- A. Reassess adherence and contributing factors before escalating therapy **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reassessing adherence and triggers precedes intensifying treatment.
- B. Immediately add a new medication  
  _Rationale:_ Escalation without reassessment may mask the real cause.
- C. Stop all current therapy  
  _Rationale:_ Stopping effective therapy is not justified.
- D. Refer for surgery  
  _Rationale:_ Surgery is not indicated from the information given.

**MST-0345-Q0003** (multiple-answer-selection, Select TWO) Which TWO are appropriate when managing an acute, undifferentiated presentation?

- A. Use pretest probability to guide testing **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: pretest probability focuses the work-up efficiently.
- B. Stabilise the patient while investigating **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: stabilisation proceeds alongside diagnosis.
- C. Order every available test at once  
  _Rationale:_ Indiscriminate testing increases false positives and cost.
- D. Delay all treatment until a final diagnosis  
  _Rationale:_ Treatment of immediate threats should not wait for a final diagnosis.
- E. Rely only on the patient's self-diagnosis  
  _Rationale:_ Self-diagnosis is not a substitute for clinical assessment.
