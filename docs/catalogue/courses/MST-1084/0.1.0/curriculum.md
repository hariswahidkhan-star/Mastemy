## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1084-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Effective structured onboarding is best understood as:

- A. A single day of paperwork and introductions  
  _Rationale:_ Compressing onboarding into one day leaves new hires unsupported.
- B. A planned process over weeks that builds competence and belonging **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: good onboarding spans the first weeks and months, not one day.
- C. Something only the new hire is responsible for  
  _Rationale:_ Onboarding is a shared responsibility of manager, HR and team.
- D. Optional once the contract is signed  
  _Rationale:_ Onboarding is a key driver of retention and ramp-up, not optional.

**MST-1084-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under data-protection good practice, HR should keep personal employee data:

- A. Forever, in case it is ever useful  
  _Rationale:_ Indefinite retention breaches data-minimisation and retention principles.
- B. Only as long as there is a lawful, necessary reason, then securely dispose of it **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: data should be retained only as long as needed and then securely removed.
- C. Openly accessible to all staff  
  _Rationale:_ Personal data must be access-controlled, not open to everyone.
- D. On personal devices for convenience  
  _Rationale:_ Storing employee data on uncontrolled personal devices is a serious risk.

**MST-1084-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO belong on a sound offboarding checklist? (Select TWO)

- A. Revoke system and building access on the leaving date **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: timely access removal is a core security control.
- B. Capture handover of knowledge and responsibilities **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: knowledge transfer prevents operational gaps after departure.
- C. Delete the payroll record immediately with no retention  
  _Rationale:_ Some records must be retained for a lawful period after exit.
- D. Skip the exit conversation to save time  
  _Rationale:_ Exit insight is valuable and should not be skipped.
