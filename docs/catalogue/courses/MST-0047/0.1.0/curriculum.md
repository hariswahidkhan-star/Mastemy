## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0047-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A product sells for $20, has variable cost of $12 per unit, and the company incurs $40,000 of fixed costs. How many units must be sold to break even?

- A. 5,000 units **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: contribution per unit = $20 - $12 = $8; break-even = $40,000 / $8 = 5,000 units.
- B. 2,000 units  
  _Rationale:_ This divides fixed cost by the selling price, not by contribution.
- C. 3,333 units  
  _Rationale:_ This divides fixed cost by the variable cost, which is not the break-even formula.
- D. 8,000 units  
  _Rationale:_ This uses a $5 contribution; contribution here is $8 per unit.

**MST-0047-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under marginal costing, how are fixed production overheads treated?

- A. Written off in full as a period cost in the period incurred **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: marginal costing treats fixed production overhead as a period cost, not part of unit cost.
- B. Absorbed into the cost of each unit produced  
  _Rationale:_ That is absorption costing, not marginal costing.
- C. Capitalised as a non-current asset  
  _Rationale:_ Fixed overheads are expenses, not assets.
- D. Ignored entirely  
  _Rationale:_ They are charged in full to the period, not ignored.

**MST-0047-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO actions that would improve a company's short-term cash position.

- A. Negotiating faster collection of receivables **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: collecting cash from customers sooner increases available cash.
- B. Delaying non-essential discretionary spending **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: deferring non-essential cash outflows preserves cash in the short term.
- C. Paying all suppliers immediately in advance  
  _Rationale:_ Paying early reduces available cash rather than improving the position.
- D. Buying additional inventory on cash terms  
  _Rationale:_ Tying up cash in extra inventory worsens the short-term cash position.
- E. Recording a non-cash depreciation charge  
  _Rationale:_ Depreciation is a non-cash entry and does not change the cash position.
