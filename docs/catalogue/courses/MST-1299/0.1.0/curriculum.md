## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the contract management body of knowledge across commercial and government contexts (knowledge practice)
2. Apply pre-award competencies: planning, solicitation, pricing and negotiation
3. Describe award and post-award administration across environments
4. Analyse leadership, ethics and the business environment of contract management

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1299-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the main purpose of price or cost analysis before award?

- A. To pick the vendor with the flashiest proposal  
  _Rationale:_ Proposal styling is not the basis of price analysis.
- B. To determine whether the proposed price is fair and reasonable **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: price/cost analysis establishes fairness and reasonableness of the proposed price.
- C. To guarantee the lowest possible price regardless of value  
  _Rationale:_ Reasonableness, not merely lowest price, is the aim.
- D. To avoid negotiating at all  
  _Rationale:_ Analysis supports negotiation; it does not replace it.

**MST-1299-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Before entering a negotiation, why should a contract manager define a BATNA?

- A. To ensure they never reach agreement  
  _Rationale:_ A BATNA supports good agreements, not avoidance of them.
- B. To know their best alternative and set a rational walk-away point **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a BATNA defines the walk-away point and strengthens negotiating position.
- C. To hide information from their own team  
  _Rationale:_ A BATNA is internal preparation, not concealment from the team.
- D. To fix the price before talks begin  
  _Rationale:_ A BATNA informs flexibility rather than fixing price.

**MST-1299-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO behaviours reflect strong ethics in contract management?

- A. Disclosing and managing conflicts of interest **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: disclosing and managing conflicts is a core ethical behaviour.
- B. Declining gifts that could improperly influence a decision **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: refusing improper gifts preserves integrity.
- C. Accepting undisclosed payments from a supplier  
  _Rationale:_ Undisclosed payments are unethical and often illegal.
- D. Steering an award to a relative's company secretly  
  _Rationale:_ Covert steering is a serious ethics violation.
