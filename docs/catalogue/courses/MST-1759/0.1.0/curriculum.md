## Learning outcomes

2. Stakeholder communication needs

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Selection items cannot demonstrate facilitating a live meeting or difficult conversation; practice comes through instructor-led simulations.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1759-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A project communications plan primarily specifies:

- A. Who needs what information, in what format, how often, and from whom **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the plan defines audience, content, format, cadence and owner.
- B. The project's technical architecture  
  _Rationale:_ Architecture is a different artefact.
- C. The detailed cost baseline  
  _Rationale:_ Cost belongs in the budget, not the comms plan.
- D. The list of hardware to purchase  
  _Rationale:_ Procurement is unrelated to the comms plan.

**MST-1759-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are good practices for delivering bad news to a sponsor? (Select TWO.)

- A. Share it early, with the facts and impact **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: early, factual disclosure preserves trust and options.
- B. Bring at least one option or recommended response **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: pairing the problem with options supports decision-making.
- C. Wait until the next quarterly review  
  _Rationale:_ Delaying bad news reduces the options available.
- D. Blame a named team member in the report  
  _Rationale:_ Blame undermines trust and solves nothing.

**MST-1759-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Tailoring a message to executives usually means:

- A. Leading with the decision, impact and ask, not the technical detail **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: executives need outcomes and decisions, not deep detail.
- B. Including every technical log and metric  
  _Rationale:_ Excess detail obscures the decision for executives.
- C. Using as much jargon as possible  
  _Rationale:_ Jargon hinders, not helps, executive communication.
- D. Making the message as long as possible  
  _Rationale:_ Length is not the goal; clarity is.
