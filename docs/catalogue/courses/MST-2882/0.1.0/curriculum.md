## Learning outcomes

1. Understand the purpose and structure of a basic essay
2. Write a clear thesis statement or main point
3. Organise ideas into focused paragraphs
4. Support points with reasons and examples
5. Write effective introductions and conclusions
6. Use linking words to connect ideas

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2882-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the job of a thesis statement in an essay?

- A. To state the main point or position clearly **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the thesis states the essay's central claim.
- B. To list every spelling you used  
  _Rationale:_ A thesis is not a spelling list.
- C. To decorate the top of the page  
  _Rationale:_ It is content, not decoration.
- D. To ask the reader lots of questions  
  _Rationale:_ A thesis states a position rather than only asking questions.

**MST-2882-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which sentence is the best topic sentence for a paragraph about why reading helps you?

- A. Reading regularly builds a wider vocabulary. **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it states one clear point the paragraph will prove.
- B. Books.  
  _Rationale:_ A single word is not a topic sentence.
- C. I read a lot and also I like pizza.  
  _Rationale:_ This is unfocused and off-topic.
- D. The end.  
  _Rationale:_ This signals an ending, not a paragraph's main point.

**MST-2882-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO parts belong in a basic essay? (Select TWO.)

- A. An introduction **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an introduction opens the essay.
- B. A conclusion **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a conclusion closes the essay.
- C. A shopping list  
  _Rationale:_ A shopping list is not part of an essay.
- D. A drawing of the author  
  _Rationale:_ A self-portrait is not an essay component.
