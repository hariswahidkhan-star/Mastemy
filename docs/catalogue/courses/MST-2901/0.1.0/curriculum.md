## Learning outcomes

1. Describe helpful, everyday uses of AI tools
2. Explain why you should not share private or personal information with AI tools
3. Check and question AI answers instead of trusting them blindly
4. Recognise that AI can be wrong, biased or make things up
5. Give credit and be honest about when AI helped with work
6. Know when to ask a trusted adult for help

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2901-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why should you avoid typing personal details like your full name and address into an AI tool?

- A. You cannot be sure how that information is stored or used, so keeping it private protects you **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: protecting private information keeps you safe.
- B. Because AI tools charge money per letter  
  _Rationale:_ The reason is privacy and safety, not cost.
- C. Because it makes the AI answer slower  
  _Rationale:_ The concern is privacy, not speed.
- D. There is no reason; it is always safe  
  _Rationale:_ It is safer to keep personal details private.

**MST-2901-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are responsible ways to use an AI tool for schoolwork? (Select TWO.)

- A. Checking the AI's answer against a trusted source **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: verifying answers guards against mistakes.
- B. Being honest that AI helped and giving credit **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: honesty about AI's help is responsible.
- C. Copying the answer without reading or checking it  
  _Rationale:_ Blindly copying risks spreading mistakes and is dishonest.
- D. Typing a classmate's private information into it  
  _Rationale:_ Sharing others' private details is unsafe and unkind.

**MST-2901-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) An AI gives a very confident answer that you suspect is wrong. What should you do?

- A. Check it against a trusted source before using it **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI can be confidently wrong, so verify important answers.
- B. Trust it because it sounded sure  
  _Rationale:_ A confident tone does not mean the answer is correct.
- C. Share it everywhere immediately  
  _Rationale:_ Spreading unchecked answers can pass on mistakes.
- D. Assume AI is never wrong  
  _Rationale:_ AI can make mistakes and should be checked.
