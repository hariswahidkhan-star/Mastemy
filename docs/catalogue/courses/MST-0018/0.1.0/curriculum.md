## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0018-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A taxpayer sells land (adjusted basis 40,000) for 100,000. The realized gain is:

- A. 60,000 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: amount realized 100,000 minus adjusted basis 40,000 equals 60,000.
- B. 100,000  
  _Rationale:_ That ignores the basis subtraction.
- C. 40,000  
  _Rationale:_ That is the basis, not the gain.
- D. 140,000  
  _Rationale:_ Basis is subtracted, not added.

**MST-0018-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under Circular 230, a practitioner who discovers a client error on a prior return must:

- A. Advise the client of the error and its consequences **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the practitioner must inform the client of the error and potential consequences.
- B. Secretly amend the return without telling the client  
  _Rationale:_ Acting without the client is improper.
- C. Ignore it because it is the client’s problem  
  _Rationale:_ The practitioner has a duty to advise the client.
- D. Report the client to the police  
  _Rationale:_ That is not the Circular 230 requirement.

**MST-0018-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO items are generally pass-through to partners on a partnership return? (Select TWO)

- A. Ordinary business income **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ordinary business income passes through to partners.
- B. Separately stated capital gains **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: certain items such as capital gains are separately stated and pass through.
- C. A corporate-level income tax paid by the partnership  
  _Rationale:_ Partnerships generally do not pay entity-level income tax.
- D. Partner personal medical bills  
  _Rationale:_ Personal expenses are not partnership pass-through items.
