## Learning outcomes

1. Apply reasoning-through-language-arts reading and editing skills
2. Solve mathematical-reasoning items across number, algebra and geometry
3. Reason about science passages, data and experimental design
4. Analyse social-studies sources, data and arguments

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess:

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0416-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A composite figure is a 4 by 3 rectangle with a 1 by 1 square removed. What is its area?

- A. 11 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 4x3 = 12, minus 1x1 = 11.
- B. 12  
  _Rationale:_ This ignores the removed square.
- C. 13  
  _Rationale:_ This adds the square instead of subtracting it.
- D. 7  
  _Rationale:_ Incorrect arithmetic for the composite area.

**MST-0416-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A reading item asks which claim the passage supports. The best answer is the one that:

- A. Is directly backed by evidence in the passage **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: support must come from the text.
- B. Agrees with your personal opinion  
  _Rationale:_ Personal opinion is not textual support.
- C. Uses the most difficult vocabulary  
  _Rationale:_ Vocabulary difficulty is irrelevant.
- D. Is the longest option  
  _Rationale:_ Length does not indicate support.

**MST-0416-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are good strategies for GED science and social-studies items?

- A. Base answers on the provided passage, data or source **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: answers must be grounded in the given material.
- B. Read charts and maps carefully for the asked detail **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: data items require careful reading of the figure.
- C. Rely on prior opinions over the source  
  _Rationale:_ Sources, not opinions, govern the answer.
- D. Guess based on answer length  
  _Rationale:_ Length is not a reliability cue.
- E. Skip all figures  
  _Rationale:_ Figures often hold the needed data.
