## Learning outcomes

1. Describe realistic societal benefits of AI
2. Identify genuine risks and harms of AI systems
3. Separate common AI myths from evidence
4. Discuss jobs, fairness and accountability issues
5. Form balanced, evidence-based views on AI in society

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1311-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which is the most accurate framing of AI's effect on jobs today?

- A. AI often automates specific tasks, reshaping rather than simply erasing jobs **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: task-level change is the evidenced pattern.
- B. AI has already eliminated most jobs  
  _Rationale:_ This overstates current effects.
- C. AI has no effect on any work  
  _Rationale:_ This understates real change.
- D. AI only creates jobs and never changes any  
  _Rationale:_ It both changes and creates roles.

**MST-1311-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are genuine, evidenced risks of AI systems? (Select TWO.)

- A. Biased outputs that disadvantage some groups **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: documented bias harms are real.
- B. Scaled production of convincing misinformation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: generative tools can scale misinformation.
- C. AI spontaneously becoming conscious tomorrow  
  _Rationale:_ This is speculative, not evidenced.
- D. AI physically rebelling like in films  
  _Rationale:_ This is science fiction, not current risk.

**MST-1311-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Someone claims 'AI is basically superintelligent already'. The best evidence-based response is:

- A. Current AI is narrow and error-prone, not general superintelligence **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: today's systems are narrow and fallible.
- B. Yes, it already surpasses humans at everything  
  _Rationale:_ It does not surpass humans generally.
- C. AI has no capabilities at all  
  _Rationale:_ This understates real capability.
- D. The question cannot be discussed  
  _Rationale:_ It can be discussed with evidence.
