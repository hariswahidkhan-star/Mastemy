## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0429-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Choose the particle that best completes: 「日本語＿勉強しています。」 (I am studying Japanese.)

- A. を **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: を marks the direct object (Japanese) of the verb 勉強する.
- B. が  
  _Rationale:_ が marks a subject, not the object being studied here.
- C. に  
  _Rationale:_ に marks a destination or time, not this object.
- D. で  
  _Rationale:_ で marks a location or means, not the object.

**MST-0429-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the most common reading of the kanji 経済 (economy)?

- A. けいざい **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 経済 is read けいざい.
- B. けいさい  
  _Rationale:_ 済 is read ざい here, not さい.
- C. きょうざい  
  _Rationale:_ 経 is read けい, not きょう, in this word.
- D. けいせい  
  _Rationale:_ That is a different word (形勢/形成), not 経済.

**MST-0429-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) To improve timed JLPT N2 listening, select TWO practices that most directly help.

- A. Practise with audio at natural native speed **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: training at native speed builds the needed processing speed.
- B. Learn to grasp the main point without decoding every word **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: gist comprehension prevents stalling on unknown words.
- C. Pause after every sentence to translate to English  
  _Rationale:_ Translating each sentence is too slow for timed listening.
- D. Only study written transcripts, never audio  
  _Rationale:_ Reading alone does not train listening.
- E. Memorise the answer options before hearing the audio  
  _Rationale:_ Options are not known in advance and cannot be pre-memorised.
