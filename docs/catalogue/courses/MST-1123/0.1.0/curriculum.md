## Learning outcomes

1. Explain how social media fits an organisation's marketing and goals
2. Set audience-based strategy, platform choices and objectives
3. Plan content, formats and a publishing calendar
4. Build and manage an engaged community
5. Measure performance, handle issues and iterate

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A mid-size outdoor-gear retailer wants to grow an engaged community rather than just followers. Set social objectives tied to business goals, choose platforms and content pillars, build a publishing and engagement plan, define community guidelines and a response approach, and select the metrics that show real engagement and impact.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1123-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why is follower count alone a weak measure of social media success?

- A. It does not show engagement or impact on business goals **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a large but inactive or irrelevant audience adds little value.
- B. Followers are impossible to count  
  _Rationale:_ They are countable; they are just a weak success measure alone.
- C. Platforms hide follower numbers  
  _Rationale:_ Follower numbers are generally visible.
- D. Followers always convert to sales  
  _Rationale:_ They often do not; that is the point of the critique.

**MST-1123-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) A brand wants to build an engaged community, not just an audience. Which TWO practices support that? (Select TWO.)

- A. Respond to and converse with followers, not only broadcast **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: two-way interaction builds community and belonging.
- B. Encourage and feature user-generated content **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: involving members deepens engagement and ownership.
- C. Post only promotional offers  
  _Rationale:_ Purely promotional feeds reduce engagement.
- D. Delete all negative comments on sight  
  _Rationale:_ Silencing feedback erodes trust; thoughtful responses build it.

**MST-1123-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A customer posts a public complaint. What is the best first response?

- A. Reply publicly, acknowledge the issue, and move to resolve it **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a prompt, calm public acknowledgement builds trust and shows others you care.
- B. Delete the comment immediately  
  _Rationale:_ Deleting legitimate complaints damages trust.
- C. Ignore it so it fades away  
  _Rationale:_ Ignoring public complaints signals indifference.
- D. Argue publicly that the customer is wrong  
  _Rationale:_ Public arguments escalate and harm the brand.
