## Learning outcomes

1. Apply nouns, articles and determiners correctly in edited writing
2. Apply pronouns and agreement correctly in edited writing
3. Apply verb tenses and aspect correctly in edited writing
4. Apply modifiers: adjectives and adverbs correctly in edited writing
5. Apply sentence structure and clauses correctly in edited writing
6. Apply punctuation and mechanics correctly in edited writing

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items (MCQ/MR) only; does not assess live speaking, extended writing production or any hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1856-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Choose the correct verb: "The list of items ___ on the desk."

- A. is **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the subject is "list" (singular); the intervening "of items" does not change agreement, so "is" is correct.
- B. are  
  _Rationale:_ "Are" agrees with "items", but the true subject is the singular "list".
- C. were  
  _Rationale:_ "Were" is plural (and past); the singular subject needs "is".
- D. have been  
  _Rationale:_ This is plural in sense and changes the meaning; the singular "is" fits.

**MST-1856-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Choose the correct option: "I need ___ information before I decide."

- A. some **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: "information" is uncountable, so the quantifier "some" fits, not "a".
- B. a  
  _Rationale:_ "A" cannot precede the uncountable noun "information".
- C. an  
  _Rationale:_ "An" cannot precede the uncountable noun "information".
- D. many  
  _Rationale:_ "Many" is used with countable nouns, not uncountable "information".

**MST-1856-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO sentences are correctly punctuated and free of run-on errors?

- A. She finished the report, and she sent it to her manager. **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: two independent clauses joined by a comma plus the coordinating conjunction "and".
- B. Because it was late, we postponed the meeting. **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a dependent clause followed by a comma and the independent clause.
- C. She finished the report, she sent it to her manager.  
  _Rationale:_ This is a comma splice joining two independent clauses with only a comma.
- D. We postponed the meeting it was late.  
  _Rationale:_ This is a run-on with no connector or punctuation between clauses.
- E. Finished the report and sent it.  
  _Rationale:_ This fragment lacks a stated subject and is not a complete sentence.
