## Learning outcomes

1. Demonstrate healthcare-context reading comprehension relevant to nursing
2. Apply healthcare English vocabulary, grammar and register knowledge
3. Demonstrate listening comprehension of healthcare consultations and briefings
4. Describe writing and speaking task expectations for nursing (knowledge-based)

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: This preparation assesses reading and listening comprehension and knowledge about the writing and speaking sub-tests through MCQ/MR only; it does not assess productive writing or spoken-performance sub-tests.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0401-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A nurse must explain 'administer the medication orally twice daily' to a patient. Which rewording is most appropriate for the patient?

- A. 'Take this medicine by mouth two times a day.' **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: plain language and correct meaning are appropriate for a patient.
- B. 'Administer the pharmacological agent per os bis in die.'  
  _Rationale:_ Latin and jargon are inappropriate for a patient audience.
- C. 'Do whatever the label says.'  
  _Rationale:_ This is vague and does not convey the instruction.
- D. 'The medication is orally bioavailable.'  
  _Rationale:_ This changes the meaning and is not an instruction.

**MST-0401-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) When scanning a clinical protocol to find a specific dose, what is the most efficient reading strategy?

- A. Scan headings and numbers to locate the relevant section, then read closely **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: scanning for the target then reading closely is efficient and accurate.
- B. Read every word from the start until you reach it  
  _Rationale:_ Reading everything is slow and unnecessary for locating a specific detail.
- C. Guess the dose from memory without checking  
  _Rationale:_ Guessing a dose is unsafe and defeats the purpose of the protocol.
- D. Only read the title page  
  _Rationale:_ The title alone will not contain the specific dose.

**MST-0401-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO pieces of information a nursing referral/transfer letter should include.

- A. The patient's current condition and relevant history **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the receiving team needs the condition and pertinent history.
- B. The reason for the referral or transfer **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the purpose must be stated clearly.
- C. The nurse's personal holiday plans  
  _Rationale:_ Irrelevant to patient care and inappropriate.
- D. Unrelated hospital gossip  
  _Rationale:_ Not clinical and not appropriate in a referral.
- E. The brand of pen used to write the letter  
  _Rationale:_ Irrelevant to the referral content.
