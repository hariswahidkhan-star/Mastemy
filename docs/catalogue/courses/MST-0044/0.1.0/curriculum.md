## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0044-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A business sells goods on credit and issues a document requesting payment. In which book of prime entry is this transaction first recorded?

- A. The sales day book **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: credit sales (evidenced by sales invoices) are first listed in the sales day book.
- B. The cash book  
  _Rationale:_ The cash book records receipts and payments of cash, not credit sales at the point of invoice.
- C. The purchases day book  
  _Rationale:_ The purchases day book records credit purchases, not sales.
- D. The petty cash book  
  _Rationale:_ The petty cash book records small cash payments, not credit sales.

**MST-0044-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A trial balance fails to balance because a transaction was posted to the correct side but with the figures reversed between two accounts. What type of error is this best described as?

- A. An error that a trial balance can reveal because the debits and credits no longer agree **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: if the postings no longer have equal debits and credits, the trial balance will not balance and the error is revealed.
- B. An error of principle, which a trial balance always reveals  
  _Rationale:_ An error of principle keeps debits equal to credits, so a trial balance does not reveal it.
- C. A compensating error  
  _Rationale:_ A compensating error is one whose effect is cancelled by another; it would still balance.
- D. An error of omission  
  _Rationale:_ An omission leaves the trial balance in balance because nothing was posted at all.

**MST-0044-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO items that would appear as reconciling items on a bank reconciliation statement prepared from the cash book.

- A. Unpresented cheques **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: cheques recorded in the cash book but not yet cleared by the bank are a reconciling item.
- B. Outstanding (uncleared) lodgements **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: deposits recorded in the cash book but not yet on the bank statement are a reconciling item.
- C. Credit sales for the period  
  _Rationale:_ Credit sales do not pass through the bank and are not bank reconciliation items.
- D. Closing inventory  
  _Rationale:_ Inventory is not a cash or bank item.
- E. Depreciation for the year  
  _Rationale:_ Depreciation is a non-cash adjustment, not a bank reconciling item.
