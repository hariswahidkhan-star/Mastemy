## Learning outcomes

1. Explain how academic word lists and word families organise high-frequency vocabulary
2. Apply affixes, collocations and register knowledge to infer and use academic words
3. Select precise academic vocabulary for a given context and avoid common confusions

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning (vocabulary, rules, comprehension) through selection items (MCQ/MR) only; does not assess live speaking, extended writing production or any hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1857-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In the word 'unpredictable', which part is the prefix that reverses the meaning of the root?

- A. un- **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the prefix 'un-' negates or reverses the meaning of the root 'predict'.
- B. -able  
  _Rationale:_ '-able' is a suffix that makes an adjective; it does not reverse meaning.
- C. predict  
  _Rationale:_ 'predict' is the root word, not an affix.
- D. -ion  
  _Rationale:_ '-ion' is not present in this word and is a noun-forming suffix.

**MST-1857-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO of the following are conventional academic collocations? (Select TWO.)

- A. conduct research **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'conduct research' is a standard academic collocation.
- B. draw a conclusion **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'draw a conclusion' is a standard academic collocation.
- C. make a research  
  _Rationale:_ Incorrect: 'research' is uncountable here; 'make a research' is not used.
- D. do a conclusion  
  _Rationale:_ Incorrect: conclusions are 'drawn' or 'reached', not 'done'.

**MST-1857-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Choose the most precise word: 'The results clearly ____ the hypothesis, leaving little doubt.'

- A. support  
  _Rationale:_ 'support' is correct but weaker than an option that matches 'little doubt'.
- B. corroborate **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'corroborate' precisely means to confirm with strong evidence, matching 'little doubt'.
- C. mention  
  _Rationale:_ 'mention' does not express confirmation of a hypothesis.
- D. touch  
  _Rationale:_ 'touch' is informal and does not express evidential confirmation.
