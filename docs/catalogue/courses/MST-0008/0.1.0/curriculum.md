## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0008-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) The objective of an external audit of financial statements is to:

- A. Provide reasonable assurance that the statements are free from material misstatement **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an audit provides reasonable (not absolute) assurance on material misstatement.
- B. Guarantee the statements are completely accurate  
  _Rationale:_ Audits provide reasonable, not absolute, assurance.
- C. Detect all instances of fraud  
  _Rationale:_ Audits are not designed to detect all fraud.
- D. Prepare the financial statements  
  _Rationale:_ Preparation is management's responsibility.

**MST-0008-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which of the following best describes audit risk?

- A. The risk that the auditor expresses an inappropriate opinion when the statements are materially misstated **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: audit risk is the risk of an inappropriate opinion on materially misstated statements.
- B. The risk that the client becomes insolvent  
  _Rationale:_ That is business/going-concern risk, not audit risk as defined.
- C. The risk of losing the audit engagement  
  _Rationale:_ That is a commercial risk, not audit risk.
- D. The risk that staff are under-trained  
  _Rationale:_ That is an operational risk within the firm.

**MST-0008-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO components of audit risk under the audit risk model.

- A. Inherent risk **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: inherent risk is a component of audit risk.
- B. Detection risk **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: detection risk is a component of audit risk.
- C. Operational risk  
  _Rationale:_ Operational risk is not part of the audit risk model.
- D. Liquidity risk  
  _Rationale:_ Liquidity risk is a financial risk, not part of the model.
- E. Reputational risk  
  _Rationale:_ Reputational risk is not a component of the audit risk model.
