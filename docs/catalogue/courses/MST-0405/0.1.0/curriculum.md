## Learning outcomes

1. Demonstrate C1-level reading comprehension across text types and purposes
2. Apply advanced grammar and vocabulary in use (controlled language tasks)
3. Demonstrate C1-level listening comprehension for detail, gist and attitude
4. Plan and structure C1-level written responses (knowledge of task types and criteria)

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: This preparation assesses the knowledge- and reception-based components (reading, listening, use of English) and knowledge about writing/speaking criteria through MCQ/MR only; it does not assess productive speaking or extended writing performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0405-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Read: 'While the proposal is undeniably ambitious, it glosses over the practical costs.' What is the writer's attitude to the proposal?

- A. Admiring of its ambition but critical of its lack of practical detail **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'undeniably ambitious' praises scope while 'glosses over' signals criticism.
- B. Wholly enthusiastic with no reservations  
  _Rationale:_ 'Glosses over the practical costs' expresses a clear reservation.
- C. Completely dismissive of the entire idea  
  _Rationale:_ 'Undeniably ambitious' is partly positive, so not wholly dismissive.
- D. Neutral and purely factual  
  _Rationale:_ The evaluative language shows an opinion, not neutrality.

**MST-0405-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Choose the word formed from 'ECONOMY' that best fits: 'The plan is not ___ in the long run.' 

- A. economical **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an adjective describing the plan ('not economical') fits the slot.
- B. economy  
  _Rationale:_ The noun does not fit after 'not' describing the plan.
- C. economics  
  _Rationale:_ 'Economics' is the field of study, not an adjective describing the plan.
- D. economize  
  _Rationale:_ 'Economize' is a verb and does not fit the adjective slot.

**MST-0405-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO things examiners assess in a C1 essay, as described in the assessment criteria.

- A. Whether the response addresses all parts of the task **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: full task achievement is an assessed criterion.
- B. Organization and cohesion of ideas **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: coherent organization is part of the criteria.
- C. The exact number of words memorized  
  _Rationale:_ Rote word count is not an assessed quality criterion.
- D. The color of ink used  
  _Rationale:_ This is irrelevant to assessment.
- E. How quickly the candidate finishes  
  _Rationale:_ Speed is not a graded criterion of essay quality.
