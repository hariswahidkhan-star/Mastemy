## Learning outcomes

1. Apply healthcare-English reading and listening comprehension strategies to medical texts and consultations
2. Analyse profession-specific vocabulary and register for doctor-patient communication
3. Plan structured written referrals and case notes to a professional standard (self-checked via models)
4. Evaluate patient-centred spoken communication strategies for medical consultations (self-checked via models)

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess:
  - Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.
  - Productive skills (consultation role-plays and extended professional writing (referral letters)) are practised and self-checked against model responses only; they are NOT machine-scored or certified by this course and do not count toward the completion threshold.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0402-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which opening best suits an OET-style referral letter to a specialist?

- A. A concise statement of the reason for referral and the main clinical concern **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: states purpose and principal problem for the reader to act on.
- B. A detailed restatement of the patient's entire record  
  _Rationale:_ Full record restatement buries the purpose and is not concise.
- C. A list of the writing doctor's publications  
  _Rationale:_ Irrelevant to a clinical referral's purpose.
- D. The hospital's parking instructions  
  _Rationale:_ Not relevant clinical information.

**MST-0402-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) During a consultation role-play, a patient appears anxious. Which approach best fits patient-centred communication?

- A. Acknowledge the concern, explain clearly and check understanding **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: empathy, clarity and confirmation are central to the criteria.
- B. Speed up to finish the consultation quickly  
  _Rationale:_ Rushing ignores the patient's concern and reduces clarity.
- C. Use technical terms without explanation  
  _Rationale:_ Jargon without explanation is not patient-centred.
- D. Avoid discussing the diagnosis  
  _Rationale:_ Withholding relevant information does not meet the communicative purpose.

**MST-0402-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are assessed strengths of an effective OET-style medical referral letter?

- A. Clear statement of the purpose of writing **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: purpose clarity is a core criterion.
- B. Selection of reader-relevant clinical information **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: relevance and selection are assessed.
- C. Maximum length to show thoroughness  
  _Rationale:_ Length is not a criterion; concise relevance is.
- D. Use of unexplained abbreviations throughout  
  _Rationale:_ Overuse of unexplained abbreviations harms clarity.
- E. Omitting the diagnosis to save space  
  _Rationale:_ Key clinical facts must be included.
