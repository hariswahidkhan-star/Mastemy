## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the role and independence of internal audit
2. Build a risk-based annual audit plan
3. Scope an individual audit engagement with clear objectives
4. Gather and document sufficient appropriate audit evidence
5. Write findings and recommendations that drive remediation

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1050-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) The most reliable form of audit evidence in the list below is generally:

- A. Externally sourced documentation obtained directly by the auditor **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: independent external evidence obtained directly is highly reliable.
- B. A verbal assurance from the process owner  
  _Rationale:_ Inquiry alone is the least reliable form of evidence.
- C. The auditee's own uncorroborated spreadsheet  
  _Rationale:_ Internally generated, uncorroborated evidence is weaker.
- D. An auditor's unsupported assumption  
  _Rationale:_ Assumptions are not evidence.

**MST-1050-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) A well-written audit finding should include which TWO of the following? (Select TWO.)

- A. The root cause of the issue **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: root cause is needed so the fix addresses the real problem.
- B. The effect or risk arising from the condition **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the effect shows why the finding matters and sets priority.
- C. The auditor's personal opinion of the staff involved  
  _Rationale:_ Findings address process and control, not personalities.
- D. A guarantee that no issues remain anywhere  
  _Rationale:_ Audit gives reasonable, not absolute, assurance.

**MST-1050-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A risk-based audit plan allocates audit effort primarily according to:

- A. The assessed risk and significance of each area in the audit universe **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: risk-based planning directs resources to higher-risk areas.
- B. Alphabetical order of departments  
  _Rationale:_ Ordering by name ignores risk.
- C. Whichever manager asks most loudly  
  _Rationale:_ Planning should be objective and risk-driven.
- D. Equal time to every process regardless of risk  
  _Rationale:_ Equal allocation wastes effort on low-risk areas.
