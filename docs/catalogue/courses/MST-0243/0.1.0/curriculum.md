## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0243-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which document type is mandatory and specifies exact, measurable requirements an organisation must follow?

- A. Standard **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: standards are mandatory and specify precise requirements.
- B. Guideline  
  _Rationale:_ Guidelines are recommended, not mandatory.
- C. Procedure  
  _Rationale:_ A procedure gives step-by-step instructions, not the overarching requirement.
- D. Baseline  
  _Rationale:_ A baseline is a minimum configuration reference, derived from standards.

**MST-0243-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A CISO must prioritise risk treatment. A risk has low likelihood but catastrophic impact and no feasible control. Which treatment is most appropriate?

- A. Transfer the risk, for example through insurance **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: when a severe risk cannot be reduced cost-effectively, transfer shares the financial impact.
- B. Accept the risk silently  
  _Rationale:_ Silent acceptance of a catastrophic risk without a decision record is poor governance.
- C. Ignore it because likelihood is low  
  _Rationale:_ Low likelihood with catastrophic impact still demands treatment.
- D. Mitigate with a control that does not exist yet  
  _Rationale:_ No feasible control exists, so mitigation is not currently available.

**MST-0243-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are core principles of secure design?

- A. Defence in depth **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: layered controls reduce reliance on any single defence.
- B. Least privilege **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: granting minimal access limits blast radius.
- C. Security through obscurity as the sole control  
  _Rationale:_ Hiding details is not a reliable standalone control.
- D. Single shared administrator account  
  _Rationale:_ Shared high privilege breaks accountability.
- E. Disabling audit logging  
  _Rationale:_ Logging is essential to detection and accountability.
