## Learning outcomes

1. Explain brand strategy and positioning
2. Define brand identity and expression
3. Explain brand architecture and portfolio
4. Measure and manage brand equity

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A marketer must reposition a maturing brand losing relevance. They must define strategy and positioning, align identity and voice, decide brand architecture for new lines, and set brand-equity metrics, defending the plan to leadership on both differentiation and measurability.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1840-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Brand positioning is best described as:

- A. The distinct place a brand aims to occupy in the customer's mind **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: positioning defines perceived distinctiveness and relevance.
- B. The colour palette of the logo  
  _Rationale:_ That is identity, not positioning.
- C. The quarterly ad budget  
  _Rationale:_ Budget is not positioning.
- D. The number of SKUs sold  
  _Rationale:_ Unrelated to positioning.

**MST-1840-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are elements of brand identity? (Select TWO.)

- A. Visual expression such as logo, colour and type **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: visuals are part of identity.
- B. Verbal identity such as voice and tone **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: how a brand speaks is identity.
- C. The competitor's pricing sheet  
  _Rationale:_ That belongs to a competitor, not your identity.
- D. The warehouse postal code  
  _Rationale:_ Irrelevant to identity.

**MST-1840-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A branded-house architecture (vs house-of-brands) tends to:

- A. Concentrate equity under one master brand **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a branded house leverages one strong brand.
- B. Give every product a wholly unrelated brand  
  _Rationale:_ That describes a house of brands.
- C. Eliminate the need for positioning  
  _Rationale:_ Positioning still applies.
- D. Guarantee higher prices automatically  
  _Rationale:_ No such guarantee.
