## Learning outcomes

1. Write for an executive audience by leading with the decision
2. Structure a board paper around the ask, options and recommendation
3. Make a clear recommendation and present risks and trade-offs honestly
4. Verify board-paper content and handle confidential material appropriately
5. Prepare executive delivery materials that support a decision discussion

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Selection items cannot demonstrate live tool use or writing quality; those are taught through instructor demonstrations and model-answer analysis.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0517-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the primary job of a board paper?

- A. To enable a specific decision by the board **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a board paper exists to support a decision, not merely to inform.
- B. To record every operational detail of a programme  
  _Rationale:_ Exhaustive detail belongs elsewhere; the board needs the decision.
- C. To demonstrate how much work the team did  
  _Rationale:_ Effort is not the purpose of a board paper.
- D. To summarise the last meeting's minutes  
  _Rationale:_ Minutes are a separate document.

**MST-0517-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO belong in a well-structured recommendation section? (Select TWO.)

- A. A clear recommended option **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the board needs an explicit recommendation.
- B. The rationale linking evidence to the recommendation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a recommendation needs its reasoning to be defensible.
- C. A list of options with no stated preference  
  _Rationale:_ Leaving the board to guess is not a recommendation.
- D. Omission of any risks  
  _Rationale:_ Hiding risks undermines a defensible recommendation.

**MST-0517-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) You plan to draft a board paper containing unreleased financial results with Claude. What must you check first?

- A. Your organisation's data-handling and confidentiality settings for the workspace **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: confidential material requires confirming retention and sharing settings first.
- B. Whether the paper uses the latest font  
  _Rationale:_ Typography is not a confidentiality control.
- C. The length of the chat history  
  _Rationale:_ Chat length is irrelevant to confidentiality.
- D. Nothing; results are safe to paste anywhere  
  _Rationale:_ Unreleased results are highly sensitive and must be handled carefully.
