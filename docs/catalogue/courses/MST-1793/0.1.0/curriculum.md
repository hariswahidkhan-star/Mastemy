## Integrative case

An organisation is preparing for ISO 9001 certification but its procedures are ignored in practice. The learner must apply the process approach and risk-based thinking, define context and objectives, establish controlled information and competence, and set up audit, management review and corrective action, then recommend a path to a defensible QMS.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1793-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) ISO 9001 certification confirms that an organisation:

- A. Operates a quality management system meeting the standard's requirements **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ISO 9001 certifies the management system, not individual products.
- B. Makes the highest-quality product in its market  
  _Rationale:_ The standard does not rank product quality.
- C. Has zero defects in everything it produces  
  _Rationale:_ Certification does not guarantee zero defects.
- D. Is legally required to exist to trade  
  _Rationale:_ ISO 9001 is voluntary, not a legal requirement.

**MST-1793-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO steps follow a detected nonconformity under ISO 9001? (Select TWO.)

- A. Take action to correct and control the immediate issue **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: correction deals with the nonconformity itself.
- B. Analyse the root cause and apply corrective action to prevent recurrence **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: corrective action addresses the cause, not just the symptom.
- C. Hide the nonconformity from the next audit  
  _Rationale:_ Concealment violates the standard's intent.
- D. Immediately seek re-certification regardless of the cause  
  _Rationale:_ Re-certification is unrelated to closing one nonconformity.

**MST-1793-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Risk-based thinking in ISO 9001 requires an organisation to:

- A. Identify risks and opportunities that could affect conformity and act on them **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the standard embeds proactive risk consideration throughout the QMS.
- B. Maintain a separate, formal risk register in a fixed format  
  _Rationale:_ The standard does not mandate a specific risk-register format.
- C. Eliminate all risk from every process  
  _Rationale:_ Eliminating all risk is neither possible nor required.
- D. Ignore risk unless a customer complains  
  _Rationale:_ Risk-based thinking is proactive, not complaint-driven.
