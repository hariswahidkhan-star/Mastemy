## Learning outcomes

1. Name the stages of the water cycle and put them in order
2. Describe evaporation, condensation, precipitation and collection
3. Explain how the Sun drives the movement of water
4. Identify where water is stored on Earth
5. Relate the water cycle to clouds, rain and rivers
6. Explain in simple terms why fresh water should be used carefully

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

After a rainy week, a class investigates where puddle water goes, builds a sealed bag 'mini water cycle' on a sunny window, explains evaporation, condensation and precipitation, and makes a poster showing why the same water keeps cycling and should not be wasted.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2833-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which process turns liquid water into water vapour that rises into the air?

- A. Evaporation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: evaporation turns liquid water into vapour, driven by the Sun's heat.
- B. Condensation  
  _Rationale:_ Condensation turns vapour back into liquid.
- C. Precipitation  
  _Rationale:_ Precipitation is water falling as rain or snow.
- D. Collection  
  _Rationale:_ Collection is water gathering in rivers and seas.

**MST-2833-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) What makes the water cycle keep going? (Select ONE)

- A. Energy from the Sun heating water **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the Sun's energy drives evaporation and the whole cycle.
- B. Water being created new each day  
  _Rationale:_ Water is recycled, not freshly created.
- C. Wind blowing clouds into pieces  
  _Rationale:_ Wind moves clouds but does not power the cycle.
- D. People pouring water into the sky  
  _Rationale:_ People do not power the water cycle.

**MST-2833-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO places where Earth's water is stored. (Select TWO)

- A. Oceans and seas **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: most of Earth's water is stored in oceans.
- B. Glaciers and ice caps **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: large amounts of fresh water are frozen in ice.
- C. Inside light bulbs  
  _Rationale:_ Light bulbs do not store Earth's water.
- D. Inside solid rocks that never touch water  
  _Rationale:_ This is not a normal water store in the cycle.
