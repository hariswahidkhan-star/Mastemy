## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1069-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What should you establish before drafting any business document?

- A. Its purpose and intended audience **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: purpose and audience shape structure, tone and content.
- B. The longest possible word count  
  _Rationale:_ Length is not a goal; clarity is.
- C. The most complex vocabulary available  
  _Rationale:_ Plain language usually communicates better than complex vocabulary.
- D. The font and colour scheme first  
  _Rationale:_ Formatting follows purpose and content, not the reverse.

**MST-1069-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO practices make a professional email more effective? (Select TWO)

- A. A specific, informative subject line **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a clear subject line tells the reader what to expect and act on.
- B. Leading with the key message and any request **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: putting the key point first respects the reader's time and reduces missed actions.
- C. Using dense jargon to sound authoritative  
  _Rationale:_ Jargon reduces clarity and can confuse readers.
- D. Copying the whole department on every message  
  _Rationale:_ Over-copying creates noise and dilutes accountability.

**MST-1069-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why should an executive summary be able to stand alone?

- A. Senior readers may act on it without reading the full document **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an executive summary must convey the conclusion and key support on its own.
- B. It is only decoration and will not be read  
  _Rationale:_ It is often the most-read part, not decoration.
- C. It should contain no conclusions  
  _Rationale:_ It must state the conclusion up front.
- D. It must be longer than the report  
  _Rationale:_ It is a concise distillation, not longer than the report.
