## Learning outcomes

1. Describe integrated governance, risk and compliance concepts and terminology
2. Apply a principled-performance approach to objectives and context
3. Evaluate the design of controls, policies and risk responses
4. Analyse monitoring, assurance and continuous improvement of a GRC capability

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0116-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A risk falls well within the organisation's stated risk appetite. The most defensible response is often to:

- A. Accept the risk and monitor it **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: risks within appetite are commonly accepted and monitored.
- B. Spend heavily to eliminate it entirely  
  _Rationale:_ Eliminating a within-appetite risk wastes resources.
- C. Ignore it and stop monitoring  
  _Rationale:_ Acceptance still requires monitoring.
- D. Transfer it regardless of cost  
  _Rationale:_ Transfer is not justified for a within-appetite risk at any cost.

**MST-0116-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which best describes 'principled performance' in an integrated GRC capability?

- A. Reliably achieving objectives while addressing uncertainty and acting with integrity **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: principled performance integrates objectives, risk and integrity.
- B. Maximising short-term profit regardless of risk  
  _Rationale:_ Ignoring risk and integrity is the opposite of the concept.
- C. Running governance, risk and compliance as separate silos  
  _Rationale:_ Siloed functions contradict integrated GRC.
- D. Eliminating all documentation  
  _Rationale:_ Documentation supports, not undermines, the capability.

**MST-0116-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO signals best demonstrate that a control is operating effectively over time? (Select TWO)

- A. Monitoring evidence showing the control runs as designed **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ongoing monitoring evidences operating effectiveness.
- B. Independent assurance testing confirming results **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: independent testing corroborates effectiveness.
- C. A single sign-off from last year  
  _Rationale:_ A one-time sign-off does not prove ongoing effectiveness.
- D. The control owner's confidence alone  
  _Rationale:_ Self-confidence is not objective evidence.
- E. A marketing claim about compliance  
  _Rationale:_ Marketing claims are not control evidence.
