## Learning outcomes

1. Set up a business document for a specific reader and goal
2. Produce a clear first draft efficiently
3. Edit AI drafts to a professional standard
4. Apply confidentiality, accuracy and human-review controls to the workflow

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every course outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Hands-on operation of ChatGPT, the quality of live AI outputs, and professional judgement on accepting AI suggestions are taught by video demonstration and model-answer analysis; the certificate assessment uses single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only and never requires uploads, recorded work, graded prompts or peer review.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0477-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What should be decided first when drafting an executive memo with ChatGPT?

- A. The audience and the decision the memo supports **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: audience and purpose drive structure, tone and content; they come before drafting.
- B. The font and colour scheme  
  _Rationale:_ Visual styling is a late, minor concern relative to purpose.
- C. The exact word count  
  _Rationale:_ Length follows from purpose and content, not the reverse.
- D. Which model version is newest  
  _Rationale:_ Model choice does not determine the document's purpose.

**MST-0477-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) An AI-drafted brief reads well but cites a revenue figure. What is the key editing step?

- A. Verify the figure against the source data **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: fluent writing does not guarantee accuracy, so numbers must be checked against sources.
- B. Trust it because the prose is polished  
  _Rationale:_ Polish is unrelated to factual accuracy.
- C. Add more adjectives  
  _Rationale:_ Embellishment does not confirm the figure.
- D. Make the brief longer  
  _Rationale:_ Length does not verify the number.

**MST-0477-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO make an executive summary more effective? (Select TWO)

- A. Lead with the recommendation or ask **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: executives want the decision up front.
- B. Keep it concise and skimmable **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: brevity and structure respect a busy reader.
- C. Bury the conclusion at the end  
  _Rationale:_ Hiding the ask forces the reader to hunt for it.
- D. Include every supporting detail  
  _Rationale:_ Exhaustive detail belongs in the body or appendix, not the summary.
