## Learning outcomes

1. Explain payroll concepts, worker classification and pay computation
2. Apply payroll taxation, withholding and deposit fundamentals
3. Describe payroll compliance, recordkeeping and reporting
4. Explain payroll systems, accounting entries and controls

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0302-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) When determining whether a worker is an employee or an independent contractor, the key consideration is:

- A. The degree of control and the nature of the working relationship **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: classification rests on control and relationship facts, not labels.
- B. Whichever label the worker prefers  
  _Rationale:_ Preference does not determine classification.
- C. The job title alone  
  _Rationale:_ A title alone does not determine status.
- D. Whether the worker has a business card  
  _Rationale:_ A business card is irrelevant to classification.

**MST-0302-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A payroll register must be reconciled to the general ledger primarily to:

- A. Ensure recorded payroll matches amounts actually paid and posted **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reconciliation catches discrepancies between payroll and the ledger.
- B. Make the report longer  
  _Rationale:_ Length is not the purpose.
- C. Avoid keeping any records  
  _Rationale:_ Reconciliation supports, not replaces, recordkeeping.
- D. Replace the need for controls  
  _Rationale:_ Reconciliation complements controls, not replaces them.

**MST-0302-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO controls that help prevent fraudulent or duplicate payroll payments.

- A. Segregation of duties between setup and payment approval **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: separating duties limits single-person fraud.
- B. Reconciling the payroll register before disbursement **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reconciliation catches duplicates and errors.
- C. Letting one person both add employees and release pay  
  _Rationale:_ Concentrated duties increase fraud risk.
- D. Skipping all reviews to save time  
  _Rationale:_ No review removes a key control.
- E. Disabling audit logs  
  _Rationale:_ Audit logs support detection, not removal.
