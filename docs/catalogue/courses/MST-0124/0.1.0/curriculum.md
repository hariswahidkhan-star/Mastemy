## Learning outcomes

1. Set risk strategy and plan the risk approach
2. Engage stakeholders and facilitate the risk process
3. Monitor, report and perform specialised risk analyses

- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0124-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What distinguishes a risk management plan from a risk register?

- A. The plan defines how risk will be managed; the register lists the identified risks **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the plan sets the approach; the register records specific risks.
- B. They are the same document  
  _Rationale:_ They serve different purposes.
- C. The register defines appetite and thresholds  
  _Rationale:_ Appetite and thresholds belong to the plan.
- D. The plan lists each individual risk  
  _Rationale:_ Listing risks is the register's role.

**MST-0124-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A Monte Carlo schedule simulation most directly produces:

- A. A single deterministic completion date  
  _Rationale:_ Monte Carlo yields a distribution, not a single date.
- B. A probability distribution of completion dates (an S-curve) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it produces a distribution of outcomes read as an S-curve.
- C. A fixed budget baseline  
  _Rationale:_ It models uncertainty, not a fixed baseline.
- D. A stakeholder register  
  _Rationale:_ That is unrelated to the simulation.

**MST-0124-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO items that a risk report to executives should include.

- A. Trend in overall exposure since the last report **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: executives need the exposure trend.
- B. The full raw risk register with every field  
  _Rationale:_ Executives need a summary, not the raw register.
- C. Only the threats, omitting opportunities  
  _Rationale:_ Opportunities should be reported alongside threats.
- D. Top risks with owners and response status **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: top risks, owners and status belong in an executive report.
- E. A list of every meeting held  
  _Rationale:_ Meeting logs are not the point of a risk report.
