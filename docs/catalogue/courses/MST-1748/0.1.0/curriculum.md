## Integrative case

A capital project is tracking over budget at the halfway point. The learner must re-examine the estimate basis and reserves, rebuild a time-phased budget, compute earned-value indices, forecast the final cost, and propose corrective actions through change control, then brief the sponsor.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1748-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A cost performance index (CPI) below 1.0 indicates the project is:

- A. Spending more than the value of work completed (over budget) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: CPI = EV/AC; below 1.0 means cost overrun.
- B. Ahead of schedule  
  _Rationale:_ That would be indicated by SPI, not CPI.
- C. Under budget  
  _Rationale:_ CPI below 1.0 means over, not under, budget.
- D. Exactly on plan for cost  
  _Rationale:_ On-plan cost gives CPI = 1.0.

**MST-1748-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO statements about reserves are correct? (Select TWO.)

- A. Contingency reserve covers identified ('known') risks **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: contingency is for analysed risks within the baseline.
- B. Management reserve covers unforeseen ('unknown') work **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: management reserve sits outside the baseline for unknowns.
- C. Contingency reserve should be spent freely by the team  
  _Rationale:_ Contingency use is governed by the risk response plan.
- D. Reserves are the same as profit margin  
  _Rationale:_ Reserves fund risk, not profit.

**MST-1748-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Parametric estimating works by:

- A. Multiplying a measured quantity by a historical unit rate **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: parametric uses statistical rates (e.g. cost per square metre).
- B. Copying the total from a single similar past project  
  _Rationale:_ That is analogous estimating.
- C. Summing detailed estimates for every work package  
  _Rationale:_ That is bottom-up estimating.
- D. Guessing a round number with no basis  
  _Rationale:_ That is not an estimating technique.
