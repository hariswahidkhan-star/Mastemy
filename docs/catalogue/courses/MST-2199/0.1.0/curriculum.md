## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the construction project lifecycle and the roles of owner, designer, contractor and subcontractors
2. Explain common project delivery methods and contract types and when each is appropriate
3. Interpret the core construction documents: drawings, specifications and the contract set
4. Apply the basics of scope, time, cost and quality management to a construction project
5. Identify risks, change orders and claims and how they are managed on site
6. Communicate project status to stakeholders using clear, honest reporting

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2199-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A note on a structural drawing conflicts with the written specification. Which document generally governs when the contract sets a precedence order favouring specifications?

- A. The specification, because the contract's order of precedence resolves the conflict **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the contract's precedence clause decides; here specifications govern.
- B. Whichever was issued most recently  
  _Rationale:_ Issue date does not override a stated precedence order.
- C. The drawing, because drawings always win  
  _Rationale:_ Drawings do not automatically govern; the contract sets precedence.
- D. Neither; the work stops permanently  
  _Rationale:_ Conflicts are resolved by precedence and RFIs, not by stopping work.

**MST-2199-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are typically the owner's responsibilities rather than the general contractor's? (Select TWO.)

- A. Securing project financing **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: arranging funding is an owner responsibility.
- B. Defining the project program and budget **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the owner sets the program and budget.
- C. Sequencing the trades on site  
  _Rationale:_ Means and methods, including trade sequencing, are the contractor's.
- D. Supervising subcontractor crews daily  
  _Rationale:_ Daily supervision of crews is the contractor's role.

**MST-2199-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) An owner wants to overlap design and construction to finish sooner on a warehouse with a well-understood scope. Which delivery method best supports this?

- A. Design-build, which integrates design and construction under one entity to enable fast-tracking **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: design-build supports overlapping design and construction.
- B. Traditional design-bid-build with full design before bidding  
  _Rationale:_ That sequences design fully before construction, slowing delivery.
- C. Sole-source procurement with no contract  
  _Rationale:_ Operating without a contract is not a delivery method and is high risk.
- D. Waiting until all permits are closed before any design  
  _Rationale:_ This delays rather than accelerates delivery.
