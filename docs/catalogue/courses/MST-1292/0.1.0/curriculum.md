2. Apply evidence collection, preservation and chain-of-custody principles

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1292-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why is an unbroken chain of custody important for physical evidence?

- A. It makes evidence collection faster  
  _Rationale:_ Chain of custody concerns integrity, not speed.
- B. It demonstrates the evidence was not altered or substituted, supporting admissibility **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a documented, unbroken chain shows integrity and supports admissibility.
- C. It replaces the need to collect evidence lawfully  
  _Rationale:_ Lawful collection is still required regardless of custody records.
- D. It guarantees a conviction  
  _Rationale:_ Chain of custody supports integrity but does not guarantee outcomes.

**MST-1292-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which questioning approach yields the most reliable information early in an interview?

- A. Leading questions that suggest the answer  
  _Rationale:_ Leading questions can contaminate accounts and reduce reliability.
- B. Open-ended questions that let the subject give a free account **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: open-ended questions elicit fuller, less-contaminated accounts.
- C. Rapid-fire accusations  
  _Rationale:_ Accusatory pressure risks unreliable statements.
- D. Silence for the entire interview  
  _Rationale:_ Providing no structure fails to gather information.

**MST-1292-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO practices keep an investigative report defensible?

- A. Separating documented facts from the investigator's opinions **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: distinguishing fact from opinion keeps the report defensible.
- B. Supporting each conclusion with cited evidence **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: evidence-linked conclusions withstand scrutiny.
- C. Including speculation presented as established fact  
  _Rationale:_ Speculation stated as fact undermines credibility.
- D. Omitting information that contradicts the theory  
  _Rationale:_ Suppressing contrary evidence is improper and indefensible.
