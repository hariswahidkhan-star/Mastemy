## Learning outcomes

1. Classify rocks as igneous, sedimentary or metamorphic by how they form
2. Describe the rock cycle and the processes that change rocks
3. Explain how weathering and erosion break down and move rock
4. Describe how soil forms and name its main components
5. Identify minerals using simple physical properties
6. Relate rock and soil types to their everyday uses

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2835-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) How do igneous rocks form?

- A. Molten rock (magma or lava) cools and solidifies **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: igneous rocks crystallise from cooling molten rock.
- B. Layers of sediment are pressed and cemented together  
  _Rationale:_ That describes sedimentary rocks.
- C. Existing rock is changed by heat and pressure without melting fully  
  _Rationale:_ That describes metamorphic rocks.
- D. Dead plants are compressed in swamps  
  _Rationale:_ That describes coal formation, not igneous rock generally.

**MST-2835-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A smooth boulder in a river becomes rounder over the years. Which process best explains this? (Select ONE)

- A. Weathering and erosion wearing away its edges as it tumbles **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: physical weathering and erosion round and smooth rock.
- B. The rock growing new crystals to reshape itself  
  _Rationale:_ Rocks do not grow themselves rounder.
- C. Metamorphism melting the surface  
  _Rationale:_ Rounding is not caused by melting.
- D. Soil formation inside the rock  
  _Rationale:_ Soil forming is not what rounds a boulder.

**MST-2835-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO physical properties commonly used to identify a mineral. (Select TWO)

- A. Hardness **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: hardness (e.g. the Mohs scale) helps identify minerals.
- B. Streak colour **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the streak left on a tile is a useful identifying property.
- C. The price it sold for last year  
  _Rationale:_ Price is economic, not a physical identifying property.
- D. The name of the person who found it  
  _Rationale:_ Who found it is not a property of the mineral.
