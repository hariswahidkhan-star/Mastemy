## Learning outcomes

1. Describe how PRINCE2 and agile ways of working combine
2. Apply fixing and flexing of project variables in an agile PRINCE2 context
3. Evaluate agile concepts, behaviours and techniques within PRINCE2 governance
4. Analyse tailoring PRINCE2 themes and processes for agile delivery

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0133-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In PRINCE2 Agile's fix/flex approach for a release with a hard launch date, which variable is typically FIXED while scope flexes?

- A. Time, so the team delivers the most valuable scope achievable by the date **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: PRINCE2 Agile often fixes time and cost and flexes scope by prioritisation.
- B. Scope, delivering every planned feature no matter the date  
  _Rationale:_ Fixing scope against a hard date is the traditional assumption PRINCE2 Agile avoids.
- C. Quality tolerances, lowered freely to hit the date  
  _Rationale:_ Quality is protected, not casually traded away.
- D. Benefits, which are abandoned to save time  
  _Rationale:_ Benefits are the point of the project, not a flex variable to abandon.

**MST-0133-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why does an agile team benefit from being run inside PRINCE2 governance on a larger project?

- A. It adds direction, business justification and controlled stage boundaries the team alone may lack **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: PRINCE2 supplies governance and justification around agile delivery.
- B. It forces the team to abandon iterations  
  _Rationale:_ PRINCE2 Agile preserves iterative delivery.
- C. It removes the need for any customer involvement  
  _Rationale:_ Customer collaboration remains central.
- D. It guarantees no change can occur  
  _Rationale:_ Agile embraces change; governance manages it.

**MST-0133-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO statements correctly describe PRINCE2 Agile's view of combining the approaches? (Select TWO)

- A. PRINCE2 provides governance while agile provides flexible delivery **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the combination pairs governance with flexible delivery.
- B. Tailoring is used to adapt themes and processes for agile working **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: tailoring is how PRINCE2 flexes to agile delivery.
- C. Agile delivery means the project board is no longer needed  
  _Rationale:_ The board's direction role remains.
- D. Combining them means dropping the PRINCE2 principles  
  _Rationale:_ Principles are retained.
- E. Fixing and flexing applies only to cost  
  _Rationale:_ Several variables are considered, not cost alone.
