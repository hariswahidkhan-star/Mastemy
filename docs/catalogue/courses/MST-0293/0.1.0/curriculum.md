## Learning outcomes

1. Explain core HR operations, the employee lifecycle and HR's role in an organization
2. Describe recruitment, onboarding, compensation and benefits fundamentals
3. Explain employee relations, performance, learning and development basics
4. Describe HR compliance, recordkeeping, health and safety and ethical practice

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0293-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which document set should be kept confidential and access-restricted within HR?

- A. Employee medical and personal records **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: personal and medical records are confidential and access must be limited.
- B. The public company holiday calendar  
  _Rationale:_ A holiday calendar is general information, not confidential.
- C. The office floor plan posted in the lobby  
  _Rationale:_ A posted floor plan is already public.
- D. The published code of conduct  
  _Rationale:_ A code of conduct is meant to be shared with all staff.

**MST-0293-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) An employee reports possible harassment to the HR coordinator. What is the appropriate first action?

- A. Document the report and begin the organization's defined complaint procedure **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: record the concern and follow the established, fair process.
- B. Tell the employee to resolve it directly with the alleged harasser alone  
  _Rationale:_ Forcing direct confrontation can worsen harm and ignores policy.
- C. Dismiss it unless there are witnesses  
  _Rationale:_ Reports should be taken seriously and investigated, not pre-judged.
- D. Announce the complaint to the whole team  
  _Rationale:_ Broadcasting breaches confidentiality and can harm those involved.

**MST-0293-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO elements that make a job-selection process more fair and defensible.

- A. Using job-related, consistent screening criteria for all candidates **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: consistent, job-related criteria reduce bias and support defensibility.
- B. Documenting the reasons for each selection decision **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: records show decisions were based on legitimate criteria.
- C. Choosing candidates based on personal friendships  
  _Rationale:_ Favoritism undermines fairness and legal defensibility.
- D. Asking about protected characteristics such as age or religion  
  _Rationale:_ Such questions can be discriminatory and are not job-related.
- E. Changing the criteria for each applicant  
  _Rationale:_ Inconsistent criteria make decisions biased and hard to defend.
