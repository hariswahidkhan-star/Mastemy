## Learning outcomes

1. Explain what evidence-based practice is and the core steps that make it up
2. Formulate answerable practice questions using a structured format such as PICO
3. Locate sources of evidence and describe the hierarchy of evidence
4. Appraise study validity, bias and applicability to a given context
5. Integrate evidence with professional judgement and patient values, then evaluate the result

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Selection items assess understanding and appraisal reasoning only; they do not demonstrate a learner's ability to conduct a full critical appraisal or lead a real practice change.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1784-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In the PICO framework used to frame an answerable question, what does the 'C' most commonly represent?

- A. Comparison or comparator **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: C is the comparison or comparator, the alternative against which the intervention is weighed.
- B. Cause  
  _Rationale:_ PICO does not include 'cause'; the comparator is what C denotes.
- C. Condition  
  _Rationale:_ The condition is captured in the Population (P), not in C.
- D. Conclusion  
  _Rationale:_ A conclusion is an output of appraisal, not an element of the PICO question.

**MST-1784-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Evidence-based practice integrates best available evidence with which TWO other elements? (Select TWO.)

- A. The clinician's professional expertise **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: professional expertise is one of the three pillars of EBP.
- B. The patient's values and preferences **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: patient values and preferences are an essential pillar alongside evidence and expertise.
- C. The most senior colleague's personal opinion  
  _Rationale:_ Deferring to seniority alone is authority-based, not evidence-based, practice.
- D. Whatever option is cheapest to implement  
  _Rationale:_ Cost may inform feasibility but is not a defining pillar of EBP.

**MST-1784-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Considering only study design, which source generally sits highest in the hierarchy of evidence for a question about treatment effectiveness?

- A. A systematic review of randomised controlled trials **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a well-conducted systematic review of RCTs generally sits at the top for treatment-effectiveness questions.
- B. A single expert's opinion piece  
  _Rationale:_ Expert opinion sits near the bottom of the hierarchy because it is most prone to bias.
- C. A single case report  
  _Rationale:_ A case report describes one instance and cannot establish effectiveness.
- D. A narrative commentary with no stated methods  
  _Rationale:_ Without a stated method the work cannot be appraised and ranks low.
