## Integrative case

You are overloaded while your team is underused: decide what to delegate and to whom, brief each task with a clear outcome and level of authority, agree accountability and success measures, set proportionate check-ins, and close the loop with feedback; then justify how you balanced control with ownership.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1065-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A common delegation failure is handing over a task but not the matching authority. Why is that a problem?

- A. The person is accountable for the outcome but cannot make the decisions needed to deliver it **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: responsibility without authority stalls the work and frustrates the owner.
- B. It makes the task finish faster  
  _Rationale:_ It typically slows the work down.
- C. Authority is irrelevant to delegation  
  _Rationale:_ Matching authority to the task is central to delegation.
- D. It removes all need for check-ins  
  _Rationale:_ Check-ins are still needed; the issue is missing authority.

**MST-1065-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO describe healthy follow-through on a delegated task? (Select TWO)

- A. Agreeing check-in points proportionate to the risk and the person's readiness **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: right-sized check-ins give support without micromanaging.
- B. Course-correcting through questions rather than taking the task back **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: coaching the owner preserves ownership while fixing course.
- C. Reviewing every small step the person takes  
  _Rationale:_ That is micromanaging and undermines ownership.
- D. Ignoring the task entirely until the deadline  
  _Rationale:_ Abandonment is as harmful as micromanaging.

**MST-1065-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the difference between accountability and responsibility in delegation?

- A. Accountability is ultimate ownership of the outcome; responsibility is doing the work to deliver it **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: one person is accountable for the outcome even when work is shared as responsibility.
- B. They are two words for exactly the same thing  
  _Rationale:_ They differ: ownership of outcome vs doing the work.
- C. Responsibility can never be delegated  
  _Rationale:_ Responsibility for tasks is routinely delegated.
- D. Accountability means blaming the team  
  _Rationale:_ Accountability is ownership, not blame.
