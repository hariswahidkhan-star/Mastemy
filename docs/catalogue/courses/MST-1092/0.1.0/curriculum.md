## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1092-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In value-proposition design, a 'pain reliever' is an element that:

- A. Describes a feature of the product  
  _Rationale:_ A feature is not automatically a pain reliever.
- B. Reduces a specific frustration in the customer's job **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: pain relievers address identified customer pains.
- C. Lowers the company's internal costs  
  _Rationale:_ Internal cost is the cost side, not customer value.
- D. Adds a channel to reach customers  
  _Rationale:_ Channels are a separate building block.

**MST-1092-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Value-based pricing differs from cost-plus pricing mainly because it starts from:

- A. The cost to produce plus a fixed margin  
  _Rationale:_ That is cost-plus pricing.
- B. The value the offering creates for the customer **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: value-based pricing anchors on customer-perceived value.
- C. The price of the cheapest competitor  
  _Rationale:_ Matching the cheapest rival is not value-based pricing.
- D. A round number that is easy to remember  
  _Rationale:_ Memorable numbers are a tactic, not the pricing basis.

**MST-1092-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) To test a business model fully, which TWO questions must be answered? (Select TWO)

- A. Do customers want it (desirability)? **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: desirability tests whether there is real demand.
- B. Can we deliver and sustain it profitably (viability)? **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: viability tests whether the economics work.
- C. Does the canvas look visually balanced?  
  _Rationale:_ Visual balance says nothing about whether the model works.
- D. Is the idea novel to investors?  
  _Rationale:_ Novelty is not a test of model validity.
