## Learning outcomes

1. Getting started
2. Sales and customers
3. Purchases and expenses

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses conceptual knowledge of QuickBooks Online workflows through selection items; does not assess hands-on use of the live software or a real company's books.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1720-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In QuickBooks Online, which document should be used when a customer pays at the moment of sale with no invoice outstanding?

- A. Sales receipt **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a sales receipt records an immediate paid sale.
- B. Invoice  
  _Rationale:_ An invoice is for amounts to be paid later.
- C. Bill  
  _Rationale:_ A bill records money the business owes a vendor.
- D. Purchase order  
  _Rationale:_ A purchase order requests goods; it is not a sale record.

**MST-1720-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select ALL that apply) Which two statements about bank reconciliation in QuickBooks Online are correct? (Select TWO) (Select TWO)

- A. It compares recorded transactions against the bank statement **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reconciliation matches the books to the bank.
- B. It should end with a zero difference before completing **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an unexplained difference means it is not reconciled.
- C. It automatically creates the company's chart of accounts  
  _Rationale:_ Reconciliation does not build the chart of accounts.
- D. It is only needed once when the company is created  
  _Rationale:_ Reconciliation is a recurring task, not one-off.

**MST-1720-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Entering both a 'bill' and an 'expense' for the very same purchase will most likely cause:

- A. The cost to be recorded twice (double counting) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: recording both duplicates the expense.
- B. The cost to be omitted entirely  
  _Rationale:_ The problem is duplication, not omission.
- C. The bank to reconcile automatically  
  _Rationale:_ It does not auto-reconcile; it creates an error.
- D. No effect on the accounts  
  _Rationale:_ It overstates expenses and payables.
