## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0343-Q0001** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) A patient has a deficiency of an enzyme early in a metabolic pathway. The most direct expected consequence is:

- A. Accumulation of the substrate before the block **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: substrate upstream of a blocked step accumulates.
- B. Increased final product  
  _Rationale:_ A block reduces downstream product, not increases it.
- C. No metabolic change  
  _Rationale:_ An early block does affect the pathway.
- D. Faster flux through the pathway  
  _Rationale:_ A block slows, not speeds, the pathway.

**MST-0343-Q0002** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) Hypertrophy and hyperplasia are best distinguished in that hypertrophy involves:

- A. An increase in cell size **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: hypertrophy is larger cells; hyperplasia is more cells.
- B. An increase in cell number  
  _Rationale:_ That describes hyperplasia.
- C. Programmed cell death  
  _Rationale:_ That describes apoptosis.
- D. Replacement by a different cell type  
  _Rationale:_ That describes metaplasia.

**MST-0343-Q0003** (multiple-answer-selection, Select TWO) Which TWO describe pharmacokinetic processes?

- A. Absorption of a drug **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: absorption is a pharmacokinetic process.
- B. Elimination (clearance) of a drug **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: elimination is pharmacokinetic.
- C. Receptor binding affinity  
  _Rationale:_ That is pharmacodynamic, not pharmacokinetic.
- D. Dose-response efficacy  
  _Rationale:_ Efficacy is pharmacodynamic.
- E. Mechanism of action at the target  
  _Rationale:_ Mechanism of action is pharmacodynamic.
