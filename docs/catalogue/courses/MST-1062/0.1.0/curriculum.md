## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1062-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What best distinguishes strategic leadership from day-to-day management?

- A. Setting direction and enabling change versus organising existing work well **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: leadership sets direction and drives change; management executes and coordinates current work.
- B. Leadership is only about giving orders  
  _Rationale:_ Leadership is about direction and influence, not merely commands.
- C. Management requires no planning  
  _Rationale:_ Management involves substantial planning and coordination.
- D. They are identical activities  
  _Rationale:_ They overlap but are distinct; the distinction is the point.

**MST-1062-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO practices improve decision-making under uncertainty? (Select TWO)

- A. Judging decisions by their process quality, not only outcomes **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: good processes matter because outcomes are partly luck under uncertainty.
- B. Stress-testing a choice against multiple scenarios **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: scenarios reveal how robust a decision is to different futures.
- C. Avoiding any decision until all uncertainty is removed  
  _Rationale:_ Waiting for certainty is usually impossible and costly.
- D. Rewarding only decisions that happened to succeed  
  _Rationale:_ Outcome-only judgement ignores decision quality and luck.

**MST-1062-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why can a well-formulated strategy still fail?

- A. Execution, alignment and change management are neglected **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: strategy delivers value only when resources, incentives and people are aligned to execute it.
- B. Because formulating strategy is unnecessary  
  _Rationale:_ Formulation matters; the point is that execution also matters.
- C. Because measures are never useful  
  _Rationale:_ Measures help; neglecting them is a cause of failure, not measures themselves.
- D. Because stakeholders should be ignored  
  _Rationale:_ Ignoring stakeholders increases, not reduces, the risk of failure.
