## Learning outcomes

1. Identify where AI supports the content creation workflow
2. Use AI to ideate, draft and repurpose content responsibly
3. Maintain authenticity, voice and quality with AI assistance
4. Recognise copyright, disclosure and platform-policy risks
5. Build a sustainable, honest AI-assisted content process

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1382-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why can over-reliance on default AI drafting hurt a creator over time?

- A. It can flatten a distinctive voice and reduce audience trust **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: generic output can erode the authenticity audiences follow for.
- B. It guarantees the content goes viral  
  _Rationale:_ AI drafting does not guarantee reach.
- C. It makes every post unique automatically  
  _Rationale:_ Default AI output often sounds generic, not unique.
- D. It removes the need for any editing  
  _Rationale:_ Editing is still needed for quality and voice.

**MST-1382-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO practices keep AI-assisted content compliant and trustworthy? (Select TWO.)

- A. Disclose AI-generated media where a platform or audience expects it **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: disclosure meets policy and maintains trust.
- B. Check that AI outputs do not infringe others' work **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: infringement review avoids legal and takedown risk.
- C. Pass off AI-generated media as a real recording  
  _Rationale:_ Misrepresenting synthetic media is deceptive and often against policy.
- D. Ignore platform rules on AI content  
  _Rationale:_ Ignoring rules risks removal or penalties.

**MST-1382-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A creator uses AI to draft a tutorial that states a product does something. Before publishing, they should:

- A. Verify the claim is accurate and add their own tested insight **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: verification and original insight protect accuracy and value.
- B. Publish as-is to post faster  
  _Rationale:_ Unverified claims can mislead the audience.
- C. Assume the AI tested the product  
  _Rationale:_ AI does not test products.
- D. Add hype to make it more engaging  
  _Rationale:_ Hype does not fix an unverified claim.
