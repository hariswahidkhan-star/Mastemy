## Learning outcomes

1. Demonstrate intermediate-to-advanced Korean reading comprehension (knowledge-based)
2. Apply Korean grammar, particles and sentence structure in controlled tasks
3. Demonstrate Korean listening comprehension for detail, gist and intent
4. Describe writing task types and the knowledge needed to plan a response

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: This preparation assesses reading, listening and language-knowledge components and knowledge about writing through MCQ/MR only; it does not assess extended productive writing performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0430-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In Korean, what is the main difference between the topic marker and the subject marker?

- A. The topic marker frames what the sentence is about; the subject marker identifies the grammatical subject **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: they serve different discourse and grammatical roles and are not interchangeable.
- B. They are identical and always interchangeable  
  _Rationale:_ They differ in function and often change nuance.
- C. The subject marker is only used in questions  
  _Rationale:_ The subject marker is not restricted to questions.
- D. The topic marker only appears on verbs  
  _Rationale:_ Markers attach to nouns/noun phrases, not verbs.

**MST-0430-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A learner reads a passage and recognizes only one word in a sentence. What is the best comprehension strategy?

- A. Use surrounding context and sentence structure to infer overall meaning **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: context and structure support comprehension beyond a single word.
- B. Assume the sentence means whatever that one word means  
  _Rationale:_ One word rarely carries the whole sentence's meaning.
- C. Skip the entire passage  
  _Rationale:_ Skipping forfeits comprehension that context could provide.
- D. Translate every word with no attention to grammar  
  _Rationale:_ Ignoring grammar loses meaning that structure conveys.

**MST-0430-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO factors that should guide the choice of verb ending in Korean.

- A. The level of politeness or formality required **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: speech level/honorifics depend on social context.
- B. The relationship and status between speakers **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: relative status shapes the honorific ending chosen.
- C. The color of the paper  
  _Rationale:_ This is irrelevant to verb endings.
- D. The number of pages in the textbook  
  _Rationale:_ This has no bearing on ending choice.
- E. The time of day the sentence is written  
  _Rationale:_ Time of day does not determine the verb ending.
