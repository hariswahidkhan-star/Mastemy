## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the purpose and structure of an environmental management system (EMS)
2. Identify environmental aspects and impacts and evaluate their significance
3. Map legal and other requirements and maintain compliance obligations
4. Design operational controls, objectives and monitoring
5. Run audits, management review and continual improvement of the EMS

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1172-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In an EMS, what is the difference between an environmental aspect and an environmental impact?

- A. The aspect is the element that interacts with the environment; the impact is the resulting change **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: e.g. the aspect is 'solvent emission'; the impact is 'air quality degradation'.
- B. They are two words for the same thing  
  _Rationale:_ They are distinct: cause (aspect) versus effect (impact).
- C. The impact is the activity and the aspect is the result  
  _Rationale:_ This reverses the definitions.
- D. Aspects apply only to emergencies  
  _Rationale:_ Aspects cover normal, abnormal and emergency conditions.

**MST-1172-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO activities belong to the 'Check' stage of the EMS Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle? (Select TWO.)

- A. Internal auditing of the EMS **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: internal audit evaluates conformity and effectiveness in the Check stage.
- B. Monitoring and measuring key environmental parameters **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: monitoring results are a core Check activity.
- C. Setting environmental objectives and targets  
  _Rationale:_ Setting objectives is part of Plan, not Check.
- D. Implementing operational controls on the shop floor  
  _Rationale:_ Carrying out controls is part of Do, not Check.

**MST-1172-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which environmental objective is well formed for an EMS?

- A. Reduce solvent use by 15% within 12 months, owned by the production manager **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it is measurable, time-bound and has a clear owner.
- B. Try to be more environmentally friendly  
  _Rationale:_ It is vague, unmeasurable and has no owner or timeframe.
- C. Comply with all laws at all times  
  _Rationale:_ This is a baseline obligation, not an improvement objective with a target.
- D. Buy a new scrubber someday  
  _Rationale:_ It names an action without a measurable target, date or owner.
