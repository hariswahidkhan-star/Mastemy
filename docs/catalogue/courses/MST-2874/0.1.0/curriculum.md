## Learning outcomes

1. Recognise every letter of the alphabet in upper and lower case
2. Match each letter and common digraph to the sound it makes
3. Blend individual sounds together to read simple words
4. Segment spoken words into their separate sounds for spelling
5. Decode short sentences built from known letter sounds
6. Read common tricky words that do not follow regular spelling patterns

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2874-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the first sound you hear in the word 'sun'?

- A. /s/ **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'sun' begins with the /s/ sound.
- B. /u/  
  _Rationale:_ That is the middle vowel sound, not the first sound.
- C. /n/  
  _Rationale:_ That is the last sound in the word.
- D. /sh/  
  _Rationale:_ 'sun' starts with a single /s/, not the 'sh' digraph.

**MST-2874-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) You blend the sounds d-o-g together. What word do you read?

- A. dog **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: d-o-g blends into 'dog'.
- B. god  
  _Rationale:_ Those sounds in that order make a different word.
- C. dig  
  _Rationale:_ That would need the middle sound /i/, not /o/.
- D. do  
  _Rationale:_ That leaves out the final /g/ sound.

**MST-2874-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO of these are 'tricky' words that you cannot fully sound out? (Select TWO.)

- A. the **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'the' is a common tricky word read by sight.
- B. said **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'said' does not follow regular sounding-out.
- C. cat  
  _Rationale:_ 'cat' is fully decodable by blending c-a-t.
- D. pin  
  _Rationale:_ 'pin' is fully decodable by blending p-i-n.
