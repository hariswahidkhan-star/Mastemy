## Learning outcomes

1. Identify where AI adds value in customer support and where a human must handle the case
2. Use AI to assist drafting replies and knowledge retrieval with verification
3. Apply AI to summarisation, routing and quality monitoring responsibly
4. Recognise tone, bias, hallucination and data-privacy risks in support AI
5. Keep human oversight, escalation and service quality over AI-assisted support

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Hands-on operation of the tools and workflows taught, the quality of live outputs, and professional judgement on the job are taught by video demonstration and model-answer analysis; the certificate assessment uses single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only and never requires uploads, recorded work, graded prompts or peer review.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1213-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which customer-support case is least suitable for an autonomous AI reply?

- A. A distressed customer with a complex complaint and compensation request **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: high-emotion, high-stakes cases need human empathy and judgement.
- B. A routine password-reset instruction request  
  _Rationale:_ Routine, low-risk requests are well suited to AI assistance.
- C. A request for standard opening hours  
  _Rationale:_ Simple factual answers suit AI with verification.
- D. A frequently asked how-to question  
  _Rationale:_ Common how-to questions are a good AI fit.

**MST-1213-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Before sending an AI-drafted reply to a customer, which TWO checks matter most? (Select TWO.)

- A. Confirm the stated policy or fact against the knowledge base **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI can invent policies, so facts must be verified.
- B. Check the tone is appropriate for the customer's situation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: tone must fit the context, especially for upset customers.
- C. Send it faster than any human could  
  _Rationale:_ Speed is not a correctness check and can cause harm.
- D. Assume the AI is always right about policy  
  _Rationale:_ Assuming correctness is exactly the risk to avoid.

**MST-1213-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A team measures only average handling time after adding AI. What is the risk?

- A. Service quality can fall unnoticed because quality is not being measured **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: optimising speed alone can mask declining resolution quality and satisfaction.
- B. Handling time is the only metric that matters  
  _Rationale:_ Quality and resolution matter alongside speed.
- C. AI guarantees quality automatically  
  _Rationale:_ AI does not guarantee quality; it must be measured.
- D. There is no risk to tracking one metric  
  _Rationale:_ A single metric can hide important failures.
