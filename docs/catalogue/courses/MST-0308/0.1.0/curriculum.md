## Learning outcomes

1. Explain integration of supply management across the organization and supply base
2. Apply category management, sourcing and supplier relationship fundamentals
3. Describe risk, sustainability and compliance in supply management
4. Explain finance, metrics and technology supporting integrated supply management

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0308-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) When comparing two supplier offers, the most complete basis for the decision is:

- A. Total cost of ownership across the relationship, not just unit price **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: total cost of ownership captures value beyond unit price.
- B. The lowest unit price alone  
  _Rationale:_ Unit price ignores other costs and risks.
- C. The supplier with the nicest brochure  
  _Rationale:_ Marketing material is not a decision basis.
- D. Whichever supplier replies first  
  _Rationale:_ Response speed alone is not a value measure.

**MST-0308-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A supplier is single-source for a critical part. A sound response is to:

- A. Develop a risk-mitigation plan such as safety stock or a backup source **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: single-source risk needs active mitigation.
- B. Assume the signed contract removes all risk  
  _Rationale:_ A contract does not eliminate supply risk.
- C. Ignore it unless a disruption happens  
  _Rationale:_ Reactive handling leaves exposure.
- D. Stop measuring supplier performance  
  _Rationale:_ Monitoring is part of managing risk.

**MST-0308-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO elements of a sound strategic sourcing process.

- A. Analyzing spend and the supply market first **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: analysis informs the sourcing strategy.
- B. Evaluating suppliers against defined, weighted criteria **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: weighted criteria support objective selection.
- C. Choosing a supplier before defining needs  
  _Rationale:_ Choosing first inverts the process.
- D. Ignoring supply risk entirely  
  _Rationale:_ Risk must be considered.
- E. Selecting purely on brand familiarity  
  _Rationale:_ Familiarity is not an objective criterion.
