## Learning outcomes

1. Explain that traits are inherited through genes
2. Describe the roles of DNA, genes and chromosomes
3. Distinguish dominant and recessive alleles
4. Use a Punnett square to predict simple inheritance
5. Explain the difference between genotype and phenotype
6. Discuss how environment and genes together shape traits

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2870-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A recessive allele shows in the phenotype only when:

- A. Both alleles are recessive **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a recessive trait appears only with two recessive alleles.
- B. One allele is dominant  
  _Rationale:_ A dominant allele would mask the recessive one.
- C. There are no alleles present  
  _Rationale:_ Alleles are always present for a gene.
- D. The organism is an adult  
  _Rationale:_ Age does not determine whether a recessive allele shows.

**MST-2870-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) What does a Punnett square predict?

- A. The probability of offspring genotypes **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a Punnett square shows the likely genotype ratios of offspring.
- B. The exact number of offspring born  
  _Rationale:_ It gives probabilities, not guaranteed counts.
- C. The age an organism will reach  
  _Rationale:_ It says nothing about lifespan.
- D. The colour of the parents' eyes only  
  _Rationale:_ It models inheritance in general, not one fixed trait.

**MST-2870-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO terms describe a genotype with two different alleles and the version that shows? (Select TWO.)

- A. Heterozygous **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: heterozygous means two different alleles.
- B. Dominant allele is expressed **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: in a heterozygote the dominant allele is shown.
- C. Homozygous  
  _Rationale:_ Homozygous means two identical alleles, not different ones.
- D. No alleles are present  
  _Rationale:_ There are always alleles for the gene.
