## Learning outcomes

1. Identify high-value AI use cases across retail and e-commerce
2. Apply AI to recommendations, pricing and demand forecasting
3. Use AI for customer service and content responsibly
4. Interpret AI outputs with attention to bias and customer trust
5. Recognise data, privacy and fairness risks in retail AI

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1387-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An AI recommends a dynamic price change. What should the retailer check before applying it broadly?

- A. The assumptions behind it and any unfair or harmful impact on customers **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: pricing models can rely on flawed assumptions or create unfair outcomes.
- B. Nothing; AI prices are always optimal  
  _Rationale:_ AI prices can be wrong or unfair.
- C. Only whether it increases short-term revenue  
  _Rationale:_ Short-term revenue ignores fairness and trust risks.
- D. Whether competitors would be impressed  
  _Rationale:_ Competitor impression is irrelevant to the decision.

**MST-1387-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO practices support responsible AI use in e-commerce? (Select TWO.)

- A. Use customer data within consent and privacy rules **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: lawful, consented data use protects customers and the business.
- B. Check segmentation and pricing models for unfair bias **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: bias review protects fairness and trust.
- C. Reuse personal data for any new purpose without notice  
  _Rationale:_ Unnotified reuse breaches privacy expectations and rules.
- D. Hide from customers that decisions are automated  
  _Rationale:_ Concealing automation undermines transparency and trust.

**MST-1387-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why should an AI customer-service chatbot include a path to a human agent?

- A. AI can fail or mishandle complex or sensitive cases that need a person **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: escalation protects customers when AI is out of its depth.
- B. Chatbots handle every case perfectly  
  _Rationale:_ Chatbots struggle with complex or sensitive cases.
- C. Human agents are never needed  
  _Rationale:_ Human escalation remains essential.
- D. Escalation always reduces customer satisfaction  
  _Rationale:_ Appropriate escalation usually improves outcomes.
