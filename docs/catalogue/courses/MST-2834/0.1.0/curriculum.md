## Learning outcomes

1. Name the Sun, the eight planets and their order from the Sun
2. Describe the difference between a star, a planet and a moon
3. Explain why we have day and night and a year
4. Compare rocky and gas planets in simple terms
5. Describe the Moon's phases and why the Moon shines
6. Explain how gravity keeps planets orbiting the Sun

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2834-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which object is the star at the centre of our Solar System?

- A. The Sun **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the Sun is a star and sits at the centre of the Solar System.
- B. The Moon  
  _Rationale:_ The Moon is a natural satellite of Earth, not a star.
- C. Mars  
  _Rationale:_ Mars is a planet, not a star.
- D. Earth  
  _Rationale:_ Earth is a planet that orbits the Sun.

**MST-2834-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why does the same place on Earth have day and then night? (Select ONE)

- A. The Earth spins on its axis, turning places toward and away from the Sun **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: Earth's rotation causes day and night.
- B. The Sun turns off at night  
  _Rationale:_ The Sun keeps shining; the Earth turns away.
- C. The Moon covers the Sun every night  
  _Rationale:_ That is not what causes night.
- D. Clouds hide the Sun each evening  
  _Rationale:_ Clouds are not the cause of night.

**MST-2834-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO statements that are true about the Moon. (Select TWO)

- A. It reflects light from the Sun **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the Moon shines by reflecting sunlight.
- B. It appears to change shape through its phases **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: we see phases as the lit part we can see changes.
- C. It is a bright star  
  _Rationale:_ The Moon is not a star; it makes no light of its own.
- D. It is larger than the Sun  
  _Rationale:_ The Sun is vastly larger than the Moon.
