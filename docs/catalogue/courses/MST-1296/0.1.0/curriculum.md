## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the LEED O+M rating system structure and certification process (knowledge practice)
2. Apply credit categories for energy, water and indoor environmental quality in existing buildings
3. Explain sustainable operations, performance tracking and site/materials management
4. Analyse the certification workflow, documentation and performance periods

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1296-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which situation is the correct fit for the LEED Operations and Maintenance rating system?

- A. A building under new ground-up construction  
  _Rationale:_ New construction is addressed by BD+C, not O+M.
- B. An existing, occupied building improving its ongoing operations **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: O+M targets existing, occupied buildings and their operational performance.
- C. An undeveloped greenfield site with no building  
  _Rationale:_ With no building to operate, O+M does not apply.
- D. A product seeking a material ingredient label  
  _Rationale:_ That is a product certification, not building O+M.

**MST-1296-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the primary purpose of a performance period in LEED O+M?

- A. To schedule the construction crew  
  _Rationale:_ O+M concerns operations, not construction scheduling.
- B. To demonstrate actual building performance over a defined span of operation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the performance period captures real operational data to demonstrate ongoing performance.
- C. To choose the paint colour  
  _Rationale:_ Aesthetics are not the purpose of a performance period.
- D. To replace all documentation requirements  
  _Rationale:_ Documentation is still required; the period provides the data.

**MST-1296-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO operational measures support sustainable building operations under O+M?

- A. Implementing a green-cleaning policy with preferred products **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: green-cleaning policies support sustainable operations and IEQ.
- B. Ongoing energy and water performance benchmarking **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: continuous benchmarking is central to O+M performance.
- C. Disabling ventilation to save energy regardless of air quality  
  _Rationale:_ Compromising air quality harms IEQ and is not sustainable operation.
- D. Discarding all monitoring data each month  
  _Rationale:_ Destroying data defeats performance tracking.
