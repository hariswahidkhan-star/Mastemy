## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the regulation of the legal profession and the lawyer-client relationship
2. Apply rules on competence, confidentiality and conflicts of interest
3. Analyse duties to clients, courts, third parties and the public
4. Evaluate issues in fees, advertising, and the integrity of the profession

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1276-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A lawyer learns during trial that the client gave materially false testimony. What is the lawyer's primary obligation?

- A. Do nothing because of confidentiality  
  _Rationale:_ Confidentiality does not override the duty of candor to the tribunal regarding false evidence.
- B. Take reasonable remedial measures, which may include disclosure to the tribunal **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the duty of candor requires reasonable remedial measures when the lawyer learns of material false testimony.
- C. Immediately withdraw and say nothing to anyone  
  _Rationale:_ Withdrawal alone may not cure the fraud on the tribunal; remedial measures are required.
- D. Argue the false testimony more forcefully  
  _Rationale:_ Advancing known false evidence violates candor duties.

**MST-1276-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which situation most clearly permits disclosure of otherwise confidential client information?

- A. The lawyer finds the information embarrassing to the client  
  _Rationale:_ Embarrassment is not a recognised confidentiality exception.
- B. To prevent reasonably certain death or substantial bodily harm **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: preventing reasonably certain death or substantial bodily harm is a recognised exception permitting disclosure.
- C. A journalist asks about the case  
  _Rationale:_ Media interest is not a basis to disclose client confidences.
- D. The client is mildly uncooperative  
  _Rationale:_ Uncooperativeness does not authorise disclosure.

**MST-1276-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO handling practices for client funds are required to maintain professional integrity?

- A. Keeping client funds in a separate client trust account **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: client funds must be held separately in a trust account.
- B. Maintaining complete records of trust-account transactions **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: accurate recordkeeping of trust funds is required.
- C. Mixing client funds with the lawyer's operating account  
  _Rationale:_ Commingling client and lawyer funds is prohibited.
- D. Using client trust funds for office expenses  
  _Rationale:_ Using client funds for the lawyer's expenses is misappropriation.
