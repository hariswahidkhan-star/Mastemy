## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the role of creative direction
2. Develop concepts and creative briefs
3. Guide execution and give creative feedback

- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A creative lead must direct a small campaign across design and video. They must set a creative vision, develop concepts and a clear brief, and guide execution with actionable feedback, defending a brief-led process over ad-hoc taste-based direction.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1877-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A creative brief primarily serves to:

- A. Align the team on objective, audience and constraints **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the brief gives shared direction for execution.
- B. Replace the final creative work  
  _Rationale:_ The brief guides, not replaces, the work.
- C. List the office supplies needed  
  _Rationale:_ Not a creative brief.
- D. Set the company's payroll  
  _Rationale:_ Unrelated.

**MST-1877-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO make creative feedback effective? (Select TWO.)

- A. It is specific and tied to the brief's objective **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: specific, goal-linked feedback is actionable.
- B. It explains the why, not just a personal preference **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reasoning helps the team act on it.
- C. It is vague, like 'make it pop'  
  _Rationale:_ Vague notes are not actionable.
- D. It changes the objective every review  
  _Rationale:_ Shifting goals undermines execution.

**MST-1877-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A common misconception about creative direction is that the director:

- A. Must personally produce all the design and production **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: direction is about guiding others to a vision, not doing everything.
- B. Sets a vision and guides the team  
  _Rationale:_ That is the correct role, not a misconception.
- C. Writes briefs to align the team  
  _Rationale:_ That is good practice, not a misconception.
- D. Gives actionable feedback  
  _Rationale:_ That is correct practice.
