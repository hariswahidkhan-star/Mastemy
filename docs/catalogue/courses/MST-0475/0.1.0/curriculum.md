## Learning outcomes

1. Plan a research task and gather current sources with ChatGPT
2. Verify AI-provided claims and cite sources correctly
3. Synthesise verified findings into a clear, honest summary
4. Apply confidentiality, accuracy and human-review controls to the workflow

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every course outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Hands-on operation of ChatGPT, the quality of live AI outputs, and professional judgement on accepting AI suggestions are taught by video demonstration and model-answer analysis; the certificate assessment uses single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only and never requires uploads, recorded work, graded prompts or peer review.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0475-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) ChatGPT returns an answer with a citation to a study. What should you do before using the claim?

- A. Open the cited source and confirm it says what is claimed **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: citations can be fabricated or misattributed, so the source must be checked directly.
- B. Assume the citation proves the claim  
  _Rationale:_ A citation's presence does not prove it supports the claim; models can invent references.
- C. Ask the model if it is sure  
  _Rationale:_ The model's self-assurance is not independent evidence.
- D. Rephrase the question  
  _Rationale:_ Rephrasing does not verify the original claim.

**MST-0475-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why framing a broad topic into specific sub-questions improves AI research?

- A. It makes gaps and sources easier to target and verify **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: specific questions produce focused, checkable answers and reveal missing evidence.
- B. It guarantees the model will browse  
  _Rationale:_ Sub-questions do not force browsing; that depends on mode and need.
- C. It removes the need to verify  
  _Rationale:_ Verification is still required regardless of how the question is framed.
- D. It makes answers longer  
  _Rationale:_ Length is not the goal; focus and verifiability are.

**MST-0475-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are signs an AI research answer needs more scrutiny? (Select TWO)

- A. Confident claims with no checkable source **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: unsupported confident claims are a classic hallucination risk.
- B. A citation that does not match the claim when opened **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a mismatched citation signals misattribution or fabrication.
- C. A clearly labelled, verifiable source link  
  _Rationale:_ A genuine, checkable source reduces rather than raises concern.
- D. An explicit statement of uncertainty  
  _Rationale:_ Stating uncertainty is honest and does not by itself indicate error.
