## Learning outcomes

1. Define a habitat and give examples
2. Describe the main features of major biomes
3. Explain how animals and plants are suited to their habitat
4. Compare a desert, a rainforest and a polar habitat
5. Identify how living things depend on their habitat
6. Describe simple threats to habitats and biomes

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2863-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which feature helps a cactus survive in the desert?

- A. Storing water in a thick stem **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: cacti store water to survive long dry spells.
- B. Very wide, thin leaves  
  _Rationale:_ Wide leaves lose too much water in a desert.
- C. Thick fur for warmth  
  _Rationale:_ Plants do not have fur; this suits polar animals.
- D. Living underwater  
  _Rationale:_ Cacti live in dry places, not underwater.

**MST-2863-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A rainforest biome is best described as:

- A. Warm and very rainy with many kinds of life **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: rainforests are warm, wet and full of species.
- B. Cold and covered in ice all year  
  _Rationale:_ That describes a polar biome.
- C. Hot, dry and mostly sand  
  _Rationale:_ That describes a desert.
- D. Flat grassland with few trees  
  _Rationale:_ That describes a grassland, not a rainforest.

**MST-2863-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO adaptations help polar animals survive the cold? (Select TWO.)

- A. A thick layer of fat (blubber) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: blubber keeps polar animals warm.
- B. Dense fur or feathers **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: thick fur or feathers trap heat.
- C. Thin skin with no fat  
  _Rationale:_ Thin skin would not keep an animal warm.
- D. Wide leaves to catch sun  
  _Rationale:_ Animals do not have leaves.
