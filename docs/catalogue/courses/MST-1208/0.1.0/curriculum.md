## Learning outcomes

1. Identify where AI supports legal operations and where it must not substitute for legal advice
2. Use AI to assist contract review and clause extraction with human verification
3. Apply AI to legal document drafting and summarisation responsibly
4. Recognise confidentiality, privilege, bias and hallucination risks in legal AI
5. Maintain human oversight, auditability and appropriate escalation to lawyers

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Hands-on operation of the tools and workflows taught, the quality of live outputs, and professional judgement on the job are taught by video demonstration and model-answer analysis; the certificate assessment uses single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only and never requires uploads, recorded work, graded prompts or peer review.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1208-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A client asks whether an AI contract summary counts as legal advice they can rely on. What is correct?

- A. No; AI output is support only and legal advice must come from a qualified lawyer **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI assists but cannot provide reliable legal advice or accept professional responsibility.
- B. Yes, if the tool is advanced enough  
  _Rationale:_ Tool capability does not make output legal advice.
- C. Yes, because the summary is in writing  
  _Rationale:_ Written form does not make something legal advice.
- D. Only if the client agrees in advance  
  _Rationale:_ Client agreement cannot convert AI output into legal advice.

**MST-1208-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO risks are most specific to using AI on legal matters? (Select TWO.)

- A. Loss of privilege if confidential material is entered into an uncontrolled tool **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: privilege and confidentiality are acute risks in legal AI use.
- B. Fabricated case citations that look authentic **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: hallucinated citations are a well-documented legal-AI hazard.
- C. Faster first-draft clause suggestions  
  _Rationale:_ A faster first draft is a benefit, not a risk.
- D. Searchable document indexes  
  _Rationale:_ Indexing is a benefit, not a legal risk.

**MST-1208-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) AI extracts a limitation-of-liability clause from a contract. What is the required next step before relying on it?

- A. Verify the extracted clause against the actual contract text **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI extraction must be checked against the source before reliance.
- B. Rely on the extraction without checking  
  _Rationale:_ Unverified extraction may be wrong or incomplete.
- C. Assume the clause is standard and skip review  
  _Rationale:_ Assuming a standard clause risks missing a non-standard term.
- D. Delete the original contract  
  _Rationale:_ The source must be retained for verification.
