## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the role of the IFRS Foundation, the IASB and the IFRS Interpretations Committee in setting standards
2. Describe the objective of general purpose financial reporting and the primary user groups it serves
3. Apply the qualitative characteristics of useful financial information to reporting choices
4. Define the elements of financial statements and the recognition and derecognition criteria at a conceptual level
5. Distinguish the main measurement bases and the factors relevant to selecting one
6. Relate the reporting entity, going concern and accrual concepts to how statements are prepared

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2509-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under the Conceptual Framework, what is the stated objective of general purpose financial reporting?

- A. To provide financial information useful to existing and potential investors, lenders and other creditors in making decisions about providing resources **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the objective is decision-useful information for the primary users who cannot command information directly.
- B. To calculate the amount of tax the entity must pay in the period  
  _Rationale:_ Tax computation is a jurisdiction-specific exercise, not the objective of general purpose reporting.
- C. To demonstrate management's compliance with the entity's internal budget  
  _Rationale:_ Internal budget compliance is a management-accounting purpose, not the reporting objective.
- D. To value the entity precisely for a potential sale  
  _Rationale:_ Financial statements are not designed to show the entity's market value.

**MST-2509-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO of the following are the fundamental qualitative characteristics of useful financial information under the Conceptual Framework? (Select TWO.)

- A. Relevance **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: relevance is one of the two fundamental qualitative characteristics.
- B. Faithful representation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: faithful representation is the other fundamental qualitative characteristic.
- C. Comparability  
  _Rationale:_ Comparability is an enhancing characteristic, not a fundamental one.
- D. Timeliness  
  _Rationale:_ Timeliness is an enhancing characteristic, not a fundamental one.

**MST-2509-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A finance team argues that because the Conceptual Framework describes how to recognise an asset, it can override a specific IFRS Standard that gives different guidance. Why is that reasoning unsound?

- A. The Framework is not a Standard; where a specific Standard applies, its requirements take precedence over the Framework **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the Framework assists the Board and preparers but does not override a specific Standard.
- B. The Framework applies only to listed entities  
  _Rationale:_ The Framework's scope is not limited to listed entities; the issue is its status relative to Standards.
- C. The Framework has been withdrawn and no longer has any status  
  _Rationale:_ The Framework remains in force; it simply does not override Standards.
- D. Only auditors may apply the Framework  
  _Rationale:_ Preparers also use the Framework; the real point is precedence of a specific Standard.
