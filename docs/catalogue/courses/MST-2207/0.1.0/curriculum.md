## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the drivers and principles of sustainable building
2. Distinguish operational and embodied carbon and the whole-life view
3. Describe passive design, energy efficiency and renewable strategies
4. Relate material choice, water and waste to environmental impact
5. Outline how green rating systems and performance targets work at a high level
6. Evaluate green claims critically and avoid greenwashing

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2207-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A team adds rooftop solar panels to a poorly insulated, draughty building and calls it green. What is the main flaw in this approach?

- A. Energy demand should be reduced first; adding renewables to a wasteful building is inefficient **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reduce demand before adding supply (fabric first).
- B. Solar panels never generate any energy  
  _Rationale:_ Solar panels do generate energy; the issue is unreduced demand.
- C. Insulation and solar are unrelated to energy  
  _Rationale:_ Both directly affect energy performance.
- D. Green buildings must never use electricity  
  _Rationale:_ Green buildings use energy; the aim is to use it efficiently.

**MST-2207-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO contribute to a building's embodied carbon? (Select TWO.)

- A. Manufacturing of the structural materials **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: material manufacture is a major embodied-carbon source.
- B. Transport of materials to site **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: transport of materials adds to embodied carbon.
- C. Electricity used by occupants after handover  
  _Rationale:_ Occupant electricity is operational, not embodied, carbon.
- D. Heating the building in winter  
  _Rationale:_ Space heating is operational carbon, not embodied.

**MST-2207-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is greenwashing in the context of a building project?

- A. Making sustainability claims that are exaggerated or unsupported by evidence **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: greenwashing is unsubstantiated or misleading green claims.
- B. Painting the exterior walls green  
  _Rationale:_ Colour choice is not greenwashing.
- C. Washing the windows with rainwater  
  _Rationale:_ That is a maintenance practice, not greenwashing.
- D. Achieving genuine, evidenced performance targets  
  _Rationale:_ Honest, evidenced performance is the opposite of greenwashing.
