## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the structure and classification of bacteria, viruses, fungi and parasites
2. Explain microbial growth, metabolism and mechanisms of pathogenicity
3. Explain host defences, the normal microbiota and host-microbe interaction
4. Describe diagnostic microbiology, antimicrobials and resistance

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every course outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Hands-on operation, the quality of tools and live outputs, and professional judgement are taught by video demonstration and model-answer analysis; the certificate assessment uses single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only and never requires uploads, recorded work, submitted code or peer review.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1767-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A Gram stain shows purple cocci in clusters. The purple colour indicates the organisms are:

- A. Gram-positive, with a thick peptidoglycan cell wall **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: Gram-positive cells retain the crystal violet stain because of their thick peptidoglycan wall.
- B. Gram-negative, with a thin peptidoglycan layer  
  _Rationale:_ Gram-negative cells lose the crystal violet and appear pink/red, not purple.
- C. Viruses, which have no cell wall  
  _Rationale:_ Viruses are not visualised by Gram stain and have no peptidoglycan.
- D. Fungi with chitin walls  
  _Rationale:_ Fungal walls contain chitin and are not classified by the Gram reaction.**MST-1767-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why are antibiotics ineffective against the common cold?

- A. The cold is caused by viruses, which lack the bacterial targets antibiotics act on **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: antibiotics target bacterial structures and processes that viruses do not possess.
- B. The dose of antibiotic is always too low  
  _Rationale:_ The problem is the target, not the dose; viruses lack the bacterial targets entirely.
- C. Cold viruses are resistant forms of bacteria  
  _Rationale:_ Viruses are not bacteria and are not a resistant bacterial form.
- D. Antibiotics only work after symptoms resolve  
  _Rationale:_ Timing is not the issue; antibiotics have no antiviral mechanism here.**MST-1767-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are links in the chain of infection that an intervention could break? (Select TWO)

- A. Mode of transmission **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: interrupting transmission, for example by hand hygiene, breaks the chain.
- B. Portal of entry **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: protecting the portal of entry, for example by wound dressing, breaks the chain.
- C. Gram reaction of the organism  
  _Rationale:_ The Gram reaction is a classification feature, not a link in the chain of infection.
- D. The antibiotic's half-life  
  _Rationale:_ Half-life is a pharmacokinetic property, not a link in the chain of infection.
