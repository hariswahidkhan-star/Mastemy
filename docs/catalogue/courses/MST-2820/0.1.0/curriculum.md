## Learning outcomes

1. Understand and simplify ratios
2. Divide a quantity into a given ratio
3. Recognise and use direct proportion
4. Convert between fractions, decimals and percentages
5. Find a percentage of an amount
6. Solve simple percentage increase and decrease problems

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2820-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Simplify the ratio 10:15.

- A. 2:3 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: dividing both parts by 5 gives 2:3.
- B. 5:10  
  _Rationale:_ Both parts can still be divided; the simplest form is 2:3.
- C. 10:15  
  _Rationale:_ This is not simplified; both share a factor of 5.
- D. 3:2  
  _Rationale:_ That reverses the order; 10:15 simplifies to 2:3.

**MST-2820-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO amounts that are equal to 50%. (Select TWO.)

- A. 1/2 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: one half is 50%.
- B. 0.5 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 0.5 is the decimal for 50%.
- C. 1/4  
  _Rationale:_ One quarter is 25%, not 50%.
- D. 0.05  
  _Rationale:_ 0.05 is 5%, not 50%.

**MST-2820-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is 20% of 150?

- A. 30 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 10% of 150 is 15, so 20% is 30.
- B. 20  
  _Rationale:_ 20 is the percentage, not 20% of 150.
- C. 300  
  _Rationale:_ That multiplies by 2; 20% is one fifth, which is 30.
- D. 15  
  _Rationale:_ 15 is 10% of 150; 20% is double that, 30.
