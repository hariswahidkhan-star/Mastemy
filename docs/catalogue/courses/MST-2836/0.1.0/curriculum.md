## Learning outcomes

1. Define ecosystem, habitat, population and community
2. Build food chains and food webs using producers and consumers
3. Explain energy flow and loss between trophic levels
4. Describe the roles of producers, consumers and decomposers
5. Predict effects on a web when one population changes
6. Explain how organisms are adapted to their habitat

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A nature reserve team studies a meadow where rabbit numbers have surged after foxes declined: they map the food web, use an energy pyramid to explain the shortage of top predators, predict knock-on effects on plants and insects, and propose a balanced management plan.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2836-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In a food chain, which organism is the producer?

- A. A grass plant that makes its own food by photosynthesis **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: producers make their own food and begin the chain.
- B. A fox that eats rabbits  
  _Rationale:_ A fox is a consumer, not a producer.
- C. A mushroom breaking down dead leaves  
  _Rationale:_ That is a decomposer.
- D. A rabbit that eats grass  
  _Rationale:_ A rabbit is a primary consumer.

**MST-2836-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why are there usually fewer top predators than plants in an ecosystem? (Select ONE)

- A. Energy is lost at each step, so less is available higher up the chain **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: energy transfer is inefficient, limiting higher levels.
- B. Predators choose to live alone for no reason  
  _Rationale:_ It is energy availability, not preference.
- C. Plants eat the predators  
  _Rationale:_ Plants do not eat predators.
- D. Energy increases at each step up the chain  
  _Rationale:_ Energy decreases, not increases, up the chain.

**MST-2836-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO roles that decomposers play in an ecosystem. (Select TWO)

- A. Breaking down dead organisms **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: decomposers break down dead material.
- B. Returning nutrients to the soil **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: they recycle nutrients for producers to reuse.
- C. Producing food from sunlight for the whole web  
  _Rationale:_ That is the role of producers, not decomposers.
- D. Hunting the largest predators for energy  
  _Rationale:_ Decomposers do not hunt top predators.
