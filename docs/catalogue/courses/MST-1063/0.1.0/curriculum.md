## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1063-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the key mindset shift when moving from individual contributor to manager?

- A. Success is measured through the team's outcomes, not only your own output **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a manager delivers through others, so team results become the measure of success.
- B. You should personally do the hardest tasks yourself  
  _Rationale:_ That undermines delegation and the team's growth.
- C. Your individual output matters more than before  
  _Rationale:_ The emphasis shifts from personal output to enabling the team.
- D. People management can be ignored if you are technically strong  
  _Rationale:_ Technical skill does not replace managing people.

**MST-1063-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO describe effective performance feedback? (Select TWO)

- A. It is specific about the behaviour and its impact **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: specific, behaviour-and-impact feedback is actionable.
- B. It is given close in time to the event **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: timely feedback is more useful than delayed feedback.
- C. It is saved up for the annual review  
  _Rationale:_ Delaying feedback reduces its value and fairness.
- D. It focuses on personality rather than behaviour  
  _Rationale:_ Feedback on fixed traits is less actionable and more defensive.

**MST-1063-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) What does psychological safety on a team primarily enable?

- A. People speak up with ideas, questions and mistakes without fear of punishment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: psychological safety lets people raise concerns and admit errors, improving learning.
- B. Managers avoid giving any critical feedback  
  _Rationale:_ Safety supports candour, it does not remove accountability.
- C. Teams never disagree  
  _Rationale:_ Healthy disagreement is a sign of safety, not its absence.
- D. Performance standards are lowered  
  _Rationale:_ Safety and high standards coexist.
