## Learning outcomes

1. Read the three primary financial statements
2. Interpret profitability, liquidity and leverage ratios
3. Explain the difference between profit and cash
4. Apply basic investment appraisal to a decision
5. Explain budgeting, cost behaviour and variance
6. Connect financial metrics to management decisions

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A department head receives a budget pack, a profitable monthly P&L, and a warning from finance about cash. They must explain to their team why profit and cash differ, read the relevant ratios, and choose between two small investments using simple appraisal, then present the reasoning to the finance director.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1055-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A company reports a healthy profit yet cannot pay suppliers. The most likely explanation is:

- A. Cash is tied up in receivables and inventory, so profit is not yet cash **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: accrual profit differs from cash due to working-capital timing.
- B. Profit and cash are always identical, so the report is wrong  
  _Rationale:_ Profit and cash routinely differ.
- C. The company has no expenses  
  _Rationale:_ Irrelevant to the profit-versus-cash gap.
- D. Depreciation increased the bank balance  
  _Rationale:_ Depreciation is a non-cash expense; it does not add cash.

**MST-1055-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO statements about the primary financial statements are correct? (Select TWO.)

- A. The balance sheet is a snapshot at a point in time **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it reports position on a date.
- B. The cash flow statement shows movements of cash over a period **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it explains the change in cash.
- C. The income statement reports the closing bank balance  
  _Rationale:_ That is not what the income statement shows.
- D. The balance sheet reports revenue for the year  
  _Rationale:_ Revenue is on the income statement.

**MST-1055-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A manager must choose between two projects returning the same total cash but at different times. All else equal, finance theory prefers the one that:

- A. Returns cash sooner, because earlier cash is worth more **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the time value of money favours earlier cash.
- B. Returns cash later, to defer effort  
  _Rationale:_ Later cash is worth less in present-value terms.
- C. Has the larger number of line items  
  _Rationale:_ Irrelevant to value.
- D. Is championed by the most senior manager  
  _Rationale:_ Not a financial criterion.
