## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the regulation of broker-dealers and their agents under state law
2. Explain the regulation of investment advisers and their representatives under state law
3. Describe the regulation of securities and issuers, including registration and exemptions
4. Apply ethical practices, fiduciary obligations and prohibited conduct under the Uniform Securities Act

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0074-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under the Uniform Securities Act, what standard of care does an investment adviser owe its clients?

- A. Suitability only  
  _Rationale:_ Suitability is the broker-dealer standard; advisers owe more.
- B. A fiduciary duty to act in the client's best interest **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: investment advisers are held to a fiduciary standard and must place client interests first.
- C. No duty beyond honesty  
  _Rationale:_ Advisers owe an affirmative fiduciary duty.
- D. A duty only to disclose fees  
  _Rationale:_ Fee disclosure is part of, but not the whole, fiduciary duty.

**MST-0074-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which is an example of an exempt transaction rather than an exempt security?

- A. A US Treasury bond  
  _Rationale:_ That is an exempt security, not a transaction.
- B. An isolated non-issuer sale between two individuals **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an isolated non-issuer transaction is a classic exempt transaction under the Act.
- C. A municipal bond  
  _Rationale:_ That is an exempt security.
- D. A security issued by a bank  
  _Rationale:_ That is an exempt security.

**MST-0074-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO actions by an agent are prohibited under the Uniform Securities Act? (Select TWO)

- A. Guaranteeing a customer against loss **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: guaranteeing a customer against loss is a prohibited practice.
- B. Effecting unauthorized transactions in a customer account **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: making unauthorized trades is prohibited.
- C. Recommending a security that is suitable and disclosed  
  _Rationale:_ Suitable, disclosed recommendations are permitted.
- D. Providing a prospectus to a client  
  _Rationale:_ Delivering required disclosures is permitted and expected.
- E. Charging a disclosed advisory fee  
  _Rationale:_ A disclosed, reasonable fee is permitted.
