## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the role of financial planning and analysis as a business partner
2. Build budgets, forecasts and driver-based models
3. Analyse variances and explain performance to the business
4. Design management reports, KPIs and dashboards
5. Support decisions with scenario and sensitivity analysis

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1713-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the main advantage of a driver-based forecast over a line-item forecast?

- A. It links outputs to business assumptions you can test and update **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: drivers make the forecast explainable and quick to flex.
- B. It is always more accurate in every period  
  _Rationale:_ No forecast guarantees accuracy; drivers aid insight and agility.
- C. It removes the need for actuals  
  _Rationale:_ Actuals are still needed to validate drivers.
- D. It eliminates uncertainty  
  _Rationale:_ It frames uncertainty; it does not remove it.

**MST-1713-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Revenue was below plan. Which TWO analyses best isolate why? (Select TWO.)

- A. A price-versus-volume variance decomposition **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it separates rate effects from quantity effects.
- B. A customer or product mix analysis **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: shifts in mix can move revenue even at planned prices and volumes.
- C. Recomputing last year's statutory accounts  
  _Rationale:_ Historical statutory accounts do not explain this period's miss.
- D. Changing the chart of accounts  
  _Rationale:_ That is a data structure change, not variance analysis.

**MST-1713-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A board pack shows 40 metrics with no commentary. The main FP&A weakness is:

- A. No prioritisation or narrative to support decisions **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: effective reporting highlights the few metrics that drive decisions and explains them.
- B. Too few metrics to be useful  
  _Rationale:_ The problem is too many, unprioritised metrics.
- C. Using KPIs at all  
  _Rationale:_ KPIs are appropriate; the issue is focus and narrative.
- D. Including any leading indicators  
  _Rationale:_ Leading indicators are valuable when chosen deliberately.
