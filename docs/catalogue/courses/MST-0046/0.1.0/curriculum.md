## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0046-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under the accruals (matching) concept, how should rent paid in advance for the next accounting period be treated at the year end?

- A. Carried forward as a prepayment (current asset) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: rent relating to a future period is a prepayment and is carried forward as an asset.
- B. Charged in full to this year's profit or loss  
  _Rationale:_ Charging it all this year breaches the matching concept.
- C. Recorded as a payable (liability)  
  _Rationale:_ It has been paid, so it is not a liability; it is a prepaid asset.
- D. Ignored until it is used  
  _Rationale:_ The accruals concept requires recognising the prepayment now.

**MST-0046-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A receivables ledger control account is used primarily to:

- A. Provide a total check on the individual customer accounts in the receivables ledger **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the control account's balance should agree with the sum of the individual receivable balances, giving a control check.
- B. Record cash sales only  
  _Rationale:_ Cash sales do not create receivables; the control account summarises credit customers.
- C. Replace the need for individual customer accounts  
  _Rationale:_ Individual accounts are still kept; the control account summarises them.
- D. Calculate depreciation on assets  
  _Rationale:_ Depreciation is unrelated to the receivables control account.

**MST-0046-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO entries required to record the year's depreciation charge on a non-current asset held at cost.

- A. Debit depreciation expense in profit or loss **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the charge is an expense debited to profit or loss.
- B. Credit accumulated depreciation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the credit increases the accumulated depreciation (a contra-asset).
- C. Credit the bank account  
  _Rationale:_ Depreciation is a non-cash adjustment; no cash moves.
- D. Debit the asset cost account  
  _Rationale:_ The cost account is unchanged; depreciation accumulates separately.
- E. Credit sales revenue  
  _Rationale:_ Depreciation has nothing to do with revenue.
