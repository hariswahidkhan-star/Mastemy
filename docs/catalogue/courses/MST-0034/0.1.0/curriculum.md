## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0034-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) When a parent acquires a subsidiary, goodwill at acquisition is calculated as:

- A. Consideration plus non-controlling interest, less the fair value of net identifiable assets **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: goodwill is the excess of consideration plus NCI over the fair value of net identifiable assets acquired.
- B. Consideration less the book value of the subsidiary's share capital  
  _Rationale:_ Book value and share capital alone ignore fair values and NCI.
- C. The subsidiary's retained earnings at acquisition  
  _Rationale:_ Retained earnings are not goodwill; goodwill is a residual over net assets.
- D. Consideration less the subsidiary's total liabilities  
  _Rationale:_ Liabilities alone are not the measurement base for goodwill.

**MST-0034-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) An entity has a taxable temporary difference because an asset's carrying amount exceeds its tax base. This gives rise to:

- A. A deferred tax liability **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a taxable temporary difference (carrying amount above tax base) creates a deferred tax liability.
- B. A deferred tax asset  
  _Rationale:_ A deductible temporary difference, not a taxable one, creates a deferred tax asset.
- C. A current tax refund  
  _Rationale:_ Temporary differences affect deferred tax, not an immediate current refund.
- D. No tax effect  
  _Rationale:_ A taxable temporary difference does have a deferred tax effect.

**MST-0034-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO items that are typically eliminated on consolidation of a parent and its subsidiary.

- A. Intragroup sales and purchases between the two entities **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: intragroup trading is eliminated to avoid double counting.
- B. Unrealised profit in closing inventory from intragroup sales **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: unrealised profit on inventory still held within the group is eliminated.
- C. Sales made by the subsidiary to external customers  
  _Rationale:_ Genuine external sales are not eliminated.
- D. The parent's dividend paid to its own external shareholders  
  _Rationale:_ External dividends are not intragroup items.
- E. Salaries paid to the group's employees  
  _Rationale:_ Employee salaries are normal expenses, not intragroup eliminations.
