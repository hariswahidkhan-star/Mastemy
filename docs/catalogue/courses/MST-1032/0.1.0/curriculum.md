## Module and lesson plan

### M01 — The month-end close  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 26%)_

- M01L01 — Purpose of the close and the close calendar (120 min)
- M01L02 — The close checklist, cut-off and roles (120 min)
- Worked applications: Build a day-by-day close calendar for a small finance team; Apply cut-off to decide which invoices belong in the period
- Common misconception addressed: Treating the close as a single end-of-month task rather than a planned process

### M02 — Accruals and adjustments  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 26%)_

- M02L01 — Accruals, prepayments and deferrals (120 min)
- M02L02 — Journal entries, accruals reversal and provisions (120 min)
- Worked applications: Post an accrual for services received but not yet invoiced; Record and reverse a prepayment across two periods
- Common misconception addressed: Forgetting to reverse an accrual, causing a double count next period

### M03 — Balance-sheet reconciliation  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 24%)_

- M03L01 — Reconciliation fundamentals and supporting evidence (120 min)
- M03L02 — Bank, control-account and subledger reconciliations (120 min)
- Worked applications: Reconcile the bank balance to the ledger and list reconciling items; Tie an accounts-receivable control account to its subledger
- Common misconception addressed: Calling a reconciliation complete when the balances match but items are unexplained

### M04 — Review, controls and reporting  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 24%)_

- M04L01 — Reconciling-item clearance and review (120 min)
- M04L02 — Controls, segregation of duties and the close package (120 min)
- Worked applications: Clear an aged reconciling item and document the resolution; Assemble a review-ready close package with reconciliations and sign-offs
- Common misconception addressed: Assuming one person preparing and approving entries is acceptable control

## Integrative case

Run a disciplined month-end close for a company: build a close calendar and checklist, post accruals and prepayments, reconcile key balance-sheet accounts with evidence, clear reconciling items, and hand off a review-ready close package.
