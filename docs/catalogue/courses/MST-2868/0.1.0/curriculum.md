## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the parts of a plant and their functions
2. Explain the process of photosynthesis in simple terms
3. Identify what plants need for photosynthesis
4. Describe how water and minerals move through a plant
5. Explain the role of plants in producing oxygen
6. Relate photosynthesis to food chains and the environment

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2868-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which gas do plants take IN during photosynthesis?

- A. Carbon dioxide **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: plants take in carbon dioxide to make food.
- B. Oxygen  
  _Rationale:_ Oxygen is released, not taken in, during photosynthesis.
- C. Nitrogen  
  _Rationale:_ Nitrogen is not the gas used in photosynthesis.
- D. Helium  
  _Rationale:_ Helium plays no part in photosynthesis.

**MST-2868-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which green substance in leaves captures light energy?

- A. Chlorophyll **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: chlorophyll captures light energy for photosynthesis.
- B. Starch  
  _Rationale:_ Starch is a stored food, not the light-capturing substance.
- C. Water  
  _Rationale:_ Water is a raw material but does not capture light.
- D. Oxygen  
  _Rationale:_ Oxygen is a product, not the light-capturing pigment.

**MST-2868-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO things does a plant need to carry out photosynthesis? (Select TWO.)

- A. Light **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: light energy drives photosynthesis.
- B. Water **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: water is a raw material for photosynthesis.
- C. Darkness  
  _Rationale:_ Photosynthesis needs light, not darkness.
- D. Salt  
  _Rationale:_ Salt is not a raw material for photosynthesis.
