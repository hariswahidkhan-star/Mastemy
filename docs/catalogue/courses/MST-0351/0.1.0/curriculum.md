## Learning outcomes

1. Describe clinical competencies including patient care and procedures
2. Describe administrative competencies including scheduling and records
3. Explain general competencies including communication, law and ethics
4. Apply safety, infection control and professionalism in the medical office

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: official item formats not reproducible as MCQ/MR (for example performance, speaking, essay, hands-on or simulation tasks) are not reproduced here; see the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0351-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which action is a core element of standard (universal) precautions in the medical office?

- A. Performing hand hygiene before and after patient contact **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: hand hygiene before and after contact is foundational to standard precautions.
- B. Wearing gloves only for patients known to have an infection  
  _Rationale:_ Standard precautions apply to all patients, not only known infections.
- C. Recapping used needles by hand to save sharps space  
  _Rationale:_ Recapping by hand is unsafe and contrary to sharps-safety practice.
- D. Reusing single-use items between patients  
  _Rationale:_ Single-use items must not be reused between patients.

**MST-0351-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A patient asks the medical assistant to release their records to a relative. What is the appropriate first step?

- A. Obtain the patient's written authorisation before releasing records **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: protected health information requires the patient's authorisation before release.
- B. Release the records immediately to any family member  
  _Rationale:_ Records cannot be released to family without proper authorisation.
- C. Refuse all record requests permanently  
  _Rationale:_ Records can be released with proper authorisation; blanket refusal is incorrect.
- D. Post the records where the relative can see them  
  _Rationale:_ Exposing records breaches confidentiality.

**MST-0351-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO measurements that are part of a standard set of vital signs.

- A. Blood pressure **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: blood pressure is a standard vital sign.
- B. Body temperature **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: temperature is a standard vital sign.
- C. The patient's home address  
  _Rationale:_ An address is demographic data, not a vital sign.
- D. The patient's insurance plan name  
  _Rationale:_ Insurance information is administrative, not a vital sign.
- E. The patient's occupation  
  _Rationale:_ Occupation is social history, not a vital sign.
