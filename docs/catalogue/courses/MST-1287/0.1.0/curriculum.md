## Learning outcomes

1. Apply assessment and prioritisation to adult medical-surgical patients (knowledge practice)
2. Describe management of common medical-surgical conditions by body system
3. Explain safe medication administration, perioperative and patient-safety practices
4. Analyse professional practice, patient education and care coordination

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1287-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Four patients need attention at once. Which should the nurse assess first?

- A. A patient requesting a warm blanket  
  _Rationale:_ Comfort requests are low priority relative to physiologic instability.
- B. A patient with new-onset shortness of breath and falling oxygen saturation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a threatened airway/breathing problem is the highest priority (ABC).
- C. A patient asking about the lunch menu  
  _Rationale:_ A non-urgent question is low priority.
- D. A stable patient due for routine vitals in an hour  
  _Rationale:_ Routine scheduled care is not the immediate priority.

**MST-1287-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Before administering a high-alert medication, which safety practice is most important?

- A. Administer quickly to save time  
  _Rationale:_ Speed over verification increases error risk for high-alert drugs.
- B. Perform an independent double-check and verify the rights of administration **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: independent double-checks and verifying the rights reduce high-alert medication errors.
- C. Rely on memory of a similar patient  
  _Rationale:_ Memory of another patient is not a safety verification.
- D. Skip identity checks for known patients  
  _Rationale:_ Patient identification must always be verified.

**MST-1287-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO tasks may a registered nurse appropriately delegate to unlicensed assistive personnel?

- A. Measuring and recording routine vital signs on a stable patient **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: routine vitals on stable patients are commonly delegable.
- B. Assisting a stable patient with hygiene and ambulation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: basic ADL assistance for stable patients is delegable.
- C. Performing the initial assessment of a newly admitted unstable patient  
  _Rationale:_ Initial assessment of an unstable patient requires the RN.
- D. Developing the nursing care plan  
  _Rationale:_ Care planning is a professional RN responsibility, not delegable.
