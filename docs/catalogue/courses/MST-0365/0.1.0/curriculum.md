## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0365-Q0001** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) An occupational therapist receives a new referral. According to the OT process, the first step is to:

- A. Screen and evaluate to gather information **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: evaluation precedes intervention in the OT process.
- B. Begin intervention immediately  
  _Rationale:_ Intervention before evaluation is not client-centred or safe.
- C. Discharge the client  
  _Rationale:_ Discharge is the final, not first, step.
- D. Order equipment first  
  _Rationale:_ Equipment follows assessment of need.

**MST-0365-Q0002** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) When setting goals with a client, goals should primarily be:

- A. Client-centred and meaningful to the client **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: OT goals are grounded in the client's valued occupations.
- B. Decided solely by the therapist  
  _Rationale:_ Excluding the client is not client-centred.
- C. Identical for all clients with the condition  
  _Rationale:_ Goals must be individualised.
- D. Based only on reimbursement limits  
  _Rationale:_ Reimbursement does not define therapeutic goals.

**MST-0365-Q0003** (multiple-answer-selection, Select TWO) Which TWO reflect ethical, competent OT practice?

- A. Documenting only services actually provided **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: honest documentation is an ethical requirement.
- B. Reassessing before continuing an intervention **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ongoing reassessment guides safe, effective care.
- C. Billing for undelivered services  
  _Rationale:_ This is fraudulent and unethical.
- D. Practising beyond one's competence  
  _Rationale:_ Therapists must work within their competence.
- E. Ignoring client safety to meet a schedule  
  _Rationale:_ Safety must not be compromised.
