## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the purpose, audiences and main frameworks for ESG reporting
2. Apply materiality to decide what to report
3. Define ESG metrics and manage their data quality and lineage
4. Assemble a sustainability report aligned to a chosen framework
5. Prepare ESG data for assurance and avoid greenwashing

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1173-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What does 'double materiality' add beyond traditional financial materiality in ESG reporting?

- A. It also considers the organisation's impacts on society and the environment, not only impacts on the company **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: double materiality covers both outward impact and financial effect on the company.
- B. It requires reporting every topic twice  
  _Rationale:_ It is not about duplication; it is two lenses on relevance.
- C. It removes the need for a materiality assessment  
  _Rationale:_ It shapes, not removes, the assessment.
- D. It applies only to financial statements  
  _Rationale:_ It specifically extends beyond financial-only relevance.

**MST-1173-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO practices most improve the credibility of an ESG metric? (Select TWO.)

- A. Documenting the metric's boundary and calculation method **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a clear definition and boundary makes the figure comparable and checkable.
- B. Maintaining data lineage from source system to reported value **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: traceability lets the number be verified and assured.
- C. Rounding figures generously to look better  
  _Rationale:_ Distorting figures undermines credibility and risks greenwashing.
- D. Reporting only the metrics that show improvement  
  _Rationale:_ Selective disclosure misleads users and damages trust.

**MST-1173-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which statement best avoids greenwashing?

- A. 'We cut Scope 1 and 2 emissions 12% versus a 2022 baseline, externally assured' **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it is specific, scoped, baselined and evidenced by assurance.
- B. 'We are a green, eco-friendly company'  
  _Rationale:_ A vague, unevidenced claim is classic greenwashing.
- C. 'Our products are 100% sustainable'  
  _Rationale:_ An absolute, undefined claim cannot be substantiated.
- D. 'We care deeply about the planet'  
  _Rationale:_ Sentiment without evidence or metrics is not a credible disclosure.
