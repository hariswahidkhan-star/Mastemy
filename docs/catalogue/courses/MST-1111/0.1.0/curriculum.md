## Learning outcomes

1. Plan a LinkedIn advertising account and B2B campaign structure
2. Build and target audiences for B2B demand generation
3. Design ad formats and creative for the buying committee
4. Measure, attribute and optimise B2B campaign performance

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A B2B SaaS company with a long sales cycle must launch a LinkedIn demand-generation programme. The marketer must structure the account, define target audiences and accounts, pick ad formats for a multi-role buying committee, set conversion tracking, and defend a budget plan to the CMO on cost-per-qualified-lead rather than clicks.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1111-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) The main reason LinkedIn advertising suits B2B over broad consumer platforms is that it allows targeting by:

- A. Professional attributes such as job title, function and company **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: LinkedIn's profile data enables firmographic and role targeting.
- B. Household income brackets only  
  _Rationale:_ That is a consumer-demographic signal, not LinkedIn's B2B strength.
- C. Television viewing habits  
  _Rationale:_ Not a LinkedIn targeting dimension.
- D. Retail loyalty-card history  
  _Rationale:_ Not available on LinkedIn.

**MST-1111-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO practices improve account-based B2B campaigns on LinkedIn? (Select TWO.)

- A. Upload a prioritised target-account list for ABM targeting **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: matched company lists focus spend on named accounts.
- B. Tailor creative to the role of each committee member **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: economic, technical and user buyers respond to different messages.
- C. Use a single generic ad for every seniority level  
  _Rationale:_ One message rarely fits the whole committee.
- D. Judge success only by immediate click-through rate  
  _Rationale:_ B2B value is in qualified pipeline, not clicks.

**MST-1111-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A LinkedIn conversion window defines:

- A. The period after an ad interaction within which a conversion is credited **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it bounds attribution of conversions to the ad.
- B. The maximum daily budget for a campaign  
  _Rationale:_ That is a budget setting, not attribution.
- C. The number of audiences allowed per campaign  
  _Rationale:_ Unrelated to attribution windows.
- D. The creative refresh frequency  
  _Rationale:_ Not what a conversion window controls.
