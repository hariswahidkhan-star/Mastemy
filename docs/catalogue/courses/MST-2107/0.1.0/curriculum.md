## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the major pre-Columbian civilisations and their achievements
2. Explain the Spanish and Portuguese conquest and colonial systems
3. Analyse colonial society, economy and the Columbian Exchange
4. Evaluate the independence movements of the early 19th century
5. Explain nation-building, export economies and political instability after independence
6. Assess revolution, dictatorship, democracy and development in the 20th century

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2107-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why is it inaccurate to say the Spanish conquered the Americas 'simply because of superior weapons'?

- A. Epidemic disease, Indigenous allies and internal divisions were decisive alongside weaponry **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: demographic collapse and local alliances were central, not weapons alone.
- B. Because the Spanish had no weapons  
  _Rationale:_ They had weapons; the point is they were not the sole factor.
- C. Because there was no conquest  
  _Rationale:_ Conquest occurred; the issue is explaining it fully.
- D. Because Indigenous states did not exist  
  _Rationale:_ Large states existed and shaped events.

**MST-2107-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO were major consequences of the Columbian Exchange for the Americas? (Select TWO.)

- A. Catastrophic population decline among Indigenous peoples from new diseases **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: introduced epidemics caused massive mortality.
- B. The introduction of new crops and animals that reshaped economies **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: exchanged species transformed agriculture and diets.
- C. The immediate industrialisation of the Andes  
  _Rationale:_ Industrialisation did not follow immediately.
- D. The disappearance of all long-distance trade  
  _Rationale:_ Trade continued and was reorganised, not ended.

**MST-2107-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A student writes that independence 'instantly created stable democracies' across Latin America. What is wrong?

- A. New states faced instability, caudillo rule and contested power long after independence **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: independence was followed by prolonged political struggle, not instant stable democracy.
- B. No countries became independent  
  _Rationale:_ Many did; the issue is what followed.
- C. Independence happened only in the 20th century  
  _Rationale:_ Most came in the early 19th century.
- D. There were no constitutions written  
  _Rationale:_ Constitutions were written, but stability was elusive.
