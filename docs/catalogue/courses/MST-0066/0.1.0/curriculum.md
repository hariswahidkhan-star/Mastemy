## Learning outcomes

1. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Seeks business for the broker-dealer from customers and potential customers' to exam-style problems
2. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Opens accounts after obtaining and evaluating customers' financial profile and investment objectives' to exam-style problems
3. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Provides customers with information about investments, makes suitable recommendations, transfers assets and maintains appropriate records' to exam-style problems
4. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Obtains and verifies customers' purchase and sales instructions and agreements, processes, completes and confirms transactions' to exam-style problems

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Official FINRA examinations may use item formats (for example scenario, performance-based or constructed-response tasks) that this platform's MCQ/MR forms do not reproduce; see the exam-version record. Domain structure here is a design assumption pending official confirmation.
