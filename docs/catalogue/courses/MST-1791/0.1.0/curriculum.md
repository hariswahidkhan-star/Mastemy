## Learning outcomes

1. TPM foundations
2. Autonomous maintenance
3. Planned maintenance
4. Loss and OEE
5. Focused improvement and culture

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Selection items cannot demonstrate performing maintenance on equipment; hands-on practice belongs on the floor.

## Integrative case

A line suffers frequent breakdowns and operators blame maintenance. The learner must introduce autonomous maintenance, choose planned-maintenance strategies, classify and measure the six big losses with OEE, run focused improvement on the worst loss, and plan how to sustain the culture, then present a TPM roadmap.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1791-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Autonomous maintenance means that:

- A. Operators perform basic cleaning, inspection and lubrication of their equipment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: autonomous maintenance gives operators ownership of basic care.
- B. Machines maintain themselves with no human involvement  
  _Rationale:_ Autonomous refers to operators, not self-maintaining machines.
- C. Only specialists ever touch the equipment  
  _Rationale:_ That is the opposite of autonomous maintenance.
- D. Maintenance is skipped to raise output  
  _Rationale:_ Skipping maintenance is not autonomous maintenance.

**MST-1791-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are among the 'six big losses' in TPM? (Select TWO.)

- A. Breakdowns (equipment failure) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: breakdowns are a classic availability loss.
- B. Minor stops and idling **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: small stoppages are a performance loss.
- C. The cost of raw materials  
  _Rationale:_ Material cost is not one of the six losses.
- D. The number of shifts worked  
  _Rationale:_ Shift count is not a loss category.

**MST-1791-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Predictive maintenance is best described as:

- A. Acting on the measured condition of equipment to intervene before failure **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: predictive uses condition data to time maintenance.
- B. Fixing equipment only after it breaks down  
  _Rationale:_ That is reactive maintenance.
- C. Replacing parts on a fixed calendar regardless of condition  
  _Rationale:_ That is preventive, not predictive.
- D. Never performing any maintenance  
  _Rationale:_ That guarantees failures.
