## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0425-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) One mole of any substance contains approximately:

- A. 6.02 x 10^23 representative particles (Avogadro's number) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a mole is defined by Avogadro's number of particles.
- B. 100 particles  
  _Rationale:_ A mole is vastly larger than 100 particles.
- C. The same mass in grams for every substance  
  _Rationale:_ Molar mass differs by substance; the particle count is what is constant.
- D. Exactly 1 gram of the substance  
  _Rationale:_ A mole's mass equals the molar mass, which is not 1 gram in general.

**MST-0425-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Adding more reactant to a system at chemical equilibrium will, by Le Chatelier's principle, shift the equilibrium:

- A. Toward the products (to partially consume the added reactant) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the system shifts to relieve the added reactant, forming more product.
- B. Toward the reactants  
  _Rationale:_ Adding reactant shifts toward products, not further toward reactants.
- C. So that the reaction stops entirely  
  _Rationale:_ Equilibrium shifts position; it does not stop the reaction.
- D. Only if a catalyst is also added  
  _Rationale:_ The shift occurs from the concentration change alone.

**MST-0425-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO factors generally increase the rate of a chemical reaction?

- A. Raising the temperature **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: higher temperature increases the fraction of effective collisions.
- B. Adding a suitable catalyst **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a catalyst lowers activation energy and speeds the reaction.
- C. Lowering the concentration of the reactants  
  _Rationale:_ Lower concentration generally slows the reaction.
- D. Removing the reactants from the system  
  _Rationale:_ Removing reactants cannot speed the reaction.
- E. Decreasing the surface area of a solid reactant  
  _Rationale:_ Less surface area slows a heterogeneous reaction.
