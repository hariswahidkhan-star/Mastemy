## Learning outcomes

1. Explain in simple words what artificial intelligence is
2. Give examples of AI used in everyday life
3. Explain that AI learns from examples (data) rather than being told every rule
4. Describe the difference between a human and a computer 'thinking'
5. Recognise that AI can make mistakes and is not always right
6. Discuss simple, fair and kind uses of AI

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2895-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which sentence best describes how AI usually learns to tell cats from dogs?

- A. It looks at many example pictures and finds patterns that tell them apart **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: modern AI learns patterns from lots of examples (data).
- B. Someone types in every single rule about every cat  
  _Rationale:_ AI usually learns from examples rather than every hand-typed rule.
- C. It already knows everything when it is built  
  _Rationale:_ AI must learn from data; it does not know everything at the start.
- D. It asks the cat and the dog  
  _Rationale:_ AI learns from example data, not by asking animals.

**MST-2895-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO statements about AI are true? (Select TWO.)

- A. AI can make mistakes **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI is not always right and can get things wrong.
- B. AI learns from examples (data) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI finds patterns in example data.
- C. AI has real feelings like a person  
  _Rationale:_ AI does not have feelings; it processes patterns.
- D. AI is always correct  
  _Rationale:_ AI can be wrong, so we should check its answers.

**MST-2895-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) An AI photo helper labels a muffin as a 'puppy'. What does this show?

- A. AI can make mistakes, so we should check its answers **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI is not perfect and can confuse similar-looking things.
- B. The AI is being naughty on purpose  
  _Rationale:_ AI has no intentions; it simply made an error.
- C. Muffins really are puppies  
  _Rationale:_ The label is wrong; the AI made a mistake.
- D. AI never makes mistakes  
  _Rationale:_ This is an example of AI making a mistake.
