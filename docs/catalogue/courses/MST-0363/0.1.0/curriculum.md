## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0363-Q0001** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) A patient is 1 week post-op from a tissue repair. The therapist should progress resistance only when:

- A. Tissue healing and protocol allow the added load **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: loading must respect healing timelines and the surgical protocol.
- B. The patient asks to work harder  
  _Rationale:_ Patient preference does not override healing constraints.
- C. The next appointment is scheduled  
  _Rationale:_ Scheduling is unrelated to tissue tolerance.
- D. Pain is completely absent at rest  
  _Rationale:_ Absence of rest pain alone does not confirm readiness for load.

**MST-0363-Q0002** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) During exercise, a cardiac patient's response exceeds safe vital-sign limits. The therapist should:

- A. Stop or modify the activity and reassess **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: patient safety requires halting or adjusting the activity.
- B. Continue to reach the session goal  
  _Rationale:_ Pushing past safe limits risks harm.
- C. Increase intensity to build tolerance  
  _Rationale:_ Increasing intensity when limits are exceeded is unsafe.
- D. Ignore the readings if the patient feels fine  
  _Rationale:_ Objective limits should not be ignored.

**MST-0363-Q0003** (multiple-answer-selection, Select TWO) Which TWO are appropriate before applying a new intervention protocol?

- A. Screening for contraindications **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: screening protects patient safety.
- B. Confirming the intervention fits the examination findings **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: interventions should match the evaluation.
- C. Applying it to every patient identically  
  _Rationale:_ Interventions must be individualised.
- D. Skipping documentation  
  _Rationale:_ Documentation is required.
- E. Ignoring the plan of care  
  _Rationale:_ The plan of care guides intervention.
