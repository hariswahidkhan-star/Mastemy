## Learning outcomes

1. Draft and structure documents with AI
2. Create and refine presentations with AI
3. Edit, summarise and repurpose content with AI
4. Maintain accuracy, voice and rights or privacy in AI-made content

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)


**MST-1393-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which prompt will most likely produce a usable first draft?

- A. 'Write a document.'  
  _Rationale:_ Too vague; no audience, goal or format.
- B. 'Write a one-page update for executives on project risks, in bullet points.' **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: audience, goal, format and length are specified.
- C. 'Make it good.'  
  _Rationale:_ Gives the AI no usable constraints.
- D. 'Write everything you know.'  
  _Rationale:_ Unfocused and not tied to a goal.

**MST-1393-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO checks should you run before sending an AI-drafted report? (Select TWO.)

- A. Fact-check the claims and figures **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI can produce confident but wrong facts.
- B. Confirm no confidential information is exposed **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: confidentiality must be checked before sharing.
- C. Assume the AI voice matches your brand automatically  
  _Rationale:_ Voice must be checked and aligned, not assumed.
- D. Skip review because AI rarely errs  
  _Rationale:_ AI output requires review; errors are common.

**MST-1393-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) After AI shortens a report, what is the key risk to check?

- A. Important points or caveats may have been dropped **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: summaries can omit essential detail.
- B. The document will be too long  
  _Rationale:_ Shortening makes it shorter, not longer.
- C. Fonts will change automatically  
  _Rationale:_ Summarising does not change fonts.
- D. It becomes impossible to edit  
  _Rationale:_ The shortened text remains editable.
