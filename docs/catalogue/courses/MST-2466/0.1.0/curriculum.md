## Learning outcomes

1. Explain what an operating model is and how it links strategy to execution
2. Recognise the predictable breakpoints organisations hit as they scale
3. Design structure, decision rights and processes that fit the stage
4. Build the management systems and metrics that scaling requires
5. Scale culture and leadership capacity alongside headcount
6. Avoid premature bureaucracy and the loss of speed as the firm grows

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A startup that has quadrupled headcount in 18 months is now missing deadlines, escalating every decision to the founders, and losing its culture: diagnose the breakpoint, redesign decision rights and structure, install a lightweight management cadence, and scale leadership without smothering the firm in bureaucracy.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2466-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Every significant decision in a fast-scaling firm is escalated to the two founders. What operating-model fix addresses the root cause?

- A. Define and delegate clear decision rights so decisions are made at the right level **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: explicit, delegated decision rights relieve the founder bottleneck at its source.
- B. Hire more founders  
  _Rationale:_ Adding founders does not fix unclear decision rights.
- C. Have the founders work longer hours  
  _Rationale:_ This treats a symptom, not the structural cause.
- D. Stop making decisions until the firm is smaller  
  _Rationale:_ Not a viable or structural fix.

**MST-2466-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO signs most strongly indicate a company has outgrown its operating model? (Select TWO.)

- A. Decisions that used to be quick now stall and escalate **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: decision gridlock is a classic breakpoint symptom.
- B. Coordination overhead and rework are rising sharply **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: rising coordination cost signals the model no longer fits the size.
- C. Revenue is growing  
  _Rationale:_ Growth itself is not a sign of an outgrown model.
- D. The company moved to a larger office  
  _Rationale:_ Office size is not an operating-model signal.
- E. The logo was refreshed  
  _Rationale:_ Branding is unrelated to operating-model fit.

**MST-2466-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A scaling firm responds to chaos by adding approval steps and committees everywhere. What is the risk?

- A. Premature bureaucracy slows decisions and erodes the speed that made the firm successful **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: over-processing too early trades away speed without solving the real structural gaps.
- B. More approvals always improve decision quality  
  _Rationale:_ Excess approvals usually degrade speed and ownership.
- C. Bureaucracy has no downside at scale  
  _Rationale:_ Unnecessary bureaucracy carries real costs.
- D. Committees remove the need for decision rights  
  _Rationale:_ They often blur, not clarify, decision rights.
