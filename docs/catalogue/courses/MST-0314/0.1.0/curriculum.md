## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0314-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A Master Black Belt selecting improvement projects should prioritize those that:

- A. Align with business strategy and deliver measurable benefit **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: projects must tie to strategy and quantified benefit.
- B. Are easiest regardless of impact  
  _Rationale:_ Ease without impact wastes capacity.
- C. A single manager personally prefers  
  _Rationale:_ Preference is not a selection basis.
- D. Have no defined measure of success  
  _Rationale:_ Projects need measurable goals.

**MST-0314-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) After a project closes, gains are most likely to hold when the team:

- A. Implements a control plan to monitor and sustain the process **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: control plans sustain improvements over time.
- B. Moves on with no monitoring  
  _Rationale:_ Without monitoring, gains often erode.
- C. Deletes the project documentation  
  _Rationale:_ Documentation supports sustainability.
- D. Assumes the fix is permanent automatically  
  _Rationale:_ Sustainability requires active control.

**MST-0314-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO responsibilities that distinguish a Master Black Belt from a Black Belt.

- A. Mentoring and developing other belts **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: MBBs coach and develop Black and Green Belts.
- B. Leading enterprise deployment strategy **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: MBBs shape organization-wide deployment.
- C. Avoiding all contact with projects  
  _Rationale:_ MBBs stay engaged with projects.
- D. Working only on personal projects  
  _Rationale:_ MBBs enable others, not just themselves.
- E. Ignoring business strategy  
  _Rationale:_ Strategy alignment is a core MBB duty.
