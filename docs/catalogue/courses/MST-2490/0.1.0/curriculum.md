## Learning outcomes

1. Explain what makes a KPI useful and how it links to strategy
2. Select appropriate profitability, liquidity, efficiency and return metrics
3. Calculate and interpret core financial ratios correctly
4. Design a balanced metric set that resists gaming
5. Benchmark metrics against peers, trends and targets responsibly
6. Present metrics clearly and flag their limitations

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2490-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What most clearly distinguishes a leading from a lagging indicator?

- A. A leading indicator signals likely future outcomes; a lagging one reports past results **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: leading indicators are predictive, lagging are confirmatory.
- B. A leading indicator is always financial  
  _Rationale:_ Leading indicators are often operational.
- C. A lagging indicator is always wrong  
  _Rationale:_ Lagging indicators are valid, just backward-looking.
- D. They measure exactly the same thing  
  _Rationale:_ They differ in timing and purpose.

**MST-2490-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are liquidity measures rather than profitability measures? (Select TWO.)

- A. Current ratio **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the current ratio measures short-term liquidity.
- B. Quick (acid-test) ratio **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the quick ratio measures liquidity.
- C. Operating margin  
  _Rationale:_ Operating margin is a profitability measure.
- D. Return on equity  
  _Rationale:_ Return on equity is a profitability/return measure.

**MST-2490-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A division reports a very high return on equity. Before celebrating, the most important check is whether:

- A. The high return is driven by heavy leverage that also raises risk **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: leverage inflates ROE and increases financial risk.
- B. The number is written in the correct font  
  _Rationale:_ Presentation is irrelevant to the caveat.
- C. Revenue is denominated in dollars  
  _Rationale:_ Currency is not the concern here.
- D. The report was emailed on time  
  _Rationale:_ Timeliness does not address the leverage caveat.
