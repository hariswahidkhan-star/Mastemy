## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1839-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A customer is upset about a late delivery. What should the agent do FIRST before offering a solution?

- A. Acknowledge the problem and let the customer finish explaining **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: active listening and acknowledgement de-escalate emotion and surface the real issue before problem-solving.
- B. Immediately offer a discount  
  _Rationale:_ Jumping to a remedy before understanding the issue can feel dismissive and may not address the real concern.
- C. Explain the company shipping policy  
  _Rationale:_ Leading with policy before acknowledging the customer's frustration tends to escalate the conflict.
- D. Transfer the call to a supervisor  
  _Rationale:_ Escalating by default avoids ownership and frustrates the customer further.

**MST-1839-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which metric most directly measures how satisfied a customer is with a single support interaction?

- A. CSAT (Customer Satisfaction Score) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: CSAT captures satisfaction with a specific interaction or transaction.
- B. NPS (Net Promoter Score)  
  _Rationale:_ NPS measures overall likelihood to recommend, not a single interaction.
- C. First response time  
  _Rationale:_ This is an operational speed metric, not a direct satisfaction measure.
- D. Ticket backlog  
  _Rationale:_ Backlog measures workload, not customer satisfaction.

**MST-1839-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO behaviours best demonstrate empathy in a customer conversation?

- A. Reflecting back the customer's concern in your own words **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: paraphrasing shows the customer they were heard and understood.
- B. Acknowledging how the situation affected the customer **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: naming the impact validates the customer's experience.
- C. Telling the customer not to worry about it  
  _Rationale:_ Minimising the concern dismisses rather than validates it.
- D. Reading the standard policy verbatim  
  _Rationale:_ Reciting policy does not acknowledge the customer's feelings.
- E. Ending the call quickly to improve handle time  
  _Rationale:_ Prioritising speed over the customer's concern undermines empathy.
