## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0059-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Appraising whether a manager's outperformance reflects skill rather than luck typically requires:

- A. A sufficiently long record and statistical significance of excess return **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: distinguishing skill from luck needs an adequate sample and significance testing of alpha.
- B. A single quarter of strong returns  
  _Rationale:_ One quarter is far too short to infer skill.
- C. The manager's own confidence in their process  
  _Rationale:_ Self-assessment is not evidence of statistical skill.
- D. The size of the manager's marketing budget  
  _Rationale:_ Marketing spend is irrelevant to investment skill.

**MST-0059-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) In manager due diligence, 'style drift' refers to:

- A. A manager departing from the stated investment mandate or style over time **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: style drift is movement away from the declared mandate, a key monitoring concern.
- B. A fall in the risk-free rate  
  _Rationale:_ Interest-rate moves are not style drift.
- C. A change in the fund's custodian bank  
  _Rationale:_ Changing custodian is an operational, not style, matter.
- D. An increase in the benchmark's dividend yield  
  _Rationale:_ Benchmark yield changes are not style drift.

**MST-0059-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO items that should form part of ongoing monitoring of an external manager.

- A. Tracking performance against the agreed benchmark and mandate **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: comparing results to the mandate and benchmark is core monitoring.
- B. Reviewing changes in key investment personnel **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: personnel turnover is a key monitoring signal.
- C. Ignoring the manager once hired  
  _Rationale:_ Ongoing monitoring is required; 'hire and forget' is poor practice.
- D. Guaranteeing the manager's future alpha  
  _Rationale:_ Future alpha cannot be guaranteed.
- E. Removing all reporting requirements  
  _Rationale:_ Reporting is essential to monitoring.
