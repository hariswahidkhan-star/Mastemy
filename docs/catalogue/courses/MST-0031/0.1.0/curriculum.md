## Learning outcomes

1. Interpret an operational-level case scenario and integrate costing, reporting and risk knowledge
2. Apply analysis and decision techniques to the requirements of an operational case
3. Plan and construct answers to case tasks under timed conditions

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: This course assesses knowledge and applied reasoning via single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only. The official examination is an integrated written case study responding to an unseen scenario, which this MCQ/MR format does not reproduce; see the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0031-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In an operational-level case response, what should most directly shape the content and emphasis of your answer?

- A. The specific tasks and requirements set in the exam **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: marks are awarded for addressing the stated task requirements, so they must drive the response.
- B. Every topic in the operational-level syllabus  
  _Rationale:_ Writing everything you know wastes time and misses marks tied to the actual tasks.
- C. The longest section of the pre-seen material  
  _Rationale:_ Length of pre-seen material does not determine what the tasks require.
- D. The order in which exhibits appear  
  _Rationale:_ Exhibit order is incidental; the task requirements govern the answer.

**MST-0031-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A case company reports rising revenue but falling operating cash flow. Which analysis is the most relevant first step?

- A. Examine movements in working capital such as receivables and inventory **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a revenue/cash divergence usually signals working-capital build-up, so examine receivables and inventory first.
- B. Assume the figures contain an error and ignore them  
  _Rationale:_ Dismissing data is not analysis; the divergence is a common, explainable pattern.
- C. Recommend an immediate dividend increase  
  _Rationale:_ Increasing dividends ignores the cash-flow problem and is premature.
- D. Conclude the company is insolvent  
  _Rationale:_ A single divergence does not establish insolvency without further analysis.

**MST-0031-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO practices that improve exam technique in a timed case-study response.

- A. Allocating time to tasks in proportion to their marks **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: proportioning time to marks maximises achievable marks across all tasks.
- B. Linking recommendations to the specific scenario facts **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: scenario-specific application earns application marks over generic statements.
- C. Writing a long generic essay covering the whole syllabus  
  _Rationale:_ Generic coverage ignores the tasks and wastes limited time.
- D. Leaving the highest-mark task until no time remains  
  _Rationale:_ Deprioritising high-mark tasks risks losing the most marks.
- E. Repeating the pre-seen material verbatim  
  _Rationale:_ Copying the pre-seen adds no analysis and earns no application marks.
