## Learning outcomes

1. Build a digital-marketing strategy from goals and audience
2. Map the customer journey and funnel stages
3. Plan search marketing across SEO and paid search
4. Plan social and content marketing channels
5. Plan email, lifecycle and retention marketing
6. Set up measurement, attribution and analytics
7. Allocate budget and optimise across channels
8. Apply testing, governance and ethics in campaigns

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A direct-to-consumer brand with a flat growth curve must rebuild its marketing plan. The marketer must set goals, map the funnel, choose channels across search, social, content and email, define KPIs and attribution, allocate a fixed budget, and design an experiment, then defend the plan to the founder on both ROI and privacy grounds.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1101-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) The first step in building a digital-marketing strategy should be to:

- A. Define business goals and the target audience **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: goals and audience drive every channel choice.
- B. Buy ads on the largest platform  
  _Rationale:_ Channel spend should follow strategy.
- C. Pick a posting schedule  
  _Rationale:_ Tactics come after strategy.
- D. Choose a brand colour palette  
  _Rationale:_ Not the primary strategic step.

**MST-1101-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO statements about SEO versus paid search are correct? (Select TWO.)

- A. SEO earns organic placement over time without per-click cost **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: organic results are not paid per click.
- B. Paid search can deliver immediate visibility for a cost per click **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: paid search buys placement quickly.
- C. SEO guarantees the top paid ad slot  
  _Rationale:_ SEO affects organic, not paid, placement.
- D. Paid search permanently improves organic rankings  
  _Rationale:_ Paid clicks do not raise organic rank.

**MST-1101-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A last-click attribution model tends to:

- A. Over-credit the final touchpoint and undervalue earlier ones **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: last-click ignores assisting touchpoints.
- B. Split credit evenly across every touchpoint  
  _Rationale:_ That describes a linear model.
- C. Credit only the first touchpoint  
  _Rationale:_ That describes first-click attribution.
- D. Ignore conversions entirely  
  _Rationale:_ It does attribute conversions, just to the last click.
