## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the dynamics of relationships within supply chains
2. Describe supplier relationship management processes and tools
3. Analyse positioning and portfolio approaches to supplier relationships
4. Explain how to add value through supplier development and partnering

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1266-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) On the Kraljic matrix, an item with high supply risk and high spend/profit impact is classed as:

- A. Strategic **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: high risk and high value items are strategic and warrant close relationships.
- B. Leverage  
  _Rationale:_ Leverage items are high value but low risk.
- C. Bottleneck  
  _Rationale:_ Bottleneck items are high risk but low value.
- D. Routine  
  _Rationale:_ Routine items are low risk and low value.

**MST-1266-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which tool positions suppliers by how attractive the buyer's account is and the relative value of the spend?

- A. Supplier preferencing matrix **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: supplier preferencing views the relationship from the supplier's perspective (buyer attractiveness vs value).
- B. SWOT analysis  
  _Rationale:_ SWOT assesses strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats generally.
- C. PESTLE analysis  
  _Rationale:_ PESTLE scans the macro environment, not supplier preferences.
- D. Gantt chart  
  _Rationale:_ A Gantt chart schedules tasks over time.

**MST-1266-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are appropriate contents of a supplier scorecard?

- A. Weighted KPIs covering quality, delivery and service **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: balanced, weighted KPIs beyond price belong on a scorecard.
- B. A single price metric only  
  _Rationale:_ Price alone gives an incomplete view of performance.
- C. Agreed review frequency and targets **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: targets and review cadence make the scorecard actionable.
- D. The buyer's personal opinion of the sales rep  
  _Rationale:_ Subjective personal opinion is not a reliable performance measure.
