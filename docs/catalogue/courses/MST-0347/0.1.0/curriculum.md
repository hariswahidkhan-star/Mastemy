## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0347-Q0001** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) A 58-year-old presents with crushing substernal chest pain radiating to the left arm. Which is the most appropriate immediate step?

- A. Obtain a 12-lead ECG and assess for acute coronary syndrome **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: early ECG is essential to identify a time-critical cardiac event.
- B. Reassure and schedule outpatient follow-up  
  _Rationale:_ Delaying workup of possible ACS is unsafe.
- C. Order a routine stress test for next week  
  _Rationale:_ An acute presentation requires immediate evaluation, not elective testing.
- D. Treat as musculoskeletal pain only  
  _Rationale:_ Assuming a benign cause without a differential is unsafe.

**MST-0347-Q0002** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) Before prescribing a renally cleared drug, the clinician should first:

- A. Assess renal function and adjust the dose accordingly **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: renal function guides safe dosing of renally cleared drugs.
- B. Double the dose to ensure effect  
  _Rationale:_ Increasing the dose without assessment risks toxicity.
- C. Ignore renal status if the patient feels well  
  _Rationale:_ Symptoms do not reflect renal clearance.
- D. Use the maximum dose for all patients  
  _Rationale:_ A one-size dose ignores individual clearance.

**MST-0347-Q0003** (multiple-answer-selection, Select TWO) Which TWO findings are red flags suggesting a secondary (dangerous) cause of headache?

- A. Sudden "thunderclap" onset **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: abrupt severe onset suggests a serious cause.
- B. New neurological deficit **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: focal deficits point to a secondary cause.
- C. A long history of similar mild headaches  
  _Rationale:_ A stable pattern is reassuring, not a red flag.
- D. Relief with rest and hydration  
  _Rationale:_ Relief with simple measures is not a red flag.
- E. Onset after a good night of sleep  
  _Rationale:_ This is not a danger sign.
