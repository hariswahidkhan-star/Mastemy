## Learning outcomes

1. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Fundamental ethical and professional principles' to exam-style problems
2. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'The financial reporting framework' to exam-style problems
3. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Reporting the financial performance of a range of entities' to exam-style problems
4. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Financial statements of groups of entities' to exam-style problems
5. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Interpreting financial statements for different stakeholders' to exam-style problems
6. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'The impact of changes and potential changes in accounting regulation' to exam-style problems

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Official ACCA exams use constructed-response and computational item formats (written answers, calculations, OT case scenarios) that this platform's MCQ/MR forms do not reproduce; see the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0011-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under IFRS 15, revenue is recognised when:

- A. The entity satisfies a performance obligation by transferring control of a good or service **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: IFRS 15 recognises revenue on transfer of control for each performance obligation.
- B. Cash is received from the customer  
  _Rationale:_ IFRS 15 is not a cash-based model.
- C. The contract is signed  
  _Rationale:_ Signing a contract does not by itself trigger revenue.
- D. The goods are manufactured  
  _Rationale:_ Production alone does not transfer control.

**MST-0011-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) An entity controls another entity for consolidation purposes when it has:

- A. Power over the investee, exposure to variable returns, and the ability to affect those returns **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: IFRS 10 defines control using these three elements.
- B. More than 20% of the voting rights only  
  _Rationale:_ 20% ordinarily indicates significant influence, not control.
- C. A seat on the board only  
  _Rationale:_ A board seat alone does not establish control.
- D. A long-term supply contract  
  _Rationale:_ A supply contract does not by itself confer control.

**MST-0011-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO disclosures that enhance the usefulness of financial statements to users under the Conceptual Framework.

- A. Information that is relevant to users' decisions **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: relevance is a fundamental qualitative characteristic.
- B. Information that faithfully represents transactions **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: faithful representation is a fundamental qualitative characteristic.
- C. Information deliberately biased to favour management  
  _Rationale:_ Bias undermines faithful representation.
- D. Omission of material items to shorten the report  
  _Rationale:_ Omitting material items reduces usefulness.
- E. Use of inconsistent measurement each year without disclosure  
  _Rationale:_ Undisclosed inconsistency harms comparability.
