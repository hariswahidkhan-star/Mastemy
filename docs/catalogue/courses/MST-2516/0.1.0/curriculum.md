## Learning outcomes

1. Identify contracts within the scope of the insurance contracts standard
2. Explain the general measurement model and its building blocks
3. Describe the contractual service margin and how profit is recognised over time
4. Explain when the premium allocation approach may be used
5. Describe the variable fee approach for contracts with direct participation features
6. Outline presentation and disclosure of insurance results

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2516-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under the general measurement model, how is the profit expected from a group of insurance contracts recognised?

- A. It is held as the contractual service margin and released to profit or loss as services are provided over the coverage period **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the CSM defers unearned profit and is released over the coverage period.
- B. It is recognised in full at the inception of the contract  
  _Rationale:_ Day-one profit is deferred in the CSM, not recognised immediately.
- C. It is never recognised in profit or loss  
  _Rationale:_ The CSM is released to profit or loss over time.
- D. It is recognised only when the contract matures or lapses  
  _Rationale:_ Profit is recognised as services are provided, not only at maturity.

**MST-2516-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO building blocks form the fulfilment cash flows in the general measurement model? (Select TWO.)

- A. Estimates of future cash flows adjusted for the time value of money (discounting) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: discounted future cash-flow estimates are a building block.
- B. A risk adjustment for non-financial risk **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the risk adjustment for non-financial risk is a building block.
- C. The insurer's targeted return on equity  
  _Rationale:_ A target return on equity is not a building block of fulfilment cash flows.
- D. The market value of the insurer's own shares  
  _Rationale:_ The insurer's share price is irrelevant to the measurement.

**MST-2516-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) An insurer wants to recognise the entire first-year premium on a long-term policy as revenue immediately. Why is that generally inappropriate under IFRS 17?

- A. Insurance revenue reflects services provided in the period and excludes investment components, so unearned profit is deferred rather than taken up front **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: revenue follows services provided, excluding investment components, with profit deferred in the CSM.
- B. Premiums are never revenue under any circumstances  
  _Rationale:_ Premiums can generate insurance revenue, but recognised as services are provided.
- C. All premiums must be refunded  
  _Rationale:_ There is no requirement to refund premiums.
- D. Revenue can only be recognised at contract maturity  
  _Rationale:_ Revenue is recognised over the coverage period, not solely at maturity.
