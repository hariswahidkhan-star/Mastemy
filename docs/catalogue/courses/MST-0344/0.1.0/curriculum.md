## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0344-Q0001** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) A patient's lab report shows a low serum sodium but the patient is asymptomatic and clinically euvolaemic. The best next step in reasoning is to:

- A. Interpret the value in the clinical context before treating it **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a lab value is interpreted together with volume status and symptoms, not treated in isolation.
- B. Rapidly correct the sodium with hypertonic saline  
  _Rationale:_ Rapid correction risks osmotic demyelination and is not indicated for an asymptomatic patient.
- C. Ignore the result because the patient feels well  
  _Rationale:_ The result still needs assessment; it is not simply ignored.
- D. Repeat every electrolyte panel hourly  
  _Rationale:_ Hourly repetition is excessive and not justified by the scenario.

**MST-0344-Q0002** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) In an initial trauma assessment, which concern is addressed first?

- A. Airway with cervical-spine protection **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the airway (with C-spine control) is the first priority in the primary survey.
- B. Definitive CT imaging of all regions  
  _Rationale:_ Imaging follows stabilisation, not before securing the airway.
- C. Detailed past medical history  
  _Rationale:_ History is gathered after life threats are addressed.
- D. Wound closure  
  _Rationale:_ Closure is a later, non-life-threatening step.

**MST-0344-Q0003** (multiple-answer-selection, Select TWO) Which TWO findings most directly raise concern for a hypertensive disorder of pregnancy?

- A. New-onset elevated blood pressure after 20 weeks **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: new hypertension after 20 weeks is a defining feature.
- B. Proteinuria **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: proteinuria is a classic associated finding.
- C. Mild first-trimester nausea  
  _Rationale:_ Early nausea is common and unrelated to hypertensive disorders.
- D. A single normal booking blood pressure  
  _Rationale:_ A normal reading does not raise concern.
- E. Expected weight gain within range  
  _Rationale:_ Normal weight gain is not a warning feature.
