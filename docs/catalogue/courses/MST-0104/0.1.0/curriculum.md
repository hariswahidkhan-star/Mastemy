## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0104-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A U.S. retailer launches an online loyalty program that profiles consumers. Which characteristic best describes the U.S. privacy legal framework it must navigate?

- A. A sectoral and state-by-state patchwork rather than a single omnibus federal law **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: U.S. privacy law is largely sectoral and state-driven rather than one omnibus federal statute.
- B. A single comprehensive federal privacy statute covering all sectors  
  _Rationale:_ The U.S. has no single omnibus federal privacy law covering all sectors.
- C. Identical rules in every state  
  _Rationale:_ State privacy laws differ substantially from one another.
- D. No applicable privacy obligations at all  
  _Rationale:_ Numerous sectoral and state obligations apply.

**MST-0104-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which U.S. law is primarily associated with the privacy of consumers' financial information held by financial institutions?

- A. The Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: GLBA governs privacy and safeguarding of consumer financial information at financial institutions.
- B. The Fair Labor Standards Act  
  _Rationale:_ The FLSA governs wages and hours, not financial privacy.
- C. The Clean Air Act  
  _Rationale:_ This is an environmental statute, unrelated to privacy.
- D. The Sherman Antitrust Act  
  _Rationale:_ This is competition law, not financial privacy.

**MST-0104-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO common consumer rights featured in recent U.S. state comprehensive privacy laws. (Select TWO.)

- A. The right to access personal data a business holds about the consumer **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: access/know is a common right in state comprehensive laws.
- B. The right to delete personal data, subject to exceptions **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: deletion is a common state-law consumer right, subject to exceptions.
- C. The right to a guaranteed paid vacation  
  _Rationale:_ That is an employment-benefit matter, not a privacy right.
- D. The right to free broadband from the state  
  _Rationale:_ No such privacy right exists in these laws.
- E. The right to set national monetary policy  
  _Rationale:_ This is unrelated to consumer privacy rights.
