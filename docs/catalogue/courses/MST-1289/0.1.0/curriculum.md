## Learning outcomes

1. Describe cardiovascular anatomy, physiology and hemodynamics (knowledge practice)
2. Explain cardiac catheterisation procedures, equipment and sterile technique
3. Apply hemodynamic monitoring, waveform and ECG interpretation
4. Analyse patient care, pharmacology and emergency response in the cath lab

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1289-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) During a case the monitor shows ventricular fibrillation and the patient is unresponsive. What is the first priority action?

- A. Document the event in detail first  
  _Rationale:_ Documentation must not delay life-saving intervention.
- B. Initiate defibrillation and resuscitation per protocol **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: pulseless VF requires immediate defibrillation and CPR per ACLS.
- C. Wait for a second rhythm confirmation over several minutes  
  _Rationale:_ Delaying defibrillation in pulseless VF worsens outcomes.
- D. Remove all monitoring leads  
  _Rationale:_ Removing monitoring hinders care during an arrest.

**MST-1289-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A pressure waveform shows a rapid upstroke and a dicrotic notch. Which structure is most likely being measured?

- A. A peripheral vein  
  _Rationale:_ Venous pressures are low and lack an arterial dicrotic notch.
- B. The aorta/arterial system **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a sharp upstroke with a dicrotic notch is characteristic of an arterial/aortic waveform.
- C. The lymphatic system  
  _Rationale:_ The lymphatic system is not pressure-monitored this way.
- D. The esophagus  
  _Rationale:_ The esophagus is not a cardiovascular pressure site.

**MST-1289-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO actions maintain sterile integrity in the cath lab?

- A. Keeping sterile items above waist level and within the sterile field **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: items below waist level or out of view are considered contaminated.
- B. Performing surgical hand antisepsis and donning sterile attire **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: proper hand antisepsis and sterile gowning/gloving preserve sterility.
- C. Reaching across the sterile field with ungloved hands  
  _Rationale:_ Reaching across with ungloved hands contaminates the field.
- D. Reusing single-use items between patients  
  _Rationale:_ Reusing single-use items breaches sterility and safety.
