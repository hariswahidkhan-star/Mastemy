## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the foundations of managing occupational health and safety
2. Apply risk assessment and the management of common workplace hazards
3. Apply control of physical, chemical, biological and psychosocial health hazards
4. Explain monitoring, investigation, review and continuous improvement

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0336-Q0001** (single, Select ONE) The strongest reason to present to management for investing in health and safety, beyond legal duty, is that it:

- A. Reduces harm to people and the associated financial losses **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the moral and financial cases complement the legal case.
- B. Guarantees higher product prices  
  _Rationale:_ Safety does not set product price.
- C. Removes the need for any risk assessment  
  _Rationale:_ Risk assessment is still required.
- D. Eliminates all workplace hazards instantly  
  _Rationale:_ No programme removes all hazards instantly.

**MST-0336-Q0002** (single, Select ONE) A general risk assessment should rank risks by combining severity with:

- A. The likelihood that the harm occurs **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: risk is a function of severity and likelihood.
- B. The number of managers on site  
  _Rationale:_ Manager count is not a risk factor.
- C. The age of the building only  
  _Rationale:_ Building age alone is not the combined factor.
- D. The brand of equipment only  
  _Rationale:_ Brand is not the risk dimension.

**MST-0336-Q0003** (multiple, Select TWO) Select TWO examples of a psychosocial workplace hazard.

- A. Excessive workload and time pressure **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: workload pressure is a psychosocial hazard.
- B. Workplace bullying or harassment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: bullying is a psychosocial hazard.
- C. Noise from machinery  
  _Rationale:_ Noise is a physical hazard.
- D. Solvent vapour  
  _Rationale:_ Solvent vapour is a chemical hazard.
- E. A slippery floor  
  _Rationale:_ A slip hazard is a physical/safety hazard.
