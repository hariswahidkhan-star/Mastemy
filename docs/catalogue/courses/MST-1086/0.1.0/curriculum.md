## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1086-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Compa-ratio compares an employee's pay to the:

- A. Range midpoint for their grade **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: compa-ratio is pay divided by the range midpoint, showing position in the range.
- B. Average pay of the whole company  
  _Rationale:_ That is not what compa-ratio measures; it uses the grade midpoint.
- C. Minimum wage in the jurisdiction  
  _Rationale:_ Statutory minimums are a floor, not the compa-ratio reference.
- D. Pay of the highest earner in the firm  
  _Rationale:_ Compa-ratio is grade-specific, not relative to the top earner.

**MST-1086-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) The main reason job evaluation should rate the role rather than the current holder is to:

- A. Reward long-serving employees automatically  
  _Rationale:_ Rewarding tenure is a separate decision, not the purpose of job evaluation.
- B. Keep internal pay relationships fair and consistent **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: evaluating the role supports internal equity regardless of who holds it.
- C. Reduce the total pay budget  
  _Rationale:_ Job evaluation sets relativities, not the overall budget.
- D. Avoid writing job descriptions  
  _Rationale:_ Job descriptions are an input to evaluation, not something it removes.

**MST-1086-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are warning signs that an incentive plan may drive the wrong behaviour? (Select TWO)

- A. It rewards a single metric people can game **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a lone gameable metric invites shortcuts that hurt other goals.
- B. It ignores quality while rewarding volume **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: rewarding volume alone can push people to sacrifice quality.
- C. It is explained clearly to participants  
  _Rationale:_ Clarity is a strength of a plan, not a warning sign.
- D. It is capped at an affordable amount  
  _Rationale:_ A sensible cap protects the budget; it is not a red flag.
