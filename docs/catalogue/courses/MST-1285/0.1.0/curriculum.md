## Learning outcomes

1. Apply assessment and diagnostic reasoning across the lifespan in primary care (knowledge practice)
2. Describe management of common acute and chronic conditions across age groups
3. Explain health promotion, screening and disease prevention
4. Analyse professional role, ethics and evidence-based practice for the family NP

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1285-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A clinician fixes on the first plausible diagnosis and stops considering others. Which reasoning error is this?

- A. Anchoring bias **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: anchoring is premature fixation on an initial diagnosis without weighing alternatives.
- B. Randomised control  
  _Rationale:_ Randomised control is a study design, not a reasoning error.
- C. Herd immunity  
  _Rationale:_ Herd immunity is a population-immunity concept, unrelated here.
- D. Pharmacokinetics  
  _Rationale:_ Pharmacokinetics concerns drug handling, not diagnostic reasoning.

**MST-1285-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) What best distinguishes a screening test from a diagnostic test?

- A. Screening is only done in hospitals  
  _Rationale:_ Setting does not define screening vs diagnosis.
- B. Screening is applied to asymptomatic people to detect risk or early disease **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: screening targets asymptomatic individuals, whereas diagnostic testing evaluates symptomatic presentations.
- C. Diagnostic tests are always cheaper  
  _Rationale:_ Cost does not define the distinction.
- D. Screening always confirms a diagnosis  
  _Rationale:_ Screening identifies risk; confirmation requires diagnostic testing.

**MST-1285-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO principles support evidence-based practice for a nurse practitioner?

- A. Integrating best available evidence with clinical expertise and patient values **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: this is the core definition of evidence-based practice.
- B. Preferring high-quality guidelines over a single anecdote **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: weighting stronger evidence appropriately is central to EBP.
- C. Following whichever option is cheapest regardless of evidence  
  _Rationale:_ Cost alone is not an EBP principle.
- D. Ignoring patient preferences entirely  
  _Rationale:_ EBP explicitly incorporates patient values.
