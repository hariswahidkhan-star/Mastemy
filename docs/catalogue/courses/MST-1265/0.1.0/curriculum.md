## Learning outcomes

1. Explain approaches and styles used in commercial negotiation
2. Prepare effectively for a commercial negotiation
3. Apply techniques to conduct and conclude a negotiation
4. Evaluate the influence of relationships and power on negotiation outcomes

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1265-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A negotiator's best alternative if the current deal fails is known as their:

- A. ZOPA  
  _Rationale:_ ZOPA is the zone of possible agreement between parties, not the fallback.
- B. BATNA **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: BATNA is the Best Alternative To a Negotiated Agreement.
- C. Target price  
  _Rationale:_ The target price is the desired outcome, not the fallback option.
- D. Opening position  
  _Rationale:_ The opening position is the first stated offer.

**MST-1265-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A negotiation that seeks to expand value so both parties gain is best described as:

- A. Distributive  
  _Rationale:_ Distributive negotiation divides a fixed pie, a win-lose approach.
- B. Integrative **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: integrative negotiation seeks mutual gains and expands value.
- C. Coercive  
  _Rationale:_ Coercion relies on threats, not mutual value.
- D. Avoiding  
  _Rationale:_ Avoiding means not engaging in the negotiation at all.

**MST-1265-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO actions are good practice when making concessions?

- A. Trade concessions rather than give them freely **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: concessions should be exchanged for something of value.
- B. Concede high-cost items first to build goodwill  
  _Rationale:_ Giving away high-value items early weakens your position.
- C. Keep a record of concessions given and received **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: tracking concessions maintains balance and control.
- D. Make several unilateral concessions up front  
  _Rationale:_ Unilateral early concessions signal weakness and invite more demands.
