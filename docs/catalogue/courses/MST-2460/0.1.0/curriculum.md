## Learning outcomes

1. Distinguish corporate-level strategy from business-unit strategy
2. Analyse industry structure and sources of competitive advantage
3. Choose where to compete across businesses, markets and the value chain
4. Evaluate diversification, vertical integration and corporate parenting logic
5. Build defensible advantage through positioning and distinctive capabilities
6. Test whether a corporate strategy actually creates value above the sum of parts

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2460-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A conglomerate argues it creates value by owning many unrelated businesses. Which test most directly challenges that claim?

- A. The ownership (parenting) test: is the parent the best owner of each business? **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the ownership test asks whether the parent, versus another owner, adds most value.
- B. The five-forces test of industry attractiveness  
  _Rationale:_ Five forces assesses industries, not parenting logic.
- C. The value-chain cost test  
  _Rationale:_ Value-chain analysis is about a single business's advantage.
- D. The break-even test  
  _Rationale:_ Break-even is a financial, not a corporate-ownership, test.

**MST-2460-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO conditions tend to make a competitive advantage sustainable rather than temporary? (Select TWO.)

- A. Distinctive capabilities that competitors cannot easily imitate **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: hard-to-imitate capabilities protect advantage.
- B. Isolating mechanisms such as scale economies or switching costs **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: isolating mechanisms slow erosion of advantage.
- C. A current price lower than every rival  
  _Rationale:_ A price lead is easily matched and not durable.
- D. A recent quarter of high profit  
  _Rationale:_ High profit alone does not indicate durability.
- E. A large marketing budget  
  _Rationale:_ Spending does not by itself create sustainable advantage.

**MST-2460-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A business is highly profitable yet its industry has low entry barriers and many substitutes. What is the most defensible strategic interpretation?

- A. Its profit may be vulnerable because industry structure offers weak protection **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: weak structure means current profit is at risk without firm-specific advantage.
- B. Its profit proves it has a sustainable advantage  
  _Rationale:_ Structure suggests the opposite.
- C. Industry structure is irrelevant to profitability  
  _Rationale:_ Structure strongly shapes profit potential.
- D. Substitutes always increase long-run profit  
  _Rationale:_ Substitutes typically pressure profit.
