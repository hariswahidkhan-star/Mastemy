## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0009-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) The primary financial objective of a company is usually stated as:

- A. Maximisation of shareholder wealth **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: maximising shareholder wealth is the conventional primary objective.
- B. Maximisation of accounting profit each year  
  _Rationale:_ Profit maximisation ignores risk, timing and cash flow.
- C. Minimisation of total costs  
  _Rationale:_ Cost minimisation is not the overall financial objective.
- D. Maximisation of market share  
  _Rationale:_ Market share is a strategic, not the primary financial, objective.

**MST-0009-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) When appraising a project using net present value (NPV), a project should be accepted if:

- A. The NPV is positive **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a positive NPV increases shareholder wealth.
- B. The payback period exceeds five years  
  _Rationale:_ A long payback does not justify acceptance.
- C. The NPV is negative  
  _Rationale:_ A negative NPV reduces wealth and should be rejected.
- D. The accounting rate of return is below the cost of capital  
  _Rationale:_ That would argue against acceptance.

**MST-0009-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO elements of a company's working capital.

- A. Inventory **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: inventory is a current asset forming part of working capital.
- B. Trade receivables **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: trade receivables are part of working capital.
- C. Ordinary share capital  
  _Rationale:_ Share capital is long-term finance, not working capital.
- D. Non-current assets  
  _Rationale:_ Non-current assets are not working capital.
- E. Debentures repayable in ten years  
  _Rationale:_ Long-term debt is not part of working capital.
