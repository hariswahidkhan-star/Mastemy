## Learning outcomes

1. The private capital landscape
2. Venture capital
3. Private equity buyouts
4. Economics and returns
5. Risks and the ecosystem

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses conceptual knowledge through selection items; this is general financial education, not investment advice, and it does not assess a real fund investment or deal decision.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1714-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A key difference between venture capital and leveraged buyout private equity is that VC typically:

- A. Takes minority stakes in young, high-growth companies **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: VC funds early, high-growth firms, often as a minority.
- B. Buys mature companies using large amounts of debt  
  _Rationale:_ That describes LBO private equity.
- C. Only invests in listed public companies  
  _Rationale:_ VC invests in private startups.
- D. Guarantees investors a fixed coupon  
  _Rationale:_ VC returns are equity-like and not guaranteed.

**MST-1714-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select ALL that apply) Which two items make up the typical compensation of a private fund's general partner? (Select TWO) (Select TWO)

- A. A management fee on committed capital **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the management fee is a standard GP revenue source.
- B. Carried interest on profits above a hurdle **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: carry is the GP's share of profits.
- C. A government subsidy per deal  
  _Rationale:_ No such standard subsidy exists.
- D. A fixed dividend paid by portfolio firms  
  _Rationale:_ Portfolio firms do not pay the GP a fixed dividend.

**MST-1714-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Private equity and venture funds are described as illiquid mainly because:

- A. Investor capital is locked up for years until exits occur **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: capital is committed for the fund's life until realisations.
- B. They are traded continuously on an exchange  
  _Rationale:_ They are not exchange-traded; that would be liquid.
- C. Investors can redeem daily at net asset value  
  _Rationale:_ Daily redemption describes liquid open-ended funds.
- D. They hold only cash  
  _Rationale:_ They hold long-term private stakes, not cash.
