## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0337-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In a safety management system based on Plan-Do-Check-Act, which activity belongs to the 'Check' stage?

- A. Measuring and auditing performance against objectives **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: checking is the measurement, monitoring and audit stage that evaluates results.
- B. Setting the health and safety policy and objectives  
  _Rationale:_ Setting policy and objectives is part of 'Plan'.
- C. Implementing control measures and training workers  
  _Rationale:_ Implementing controls is part of 'Do'.
- D. Revising arrangements after a management review  
  _Rationale:_ Acting on review findings is part of 'Act'.

**MST-0337-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A proactive health and safety performance indicator is one that:

- A. Measures activity before a loss occurs, such as completed inspections **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: leading/proactive indicators track preventive activity ahead of incidents.
- B. Counts the number of lost-time injuries last quarter  
  _Rationale:_ Injury counts are reactive (lagging) indicators.
- C. Totals compensation paid after accidents  
  _Rationale:_ Compensation totals are reactive outcome measures.
- D. Records enforcement notices received from a regulator  
  _Rationale:_ Enforcement notices follow failures and are reactive.

**MST-0337-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO purposes of investigating a workplace incident.

- A. To identify the underlying causes so recurrence can be prevented **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: determining root causes to prevent recurrence is a core purpose of investigation.
- B. To identify failures in the management system and controls **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: investigations reveal management-system and control weaknesses.
- C. To assign personal blame to an individual worker  
  _Rationale:_ Blame-focused investigation undermines learning and is not a legitimate purpose.
- D. To reduce the amount of recordkeeping required  
  _Rationale:_ Investigation typically increases, not reduces, documentation.
- E. To guarantee no incident can ever happen again  
  _Rationale:_ No investigation can guarantee the elimination of all future incidents.
