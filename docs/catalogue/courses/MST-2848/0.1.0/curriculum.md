## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the properties of solids, liquids and gases
2. Explain that materials can change state when heated or cooled
3. Name melting, freezing, evaporation and condensation as changes of state
4. Relate changes of state to everyday examples like ice, water and steam
5. Investigate factors that speed up evaporation safely
6. Stay safe around hot water and steam by letting an adult handle heating

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance. Any experiment ideas are optional and require adult supervision; they are not assessed.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2848-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What happens to ice when it is warmed up?

- A. It melts and becomes liquid water **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: warming ice makes it melt from solid to liquid.
- B. It turns straight into a new material  
  _Rationale:_ It is still water, just in a different state.
- C. It freezes harder  
  _Rationale:_ Warming melts ice; cooling freezes it.
- D. It disappears completely at once  
  _Rationale:_ It becomes liquid water, it does not vanish.

**MST-2848-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO things would make a dish of water evaporate FASTER? (Select TWO.)

- A. Putting it in a warm, sunny spot **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: higher temperature speeds up evaporation.
- B. Spreading it into a wide, shallow dish **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a larger surface area speeds up evaporation.
- C. Covering it with a tight lid  
  _Rationale:_ A lid traps vapour and slows evaporation.
- D. Putting it in the fridge  
  _Rationale:_ Cooling slows evaporation down.

**MST-2848-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Drops of water appear on the outside of a cold drink on a warm day. What is this called?

- A. Condensation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: water vapour in the air cools and condenses into liquid on the cold glass.
- B. Melting  
  _Rationale:_ Melting is solid turning to liquid, not vapour to liquid.
- C. Freezing  
  _Rationale:_ Freezing is liquid turning to solid.
- D. The glass leaking  
  _Rationale:_ The water comes from vapour in the air, not from inside the glass.
