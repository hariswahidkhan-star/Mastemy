## Learning outcomes

1. Describe common weather types and the clues that signal them
2. Explain how the four seasons differ in temperature and daylight
3. Read a simple thermometer, rain gauge and weather symbol
4. Relate seasonal change to the Sun and the tilt of the Earth in simple terms
5. Keep a short weather record and spot a pattern
6. Choose suitable clothing and safety actions for given weather

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2831-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which instrument measures how much rain has fallen?

- A. A rain gauge **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a rain gauge collects and measures rainfall.
- B. A thermometer  
  _Rationale:_ A thermometer measures temperature, not rainfall.
- C. A compass  
  _Rationale:_ A compass shows direction.
- D. A ruler alone  
  _Rationale:_ A plain ruler does not collect rain to measure it.

**MST-2831-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why is it colder in winter than in summer in most places? (Select ONE)

- A. The Sun is lower in the sky and days are shorter, so less warmth reaches the ground **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the tilt lowers the Sun and shortens days in winter.
- B. The Earth moves much closer to the Sun in winter  
  _Rationale:_ Distance is not the cause; the tilt is.
- C. The Sun switches off in winter  
  _Rationale:_ The Sun keeps shining all year.
- D. Clouds block all the Sun every winter day  
  _Rationale:_ Clouds vary; they are not the reason for the season.

**MST-2831-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO clues that help you tell it is autumn in a cool country. (Select TWO)

- A. Leaves on many trees turn brown and fall **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: falling leaves are a common autumn sign.
- B. Days get shorter and cooler **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: shortening, cooling days signal autumn.
- C. Flowers first appear after the coldest months  
  _Rationale:_ That describes spring, not autumn.
- D. It is the hottest part of the whole year  
  _Rationale:_ The hottest part is summer, not autumn.
