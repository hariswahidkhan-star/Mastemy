## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0419-Q0001** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) A scatterplot shows a correlation coefficient r = 0.9 between hours studied and test score. The best interpretation is:

- A. There is a strong positive linear association **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: r near +1 indicates a strong positive linear relationship.
- B. Studying causes higher scores  
  _Rationale:_ Correlation alone does not establish causation.
- C. 90% of students improved  
  _Rationale:_ r is not a percentage of students.
- D. The relationship must be perfectly linear  
  _Rationale:_ r = 0.9 is strong but not perfect.

**MST-0419-Q0002** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) A 95% confidence interval for a mean is reported as (12, 18). The correct interpretation is:

- A. The method captures the true mean in 95% of repeated samples **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the confidence level refers to the long-run capture rate of the procedure.
- B. There is a 95% probability the true mean is between 12 and 18  
  _Rationale:_ The parameter is fixed; the probability statement applies to the procedure, not this one interval.
- C. 95% of the data lie between 12 and 18  
  _Rationale:_ A confidence interval is about the mean, not the spread of data.
- D. The sample mean is 95  
  _Rationale:_ 95% is the confidence level, not the sample mean.

**MST-0419-Q0003** (multiple-answer-selection, Select TWO) Which TWO reduce bias when collecting data for an inference about a population?

- A. Using random sampling **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: random sampling reduces selection bias.
- B. Random assignment in an experiment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: random assignment balances confounders across groups.
- C. Increasing a convenience sample's size  
  _Rationale:_ A larger convenience sample is still biased.
- D. Choosing only volunteers who feel strongly  
  _Rationale:_ Voluntary response introduces bias.
- E. Discarding data that disagree with the hypothesis  
  _Rationale:_ Selectively discarding data introduces bias.
