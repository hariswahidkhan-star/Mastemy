## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0379-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Effective stress in a saturated soil is best described as:

- A. Total stress minus pore water pressure **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: Terzaghi's principle, sigma-prime = sigma - u.
- B. Total stress plus pore water pressure  
  _Rationale:_ Pore pressure is subtracted, not added, to get effective stress.
- C. Pore water pressure alone  
  _Rationale:_ Effective stress is carried by the soil skeleton, not the water.
- D. The unit weight times the water depth  
  _Rationale:_ That expression is a pore pressure term, not effective stress.

**MST-0379-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Raising the groundwater table at a site, with total stress unchanged, generally causes the effective stress at a given depth to:

- A. Decrease **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: higher pore pressure reduces effective stress when total stress is unchanged.
- B. Increase  
  _Rationale:_ Higher pore pressure lowers, not raises, effective stress here.
- C. Remain exactly constant  
  _Rationale:_ Effective stress changes because pore pressure changes.
- D. Become negative in all soils  
  _Rationale:_ It decreases but does not generally become negative.

**MST-0379-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO factors generally tend to reduce the factor of safety of a soil slope?

- A. A rise in the groundwater/pore pressure within the slope **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: higher pore pressure reduces effective strength and stability.
- B. Increasing the slope angle (making it steeper) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a steeper slope increases the driving shear and lowers safety.
- C. Flattening the slope to a gentler angle  
  _Rationale:_ A gentler slope increases stability, not decreases it.
- D. Lowering the water table through drainage  
  _Rationale:_ Drainage raises effective strength and improves stability.
- E. Removing a surcharge load from the crest  
  _Rationale:_ Removing load at the crest tends to improve stability.
