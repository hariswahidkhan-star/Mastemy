## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the principles of whole-life asset management and costing
2. Describe the stages of an asset's life cycle
3. Analyse the financial implications of asset management decisions
4. Explain maintenance, risk and disposal considerations across the asset life

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1267-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which cost is NOT normally included in a whole-life cost model for an asset?

- A. Acquisition cost  
  _Rationale:_ Acquisition cost is a core component of whole-life cost.
- B. Operating and maintenance cost  
  _Rationale:_ Operating and maintenance costs are central to whole-life cost.
- C. Disposal/decommissioning cost  
  _Rationale:_ End-of-life disposal cost is part of whole-life cost.
- D. An unrelated department's marketing budget **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: unrelated overheads not driven by the asset are excluded from its whole-life cost.

**MST-1267-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) An appraisal technique that adjusts future cash flows for the time value of money uses:

- A. Simple payback  
  _Rationale:_ Simple payback ignores the time value of money.
- B. Discounting (e.g. NPV) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: discounting via NPV reflects the time value of money.
- C. Straight-line depreciation  
  _Rationale:_ Depreciation allocates cost over time for accounting, not appraisal discounting.
- D. Break-even volume  
  _Rationale:_ Break-even finds the units needed to cover cost, not discounted value.

**MST-1267-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO decisions belong to the operate/maintain stage of the asset life cycle?

- A. Scheduling preventive maintenance **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: preventive maintenance is an operate/maintain activity.
- B. Selecting the initial supplier and specification  
  _Rationale:_ That is part of the specify/acquire stage.
- C. Monitoring reliability and condition **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: condition monitoring occurs during operation.
- D. Agreeing the original purchase price  
  _Rationale:_ Price is agreed at acquisition, not during operation.
