## Module and lesson plan

### M01 — US GAAP framework  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 26%)_

- M01L01 — US GAAP, the FASB codification and the conceptual framework (120 min)
- M01L02 — Accrual accounting, qualitative characteristics and the statements (120 min)
- Worked applications: Classify transactions under accrual versus cash accounting; Map a transaction to the correct financial statement
- Common misconception addressed: Assuming US GAAP and IFRS treat every item identically

### M02 — Revenue recognition  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 26%)_

- M02L01 — The five-step revenue model (ASC 606) (120 min)
- M02L02 — Performance obligations and timing of recognition (120 min)
- Worked applications: Apply the five steps to a bundled product-and-service contract; Decide whether revenue is recognised over time or at a point in time
- Common misconception addressed: Recognising revenue when cash is received rather than when obligations are satisfied

### M03 — Assets and liabilities  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 24%)_

- M03L01 — Inventory, receivables and long-lived assets (120 min)
- M03L02 — Liabilities, provisions and leases overview (120 min)
- Worked applications: Compute depreciation and assess an asset for impairment indicators; Classify an obligation as current or non-current with the right measurement
- Common misconception addressed: Treating all expenditures as expenses instead of capitalising qualifying assets

### M04 — Presentation and disclosure  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 24%)_

- M04L01 — The balance sheet, income statement and cash flows (120 min)
- M04L02 — Notes, disclosures and comparability (120 min)
- Worked applications: Prepare a classified balance sheet from a trial balance; Draft a revenue-recognition disclosure note
- Common misconception addressed: Thinking disclosures are optional extras rather than part of fair presentation

## Integrative case

Prepare a set of US GAAP financial statements for a mid-size company: apply the conceptual framework, recognise revenue under the five-step model, account for common assets and liabilities, and present the required statements with appropriate disclosures.
