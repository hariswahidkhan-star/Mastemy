## Learning outcomes

1. Prepare a consolidated statement of financial position including goodwill and non-controlling interest
2. Prepare a consolidated statement of profit or loss with non-controlling interest
3. Eliminate intra-group balances, trading and unrealised profit
4. Account for mid-year acquisitions and fair-value adjustments on consolidation
5. Account for associates using the equity method within group statements
6. Account for changes in ownership, including step acquisitions and disposals

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A parent acquired a subsidiary part-way through the year with fair-value adjustments, trades with it and holds unrealised profit in inventory, has an intra-group loan, and also holds a significant-influence stake in an associate; prepare the consolidated statement of financial position and profit or loss with goodwill and non-controlling interest, perform the eliminations, equity-account the associate, and explain the effect of a later partial disposal.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2528-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) When preparing a consolidated statement of financial position, how are intra-group balances such as a loan between parent and subsidiary treated?

- A. They are eliminated in full on consolidation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: intra-group balances are eliminated in full.
- B. They are reported gross as external balances  
  _Rationale:_ Intra-group balances are eliminated, not shown as external.
- C. They are recognised only as a note  
  _Rationale:_ They are eliminated from the figures, not merely noted.
- D. They are added together and shown as goodwill  
  _Rationale:_ Intra-group loans are not goodwill.

**MST-2528-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO adjustments are required when a subsidiary is acquired part-way through the reporting year? (Select TWO.)

- A. Only the post-acquisition results of the subsidiary are included in consolidated profit **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: results are included from the acquisition date onward.
- B. The subsidiary's results are time-apportioned to the period after acquisition, assuming they arise evenly, unless indicated otherwise **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: post-acquisition results are time-apportioned where appropriate.
- C. A full year of the subsidiary's profit is consolidated regardless of acquisition date  
  _Rationale:_ Only post-acquisition profit is consolidated.
- D. The subsidiary's pre-acquisition profit is added to group retained earnings  
  _Rationale:_ Pre-acquisition profit is not added to group retained earnings.

**MST-2528-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A parent sells goods to its 80%-owned subsidiary at a profit, and some remain unsold in the subsidiary's inventory at year-end. Why must the unrealised profit be eliminated?

- A. Profit is only realised to the group when goods are sold outside the group, so unrealised intra-group profit must be removed **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: unrealised profit on intra-group inventory is eliminated until sold externally.
- B. Intra-group sales are illegal and must be reversed  
  _Rationale:_ Intra-group sales are not illegal; the issue is unrealised profit.
- C. Only the subsidiary's own profit is ever eliminated  
  _Rationale:_ Unrealised profit is eliminated regardless of which entity made the sale.
- D. The inventory must be written off entirely  
  _Rationale:_ Only the unrealised profit element is removed, not the whole inventory.
