## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1098-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An executive is triple-booked for 2 p.m.: a client renewal call, an internal 1:1, and a vendor demo. What is the best first action for the assistant?

- A. Rank the three by business impact and deadline, then reschedule the lowest-priority with a proposed new time **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: triage by impact and urgency and move the least time-critical item while offering an alternative keeps commitments intact.
- B. Decline all three and leave the slot free  
  _Rationale:_ Cancelling everything throws away commitments the executive may need to keep and solves nothing.
- C. Let the executive discover the clash and decide in the moment  
  _Rationale:_ Surfacing the conflict late removes the chance to rebook gracefully and reflects poorly on the executive.
- D. Attend one meeting yourself in the executive's place without asking  
  _Rationale:_ Standing in for the executive without authority oversteps the role and risks misrepresenting them.

**MST-1098-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select ALL that apply) Which practices keep a confidential reorganisation discreet while still doing the job well? (Select TWO)

- A. Store related documents with restricted access and neutral file names **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: limiting access and avoiding revealing names reduces accidental disclosure.
- B. Discuss only on a need-to-know basis and confirm what is shareable before acting **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: need-to-know plus confirming scope prevents premature leaks.
- C. Mention the project casually to colleagues to gauge reaction  
  _Rationale:_ Fishing for reactions leaks confidential information and breaches trust.
- D. Forward the planning thread to the whole team for transparency  
  _Rationale:_ Broad forwarding of confidential material is exactly the disclosure to avoid.

**MST-1098-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) What distinguishes useful meeting minutes from a transcript?

- A. Minutes capture decisions, owners and action items with dates **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: minutes exist so people know what was decided and who does what by when.
- B. Minutes record every sentence spoken verbatim  
  _Rationale:_ A word-for-word record is a transcript; it buries the decisions readers actually need.
- C. Minutes list only who attended  
  _Rationale:_ Attendance alone omits the decisions and actions that make minutes useful.
- D. Minutes are the executive's private notes never shared  
  _Rationale:_ Minutes are shared so participants and absentees can act on the outcomes.
