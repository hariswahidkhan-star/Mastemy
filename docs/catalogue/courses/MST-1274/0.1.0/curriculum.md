2. Administer the construction contract during construction

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1274-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) During construction, reviewing a contractor's submittal is intended to:

- A. Confirm general conformance with the design intent **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: submittal review checks conformance with the design intent and contract documents.
- B. Redesign the system on the contractor's behalf  
  _Rationale:_ Review is not redesign; the contractor remains responsible for means and methods.
- C. Guarantee the contractor's quantities and dimensions  
  _Rationale:_ The contractor is responsible for field dimensions and quantities.
- D. Set the contractor's labour rates  
  _Rationale:_ Labour rates are the contractor's commercial matter.

**MST-1274-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A punch list is prepared at which point?

- A. Before bidding  
  _Rationale:_ A punch list follows construction, not precedes bidding.
- B. At substantial completion **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the punch list of remaining items is made at substantial completion.
- C. During schematic design  
  _Rationale:_ Design precedes construction and any punch list.
- D. At the programming stage  
  _Rationale:_ Programming defines needs long before closeout.

**MST-1274-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are appropriate during construction observation?

- A. Documenting observed deficiencies against the documents **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: recording nonconformance is proper observation.
- B. Directing the contractor's means and methods  
  _Rationale:_ Means and methods are the contractor's responsibility, not the architect's.
- C. Reporting general conformance with the contract documents **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reporting on conformance is a core observation duty.
- D. Taking over site safety supervision  
  _Rationale:_ Site safety is the contractor's responsibility, not the observing architect's.
