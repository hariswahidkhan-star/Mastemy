## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1849-Q0001** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) The pinyin 'mā' (with a high level tone) most likely means:

- A. mother (妈) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: first-tone mā commonly corresponds to 妈 'mother'.
- B. horse (马)  
  _Rationale:_ 'horse' is third tone, mǎ, not first tone.
- C. to scold (骂)  
  _Rationale:_ 'to scold' is fourth tone, mà.
- D. hemp (麻)  
  _Rationale:_ 'hemp' is second tone, má.

**MST-1849-Q0002** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) Choose the correct measure word: 'yì ___ shū' (one ___ book).

- A. běn (本) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 本 is the measure word for books.
- B. zhāng (张)  
  _Rationale:_ 张 is used for flat objects like paper, not books.
- C. gè (个)  
  _Rationale:_ 个 is a general measure word but 本 is standard for books.
- D. tiáo (条)  
  _Rationale:_ 条 is for long, thin objects, not books.

**MST-1849-Q0003** (multiple-answer-selection, Select TWO) Which TWO sentences use correct basic Mandarin SVO word order?

- A. 我喝水 (I drink water) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: subject-verb-object order is correct.
- B. 他吃饭 (He eats rice) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: subject-verb-object order is correct.
- C. 水喝我 (Water drinks I)  
  _Rationale:_ This reverses subject and object incorrectly.
- D. 饭吃他 (Rice eats he)  
  _Rationale:_ This reverses subject and object incorrectly.
- E. 喝我水 (Drink I water)  
  _Rationale:_ The verb cannot precede the subject here.
