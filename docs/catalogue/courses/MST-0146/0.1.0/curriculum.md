## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the Scrum framework: team, events, artifacts and empiricism
2. Apply the Scrum Master core competencies to serve the team
3. Describe how the Scrum Master serves the Product Owner and organization

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0146-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Who is accountable for maximizing the value of the product resulting from the work of the Scrum Team?

- A. The Scrum Master  
  _Rationale:_ The Scrum Master is accountable for Scrum effectiveness, not product value.
- B. The Product Owner **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the Product Owner is accountable for maximizing product value and for the Product Backlog.
- C. The Developers  
  _Rationale:_ Developers are accountable for the Increment, not overall value maximization.
- D. The project manager  
  _Rationale:_ Scrum defines no project manager role.

**MST-0146-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A Daily Scrum has become a status meeting reported to the Scrum Master. What should the Scrum Master do?

- A. Keep running it as a status report  
  _Rationale:_ That perpetuates the anti-pattern.
- B. Coach the Developers to use it to plan their work toward the Sprint Goal **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the Daily Scrum is for the Developers to inspect progress and plan their next day's work toward the Sprint Goal.
- C. Cancel the Daily Scrum entirely  
  _Rationale:_ The event is valuable; the misuse should be corrected, not the event removed.
- D. Assign each developer their tasks for the day  
  _Rationale:_ The Scrum Master does not assign tasks; the team self-manages.

**MST-0146-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are ways a Scrum Master serves the organization? (Select TWO)

- A. Leading and coaching the organization in its Scrum adoption **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: coaching the wider organization in Scrum is a service to the organization.
- B. Helping remove barriers between stakeholders and Scrum Teams **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: removing organizational impediments is a service to the organization.
- C. Writing all the product's code  
  _Rationale:_ Coding is the Developers' work.
- D. Setting individual developer salaries  
  _Rationale:_ Compensation is not a Scrum Master accountability.
- E. Approving the Product Backlog order  
  _Rationale:_ Backlog ordering is the Product Owner's accountability.
