## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the professional practices of business continuity management
2. Apply programme governance, risk and BIA at an organisation-wide scale
3. Evaluate complex recovery strategies, crisis communications and coordination
4. Analyse programme assessment, maturity and continuous improvement

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0119-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) During a cross-site outage, two departments both claim first priority for a shared recovery team. The programme should resolve this using:

- A. Enterprise recovery priorities derived from the organisation-wide BIA **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: enterprise BIA-based priorities resolve competing claims objectively.
- B. Whoever shouts loudest  
  _Rationale:_ Ad hoc escalation is not a defensible method.
- C. Alphabetical department order  
  _Rationale:_ Alphabetical order ignores business impact.
- D. The newest manager's preference  
  _Rationale:_ Personal preference is not a priority basis.

**MST-0119-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A mature continuity programme treats crisis communications as something that should be:

- A. Pre-planned, with templates, spokespeople and approval paths ready **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: effective crisis communication is prepared in advance.
- B. Fully improvised in the moment  
  _Rationale:_ Improvisation increases errors under pressure.
- C. Left entirely to social media  
  _Rationale:_ Uncontrolled channels are not a plan.
- D. Avoided to prevent panic  
  _Rationale:_ Silence usually worsens a crisis.

**MST-0119-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO practices most improve an enterprise continuity programme's maturity? (Select TWO)

- A. Regular, scenario-based exercises across sites **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: realistic exercises raise readiness and maturity.
- B. Formal post-incident review feeding improvements **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: learning from incidents drives maturity.
- C. Keeping the programme static for years  
  _Rationale:_ Stagnation lowers maturity.
- D. Hiding incidents from leadership  
  _Rationale:_ Concealment prevents improvement.
- E. Testing only the easiest scenarios  
  _Rationale:_ Avoiding hard scenarios hides real gaps.
