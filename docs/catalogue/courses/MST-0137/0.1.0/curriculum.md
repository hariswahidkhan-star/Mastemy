## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0137-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) During a Sprint, a stakeholder pressures the Developers to add unplanned work directly. According to Scrum, who decides what the Developers work on within the Sprint?

- A. The Developers, in line with the Sprint Goal and in collaboration with the Product Owner **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the Developers manage the Sprint Backlog and decide how to meet the Sprint Goal.
- B. Any stakeholder who asks  
  _Rationale:_ Stakeholders do not directly assign work into a Sprint.
- C. The Scrum Master, by directive  
  _Rationale:_ The Scrum Master is a coach, not a work assigner.
- D. The most senior manager  
  _Rationale:_ Scrum teams are self-managing; seniority does not assign Sprint work.

**MST-0137-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the primary purpose of the Sprint Retrospective?

- A. To plan improvements to the team's process, interactions and tools **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the Retrospective inspects how the team worked and plans improvements.
- B. To demonstrate the Increment to stakeholders  
  _Rationale:_ That is the Sprint Review, not the Retrospective.
- C. To assign blame for defects  
  _Rationale:_ The Retrospective is for improvement, not blame.
- D. To create the initial Product Backlog  
  _Rationale:_ Backlog creation is ongoing product work, not the Retrospective's purpose.

**MST-0137-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO accountabilities on a Scrum Team as defined by the Scrum framework. (Select TWO.)

- A. Product Owner **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: Product Owner is one of the three Scrum accountabilities.
- B. Scrum Master **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: Scrum Master is one of the three Scrum accountabilities.
- C. Project Manager  
  _Rationale:_ Scrum does not define a Project Manager accountability.
- D. Release Train Engineer  
  _Rationale:_ That is a scaled-framework role, not a core Scrum accountability.
- E. Business Sponsor  
  _Rationale:_ Sponsor is a stakeholder, not a Scrum Team accountability.
