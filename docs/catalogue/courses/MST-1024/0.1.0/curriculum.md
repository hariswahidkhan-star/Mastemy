## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1024-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under the IFRS 15 five-step model, when is revenue recognised?

- A. As each performance obligation is satisfied by transferring control to the customer **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: revenue is recognised when control of the promised good or service transfers.
- B. When the invoice is issued, regardless of delivery  
  _Rationale:_ Invoicing does not determine recognition; transfer of control does.
- C. Only when cash is received  
  _Rationale:_ IFRS 15 is not a cash-basis model.
- D. Evenly over the entity's financial year  
  _Rationale:_ Timing follows satisfaction of obligations, not the calendar.

**MST-1024-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO conditions generally support recognising a provision under IAS 37? (Select TWO)

- A. A present obligation exists from a past event **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a provision requires a present obligation arising from a past event.
- B. An outflow of resources is probable and can be reliably estimated **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: probable outflow and a reliable estimate are required.
- C. The obligation is merely possible and not estimable  
  _Rationale:_ That is a contingent liability, disclosed not provided.
- D. Management simply prefers to smooth profits  
  _Rationale:_ Provisions cannot be used to smooth earnings.

**MST-1024-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under IAS 1, how should an asset expected to be realised within the normal operating cycle be classified?

- A. As a current asset **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: assets realised within the operating cycle or 12 months are current.
- B. Always as a non-current asset  
  _Rationale:_ Only items outside the cycle/12 months are non-current.
- C. As equity  
  _Rationale:_ It is an asset, not equity.
- D. As revenue  
  _Rationale:_ Classification concerns the balance sheet, not revenue.
