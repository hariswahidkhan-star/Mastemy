## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1025-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which TWO-part test determines the classification of a financial asset under IFRS 9?

- A. The business model test and the SPPI contractual cash flow test **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: classification depends on the business model and whether cash flows are solely principal and interest.
- B. The historical cost test and the market value test  
  _Rationale:_ These are not the IFRS 9 classification tests.
- C. The majority-ownership test and the control test  
  _Rationale:_ Those relate to consolidation, not IFRS 9 classification.
- D. The incurred-loss test and the fair-value test  
  _Rationale:_ Incurred loss is the superseded IAS 39 approach.

**MST-1025-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO statements about the IFRS 9 expected credit loss model are correct? (Select TWO)

- A. Losses are recognised before a default occurs **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ECL is forward-looking and recognised ahead of actual default.
- B. A significant increase in credit risk moves an exposure to lifetime ECL **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: Stage 2 (and 3) require lifetime ECL after a significant increase in credit risk.
- C. ECL is only recognised once a loan has defaulted  
  _Rationale:_ That is the old incurred-loss approach IFRS 9 replaced.
- D. All exposures always use 12-month ECL  
  _Rationale:_ Stages 2 and 3 use lifetime ECL.

**MST-1025-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) In an ECL calculation, what does LGD represent?

- A. Loss given default: the proportion of exposure expected to be lost if default occurs **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: LGD is the share of exposure not recovered after default.
- B. The probability that a borrower defaults  
  _Rationale:_ That is PD, probability of default.
- C. The total exposure at the time of default  
  _Rationale:_ That is EAD, exposure at default.
- D. The discount rate used for cash flows  
  _Rationale:_ The discount rate is a separate input, not LGD.
