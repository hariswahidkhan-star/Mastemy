## Learning outcomes

1. Link data strategy to business strategy and value
2. Assess data maturity and prioritise initiatives
3. Design operating models, roles and data ownership
4. Govern data for value, risk and compliance
5. Build a data culture and measure strategy outcomes

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A leadership team wants more value from its data. Assess the organisation's maturity, prioritise a roadmap tied to business goals, choose an operating model and ownership, set governance that enables access while controlling risk, and define the outcome metrics that will show whether the strategy is working.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1637-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What should a data strategy be anchored to first?

- A. The organisation's business goals and the value they require **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: data strategy exists to serve business objectives.
- B. The newest available technology  
  _Rationale:_ Technology follows strategy, not the reverse.
- C. The preferences of the largest vendor  
  _Rationale:_ Vendor preference is not a strategic anchor.
- D. The number of dashboards the company can build  
  _Rationale:_ Dashboard count is an activity metric, not a goal.

**MST-1637-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO reflect good data governance for a leader? (Select TWO.)

- A. Governance enables safe access, not only restriction **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: good governance balances enablement with control.
- B. Sensitive data gets controls proportional to its risk **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: risk-based controls match protection to sensitivity.
- C. All data is locked down equally regardless of sensitivity  
  _Rationale:_ Uniform lockdown blocks value and ignores risk differences.
- D. Outcomes are measured only by number of policies written  
  _Rationale:_ Policy count is not an outcome measure.

**MST-1637-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) An organisation with no reliable data foundations wants to launch advanced AI immediately. What is the sound advice?

- A. Build the data foundations first; sequence the roadmap realistically **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: advanced initiatives depend on mature foundations.
- B. Skip foundations and deploy AI now  
  _Rationale:_ Advanced work on weak foundations tends to fail.
- C. Copy a competitor's roadmap exactly  
  _Rationale:_ Roadmaps must fit the organisation's own maturity.
- D. Measure success by tools purchased  
  _Rationale:_ Tool purchases are not outcomes.
