## Learning outcomes

1. Use Gemini across Workspace apps productively
2. Write effective prompts with clear structure
3. Work with multimodal inputs such as images and files
4. Ground responses in your own documents and data
5. Verify, refine and iterate on outputs
6. Apply responsible-use and data-handling practices

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Selection items assess knowledge and applied reasoning only; they do not assess hands-on performance in the product, which is taught through instructor-built walkthroughs.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0736-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the main benefit of grounding a Gemini response in your own Drive documents?

- A. Answers draw on your specific content rather than only general knowledge **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: grounding ties the response to your documents.
- B. It guarantees the answer is always factually correct  
  _Rationale:_ Grounding improves relevance but does not guarantee correctness.
- C. It makes the model train on your data permanently  
  _Rationale:_ Grounding is retrieval at answer time, not training.
- D. It removes the need to review the output  
  _Rationale:_ Review is still required regardless of grounding.

**MST-0736-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which element most improves a prompt's reliability for a specific task?

- A. Clear task, context and desired output format **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: structure and context make outputs more reliable.
- B. Maximum possible length regardless of relevance  
  _Rationale:_ Length alone does not improve reliability.
- C. All-capital letters for emphasis  
  _Rationale:_ Capitalisation does not meaningfully change reliability.
- D. Removing all context to keep it short  
  _Rationale:_ Removing context usually degrades results.

**MST-0736-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are good responsible-use practices when using Gemini for work? (Select TWO.)

- A. Verify facts and figures before acting on them **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: verification guards against errors.
- B. Avoid pasting data you are not permitted to share **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: respect confidentiality and data-handling rules.
- C. Assume every output is confidential automatically  
  _Rationale:_ Confidentiality depends on context and settings, not assumption.
- D. Skip human review for customer-facing messages  
  _Rationale:_ Customer-facing content needs human review.
