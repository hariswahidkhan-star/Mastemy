## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0005-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In limiting factor analysis with one scarce resource, products should be ranked by:

- A. Contribution per unit of the limiting factor **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ranking by contribution per unit of the scarce resource maximises total contribution.
- B. Contribution per unit of product  
  _Rationale:_ This ignores the scarce resource usage.
- C. Selling price per unit  
  _Rationale:_ Selling price alone ignores cost and resource usage.
- D. Total fixed cost absorbed  
  _Rationale:_ Fixed cost absorption is not the ranking basis.

**MST-0005-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A favourable sales price variance arises when:

- A. The actual selling price exceeds the standard selling price **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a higher actual price than standard gives a favourable price variance.
- B. Actual sales volume exceeds budget  
  _Rationale:_ That is a volume variance, not a price variance.
- C. Actual costs are below standard  
  _Rationale:_ That relates to cost variances.
- D. The standard price is revised upwards  
  _Rationale:_ Revising the standard is not itself a variance.

**MST-0005-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO non-financial performance indicators relevant to the 'customer' perspective of a balanced scorecard.

- A. Customer satisfaction score **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: satisfaction is a customer-perspective measure.
- B. On-time delivery rate **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: on-time delivery reflects customer experience.
- C. Return on capital employed  
  _Rationale:_ ROCE is a financial-perspective measure.
- D. Gross profit margin  
  _Rationale:_ Margin is a financial measure.
- E. Employee training hours  
  _Rationale:_ Training hours sit in the learning and growth perspective.
