## Learning outcomes

1. Describe core warehouse processes from receiving to dispatch
2. Design storage, slotting and layout for flow and accuracy
3. Manage inventory accuracy, cycle counting and stock control
4. Plan picking, packing and order fulfilment methods
5. Measure warehouse performance and improve productivity and safety

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1165-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) To cut picker travel time in a warehouse, how should fast-moving (A-class) SKUs generally be slotted?

- A. Close to dispatch and in easy-reach 'golden zone' locations **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: placing high-velocity items near shipping and at ergonomic heights minimises travel and strain.
- B. In the furthest aisles to keep dispatch clear  
  _Rationale:_ Distant slotting increases travel for the most-picked items.
- C. Spread evenly so no aisle is busy  
  _Rationale:_ Even spreading ignores velocity and raises average travel for popular SKUs.
- D. On the top racking levels to save floor space  
  _Rationale:_ Top levels slow picking and add safety risk for high-velocity items.

**MST-1165-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are advantages of ABC cycle counting over a single annual physical inventory? (Select TWO.)

- A. It finds and fixes errors continuously through the year **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: frequent counting surfaces process causes early rather than once a year.
- B. It counts high-value or fast-moving items more often **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ABC focuses effort where inaccuracy costs the most.
- C. It removes the need to investigate discrepancies  
  _Rationale:_ Discrepancies still need root-cause investigation; counting only detects them.
- D. It guarantees 100% accuracy at all times  
  _Rationale:_ No counting method guarantees perfect accuracy; it manages and improves it.

**MST-1165-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A warehouse has many orders that each contain the same few SKUs during a promotion. Which picking method is usually most efficient?

- A. Batch picking **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: batching groups many orders for the same SKUs into one pick trip, cutting travel.
- B. Single-order picking  
  _Rationale:_ Picking one order at a time repeats travel for the same SKUs.
- C. Zone picking with no batching  
  _Rationale:_ Zoning alone still walks each order separately within the zone.
- D. Random picking  
  _Rationale:_ Unstructured picking raises travel and error, especially at volume.
