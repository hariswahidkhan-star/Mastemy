## Learning outcomes

1. Apply ethics, professional responsibilities and general audit principles
2. Assess risk and develop a planned response
3. Perform further procedures and obtain audit evidence
4. Form conclusions and prepare the audit report

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.
- Format gap: The official CPA Exam includes task-based simulations (document review, journal entries, spreadsheet work). Mastemy practice uses MCQ/MR only and does NOT assess task-based simulation skills.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0016-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An audit firm's partner owns shares in the audit client. Under independence rules this is:

- A. A prohibited financial interest impairing independence **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a direct financial interest in a client impairs independence.
- B. Allowed if the shares are small  
  _Rationale:_ A direct financial interest impairs independence regardless of size.
- C. Allowed if disclosed in the report  
  _Rationale:_ Disclosure does not cure a prohibited financial interest.
- D. Only a concern for tax staff  
  _Rationale:_ It affects the firm’s independence on the engagement.

**MST-0016-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Performance materiality is set:

- A. Below overall materiality to reduce aggregation risk **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: performance materiality is set below overall materiality for aggregation risk.
- B. Equal to overall materiality  
  _Rationale:_ Setting it equal ignores aggregation risk.
- C. Above overall materiality  
  _Rationale:_ It is never set above overall materiality.
- D. At zero for all audits  
  _Rationale:_ Zero is impractical and incorrect.

**MST-0016-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO situations typically lead to a modified audit opinion? (Select TWO)

- A. A material and pervasive misstatement management will not correct **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a material, pervasive misstatement leads to an adverse opinion.
- B. An inability to obtain sufficient appropriate evidence that is material and pervasive **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a pervasive scope limitation leads to a disclaimer.
- C. Management provides all requested evidence and records are clean  
  _Rationale:_ That supports an unmodified opinion.
- D. An immaterial classification error that is corrected  
  _Rationale:_ Immaterial, corrected items do not modify the opinion.
