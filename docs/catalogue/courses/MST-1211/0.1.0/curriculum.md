## Learning outcomes

1. Explain realistic AI use cases across marketing
2. Use AI tools and control voice and grounding
3. Run content operations with AI and quality control
4. Use AI to support strategy and audience work
5. Use AI to support measurement and optimisation
6. Govern AI marketing use ethically and safely

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1211-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is a realistic role for AI in marketing?

- A. Supporting research, drafting and testing, with human strategy and review **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI supports; strategy and review stay human.
- B. Replacing marketing strategy entirely  
  _Rationale:_ Strategy and judgement remain human work.
- C. Guaranteeing campaign success  
  _Rationale:_ AI does not guarantee outcomes.
- D. Removing the need to understand the audience  
  _Rationale:_ Audience understanding is still essential.

**MST-1211-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO quality gates protect an AI-assisted content pipeline? (Select TWO.)

- A. A factual-accuracy check before publishing **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI can state wrong facts; checking protects quality.
- B. A brand-voice and tone review **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a voice review keeps output on-brand.
- C. Publishing everything the tool generates unedited  
  _Rationale:_ Unedited output risks errors and off-brand content.
- D. Optimising only to a vanity metric  
  _Rationale:_ Vanity metrics mislead optimisation.

**MST-1211-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) When should AI use in a marketing deliverable be disclosed?

- A. When disclosure is required by policy, platform rules or audience expectation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: disclosure follows policy, platform rules and audience expectation.
- B. Never, to avoid questions  
  _Rationale:_ Non-disclosure can breach rules and trust.
- C. Only if the content performs badly  
  _Rationale:_ Performance does not determine disclosure duty.
- D. Only on internal documents  
  _Rationale:_ Disclosure obligations can apply to external content.
