## Learning outcomes

1. Define artificial intelligence and distinguish it from ordinary automation
2. Describe in plain terms how modern AI systems produce their outputs
3. Give everyday examples of where AI is already in use
4. Explain what current AI can and cannot reliably do
5. Discuss AI in conversation without common misunderstandings

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1304-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which statement best describes what most modern AI systems do?

- A. They find and output statistically likely patterns learned from data **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: modern AI predicts likely outputs from learned patterns.
- B. They look up a single stored correct answer for each question  
  _Rationale:_ They do not store answers; they generate likely outputs.
- C. They follow only hand-written if-then rules a programmer typed  
  _Rationale:_ That describes rule-based automation, not modern learned AI.
- D. They consciously understand questions the way a person does  
  _Rationale:_ Current AI has no human-like understanding.

**MST-1304-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are realistic examples of AI already in everyday use? (Select TWO.)

- A. Product recommendations in an online shop **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: recommender systems are a common applied AI.
- B. Spam filtering in email **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: spam filters use learned classification.
- C. A basic calculator adding two numbers  
  _Rationale:_ Fixed arithmetic is not learned AI.
- D. A light switch turning on a bulb  
  _Rationale:_ A simple electrical circuit involves no AI.

**MST-1304-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A colleague says 'the AI is never wrong'. What is the most accurate reply?

- A. AI output can be fluent yet factually wrong, so it needs checking **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: fluency is not accuracy.
- B. AI is correct whenever it answers confidently  
  _Rationale:_ Confidence does not guarantee correctness.
- C. AI only makes mistakes if the power fails  
  _Rationale:_ Errors come from the model, not hardware faults.
- D. AI cannot make mistakes because it uses maths  
  _Rationale:_ Probabilistic methods still produce errors.
