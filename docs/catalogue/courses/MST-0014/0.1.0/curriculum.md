## Learning outcomes

1. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Knowledge and understanding of the UK tax system through TX' to exam-style problems
2. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Income tax and national insurance contributions' to exam-style problems
3. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Chargeable gains and capital gains tax' to exam-style problems
4. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Inheritance tax' to exam-style problems
5. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Corporation tax' to exam-style problems
6. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Value added tax, stamp taxes and the impact of taxes' to exam-style problems
7. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Tax planning, ethics and communicating with clients' to exam-style problems

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Official ACCA exams use constructed-response and computational item formats (written answers, calculations, OT case scenarios) that this platform's MCQ/MR forms do not reproduce; see the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0014-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) When advising a client, a tax adviser who becomes aware of a client's deliberate error in a past return should first:

- A. Advise the client to disclose the error to the tax authority **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the adviser should recommend disclosure and may cease to act if the client refuses.
- B. Ignore the error as it relates to a past period  
  _Rationale:_ A deliberate error cannot be ignored.
- C. Report the client immediately to the police  
  _Rationale:_ The first step is to advise disclosure, following professional guidance.
- D. Amend the return without telling the client  
  _Rationale:_ The adviser should not act without the client's knowledge and authority.

**MST-0014-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which relief may reduce a chargeable gain when a qualifying business asset is sold and the proceeds reinvested in a new qualifying asset?

- A. Rollover (replacement of business assets) relief **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: rollover relief defers the gain where proceeds are reinvested in qualifying assets.
- B. Private residence relief  
  _Rationale:_ That relief applies to an individual's main home.
- C. Annual exempt amount  
  _Rationale:_ The annual exempt amount is a fixed exemption, not a reinvestment relief.
- D. Gift relief on all assets automatically  
  _Rationale:_ Gift relief applies to gifts of business assets, not reinvestment of sale proceeds.

**MST-0014-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO factors that are relevant when advising on the choice between operating as a sole trader or through a company.

- A. The overall tax and national insurance cost of extracting profits **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the combined tax/NIC cost of profit extraction is a key factor.
- B. The administrative and filing obligations of each structure **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: compliance burden differs between the two and is relevant.
- C. The colour of the business logo  
  _Rationale:_ This is irrelevant to the tax structuring decision.
- D. The personal hobbies of the owner  
  _Rationale:_ Irrelevant to the structuring decision.
- E. The weather in the trading location  
  _Rationale:_ Irrelevant to the tax decision.
