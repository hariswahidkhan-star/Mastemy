## Learning outcomes

1. Describe macronutrients and their roles in energy and structure
2. Explain micronutrients, their functions and deficiency signs
3. Interpret energy balance, metabolism and body-composition basics
4. Read food labels and apply dietary reference intakes
5. Explain evidence-based dietary patterns and their trade-offs
6. Apply nutrition concepts to life stages and common conditions

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1768-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which statement about dietary fat is accurate?

- A. Unsaturated fats can support health while trans fats are best minimised **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: fat quality matters, not just quantity.
- B. All dietary fat should be eliminated  
  _Rationale:_ Essential fatty acids are required.
- C. Saturated and trans fats are nutritionally identical  
  _Rationale:_ They differ in health effects.
- D. Fat provides no usable energy  
  _Rationale:_ Fat provides about 9 kcal per gram.

**MST-1768-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are fat-soluble vitamins? (Select TWO.)

- A. Vitamin A **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: A is fat-soluble (A, D, E, K).
- B. Vitamin D **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: D is fat-soluble.
- C. Vitamin C  
  _Rationale:_ Vitamin C is water-soluble.
- D. Vitamin B12  
  _Rationale:_ B12 is water-soluble.

**MST-1768-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A label lists values 'per serving' and the package holds 2.5 servings. A buyer eats the whole package. To judge intake they should:

- A. Multiply the per-serving values by 2.5 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: total intake scales with servings consumed.
- B. Use the per-serving values as the total  
  _Rationale:_ That understates intake 2.5-fold.
- C. Ignore serving size entirely  
  _Rationale:_ Serving size is the basis for every value.
- D. Assume the package equals one serving  
  _Rationale:_ The label states 2.5 servings.
