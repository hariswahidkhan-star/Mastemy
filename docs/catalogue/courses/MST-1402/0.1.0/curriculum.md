## Learning outcomes

1. Explain how AI is changing tasks, jobs and skills
2. Assess which work AI augments, automates or leaves unchanged
3. Plan reskilling and workforce transition
4. Recognise fairness, well-being and inclusion impacts
5. Judge responsible strategies for AI adoption at work

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1402-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why is a task-level view more useful than a whole-job view when assessing AI impact?

- A. Most jobs are a mix of tasks, only some of which AI affects **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI typically changes specific tasks, not entire roles at once.
- B. Every job is fully automated or not at all  
  _Rationale:_ Jobs are rarely all-or-nothing for automation.
- C. Tasks never change with new technology  
  _Rationale:_ Tasks shift as technology changes.
- D. Whole-job views are always more accurate  
  _Rationale:_ Whole-job views obscure task-level nuance.

**MST-1402-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO practices support a responsible AI workforce transition? (Select TWO.)

- A. Communicate changes clearly and early to affected people **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: transparent communication builds trust during change.
- B. Offer reskilling pathways for affected roles **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reskilling helps people move with the change.
- C. Automate roles silently without notice  
  _Rationale:_ Silent automation erodes trust and fairness.
- D. Assume everyone adapts without support  
  _Rationale:_ People need support to transition.

**MST-1402-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) An AI rollout may benefit some workers more than others. What should leaders do?

- A. Assess fairness, access and inclusion impacts and address gaps **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: responsible adoption checks for and closes equity gaps.
- B. Assume benefits are shared equally  
  _Rationale:_ Benefits are often uneven and need checking.
- C. Ignore impacts on lower-paid roles  
  _Rationale:_ Equity impacts must be considered.
- D. Focus only on productivity gains  
  _Rationale:_ People impact matters alongside productivity.
