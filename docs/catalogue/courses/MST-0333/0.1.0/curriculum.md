## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0333-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) On a construction site, the hierarchy of controls is applied to a recurring fall hazard at an unprotected leading edge. Which control should a supervisor pursue first?

- A. Eliminate the hazard by redesigning the task so work at the edge is not required **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: elimination sits at the top of the hierarchy of controls and removes the hazard rather than relying on the worker.
- B. Issue personal fall-arrest harnesses to the crew  
  _Rationale:_ PPE is the lowest level of the hierarchy and is used only when higher-order controls are not feasible.
- C. Post a warning sign at the leading edge  
  _Rationale:_ Administrative controls rank below elimination, substitution and engineering controls.
- D. Hold a toolbox talk about fall awareness  
  _Rationale:_ Training is an administrative control and does not remove the hazard.

**MST-0333-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A supervisor is determining who is responsible for inspecting a scaffold before each work shift. Which approach is consistent with a competent-person requirement?

- A. A person able to identify hazards and authorized to take prompt corrective action inspects it **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a competent person is defined by both the ability to recognize hazards and the authority to correct them.
- B. Any available worker performs the inspection to save time  
  _Rationale:_ Inspection by an untrained worker does not satisfy the competent-person standard.
- C. Inspection is only required weekly once the scaffold is built  
  _Rationale:_ Scaffolds require inspection before each shift and after events that could affect integrity, not merely weekly.
- D. The equipment rental company is solely responsible on site  
  _Rationale:_ Responsibility on the active work site rests with the employer's competent person, not the supplier.

**MST-0333-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO conditions that, by themselves, generally require protective systems before workers enter an excavation.

- A. The excavation is 5 feet (1.5 m) or more deep in soil that is not stable rock **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: depth at or above this threshold in non-stable-rock soil generally triggers a protective system requirement.
- B. A competent person identifies signs of a potential cave-in at any depth **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: observed cave-in indicators require protection regardless of the depth threshold.
- C. The excavation will be open for less than one hour  
  _Rationale:_ Short duration alone does not remove the need for a protective system.
- D. Only experienced workers will enter the excavation  
  _Rationale:_ Worker experience does not substitute for sloping, shoring or shielding.
- E. The spoil pile is placed at the edge of the excavation  
  _Rationale:_ Placing spoil at the edge increases risk; it is a hazard, not a condition that makes protection optional.
