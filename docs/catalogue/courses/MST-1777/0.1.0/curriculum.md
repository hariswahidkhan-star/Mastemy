## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the first-aider's role, scene safety and priorities
2. Explain primary assessment and the recovery position
3. Describe the principles of CPR and choking response
4. Explain management of bleeding, shock and wounds
5. Describe response to burns, fractures and environmental injury
6. Explain recognition of common medical emergencies

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A factory safety officer reviews a near-miss where a worker collapsed. Using first-aid theory, they reconstruct the correct sequence from scene safety through primary assessment and summoning help, identify what was done out of order, and note where accredited hands-on skills would be required in practice.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1777-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) On reaching a collapsed person outdoors, the first action is to:

- A. Check the scene for danger to yourself and others **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: scene safety comes before casualty contact.
- B. Begin chest compressions immediately  
  _Rationale:_ Assessment and safety precede any intervention.
- C. Move the person to a vehicle  
  _Rationale:_ Unnecessary movement can cause harm.
- D. Give food or water  
  _Rationale:_ Not indicated and potentially dangerous.

**MST-1777-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are appropriate for controlling severe external bleeding? (Select TWO.)

- A. Apply firm direct pressure to the wound **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: direct pressure is the first-line control.
- B. Call emergency services **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: severe bleeding needs emergency help.
- C. Remove a deeply embedded object to clean the wound  
  _Rationale:_ Embedded objects should be stabilised, not removed.
- D. Give the casualty a hot drink  
  _Rationale:_ Not a bleeding-control measure.

**MST-1777-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) An unresponsive adult is breathing normally. The appropriate theory-based action is to:

- A. Place them in the recovery position and monitor **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: protects the airway while help is sought.
- B. Begin CPR  
  _Rationale:_ CPR is for a casualty not breathing normally.
- C. Sit them upright against a wall  
  _Rationale:_ Does not protect the airway if they deteriorate.
- D. Leave them supine and walk away  
  _Rationale:_ The airway may become obstructed.
