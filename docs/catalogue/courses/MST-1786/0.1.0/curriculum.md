## Learning outcomes

1. Logistics and the supply chain
2. Warehousing and storage
3. Inventory in logistics
4. Transport and distribution
5. Logistics performance

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Selection items cannot demonstrate operating a live warehouse or fleet; hands-on practice belongs on site.

## Integrative case

A retailer faces late deliveries and rising logistics cost. The learner must map the flow, redesign warehouse picking, set reorder points and safety stock, choose transport modes and a network, and define KPIs, then recommend a plan that balances cost, service and lead time.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1786-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Safety stock exists primarily to:

- A. Buffer against variability in demand and lead time **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: safety stock absorbs uncertainty to protect service levels.
- B. Replace the need for any reorder point  
  _Rationale:_ Safety stock complements, not replaces, the reorder point.
- C. Maximise the amount of cash tied up in stock  
  _Rationale:_ Tying up cash is a cost, not the purpose of safety stock.
- D. Guarantee zero holding cost  
  _Rationale:_ Safety stock increases holding cost; it does not remove it.

**MST-1786-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO trade-offs matter when choosing a transport mode? (Select TWO.)

- A. Cost per unit shipped **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: cost is a central mode-selection factor.
- B. Transit time and reliability **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: speed and reliability trade off against cost.
- C. The colour of the delivery vehicle  
  _Rationale:_ Vehicle colour is irrelevant to the decision.
- D. The driver's favourite route  
  _Rationale:_ Personal preference is not a selection criterion.

**MST-1786-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Logistics is best described as:

- A. The management of the flow and storage of goods, services and information **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: logistics manages movement and storage across the chain.
- B. Only the long-haul shipping of containers  
  _Rationale:_ Shipping is one part of logistics, not the whole.
- C. The accounting function of a company  
  _Rationale:_ Logistics is operational, not accounting.
- D. The design of the product itself  
  _Rationale:_ Product design is engineering, not logistics.
