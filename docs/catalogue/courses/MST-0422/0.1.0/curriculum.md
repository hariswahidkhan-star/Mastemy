## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0422-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A profit-maximising firm in a competitive market should produce the output where:

- A. Marginal revenue equals marginal cost **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: profit is maximised where marginal revenue equals marginal cost.
- B. Total revenue is highest  
  _Rationale:_ Maximum total revenue ignores costs and does not maximise profit.
- C. Average total cost is lowest  
  _Rationale:_ Minimum average cost does not generally maximise profit.
- D. Price equals average fixed cost  
  _Rationale:_ This is not the profit-maximising condition.

**MST-0422-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) If a good has inelastic demand and its price rises, total revenue will:

- A. Increase **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: with inelastic demand, quantity falls proportionally less than price rises, so revenue increases.
- B. Decrease  
  _Rationale:_ Revenue falls when demand is elastic, not inelastic.
- C. Stay exactly constant  
  _Rationale:_ Revenue is constant only with unit elasticity.
- D. Fall to zero  
  _Rationale:_ Quantity demanded falls only slightly, so revenue does not vanish.

**MST-0422-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are features of a pure public good?

- A. Non-rivalry in consumption **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: one person's use does not reduce availability to others.
- B. Non-excludability **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it is difficult to exclude non-payers from benefiting.
- C. Perfect excludability through pricing  
  _Rationale:_ Public goods are hard to exclude, so pricing them is difficult.
- D. Consumption by one person uses it up for others  
  _Rationale:_ That describes a rival (private) good.
- E. It is always provided efficiently by markets  
  _Rationale:_ Public goods tend to be under-provided by markets.
