## Learning outcomes

1. Explain technology foresight and why organisations scan for emerging tech
2. Apply methods such as horizon scanning, trends and signals
3. Use frameworks like the hype cycle and technology readiness levels critically
4. Build and use scenarios to stress-test strategy
5. Assess emerging technologies for relevance, risk and timing
6. Communicate foresight to decision-makers and avoid common biases

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2768-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) How should a strategist use the 'hype cycle' when assessing an emerging technology?

- A. As a critical narrative aid about expectation patterns, not a precise predictor of timing or success **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the hype cycle illustrates expectation dynamics but does not reliably predict outcomes or dates.
- B. As an exact forecast of when a technology will become profitable  
  _Rationale:_ It is not a precise timing or profitability forecast.
- C. As proof that any hyped technology will certainly succeed  
  _Rationale:_ Hype does not guarantee success; many hyped technologies fade.
- D. As a reason to ignore all new technologies  
  _Rationale:_ It is an analytical aid, not a reason to dismiss everything.

**MST-2768-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are sound foresight practices? (Select TWO.)

- A. Triangulating weak signals across multiple independent sources **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: corroborating signals across sources reduces false positives.
- B. Developing multiple scenarios to stress-test strategy **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: scenarios test robustness against several plausible futures.
- C. Committing fully to a single predicted future  
  _Rationale:_ Over-committing to one forecast is fragile under uncertainty.
- D. Treating a single viral headline as confirmed fact  
  _Rationale:_ One unverified headline is not a validated signal.

**MST-2768-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why are scenarios, rather than a single forecast, valuable in technology foresight?

- A. They stress-test strategy against several plausible futures, exposing risks and robust options **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: multiple scenarios reveal which choices hold up across different futures.
- B. Because they predict exactly which future will occur  
  _Rationale:_ Scenarios are not point predictions of the one true future.
- C. Because they remove all uncertainty from planning  
  _Rationale:_ Scenarios manage uncertainty; they do not eliminate it.
- D. Because they let you ignore risks entirely  
  _Rationale:_ Scenarios surface risks rather than letting you ignore them.
