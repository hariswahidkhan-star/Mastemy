## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0032-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which approach best describes transformational leadership?

- A. Inspiring followers around a shared vision and raising their motivation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: transformational leaders use vision, inspiration and individual development to raise motivation.
- B. Exchanging rewards strictly for completed tasks  
  _Rationale:_ That describes transactional leadership, based on contingent reward.
- C. Avoiding decisions and intervening only when problems escalate  
  _Rationale:_ That describes laissez-faire or management-by-exception leadership.
- D. Enforcing rules through close supervision and sanctions  
  _Rationale:_ That is a controlling, autocratic style, not transformational.

**MST-0032-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A project is behind schedule and over budget. Which project-control concept best quantifies the schedule position?

- A. Schedule variance from earned value analysis **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: schedule variance (earned value minus planned value) measures the schedule position in cost terms.
- B. The organisation's gearing ratio  
  _Rationale:_ Gearing is a financing measure unrelated to project schedule performance.
- C. The product's contribution margin  
  _Rationale:_ Contribution margin concerns profitability per unit, not project schedule.
- D. The company's dividend cover  
  _Rationale:_ Dividend cover is a distribution metric, not a project-control measure.

**MST-0032-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO factors that typically increase resistance to organisational change.

- A. Fear of loss of status or job security **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: perceived personal loss is a well-documented driver of resistance.
- B. Poor communication about the reasons for the change **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: lack of clear communication breeds uncertainty and resistance.
- C. Early involvement of staff in designing the change  
  _Rationale:_ Participation tends to reduce, not increase, resistance.
- D. Clear demonstration of personal benefits  
  _Rationale:_ Showing personal benefit reduces resistance.
- E. Strong, trusted leadership of the change  
  _Rationale:_ Trusted leadership lowers resistance.
