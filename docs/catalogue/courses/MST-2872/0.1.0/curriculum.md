## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the structure and function of key organ systems
2. Explain homeostasis with examples
3. Describe how the nervous and endocrine systems coordinate the body
4. Explain gas exchange and transport in the body
5. Describe how the body defends against disease
6. Relate lifestyle choices to long-term health

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2872-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What does homeostasis refer to?

- A. Keeping the body's internal conditions stable **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: homeostasis maintains stable internal conditions.
- B. Growing taller over time  
  _Rationale:_ That is growth, not homeostasis.
- C. Changing species over generations  
  _Rationale:_ That describes evolution, not homeostasis.
- D. Digesting food in the stomach  
  _Rationale:_ Digestion is one process, not the whole idea of homeostasis.

**MST-2872-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which system uses hormones carried in the blood to coordinate the body?

- A. The endocrine system **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the endocrine system uses hormones in the blood.
- B. The nervous system  
  _Rationale:_ The nervous system uses electrical impulses, not hormones in blood.
- C. The skeletal system  
  _Rationale:_ The skeletal system supports the body, not chemical coordination.
- D. The digestive system  
  _Rationale:_ The digestive system processes food.

**MST-2872-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO help the body defend against disease? (Select TWO.)

- A. White blood cells **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: white blood cells attack pathogens.
- B. The skin as a barrier **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: skin is a physical barrier against pathogens.
- C. Red blood cells carrying oxygen  
  _Rationale:_ Red blood cells carry oxygen; they do not fight disease.
- D. Bones producing saliva  
  _Rationale:_ Bones do not produce saliva or fight disease this way.
