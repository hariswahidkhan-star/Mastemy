## Learning outcomes

1. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'The conceptual and regulatory framework for financial reporting' to exam-style problems
2. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Accounting for transactions in financial statements' to exam-style problems
3. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Analysing and interpreting the financial statements of single entities and groups' to exam-style problems
4. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Preparation of financial statements, including consolidated statements' to exam-style problems

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Official ACCA exams use constructed-response and computational item formats (written answers, calculations, OT case scenarios) that this platform's MCQ/MR forms do not reproduce; see the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0007-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under IFRS, which measurement basis records an asset at the amount of cash that would be received to sell it in an orderly transaction?

- A. Fair value **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: fair value is the price to sell an asset in an orderly transaction between market participants.
- B. Historical cost  
  _Rationale:_ Historical cost is the original amount paid.
- C. Present value  
  _Rationale:_ Present value discounts future cash flows.
- D. Replacement cost  
  _Rationale:_ Replacement cost is the amount to replace an asset.

**MST-0007-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) When a parent acquires a subsidiary, goodwill on consolidation is calculated as:

- A. Consideration plus non-controlling interest less the fair value of net assets acquired **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: this is the standard goodwill computation on acquisition.
- B. Consideration less share capital of the subsidiary  
  _Rationale:_ This ignores fair values and NCI.
- C. Fair value of net assets less consideration  
  _Rationale:_ This reverses the computation.
- D. Retained earnings of the subsidiary at acquisition  
  _Rationale:_ Retained earnings alone do not measure goodwill.

**MST-0007-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO items that are presented within other comprehensive income under IFRS.

- A. A revaluation surplus on property, plant and equipment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: PPE revaluation gains are recognised in OCI.
- B. Remeasurements of a defined benefit pension plan **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: defined benefit remeasurements are in OCI.
- C. Cost of sales  
  _Rationale:_ Cost of sales is in profit or loss.
- D. Revenue from contracts with customers  
  _Rationale:_ Revenue is in profit or loss.
- E. Finance costs on borrowings  
  _Rationale:_ Finance costs are in profit or loss.
