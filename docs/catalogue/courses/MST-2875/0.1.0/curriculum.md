## Learning outcomes

1. Hold a book correctly and follow print from left to right, top to bottom
2. Use letter sounds and blending to decode unfamiliar words
3. Read a growing set of common words on sight
4. Use picture clues to support understanding of a story
5. Retell the main events of a simple story in order
6. Build reading confidence and stamina with short decodable books

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2875-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) When you read English, which way do your eyes move along a line of words?

- A. Left to right **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: English is read from left to right.
- B. Right to left  
  _Rationale:_ That is the direction for some other languages, not English.
- C. Bottom to top  
  _Rationale:_ We move down the page, not up it.
- D. In a circle  
  _Rationale:_ Lines of text are read straight across, not in circles.

**MST-2875-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) You come to a word you do not know. What is the best first thing to try?

- A. Sound out the letters and blend them **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: decoding the sounds is the best first strategy.
- B. Skip the word and never look back  
  _Rationale:_ Skipping it loses the meaning of the sentence.
- C. Say any word that starts the same  
  _Rationale:_ Guessing from the first letter often gives the wrong word.
- D. Close the book  
  _Rationale:_ Giving up does not help you read the word.

**MST-2875-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO things help you understand a story while you read? (Select TWO.)

- A. Looking at the pictures **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: pictures give clues to meaning.
- B. Thinking about what has happened so far **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: holding the story in mind aids understanding.
- C. Reading as fast as possible with no thinking  
  _Rationale:_ Speed without understanding does not build meaning.
- D. Covering the words with your hand  
  _Rationale:_ You cannot read the words if they are covered.
