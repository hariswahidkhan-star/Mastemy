## Learning outcomes

1. Apply safe and effective care management across the nursing process
2. Promote health and prevent illness across the lifespan
3. Support psychosocial integrity
4. Maintain physiological integrity including pharmacology and risk reduction
5. Apply clinical judgement to prioritisation and safety

> Outcomes are DESIGN ASSUMPTIONS derived without a verified official outline; confirm against the issuer's exam page at blueprint review.

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: NCLEX includes alternate and next-generation clinical-judgment item formats; Mastemy uses MCQ/MR only and does not replicate clinical performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0341-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A nurse is assigned four patients at the start of a shift. Which patient should be assessed first?

- A. A post-operative patient reporting sudden shortness of breath **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a potential airway/breathing emergency takes priority over stable needs.
- B. A patient requesting pain medication for chronic back pain  
  _Rationale:_ Important but not immediately life-threatening compared with acute dyspnoea.
- C. A patient awaiting discharge teaching  
  _Rationale:_ Discharge teaching is not time-critical relative to an acute change.
- D. A patient asking for help with a meal tray  
  _Rationale:_ A comfort need, lowest priority among these options.

**MST-0341-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which precaution is required for a patient with suspected pulmonary tuberculosis?

- A. Airborne precautions with a fit-tested respirator and negative-pressure room **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: TB spreads via airborne droplet nuclei, requiring airborne precautions.
- B. Contact precautions with gown and gloves only  
  _Rationale:_ Contact precautions do not protect against airborne transmission.
- C. Droplet precautions with a surgical mask only  
  _Rationale:_ Droplet precautions are insufficient for airborne pathogens like TB.
- D. Standard precautions alone  
  _Rationale:_ Standard precautions do not address airborne transmission.

**MST-0341-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO actions are required before administering a high-alert medication?

- A. Verify the dose against a weight-based or protocol calculation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: independent dose verification reduces high-alert medication errors.
- B. Complete an independent double-check with a second nurse where policy requires **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: independent double-checks are a standard high-alert safeguard.
- C. Administer first and document the order afterward  
  _Rationale:_ Giving a medication before confirming the order is unsafe.
- D. Skip identity checks if the patient is familiar  
  _Rationale:_ Patient identification is always required.
- E. Round the dose up to the nearest convenient amount  
  _Rationale:_ Altering a calculated dose for convenience is unsafe.
