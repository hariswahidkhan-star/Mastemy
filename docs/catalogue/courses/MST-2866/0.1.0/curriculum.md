## Learning outcomes

1. Explain that living things are made of cells
2. Identify the main parts of animal and plant cells
3. Compare animal and plant cells
4. Describe the jobs of the nucleus, membrane and mitochondria
5. Explain how cells form tissues and organs
6. Use a microscope idea to describe how cells are observed

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2866-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which cell part controls the cell and holds its genetic information?

- A. The nucleus **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the nucleus controls the cell and stores genetic material.
- B. The cell membrane  
  _Rationale:_ The membrane controls what enters and leaves, not the genes.
- C. The cytoplasm  
  _Rationale:_ Cytoplasm is the jelly where reactions happen.
- D. The chloroplast  
  _Rationale:_ Chloroplasts make food in plant cells; they do not hold the genes.

**MST-2866-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which structure is found in plant cells but NOT in animal cells?

- A. A cell wall **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: plant cells have a rigid cell wall; animal cells do not.
- B. A nucleus  
  _Rationale:_ Both plant and animal cells have a nucleus.
- C. A cell membrane  
  _Rationale:_ Both cell types have a membrane.
- D. Cytoplasm  
  _Rationale:_ Both plant and animal cells contain cytoplasm.

**MST-2866-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO parts are found in BOTH plant and animal cells? (Select TWO.)

- A. Nucleus **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: both cell types have a nucleus.
- B. Cell membrane **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: both cell types have a cell membrane.
- C. Chloroplast  
  _Rationale:_ Chloroplasts are only in plant cells.
- D. Cell wall  
  _Rationale:_ A cell wall is only in plant cells.
