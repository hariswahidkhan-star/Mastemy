## Learning outcomes

1. Frame an analytical finding as a clear narrative for a specific audience
2. Choose visuals that support the message without distorting the data
3. Structure a data story with context, insight and recommendation
4. Design slides and annotations that guide attention
5. Communicate uncertainty and avoid misleading presentation

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1623-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the strongest form for a chart title in a data story?

- A. A short insight statement that says what the chart means **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: insight titles tell the reader the takeaway directly.
- B. The name of the metric and date range only  
  _Rationale:_ A label leaves the reader to infer the point.
- C. A question with no answer  
  _Rationale:_ An unanswered question does not convey the message.
- D. No title, to keep the slide clean  
  _Rationale:_ Without a title the audience may miss the point.

**MST-1623-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO choices present data honestly? (Select TWO.)

- A. Start a bar chart's value axis at zero **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a zero baseline avoids exaggerating differences in bar length.
- B. Show an uncertainty range alongside a point estimate **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: showing uncertainty prevents overstating precision.
- C. Choose the time window that makes the trend look best  
  _Rationale:_ Cherry-picking the window misleads the audience.
- D. Use a dual axis to make two unrelated series appear correlated  
  _Rationale:_ Dual axes can manufacture a false visual relationship.

**MST-1623-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) An executive has two minutes. What should the data story lead with?

- A. The single key message and its recommendation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: lead with the message the decision needs.
- B. The data collection methodology in full  
  _Rationale:_ Method detail buries the message for a busy executive.
- C. Every chart produced during the analysis  
  _Rationale:_ Showing everything obscures the point.
- D. A glossary of technical terms  
  _Rationale:_ A glossary delays the message the audience needs.
