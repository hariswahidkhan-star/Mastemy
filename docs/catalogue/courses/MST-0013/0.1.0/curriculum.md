## Learning outcomes

1. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Strategic planning and control' to exam-style problems
2. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Impact of risk and uncertainty on organisational performance' to exam-style problems
3. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Performance measurement systems and design' to exam-style problems
4. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Strategic performance measurement' to exam-style problems
5. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Performance evaluation and corporate failure' to exam-style problems

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Official ACCA exams use constructed-response and computational item formats (written answers, calculations, OT case scenarios) that this platform's MCQ/MR forms do not reproduce; see the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0013-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A criticism of using return on investment (ROI) as a divisional performance measure is that it may:

- A. Lead managers to reject projects that would benefit the group as a whole **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ROI can cause dysfunctional rejection of good projects that lower the divisional ROI.
- B. Always align divisional and group interests  
  _Rationale:_ ROI often causes goal incongruence, not perfect alignment.
- C. Ignore the capital employed in a division  
  _Rationale:_ ROI explicitly uses capital employed.
- D. Be impossible to calculate from accounting data  
  _Rationale:_ ROI is readily computed from accounting data.

**MST-0013-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Residual income is calculated as divisional profit less:

- A. An imputed interest charge on the capital employed **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: residual income deducts a capital charge from profit.
- B. The depreciation for the period  
  _Rationale:_ Depreciation is already within profit; RI deducts a capital charge.
- C. Total fixed costs of the group  
  _Rationale:_ RI does not deduct group-wide fixed costs.
- D. The dividend paid to shareholders  
  _Rationale:_ Dividends are not deducted in computing RI.

**MST-0013-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO limitations of relying solely on financial performance measures.

- A. They tend to be backward-looking **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: financial measures largely report past performance.
- B. They can encourage short-term decision-making **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a short-term financial focus can harm long-term value.
- C. They capture customer satisfaction directly  
  _Rationale:_ Customer satisfaction is a non-financial measure.
- D. They measure employee morale  
  _Rationale:_ Morale is a non-financial, qualitative measure.
- E. They always predict future cash flows accurately  
  _Rationale:_ Financial measures do not reliably predict the future.
