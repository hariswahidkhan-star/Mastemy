## Learning outcomes

1. Apply the definition and recognition criteria for an intangible asset
2. Distinguish research from development and account for each
3. Account for intangibles acquired separately and in a business combination
4. Apply the cost and revaluation models for subsequent measurement
5. Account for amortisation of finite-life intangibles and testing of indefinite-life intangibles
6. Explain why internally generated goodwill and certain items are not recognised

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2524-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which of the following is part of the definition of an intangible asset under IAS 38?

- A. An identifiable non-monetary asset without physical substance **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an intangible asset is identifiable, non-monetary and lacks physical substance.
- B. A short-term monetary receivable  
  _Rationale:_ Monetary receivables are not intangible assets.
- C. A tangible item of equipment  
  _Rationale:_ Equipment has physical substance and is not intangible.
- D. Any expenditure management hopes will generate profit  
  _Rationale:_ Mere hopeful expenditure is not an intangible asset.

**MST-2524-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO costs are expensed rather than capitalised under IAS 38? (Select TWO.)

- A. Expenditure in the research phase of an internal project **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: research costs are expensed as incurred.
- B. Internally generated goodwill **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: internally generated goodwill is not recognised and such costs are expensed.
- C. Development expenditure that meets all six capitalisation criteria  
  _Rationale:_ Qualifying development costs are capitalised, not expensed.
- D. The purchase price of a separately acquired patent  
  _Rationale:_ A separately acquired patent is capitalised.

**MST-2524-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A company wants to recognise its internally generated brand, built through years of marketing, as an intangible asset. Why is this generally not permitted?

- A. Internally generated brands cannot be distinguished from the cost of developing the business as a whole, so they are not recognised **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: internally generated brands and goodwill are not recognised.
- B. Brands never provide future economic benefits  
  _Rationale:_ Brands can provide benefits; the issue is reliable separate measurement.
- C. Only tangible assets can be recognised  
  _Rationale:_ Intangibles can be recognised; internally generated brands specifically cannot.
- D. Brands must always be expensed because marketing is illegal to capitalise by law  
  _Rationale:_ There is no such legal rule; the reason is the recognition criteria.
