## Learning outcomes

1. Public-sector context
2. Cash vs accrual and IPSAS
3. Public-sector revenue
4. Assets and liabilities
5. Reporting and accountability

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses conceptual knowledge through selection items; does not assess preparation of a government entity's IPSAS-compliant financial statements or a jurisdiction-specific adoption decision.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1726-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Compared with cash-basis accounting, accrual-basis public-sector reporting mainly adds:

- A. Recognition of assets and liabilities, not just cash flows **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: accrual captures the full financial position.
- B. A ban on reporting any cash movements  
  _Rationale:_ Accrual still reports cash; it adds more, not less.
- C. A requirement to ignore future obligations  
  _Rationale:_ Accrual highlights obligations rather than ignoring them.
- D. Faster spending of the budget  
  _Rationale:_ The basis of accounting does not accelerate spending.

**MST-1726-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select ALL that apply) Which two are examples of non-exchange revenue for a government? (Select TWO) (Select TWO)

- A. Taxes collected from citizens **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: taxes are non-exchange revenue.
- B. Grants received from another government **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: transfers/grants are non-exchange revenue.
- C. Fees charged for issuing a passport at cost  
  _Rationale:_ A fee for a service is exchange revenue.
- D. Rent earned on a commercial property let  
  _Rationale:_ Rent for use of an asset is exchange revenue.

**MST-1726-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Budget-to-actual reporting in the public sector primarily supports:

- A. Accountability by showing how approved funds were actually used **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it links spending back to the approved budget.
- B. Hiding variances from the public  
  _Rationale:_ Its purpose is transparency, not concealment.
- C. Replacing the financial statements entirely  
  _Rationale:_ It complements, not replaces, the statements.
- D. Guaranteeing the entity made a profit  
  _Rationale:_ Public bodies are not profit-driven and this does not prove profit.
