## Learning outcomes

1. Explain metrology concepts, traceability and the calibration technician's role
2. Apply measurement principles, units and uncertainty basics
3. Apply calibration methods, procedures and interval management
4. Explain measurement assurance, records and quality systems

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0325-Q0001** (single, Select ONE) Traceability of a measurement result means it can be:

- A. Related to a reference standard through an unbroken chain of calibrations **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: traceability is an unbroken, documented chain to a reference standard.
- B. Repeated quickly by one technician  
  _Rationale:_ That describes repeatability, not traceability.
- C. Measured with an expensive instrument  
  _Rationale:_ Cost does not establish traceability.
- D. Stored in a climate-controlled room  
  _Rationale:_ Storage is not traceability.

**MST-0325-Q0002** (single, Select ONE) A technician records 'as-found' and 'as-left' data during calibration mainly to:

- A. Show the instrument's condition before and after adjustment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: as-found/as-left documents drift and the effect of adjustment.
- B. Increase the calibration fee  
  _Rationale:_ It is a record practice, not a billing step.
- C. Replace the uncertainty budget  
  _Rationale:_ It does not replace uncertainty estimation.
- D. Shorten the procedure  
  _Rationale:_ It adds rigour, not speed.

**MST-0325-Q0003** (multiple, Select TWO) Select TWO sources that contribute to measurement uncertainty.

- A. Reference standard uncertainty **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the standard's own uncertainty propagates to the result.
- B. Temperature variation during measurement **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: environmental temperature affects the measurement.
- C. The technician's job title  
  _Rationale:_ Job title does not affect uncertainty.
- D. The colour of the instrument case  
  _Rationale:_ Case colour is irrelevant.
- E. The lab's street address  
  _Rationale:_ Address does not affect uncertainty.
