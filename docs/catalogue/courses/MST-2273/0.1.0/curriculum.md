## Learning outcomes

1. Explain inbound marketing concepts and how a CRM supports the customer lifecycle
2. Set up contacts, companies, deals and properties to reflect a sales process
3. Build and segment lists and manage contact data and consent responsibly
4. Create emails, forms and landing pages and connect them to the CRM
5. Automate follow-up with simple workflows and lifecycle stages
6. Measure campaigns with reports and dashboards and act on the results

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2273-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A marketer imported a purchased email list and asks why sending to it is risky. What is the core problem?

- A. The contacts never gave consent, so emailing them can breach consent rules and harm deliverability and trust **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: emailing non-consenting contacts raises legal and deliverability problems and damages sender reputation.
- B. Purchased lists are always technically impossible to import  
  _Rationale:_ They can be imported; the problem is consent and quality, not capability.
- C. The CRM cannot store more than a few contacts  
  _Rationale:_ Contact capacity is not the issue here.
- D. Imported contacts cannot be segmented  
  _Rationale:_ Imported contacts can be segmented; consent is the real concern.

**MST-2273-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO signals better indicate a campaign's business impact than email opens alone? (Select TWO.)

- A. Contacts that progressed to a later lifecycle stage **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: lifecycle progression reflects real movement toward revenue.
- B. Deals created or influenced by the campaign **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: created or influenced deals tie the campaign to pipeline.
- C. The email's subject-line length  
  _Rationale:_ Subject-line length is an input, not an outcome.
- D. The colour of the call-to-action button  
  _Rationale:_ Button colour is a design detail, not a business outcome.

**MST-2273-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) New contacts from a webinar should receive a two-email welcome over three days, with a different path if they book a demo. What should the marketer build?

- A. A workflow with a delay and a branch based on the demo-booking condition **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a workflow automates timed, conditional follow-up based on contact behaviour.
- B. A single bulk email sent manually each day  
  _Rationale:_ Manual sends are not conditional and do not scale or branch.
- C. A static list with no actions  
  _Rationale:_ A list segments contacts but performs no follow-up.
- D. A landing page  
  _Rationale:_ A landing page captures contacts; it does not sequence follow-up.
