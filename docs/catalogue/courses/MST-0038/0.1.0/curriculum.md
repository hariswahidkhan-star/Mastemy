## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0038-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) All else equal, increasing financial gearing tends to:

- A. Increase the volatility of returns to equity shareholders **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: higher gearing increases financial risk and the volatility of equity returns.
- B. Reduce the company's financial risk  
  _Rationale:_ More debt raises, not reduces, financial risk.
- C. Eliminate the cost of equity  
  _Rationale:_ Gearing does not remove the cost of equity.
- D. Guarantee a higher share price  
  _Rationale:_ There is no guarantee; gearing raises risk as well as potential return.

**MST-0038-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A project has a cost of capital of 10%, year-1 cash flow of 110 and no other flows, with an initial outlay of 100. Its NPV is:

- A. 0 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: NPV = 110/1.10 - 100 = 100 - 100 = 0.
- B. 10  
  _Rationale:_ 10 ignores discounting of the 110 cash flow.
- C. -10  
  _Rationale:_ The discounted inflow equals the outlay, so NPV is 0, not negative.
- D. 100  
  _Rationale:_ 100 is the discounted inflow, not the net present value.

**MST-0038-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO methods commonly used to value a business.

- A. Discounted cash flow valuation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: DCF values a business on the present value of future cash flows.
- B. Comparable market multiples such as P/E **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: relative valuation using market multiples is a standard method.
- C. The straight-line depreciation charge  
  _Rationale:_ Depreciation is an accounting allocation, not a valuation method.
- D. The current ratio  
  _Rationale:_ The current ratio is a liquidity measure, not a valuation method.
- E. The dividend declared last year alone  
  _Rationale:_ A single past dividend is not itself a valuation method.
