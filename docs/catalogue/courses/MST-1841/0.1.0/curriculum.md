## Learning outcomes

1. Explain pricing foundations and objectives
2. Apply cost-, value- and competition-based pricing
3. Design pricing structures and tactics
4. Test, manage and optimise prices

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A product manager must set and manage prices for a new offering. They must choose pricing objectives, apply value-based pricing, design tiers and bundles, and set up price testing and margin monitoring, defending the approach against a reflexive race to the lowest price.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1841-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Value-based pricing sets price primarily according to:

- A. The value the customer perceives they receive **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: value-based pricing anchors on perceived customer value.
- B. Only the cost to produce plus a fixed margin  
  _Rationale:_ That is cost-plus pricing.
- C. The cheapest competitor's price always  
  _Rationale:_ That is a race to the bottom.
- D. A random round number  
  _Rationale:_ Not a pricing method.

**MST-1841-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are legitimate pricing structure tactics? (Select TWO.)

- A. Good-better-best tiering to serve different willingness to pay **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: tiers capture varied value segments.
- B. Bundling complementary products to raise perceived value **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: bundles can increase value capture.
- C. Hiding the price until after delivery  
  _Rationale:_ That erodes trust, not a tactic.
- D. Charging every customer a secret different price with no logic  
  _Rationale:_ Arbitrary, not a structure.

**MST-1841-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Price elasticity of demand describes how:

- A. Quantity demanded responds to a change in price **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: elasticity links price changes to demand changes.
- B. A logo should be coloured  
  _Rationale:_ Irrelevant to elasticity.
- C. Employees are scheduled  
  _Rationale:_ Unrelated.
- D. A warehouse is laid out  
  _Rationale:_ Not elasticity.
