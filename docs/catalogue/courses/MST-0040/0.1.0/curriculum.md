## Learning outcomes

1. Gather preliminary taxpayer data, confirm filing status and dependency, and apply due-diligence requirements
2. Determine taxable income from wages, investments, retirement and property transactions, including basis and gain/loss
3. Apply individual deductions, adjustments and credits correctly
4. Compute individual income tax, AMT and applicable additional taxes
5. Advise individual taxpayers on estimated payments, recordkeeping and planning
6. Prepare specialized individual returns (estates, trusts basics, foreign and amended returns)

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0040-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A single parent provides more than half the cost of keeping up a home for a qualifying child. Which filing status is generally most advantageous?

- A. Head of household **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a single taxpayer maintaining a home for a qualifying person generally qualifies for head of household, which gives a larger standard deduction than single.
- B. Married filing jointly  
  _Rationale:_ The taxpayer is single, so MFJ is unavailable.
- C. Qualifying surviving spouse  
  _Rationale:_ That status requires a deceased spouse within the prior two years.
- D. Married filing separately  
  _Rationale:_ The taxpayer is not married.

**MST-0040-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A taxpayer sells 100 shares bought with reinvested dividends. Which amount is the cost basis used to figure gain?

- A. The original purchase price only  
  _Rationale:_ Reinvested dividends that were already taxed add to basis.
- B. The purchase price plus reinvested dividends already taxed **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reinvested dividends were taxed when received, so they increase basis and reduce taxable gain.
- C. The current market value  
  _Rationale:_ Market value at sale is the amount realized, not basis.
- D. Zero, because dividends are not basis  
  _Rationale:_ Taxed reinvested dividends do add to basis.

**MST-0040-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO items are refundable credits that can produce a refund beyond tax liability for an eligible individual? (Select TWO)

- A. Earned Income Tax Credit **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the EITC is refundable.
- B. Additional Child Tax Credit **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the additional child tax credit is the refundable portion of the CTC.
- C. Lifetime Learning Credit  
  _Rationale:_ The Lifetime Learning Credit is nonrefundable.
- D. Foreign Tax Credit  
  _Rationale:_ The foreign tax credit is generally nonrefundable.
- E. Credit for Child and Dependent Care  
  _Rationale:_ This credit is nonrefundable.
