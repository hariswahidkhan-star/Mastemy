## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0048-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which of the following best describes the purpose of corporate governance in a company?

- A. To direct and control the company in the interests of its stakeholders and ensure accountability **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: governance is the system by which companies are directed and controlled, promoting accountability.
- B. To maximise short-term share price at any cost  
  _Rationale:_ Governance is broader than short-term price and includes accountability and ethics.
- C. To prepare the company's tax return  
  _Rationale:_ Tax compliance is a specific task, not the purpose of governance.
- D. To set the daily production schedule  
  _Rationale:_ Operational scheduling is a management task, not governance.

**MST-0048-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A manager uses a style where decisions are made jointly with the team and members are consulted. Which leadership style is this?

- A. Democratic (participative) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: consulting and deciding jointly with the team describes a democratic/participative style.
- B. Autocratic  
  _Rationale:_ An autocratic leader decides alone without consulting the team.
- C. Laissez-faire  
  _Rationale:_ A laissez-faire leader leaves the team to make its own decisions with little input.
- D. Bureaucratic  
  _Rationale:_ A bureaucratic style relies on strict rules and procedures, not joint decisions.

**MST-0048-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO fundamental principles of the professional accountant's code of ethics.

- A. Integrity **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: integrity is a fundamental ethical principle.
- B. Objectivity **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: objectivity is a fundamental ethical principle.
- C. Profit maximisation  
  _Rationale:_ Profit maximisation is not an ethical principle in the code.
- D. Market share growth  
  _Rationale:_ Growing market share is a business goal, not an ethical principle.
- E. Secrecy from regulators  
  _Rationale:_ Confidentiality is bounded by law; concealment from regulators is not a principle.
