## Learning outcomes

1. Apply advanced pathophysiology, pharmacology and health-assessment across the lifespan
2. Perform diagnostic reasoning and evidence-based management of common primary-care conditions
3. Apply health promotion, screening and disease prevention for individuals and families
4. Apply professional role, ethics, scope and regulatory considerations for the FNP

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1284-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A 45-year-old has two separate in-office readings consistent with stage 1 hypertension and no end-organ damage. What is a guideline-consistent initial step?

- A. Confirm with out-of-office/repeat measurement and initiate lifestyle modification, considering risk-based pharmacologic therapy **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: confirming the diagnosis and starting lifestyle change, with pharmacotherapy based on cardiovascular risk, is guideline-consistent.
- B. Immediately start three antihypertensive drugs  
  _Rationale:_ Triple therapy at first presentation of uncomplicated stage 1 hypertension is not guideline-consistent.
- C. Ignore the readings entirely  
  _Rationale:_ Elevated readings require assessment and management, not dismissal.
- D. Refer for emergency surgery  
  _Rationale:_ Stage 1 hypertension without emergency features is managed in primary care, not surgically.

**MST-1284-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why may medication dosing need adjustment in older adults?

- A. Age-related changes in renal/hepatic function and body composition alter drug handling **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic changes with age affect dosing and risk.
- B. Older adults never metabolise any drugs  
  _Rationale:_ They still metabolise drugs, though often differently; dosing is adjusted, not eliminated.
- C. Dosing is identical to paediatric dosing  
  _Rationale:_ Adult geriatric dosing is not the same as paediatric dosing.
- D. Age has no effect on drug handling  
  _Rationale:_ Age clearly affects pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics.

**MST-1284-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO responsibilities that fall within an FNP's professional role and ethics.

- A. Practising within the legal scope defined by the relevant jurisdiction **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: practising within the jurisdiction's scope is a core professional responsibility.
- B. Referring when a patient's needs exceed the FNP's scope or competence **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: timely referral when needs exceed scope/competence is a professional duty.
- C. Prescribing outside legal authority to save time  
  _Rationale:_ Prescribing beyond legal authority is outside scope and unethical.
- D. Ignoring informed consent for convenience  
  _Rationale:_ Informed consent is an ethical obligation, not optional.
- E. Guaranteeing a cure for every patient  
  _Rationale:_ Guaranteeing cures is neither possible nor ethical.
