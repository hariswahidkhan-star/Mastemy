## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0309-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Effective supply management leadership is best described as:

- A. Setting strategy and aligning the function with business goals **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: leadership links supply strategy to the business.
- B. Only approving purchase orders  
  _Rationale:_ Order approval is transactional, not leadership.
- C. Avoiding any involvement in strategy  
  _Rationale:_ Strategy is central to supply leadership.
- D. Focusing solely on the lowest price  
  _Rationale:_ Price alone is not strategic leadership.

**MST-0309-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A team resists a new sourcing tool. The leader's best first step is to:

- A. Understand concerns and build buy-in through engagement **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: engaging concerns builds adoption.
- B. Mandate it with no explanation  
  _Rationale:_ Mandates without buy-in deepen resistance.
- C. Abandon the tool at the first complaint  
  _Rationale:_ Abandoning undermines the transformation.
- D. Ignore the team and hope for adoption  
  _Rationale:_ Ignoring resistance stalls change.

**MST-0309-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO balanced measures for a supply management team.

- A. Cost savings achieved **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: cost is a core supply measure.
- B. Supply risk and service performance **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: risk and service balance cost-only views.
- C. Number of emails sent  
  _Rationale:_ Email volume is not a performance measure.
- D. Length of team meetings  
  _Rationale:_ Meeting length is not an outcome.
- E. Office seating arrangement  
  _Rationale:_ Seating is unrelated to performance.
