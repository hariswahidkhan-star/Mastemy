## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1085-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) The strongest argument for continuous feedback over a single annual review is that it:

- A. Reduces the manager's workload to zero  
  _Rationale:_ Continuous feedback is ongoing work, not an elimination of effort.
- B. Lets people adjust while there is still time to improve **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: timely feedback allows course-correction rather than a year-end surprise.
- C. Removes the need for any documentation  
  _Rationale:_ Documentation still matters for fairness and decisions.
- D. Guarantees everyone gets a top rating  
  _Rationale:_ Feedback frequency does not inflate ratings.

**MST-1085-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Before moving to formal discipline, a manager facing underperformance should first:

- A. Assume the person is not trying and issue a warning  
  _Rationale:_ Assuming lack of effort skips diagnosis and risks unfairness.
- B. Diagnose whether the cause is skill, clarity, resources or motivation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: identifying the real cause lets the manager choose the right response.
- C. Reassign the work to someone else immediately  
  _Rationale:_ Reassignment avoids rather than addresses the performance issue.
- D. Lower the person's goals without discussion  
  _Rationale:_ Silently lowering goals neither diagnoses nor resolves the problem.

**MST-1085-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) In the 70-20-10 model of development, which TWO statements are accurate? (Select TWO)

- A. Most development comes from challenging on-the-job experience **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the '70' reflects learning through real work and stretch tasks.
- B. Learning from others, such as coaching, is a meaningful share **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the '20' covers learning from relationships and feedback.
- C. Formal courses should make up the large majority of development  
  _Rationale:_ Formal training is the smallest share (the '10'), not the majority.
- D. On-the-job experience plays almost no role  
  _Rationale:_ Experience is the largest driver, not a minor one.
