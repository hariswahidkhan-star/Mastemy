## Learning outcomes

1. Describe money laundering, terrorist financing and other financial-crime risks and methods
2. Explain international AFC frameworks, standards and key regulations
3. Design and operate an anti-financial-crime compliance program
4. Apply tools and technologies used to detect and investigate financial crime

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0102-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which sequence correctly orders the three classic stages of money laundering?

- A. Layering, placement, integration  
  _Rationale:_ Placement comes first, not layering.
- B. Placement, layering, integration **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: illicit funds are first placed, then layered through transactions, then integrated into the legitimate economy.
- C. Integration, layering, placement  
  _Rationale:_ This reverses the order.
- D. Placement, integration, layering  
  _Rationale:_ Layering precedes integration.

**MST-0102-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) What does a risk-based approach to customer due diligence require?

- A. Identical checks for every customer  
  _Rationale:_ That is a rules-based, not risk-based, approach.
- B. More intensive due diligence for higher-risk customers **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a risk-based approach applies enhanced due diligence where risk is higher and simplified measures where risk is lower.
- C. No due diligence for low-value accounts  
  _Rationale:_ Some baseline CDD is still required.
- D. Due diligence only at account closure  
  _Rationale:_ CDD is performed at onboarding and on an ongoing basis.

**MST-0102-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are typical red flags warranting closer review for possible money laundering? (Select TWO)

- A. Transactions structured just below reporting thresholds **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: structuring to avoid reporting thresholds is a classic red flag.
- B. Sudden high-volume activity inconsistent with the customer profile **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: activity inconsistent with the known profile is a red flag.
- C. A salaried customer receiving a regular payroll deposit  
  _Rationale:_ Expected payroll activity is not a red flag.
- D. A customer providing complete KYC documentation  
  _Rationale:_ Full documentation is expected, not a red flag.
- E. A business paying routine supplier invoices  
  _Rationale:_ Routine expected payments are not a red flag.
