## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0033-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under activity-based costing, overhead is assigned to products on the basis of:

- A. Cost drivers that cause the activities consumed by each product **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ABC traces overheads to activities and then to products using cause-and-effect cost drivers.
- B. A single plant-wide direct-labour-hour rate  
  _Rationale:_ That is traditional absorption costing, which ABC is designed to improve on.
- C. The sales value of each product  
  _Rationale:_ Sales value is an arbitrary base that ignores actual resource consumption.
- D. An equal share across all product lines  
  _Rationale:_ Equal allocation ignores differing activity consumption and distorts costs.

**MST-0033-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A division has an asset base of 1,000 and operating profit of 150. Head office's cost of capital is 12%. What is the division's residual income?

- A. 30 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: residual income = 150 - (12% x 1,000) = 150 - 120 = 30.
- B. 150  
  _Rationale:_ 150 is operating profit before the capital charge is deducted.
- C. 120  
  _Rationale:_ 120 is the imputed capital charge, not residual income.
- D. 18  
  _Rationale:_ 18 misreads the figures; the charge is 120, giving residual income of 30.

**MST-0033-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO relevant cash flows for a short-term make-or-buy decision.

- A. Incremental variable production costs that would be avoided by buying **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: avoidable incremental costs are relevant to the decision.
- B. Opportunity cost of using freed-up capacity **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the contribution forgone or gained from the freed capacity is a relevant opportunity cost.
- C. Sunk development costs already incurred  
  _Rationale:_ Sunk costs are not relevant to a future decision.
- D. Apportioned fixed head-office overhead that will not change  
  _Rationale:_ Unavoidable apportioned overhead is irrelevant.
- E. Historic cost of machinery already owned  
  _Rationale:_ Historic cost is sunk and irrelevant.
