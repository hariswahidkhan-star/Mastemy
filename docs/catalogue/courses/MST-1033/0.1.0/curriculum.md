## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; hands-on system operation, judgement under real transactions and professional sign-off are not assessed in this format.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1033-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the purpose of three-way matching in accounts payable?

- A. To confirm the invoice agrees with the purchase order and the goods receipt before payment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: three-way matching checks invoice against PO and receipt to prevent paying for goods not ordered or received.
- B. To split one invoice across three cost centres automatically  
  _Rationale:_ Three-way matching compares three documents; it does not split costs.
- C. To pay the vendor three times for redundancy  
  _Rationale:_ It prevents overpayment, not triplicate payment.
- D. To convert the invoice into three separate approvals  
  _Rationale:_ It refers to three documents, not three approvers.

**MST-1033-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select ALL that apply) Which practices strengthen controls against accounts payable fraud? (Select TWO)

- A. Verifying vendor bank-detail changes through an independent channel **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: independently confirming bank changes defeats a common invoice-redirection scheme.
- B. Separating vendor setup from invoice approval and payment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: segregation of duties stops one person from both creating a vendor and paying it.
- C. Letting the person who approves invoices also create new vendors  
  _Rationale:_ Combining these duties removes a key control.
- D. Removing the audit trail to speed up processing  
  _Rationale:_ An audit trail is a core control, not an obstacle to remove.

**MST-1033-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A supplier offers 2/10 net 30. Taking the discount means:

- A. Paying within 10 days to take a 2 percent discount instead of paying the full amount at 30 days **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 2/10 net 30 gives a 2 percent discount for payment within 10 days, otherwise full amount by day 30.
- B. Paying 2 percent extra if you wait the full 30 days  
  _Rationale:_ It is a discount for early payment, not a surcharge.
- C. Paying in 2 days or forfeiting the order  
  _Rationale:_ The early-payment window is 10 days, not 2.
- D. Receiving 30 percent off for paying in 2 days  
  _Rationale:_ The figures are a 2 percent discount within 10 days.
