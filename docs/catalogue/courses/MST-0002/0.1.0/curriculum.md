## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0002-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A cost that stays the same in total regardless of the level of activity is best described as a:

- A. Fixed cost **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: fixed costs remain constant in total over the relevant range.
- B. Variable cost  
  _Rationale:_ Variable cost changes in total with activity.
- C. Semi-variable cost  
  _Rationale:_ A semi-variable cost has both fixed and variable elements.
- D. Marginal cost  
  _Rationale:_ Marginal cost is the cost of one additional unit.

**MST-0002-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Using the high-low method, which data points are used to estimate the variable cost per unit?

- A. The highest and lowest activity levels **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the high-low method uses the highest and lowest activity levels.
- B. The highest and lowest total costs only  
  _Rationale:_ It is driven by activity levels, not by selecting on cost.
- C. The average of all activity levels  
  _Rationale:_ Averaging is not the high-low method.
- D. Only the most recent two periods  
  _Rationale:_ Recency is not the selection rule in high-low.

**MST-0002-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO items that would be classified as prime cost in a manufacturing business.

- A. Direct materials **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: direct materials are part of prime cost.
- B. Direct labour **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: direct labour is part of prime cost.
- C. Factory rent  
  _Rationale:_ Factory rent is a production overhead, not prime cost.
- D. Administrative salaries  
  _Rationale:_ Administrative salaries are a period cost, not prime cost.
- E. Depreciation of office equipment  
  _Rationale:_ Office depreciation is a non-production overhead.
