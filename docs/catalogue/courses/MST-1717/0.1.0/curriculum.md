## Learning outcomes

1. Money laundering explained
2. The AML framework
3. Know Your Customer
4. Monitoring and reporting
5. Sanctions and wider crime

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses conceptual knowledge through selection items; this is general education, not legal advice, and it does not assess a real suspicious-activity determination or regulatory filing for a specific case.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1717-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Rapidly moving funds through many accounts and jurisdictions to obscure their origin is which stage of money laundering?

- A. Layering **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: layering disguises the trail of illicit funds.
- B. Placement  
  _Rationale:_ Placement is the initial entry of funds into the system.
- C. Integration  
  _Rationale:_ Integration returns funds to the economy as apparently legitimate.
- D. Reconciliation  
  _Rationale:_ Reconciliation is an accounting term, not an ML stage.

**MST-1717-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select ALL that apply) Which two situations typically trigger enhanced due diligence? (Select TWO) (Select TWO)

- A. A customer who is a politically exposed person (PEP) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: PEPs warrant enhanced scrutiny.
- B. A customer in a high-risk jurisdiction **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: high-risk geographies warrant EDD.
- C. A salaried local customer with a simple account  
  _Rationale:_ A low-risk profile needs only standard CDD.
- D. A dormant account with no activity  
  _Rationale:_ Dormancy alone is not an EDD trigger.

**MST-1717-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) 'Tipping-off' is prohibited because it:

- A. Could alert a suspect and prejudice an investigation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: warning the subject can defeat the investigation.
- B. Increases the bank's profits unfairly  
  _Rationale:_ The prohibition is about protecting investigations, not profit.
- C. Is only a courtesy, not a rule  
  _Rationale:_ It is a legal prohibition, not a courtesy.
- D. Speeds up the suspicious-activity report  
  _Rationale:_ It harms, rather than speeds, the process.
