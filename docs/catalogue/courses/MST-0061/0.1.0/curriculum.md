## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0061-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) The primary objective of corporate treasury liquidity management is to:

- A. Ensure the organisation can meet its obligations as they fall due **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: liquidity management ensures sufficient cash is available to meet obligations when due.
- B. Maximise reported accounting profit  
  _Rationale:_ Profit maximisation is not the core aim of liquidity management.
- C. Eliminate all short-term investments  
  _Rationale:_ Treasury invests surplus liquidity; it does not eliminate short-term investment.
- D. Increase the dividend regardless of cash  
  _Rationale:_ Paying dividends without cash would worsen liquidity.

**MST-0061-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A treasury concentrates balances from many subsidiary accounts into a single account to optimise interest and control. This technique is called:

- A. Cash pooling (concentration) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: cash pooling/concentration aggregates balances to optimise interest and funding.
- B. Factoring of receivables  
  _Rationale:_ Factoring sells receivables for cash; it is not account concentration.
- C. Issuing a corporate bond  
  _Rationale:_ Bond issuance is long-term financing, not daily cash concentration.
- D. Declaring a scrip dividend  
  _Rationale:_ A scrip dividend is a distribution choice, not cash concentration.

**MST-0061-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO instruments typically used by corporate treasurers to invest short-term cash surpluses.

- A. Money market funds **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: money market funds are a standard short-term, liquid investment for treasuries.
- B. Treasury bills **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: short-dated government treasury bills are a common low-risk short-term investment.
- C. Twenty-year corporate bonds  
  _Rationale:_ Long-dated bonds are not suitable for short-term surplus investment.
- D. Direct private equity stakes  
  _Rationale:_ Private equity is illiquid and unsuitable for short-term cash.
- E. Freehold office property  
  _Rationale:_ Property is illiquid and not a short-term cash instrument.
