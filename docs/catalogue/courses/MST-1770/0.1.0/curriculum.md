## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the principles and systems view of patient safety
2. Describe how errors and harm are reported, measured and analysed
3. Apply tools for quality improvement in healthcare
4. Describe how safety culture, human factors and teamwork reduce harm

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every course outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Hands-on operation, the quality of tools and live outputs, and professional judgement are taught by video demonstration and model-answer analysis; the certificate assessment uses single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only and never requires uploads, recorded work, submitted code or peer review.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1770-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A nurse gives the wrong dose because two drug vials look almost identical and are stored together. A systems approach would first focus on:

- A. Redesigning storage and labelling to make the error hard to repeat **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a systems view targets the conditions that allowed the error, such as look-alike storage.
- B. Disciplining the nurse to deter future errors  
  _Rationale:_ Blaming the individual ignores the system factors and does not prevent recurrence.
- C. Asking staff to be more careful  
  _Rationale:_ Exhortations to be careful are weak and unreliable against latent system hazards.
- D. Removing the incident from the record  
  _Rationale:_ Hiding the event prevents learning and improvement.**MST-1770-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) In the Model for Improvement, what is the purpose of a PDSA cycle?

- A. To test a change on a small scale and learn before wider rollout **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: Plan-Do-Study-Act tests a change in small, rapid cycles to learn and adapt.
- B. To audit compliance once a year  
  _Rationale:_ PDSA is iterative testing of change, not an annual compliance audit.
- C. To assign blame after an incident  
  _Rationale:_ PDSA is an improvement method, not an incident-blaming tool.
- D. To replace measurement with opinion  
  _Rationale:_ PDSA relies on measurement to judge whether a change is an improvement.**MST-1770-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are features of a 'just culture' in patient safety? (Select TWO)

- A. Staff are encouraged to report errors and near misses openly **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: open reporting without fear of unfair punishment is central to a just culture.
- B. A clear, fair line is drawn for reckless behaviour **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a just culture still holds reckless conduct accountable while supporting honest error.
- C. No one is ever held accountable for any action  
  _Rationale:_ That is a blame-free culture; a just culture retains accountability for recklessness.
- D. All errors are kept confidential and never analysed  
  _Rationale:_ A just culture analyses errors to learn, rather than hiding them.
