## Learning outcomes

1. Explain supplier quality management and the supplier quality professional's role
2. Apply supplier selection, qualification and risk assessment methods
3. Apply supplier performance monitoring, auditing and improvement methods
4. Explain supplier relationship management and continuous improvement

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0320-Q0001** (single, Select ONE) A buyer selects the lowest-price supplier without a qualification review. The main quality risk is:

- A. Accepting an unqualified source that may deliver nonconforming product **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: skipping qualification admits unassessed quality and capability risk.
- B. Paying too much for the part  
  _Rationale:_ Lowest price is not an overpayment risk.
- C. Having too many approved suppliers  
  _Rationale:_ That is unrelated to skipping qualification.
- D. Reduced shipping time  
  _Rationale:_ Shipping time is not the quality risk here.

**MST-0320-Q0002** (single, Select ONE) A supplier scorecard is most useful when it:

- A. Tracks agreed metrics over time against defined thresholds **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: trended metrics against thresholds drive objective decisions.
- B. Lists only the latest single shipment result  
  _Rationale:_ A single data point does not show performance over time.
- C. Records informal opinions of the buyer  
  _Rationale:_ Opinions are not objective performance metrics.
- D. Is shared only after contract termination  
  _Rationale:_ Late sharing defeats the monitoring purpose.

**MST-0320-Q0003** (multiple, Select TWO) Select TWO inputs that belong in a supplier risk assessment.

- A. Single-source dependency for a critical part **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: sole-source dependency is a supply risk factor.
- B. Supplier financial stability **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: financial health affects continuity of supply.
- C. The buyer's office location  
  _Rationale:_ Buyer location is not a supplier risk input.
- D. Colour of the product packaging  
  _Rationale:_ Packaging colour is not a risk factor.
- E. Number of letters in the supplier's name  
  _Rationale:_ This is irrelevant to risk.
