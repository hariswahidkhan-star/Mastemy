## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the research analyst role and the information used to form an investment view
2. Apply financial-statement analysis and modelling to forecast a company's performance
3. Evaluate valuation methods to derive a target and rating
4. Analyse the analytical judgement and assumptions behind a recommendation

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0072-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A company reports rising net income while operating cash flow falls and receivables balloon. What does this most likely signal to an analyst?

- A. Earnings quality concern: profits may not be converting to cash **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: diverging earnings and cash flow with rising receivables is a classic earnings-quality red flag.
- B. Strong free cash flow generation  
  _Rationale:_ Falling operating cash flow is the opposite of strong cash generation.
- C. That the balance sheet is irrelevant to the analysis  
  _Rationale:_ The balance sheet trend is exactly what raises the concern.
- D. A guaranteed accounting fraud  
  _Rationale:_ It is a flag warranting investigation, not proof of fraud.

**MST-0072-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) In a DCF, an analyst wants to make the valuation more credible. Which change most improves defensibility?

- A. Grounding the terminal growth rate in long-run economic growth and testing it **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a defensible, tested terminal growth assumption anchored to the economy improves credibility.
- B. Choosing a terminal growth rate above long-run GDP to reach the desired price  
  _Rationale:_ Reverse-engineering assumptions to a target undermines credibility.
- C. Extending explicit forecasts to 50 years for precision  
  _Rationale:_ Spurious precision over implausible horizons does not improve reliability.
- D. Removing the discount rate to simplify  
  _Rationale:_ The discount rate is essential to a DCF.

**MST-0072-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Banking and the issuer both pressure an analyst to raise a cautious rating. Which TWO responses are consistent with analyst independence obligations? (Select TWO)

- A. Keep the rating supported by the analysis and document the basis **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the rating must reflect the analyst's genuine view supported by analysis.
- B. Ensure required conflict and relationship disclosures appear in the report **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: disclosure of conflicts is central to research-rule compliance.
- C. Raise the rating to protect the banking relationship  
  _Rationale:_ Changing a view to serve banking violates independence rules.
- D. Let banking approve the rating before publication  
  _Rationale:_ Banking cannot control research ratings.
- E. Promise the issuer favourable coverage in exchange for access  
  _Rationale:_ Trading coverage for access is prohibited.
