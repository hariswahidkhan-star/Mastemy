## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1853-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Choose the correct past form: "Hier, je ___ au cinema." (completed single past action)

- A. suis alle **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a completed single past action uses the passe compose, "je suis alle".
- B. allais  
  _Rationale:_ The imparfait "allais" describes a habitual or ongoing past, not a single completed action.
- C. vais  
  _Rationale:_ "Vais" is present tense, not past.
- D. irai  
  _Rationale:_ "Irai" is future tense, not past.

**MST-1853-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) To answer a detail question on a B1 reading text, the best approach is to:

- A. Scan the text for the specific information asked **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: detail questions are answered by locating the specific information in the text.
- B. Choose the option with words copied from the text  
  _Rationale:_ Matching wording can be a distractor; meaning matters.
- C. Answer from personal opinion  
  _Rationale:_ The answer must come from the text, not opinion.
- D. Pick the shortest option  
  _Rationale:_ Option length is irrelevant.

**MST-1853-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO sentences use the correct article or preposition?

- A. Je viens de la France. **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: "venir de" with a feminine country uses "de la", giving "de la France".
- B. Il va a l'ecole. **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: "aller a" with "ecole" (vowel start) gives "a l'ecole".
- C. Je mange le pomme.  
  _Rationale:_ "Pomme" is feminine, so it needs "la pomme", not "le pomme".
- D. Nous allons a Paris en voiture rouge.  
  _Rationale:_ It needs "en voiture rouge" with an article: "dans une voiture rouge" or "en voiture"; "voiture rouge" lacks the article.
- E. Elle habite dans Lyon.  
  _Rationale:_ One says "habite a Lyon", not "dans Lyon".
