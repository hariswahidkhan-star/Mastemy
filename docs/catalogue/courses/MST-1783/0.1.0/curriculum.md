## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the purpose and scope of HIPAA and who is required to comply
2. Describe the Privacy Rule and how protected health information (PHI) may be used and disclosed
3. Describe the Security Rule safeguards: administrative, physical and technical
4. Identify core obligations such as minimum necessary, patient rights and breach notification
5. Recognise everyday compliance risks and the role of policies and workforce training

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Selection items test knowledge and applied reasoning only; they do not substitute for an organisation's required HIPAA compliance training, legal review, or role-specific procedures.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1783-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under the HIPAA Privacy Rule, which principle requires limiting PHI shared to only what is needed for the task?

- A. Minimum necessary **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the minimum-necessary standard limits use and disclosure of PHI to what is needed for the purpose.
- B. Breach notification  
  _Rationale:_ Breach notification governs informing affected parties after an impermissible disclosure, not the amount shared routinely.
- C. Right of access  
  _Rationale:_ Right of access concerns a patient obtaining their own records, not limiting what staff share.
- D. Treatment exception  
  _Rationale:_ The treatment context has broader permitted sharing; it is not the principle that limits the amount disclosed.

**MST-1783-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) The HIPAA Security Rule groups safeguards into categories. Which TWO of the following are Security Rule safeguard categories? (Select TWO.)

- A. Administrative safeguards **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: administrative safeguards (policies, training, risk management) are one of the three categories.
- B. Technical safeguards **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: technical safeguards (access controls, audit, encryption) are a defined category.
- C. Financial safeguards  
  _Rationale:_ There is no 'financial safeguards' category in the Security Rule.
- D. Marketing safeguards  
  _Rationale:_ Marketing is addressed under the Privacy Rule's use-and-disclosure provisions, not as a Security Rule safeguard category.

**MST-1783-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A clinic hires an outside billing company that will handle patient records on its behalf. What does HIPAA most directly require before sharing PHI with them?

- A. A business associate agreement **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a vendor handling PHI on a covered entity's behalf is a business associate, requiring a written business associate agreement.
- B. Individual patient authorisation for each record  
  _Rationale:_ Routine payment and operations disclosures generally do not require per-record patient authorisation.
- C. Nothing, because billing is exempt from HIPAA  
  _Rationale:_ Billing operations are not exempt; PHI handled by a vendor is covered.
- D. Public posting of the clinic's privacy notice only  
  _Rationale:_ A notice of privacy practices does not by itself authorise a vendor to process PHI.
