## Learning outcomes

1. Design thumbnails that earn clicks honestly
2. Write titles that work with thumbnails
3. Test and iterate on packaging

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A creator must package a video for YouTube. They must design a legible, high-contrast thumbnail, write a title that pairs with it and keeps an honest promise, and test variants by click-through rate, defending honest packaging over clickbait that harms retention.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1876-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A thumbnail must above all be:

- A. Legible and compelling at small size **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: thumbnails are viewed small, so clarity wins.
- B. Packed with as much small text as possible  
  _Rationale:_ Small text disappears at thumbnail size.
- C. Identical to every other video's thumbnail  
  _Rationale:_ Sameness reduces clicks.
- D. A plain screenshot with no thought  
  _Rationale:_ Weak packaging underperforms.

**MST-1876-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO describe healthy title-and-thumbnail packaging? (Select TWO.)

- A. The title and thumbnail complement, not repeat, each other **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: together they tell a fuller story.
- B. The promise is honest and the video delivers it **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: honesty protects retention and trust.
- C. The title overpromises to maximise clicks  
  _Rationale:_ Clickbait hurts retention and channel health.
- D. The thumbnail is unreadable at small size  
  _Rationale:_ Illegibility loses clicks.

**MST-1876-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A/B testing thumbnails helps a creator:

- A. Choose packaging based on measured click-through, not guesswork **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: testing replaces hunches with evidence.
- B. Improve the video's audio quality  
  _Rationale:_ Packaging tests do not change audio.
- C. Guarantee the video goes viral  
  _Rationale:_ No test guarantees virality.
- D. Edit the video automatically  
  _Rationale:_ Testing does not edit content.
