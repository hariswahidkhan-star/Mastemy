## Learning outcomes

1. Payroll basics
2. Pay components
3. Statutory deductions
4. Payroll processing
5. Compliance and controls

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses conceptual knowledge through selection items; this is general education, not payroll or legal advice, and it does not assess running a real payroll or a jurisdiction-specific compliance determination.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1719-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Net pay is best defined as:

- A. Gross pay minus all deductions **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: net pay is what remains after deductions.
- B. Gross pay plus employer contributions  
  _Rationale:_ Employer costs are not added to the employee's net pay.
- C. The same as gross pay  
  _Rationale:_ Net pay is after deductions, not equal to gross.
- D. Only the overtime element  
  _Rationale:_ Net pay covers all earnings less deductions.

**MST-1719-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select ALL that apply) Which two statements about statutory payroll costs are correct? (Select TWO) (Select TWO)

- A. Some contributions are borne by the employee via deduction **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: employees fund some contributions through deductions.
- B. Some contributions are an additional cost borne by the employer **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: employers pay their own share on top of wages.
- C. All statutory costs always fall only on the employee  
  _Rationale:_ Employers also bear statutory costs.
- D. Withholding means the employee files nothing ever  
  _Rationale:_ Withholding is collection; filing obligations can still apply.

**MST-1719-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A control that helps prevent 'ghost employee' fraud is:

- A. Independently verifying new starters against HR records before payment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: independent verification stops fictitious employees being paid.
- B. Letting one person set up and approve all new employees  
  _Rationale:_ That removes segregation and enables the fraud.
- C. Paying everyone in cash with no records  
  _Rationale:_ That weakens controls and auditability.
- D. Skipping reconciliation to save time  
  _Rationale:_ Skipping reconciliation lets errors and fraud slip through.
