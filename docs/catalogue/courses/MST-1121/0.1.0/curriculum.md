## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the job of copy and match it to the audience
2. Ground copy in research, positioning and one message
3. Write headlines and openings that pull readers in
4. Structure persuasive web and landing-page copy
5. Write concise ad and short-form copy
6. Edit, test and persuade honestly

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1121-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the core job of persuasive copy?

- A. Move a specific reader toward one action by speaking to their needs **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: copy persuades a reader toward an action.
- B. Showcase the writer's vocabulary  
  _Rationale:_ Impressing the reader is not persuading them.
- C. Describe every product feature in full  
  _Rationale:_ Feature dumps rarely persuade.
- D. Win a creative award  
  _Rationale:_ Awards are not the job of conversion copy.

**MST-1121-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO strengthen a landing page's persuasion? (Select TWO.)

- A. A single, clear call to action **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: one clear action focuses the reader.
- B. Proof that handles the main objection **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: proof reduces the reader's risk.
- C. Several competing calls to action  
  _Rationale:_ Competing CTAs dilute the decision.
- D. Overclaimed benefits with no evidence  
  _Rationale:_ Overclaiming erodes trust and conversion.

**MST-1121-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Before writing, the most valuable research input is usually:

- A. Voice-of-customer language from real customers **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: customers' own words make copy resonate and convert.
- B. The writer's personal preferences  
  _Rationale:_ Preference is not evidence of what persuades.
- C. A competitor's copy to paraphrase  
  _Rationale:_ Copying competitors ignores your customer.
- D. A longer word count  
  _Rationale:_ Length is not a research input.
