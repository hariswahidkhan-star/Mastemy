## Learning outcomes

1. Explain basic and applied exercise science (anatomy, physiology, biomechanics, nutrition)
2. Conduct client assessment and apply the OPT (Optimum Performance Training) model
3. Design progressive exercise programs for stabilisation, strength and power goals
4. Apply professional practice, client relations, behaviour change and safety/scope

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1279-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which OPT phase is the appropriate starting point for a previously sedentary client?

- A. Phase 1: Stabilisation Endurance **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: NASM's OPT model begins new/deconditioned clients in stabilisation endurance to build control and joint stability.
- B. Phase 5: Power  
  _Rationale:_ Power is an advanced phase, not a starting point for a sedentary client.
- C. Phase 4: Maximal Strength  
  _Rationale:_ Maximal strength requires a foundation built in earlier phases.
- D. Phase 3: Muscular Development  
  _Rationale:_ Hypertrophy work comes after a stabilisation base.

**MST-1279-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) During an overhead squat assessment, the client's knees move inward. Which muscles are most likely overactive?

- A. Adductors (and related hip internal rotators) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: knees caving in (valgus) commonly reflects overactive adductors and underactive gluteus medius/maximus.
- B. The biceps brachii  
  _Rationale:_ The biceps are not implicated in knee valgus during a squat.
- C. The wrist flexors  
  _Rationale:_ Wrist flexors are unrelated to knee movement.
- D. The calves only, with no hip involvement  
  _Rationale:_ Knee valgus is primarily a hip/adductor issue, not calves alone.

**MST-1279-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO actions fall WITHIN a certified personal trainer's scope of practice?

- A. Designing and progressing a safe exercise program **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: program design and progression are core trainer responsibilities.
- B. Providing general (non-medical) healthy-eating guidance **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: general healthy-eating guidance is within scope; detailed medical nutrition therapy is not.
- C. Diagnosing an injury  
  _Rationale:_ Diagnosis is outside a trainer's scope and belongs to licensed clinicians.
- D. Prescribing a therapeutic diet for a disease  
  _Rationale:_ Medical nutrition therapy requires a registered dietitian/clinician.
- E. Prescribing medication  
  _Rationale:_ Prescribing is strictly outside scope.
