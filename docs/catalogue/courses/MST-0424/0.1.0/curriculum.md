## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0424-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Enzymes increase the rate of biochemical reactions primarily by:

- A. Lowering the activation energy of the reaction **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: enzymes reduce the activation-energy barrier, speeding the reaction.
- B. Increasing the activation energy of the reaction  
  _Rationale:_ Raising activation energy would slow, not speed, the reaction.
- C. Changing the overall free-energy difference between reactants and products  
  _Rationale:_ Enzymes do not change the net energy of reactants and products.
- D. Being consumed permanently in the reaction  
  _Rationale:_ Enzymes are catalysts and are not consumed by the reaction.

**MST-0424-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) In double-stranded DNA, adenine pairs with:

- A. Thymine **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: adenine pairs with thymine (A-T) in DNA.
- B. Guanine  
  _Rationale:_ Guanine pairs with cytosine, not adenine.
- C. Cytosine  
  _Rationale:_ Cytosine pairs with guanine, not adenine.
- D. Another adenine  
  _Rationale:_ Bases pair complementarily; adenine does not pair with adenine.

**MST-0424-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO conditions are required for natural selection to change a population over time?

- A. Heritable variation among individuals **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: traits must be heritable for selection to act across generations.
- B. Differential reproductive success linked to that variation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: some variants must leave more offspring than others.
- C. Every individual being genetically identical  
  _Rationale:_ No variation means natural selection has nothing to act on.
- D. Acquired traits always being passed to offspring  
  _Rationale:_ Selection acts on heritable, not merely acquired, traits.
- E. A completely unchanging, infinite environment  
  _Rationale:_ Selection does not require an unchanging infinite environment.
