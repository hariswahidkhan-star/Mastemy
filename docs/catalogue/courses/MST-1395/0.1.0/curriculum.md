## Learning outcomes

1. Explain why organisations need an AI use policy
2. Identify the components of an effective AI use policy
3. Draft policy provisions for acceptable use, data and risk
4. Plan to implement, communicate and maintain the policy

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Written components are not assessed by MCQ/MR; model-answer analysis is shown by video only.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)


**MST-1395-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why is an existing general IT policy often insufficient for AI use?

- A. It may not address AI-specific risks like data leakage and hallucinated output **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI introduces risks older policies did not cover.
- B. IT policies are always illegal  
  _Rationale:_ IT policies are not illegal; they are just incomplete here.
- C. AI has no risks worth a policy  
  _Rationale:_ AI use carries real risks.
- D. A policy would ban all technology  
  _Rationale:_ A good policy enables safe use, not a blanket ban.

**MST-1395-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO belong in the data-handling section of an AI use policy? (Select TWO.)

- A. What categories of data may and may not be entered into AI tools **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: data classification rules are essential.
- B. How confidential and personal data must be handled **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: confidentiality and PII handling belong here.
- C. The company's holiday schedule  
  _Rationale:_ Unrelated to AI data handling.
- D. Which font the policy uses  
  _Rationale:_ Formatting is not a data-handling rule.

**MST-1395-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why do AI use policies often define risk tiers instead of one blanket rule?

- A. Different uses carry different risk and need proportionate controls **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: tiering matches controls to risk.
- B. Tiers make the policy longer for its own sake  
  _Rationale:_ Length is not the purpose; proportionate control is.
- C. All AI use is identically risky  
  _Rationale:_ Risk varies by use case.
- D. Tiers remove the need for any human oversight  
  _Rationale:_ Higher tiers usually add oversight, not remove it.
