## Learning outcomes

1. Define a YouTube channel strategy and content plan
2. Optimise videos for YouTube search and discovery
3. Design packaging: titles, thumbnails and retention
4. Analyse YouTube analytics to improve performance

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A creator wants to grow a focused YouTube channel. They must set positioning and content pillars, optimise videos for search and suggested feeds, design titles and thumbnails that earn clicks honestly, and use YouTube analytics to decide the next videos, defending the plan against chasing unrelated viral trends.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1124-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A video's click-through rate on YouTube is driven most directly by its:

- A. Title and thumbnail packaging **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: packaging determines whether an impression becomes a click.
- B. Upload file format  
  _Rationale:_ Format does not drive CTR.
- C. Number of tags only  
  _Rationale:_ Tags are a minor discovery signal, not CTR.
- D. Channel creation date  
  _Rationale:_ Unrelated to CTR.

**MST-1124-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO metrics best indicate whether a video satisfied viewers? (Select TWO.)

- A. Average view duration / retention **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: retention shows whether viewers stayed.
- B. Returning-viewer and watch-time trends **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: sustained watch time signals satisfaction.
- C. Raw impression count alone  
  _Rationale:_ Impressions are reach, not satisfaction.
- D. Channel banner dimensions  
  _Rationale:_ Not a performance metric.

**MST-1124-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Content pillars help a channel by:

- A. Giving the audience and algorithm a consistent, predictable focus **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: pillars build a coherent audience and recommendation signal.
- B. Guaranteeing monetisation on every video  
  _Rationale:_ Pillars do not guarantee revenue.
- C. Removing the need for thumbnails  
  _Rationale:_ Packaging still matters.
- D. Automatically translating the video  
  _Rationale:_ Unrelated to pillars.
