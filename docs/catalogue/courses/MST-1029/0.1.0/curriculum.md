## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1029-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A provision under IAS 37 is recognised only when which conditions are all met?

- A. A present obligation from a past event, a probable outflow, and a reliable estimate **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: all three recognition criteria must hold.
- B. Management merely expects a future cost  
  _Rationale:_ An expectation of future cost is not a present obligation.
- C. A possible obligation that might arise  
  _Rationale:_ A possible obligation is a contingent liability, not a provision.
- D. Any uncertainty in a future period  
  _Rationale:_ Uncertainty alone does not meet the recognition test.

**MST-1029-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO treatments are correct for an item that is only a contingent liability? (Select TWO)

- A. Do not recognise it as a liability on the balance sheet **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: contingent liabilities are not recognised, only disclosed.
- B. Disclose it in the notes unless the outflow is remote **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: disclosure is required unless the possibility of outflow is remote.
- C. Recognise it as a full provision immediately  
  _Rationale:_ Contingent liabilities are not recognised as provisions.
- D. Capitalise it as an asset  
  _Rationale:_ A contingent liability is never an asset.

**MST-1029-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) How should a material, long-dated provision be measured under IAS 37?

- A. At the present value of the best estimate, discounting for the time value of money **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: material long-dated provisions are discounted to present value.
- B. At the undiscounted total of expected payments  
  _Rationale:_ Discounting is required where the time value is material.
- C. At zero until the obligation is settled  
  _Rationale:_ The obligation is recognised when criteria are met, not deferred.
- D. At the highest possible outcome always  
  _Rationale:_ Measurement is a best estimate, not the worst case by default.
