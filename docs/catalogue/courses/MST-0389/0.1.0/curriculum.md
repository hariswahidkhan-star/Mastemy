## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0389-Q0001** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) A shirt costs $40 and is discounted 25%. What is the sale price?

- A. $30 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 25% of 40 is 10, so 40 - 10 = 30.
- B. $15  
  _Rationale:_ This subtracts too much; 25% of 40 is 10, not 25.
- C. $35  
  _Rationale:_ This uses a 12.5% discount, not 25%.
- D. $10  
  _Rationale:_ This is the discount amount, not the sale price.

**MST-0389-Q0002** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) If 3x + 5 = 20, then x equals:

- A. 5 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 3x = 15, so x = 5.
- B. 15  
  _Rationale:_ This forgets to divide by 3.
- C. 25  
  _Rationale:_ This adds instead of subtracting 5.
- D. 3  
  _Rationale:_ This does not satisfy the equation.

**MST-0389-Q0003** (multiple-answer-selection, Select TWO) Which TWO statements about the data set {2, 2, 3, 9} are true?

- A. The mean is 4 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: (2+2+3+9)/4 = 16/4 = 4.
- B. The median is 2.5 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the middle two values are 2 and 3, averaging 2.5.
- C. The mean equals the median  
  _Rationale:_ The mean (4) does not equal the median (2.5).
- D. The mode is 9  
  _Rationale:_ The mode is 2, the most frequent value.
- E. The range is 2  
  _Rationale:_ The range is 9 - 2 = 7.
