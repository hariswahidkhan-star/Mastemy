## Learning outcomes

1. Prepare and analyze financial statements and disclosures under US GAAP
2. Account for select balance-sheet accounts
3. Account for select transactions and events
4. Apply reporting for state and local governments

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0017-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under the ASC 606 five-step model, revenue is recognized when:

- A. The entity satisfies a performance obligation by transferring control **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: revenue is recognized as control of the good/service transfers.
- B. Cash is received from the customer  
  _Rationale:_ Cash receipt timing does not determine recognition under ASC 606.
- C. The contract is signed  
  _Rationale:_ Signing alone does not transfer control.
- D. The invoice is mailed  
  _Rationale:_ Invoicing is an administrative act, not the recognition trigger.

**MST-0017-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) In the statement of cash flows (indirect method), depreciation expense is:

- A. Added back to net income in operating activities **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: depreciation is a non-cash expense added back under the indirect method.
- B. Subtracted in investing activities  
  _Rationale:_ Depreciation is not an investing cash flow.
- C. Shown as a financing inflow  
  _Rationale:_ It is not a financing item.
- D. Ignored entirely  
  _Rationale:_ It must be adjusted for as a non-cash item.

**MST-0017-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Under ASC 842, which TWO are true for a lessee's finance lease at commencement? (Select TWO)

- A. A right-of-use asset is recognized **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: lessees recognize a right-of-use asset.
- B. A lease liability is recognized **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: lessees recognize a lease liability.
- C. Nothing is recorded on the balance sheet  
  _Rationale:_ ASC 842 brings most leases onto the balance sheet.
- D. The asset is expensed immediately in full  
  _Rationale:_ The ROU asset is amortized, not fully expensed at once.
