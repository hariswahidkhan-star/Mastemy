## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1116-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) The main purpose of a discovery call is to:

- A. Present the full product demo immediately  
  _Rationale:_ Demoing before discovery risks solving the wrong problem.
- B. Understand the customer's problem, impact and decision process **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: discovery surfaces need, impact and how the decision is made.
- C. Ask for the signature on the first call  
  _Rationale:_ Pushing to close before understanding the need is premature.
- D. Quote the lowest possible price  
  _Rationale:_ Price is not the point of discovery.

**MST-1116-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) An ideal customer profile (ICP) is most useful because it helps a seller:

- A. Treat every company as equally likely to buy  
  _Rationale:_ An ICP does the opposite: it prioritises fit.
- B. Focus effort on accounts most likely to succeed and buy **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an ICP concentrates scarce selling time on best-fit accounts.
- C. Avoid ever qualifying out a deal  
  _Rationale:_ An ICP supports qualifying out poor-fit deals.
- D. Set the final contract price  
  _Rationale:_ Pricing is a separate decision from the ICP.

**MST-1116-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are better first moves than discounting when you hit a price objection? (Select TWO)

- A. Clarify the concern behind the objection **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: understanding the real concern often removes the need to discount.
- B. Re-establish the value against the customer's cost of the problem **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: framing value against the problem's cost reframes price.
- C. Immediately offer the largest available discount  
  _Rationale:_ Discounting first trains the buyer to push on price.
- D. End the conversation to avoid the topic  
  _Rationale:_ Avoiding the objection does not resolve it.
