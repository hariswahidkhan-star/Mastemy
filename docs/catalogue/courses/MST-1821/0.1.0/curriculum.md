## Integrative case

A friend wants to open a small cafe and assumes 'if sales are high, I'll be fine'. Walk through the fundamentals: how the cafe creates value, how revenue, costs and profit differ from cash, how its functions connect, who the real customers are, and what responsible, sustainable operation requires.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1821-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A company reports a healthy profit yet cannot pay its suppliers this month. What distinction explains this?

- A. Profit is an accounting result; cash is the money actually available **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a profitable business can still run short of cash due to timing.
- B. Profit and cash are always identical  
  _Rationale:_ They differ because of timing, credit and non-cash items.
- C. Profit means suppliers are automatically paid  
  _Rationale:_ Payment depends on available cash, not reported profit.
- D. Cash is irrelevant if profit is positive  
  _Rationale:_ Cash flow determines whether obligations can be met.

**MST-1821-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which statements about the functions of a business are correct? (Select TWO)

- A. Marketing, operations, finance and people must work together **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the functions form an interconnected system.
- B. A single customer order can touch several functions **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an order flows across sales, operations and finance.
- C. Each function should work in complete isolation  
  _Rationale:_ Silos cause breakdowns in serving the customer.
- D. Finance is the only function that matters  
  _Rationale:_ All functions contribute to value creation.

**MST-1821-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A new owner says 'my target customer is everyone.' Why is this weak?

- A. Trying to serve everyone usually means serving no one well **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a defined target lets the business tailor and differentiate its offer.
- B. It is the ideal marketing strategy  
  _Rationale:_ Undifferentiated targeting dilutes the offer.
- C. Markets do not have segments  
  _Rationale:_ Markets contain distinct segments with different needs.
- D. Positioning is unnecessary for small businesses  
  _Rationale:_ Even small businesses benefit from a clear target.
