## Learning outcomes

1. Use A1 greetings, numbers and everyday vocabulary in context
2. Apply present-tense regular verbs, gender and noun-adjective agreement
3. Understand simple written and spoken Spanish on familiar topics

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning (vocabulary, rules, comprehension) through selection items (MCQ/MR) only; does not assess live speaking, extended writing production or any hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A learner reads a short A1 self-introduction in Spanish and answers about it, then plans (in writing) a six-sentence self-introduction choosing correct verb forms, articles and adjective agreement, explaining each grammar choice.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1861-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which greeting is the most appropriate formal way to address a stranger in the afternoon?

- A. Buenas tardes **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'Buenas tardes' means 'good afternoon' and is appropriately formal.
- B. Hola, tio  
  _Rationale:_ 'Hola, tio' is very informal slang, not suitable for a stranger.
- C. Buenas noches  
  _Rationale:_ 'Buenas noches' means 'good evening/night', not afternoon.
- D. Adios  
  _Rationale:_ 'Adios' means 'goodbye', not a greeting on meeting.

**MST-1861-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO phrases show correct noun-article gender agreement in Spanish? (Select TWO.)

- A. la casa (the house) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'casa' is feminine, so 'la' is the correct article.
- B. el libro (the book) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'libro' is masculine, so 'el' is the correct article.
- C. el casa  
  _Rationale:_ Incorrect: 'casa' is feminine and takes 'la', not 'el'.
- D. la libro  
  _Rationale:_ Incorrect: 'libro' is masculine and takes 'el', not 'la'.

**MST-1861-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Complete with the correctly agreeing adjective: 'Las flores son ____.' (The flowers are red.)

- A. rojas **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 'flores' is feminine plural, so 'rojas' agrees in gender and number.
- B. rojo  
  _Rationale:_ 'rojo' is masculine singular and does not agree with feminine plural.
- C. roja  
  _Rationale:_ 'roja' is feminine singular; the noun is plural.
- D. rojos  
  _Rationale:_ 'rojos' is masculine plural; the noun is feminine.
