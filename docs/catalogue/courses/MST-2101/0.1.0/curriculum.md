## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the colonial foundations and the origins of the American Revolution
2. Analyse the creation of the constitutional republic and its early tensions
3. Trace the causes, course and consequences of the Civil War and Reconstruction
4. Explain industrialisation, immigration and reform from the late 19th century
5. Evaluate the United States' changing global role across the 20th century
6. Assess movements for civil rights and the major debates of recent decades

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2101-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Historians overwhelmingly identify what as the central cause of the Civil War?

- A. Conflict over the expansion and future of slavery **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the sectional crisis turned on slavery's extension into the territories.
- B. Disagreement over the metric system  
  _Rationale:_ This was not a national dividing issue.
- C. A dispute over the national anthem  
  _Rationale:_ No such dispute drove secession.
- D. Competition over railroad gauges  
  _Rationale:_ A minor technical matter, not the cause of war.

**MST-2101-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO were direct constitutional or legal outcomes of the Civil War and Reconstruction? (Select TWO.)

- A. The abolition of slavery by the Thirteenth Amendment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the Thirteenth Amendment ended legal slavery.
- B. Birthright citizenship and equal protection under the Fourteenth Amendment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the Fourteenth Amendment established these principles.
- C. The creation of the Federal Reserve  
  _Rationale:_ The Federal Reserve was established in 1913, unrelated to Reconstruction.
- D. The direct election of senators  
  _Rationale:_ That came with the Seventeenth Amendment in 1913.

**MST-2101-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why is 'the civil rights movement was the work of one leader' a misleading summary?

- A. It depended on many organisations, local organisers and sustained grassroots action over decades **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the movement was broad, decentralised and long, not the work of one figure.
- B. Because there were no national leaders at all  
  _Rationale:_ There were prominent leaders; the error is crediting only one.
- C. Because it achieved nothing legally  
  _Rationale:_ It produced major legislation; the error is about who made it happen.
- D. Because it occurred entirely in one city  
  _Rationale:_ Campaigns spanned the country.
