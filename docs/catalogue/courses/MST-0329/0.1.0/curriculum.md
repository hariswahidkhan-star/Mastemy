## Learning outcomes

1. Explain construction safety management systems and the technician's role
2. Apply hazard recognition, assessment and control on construction sites
3. Apply safety program, training and incident management practices
4. Explain regulatory compliance, documentation and continuous improvement

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0329-Q0001** (single, Select ONE) Using the hierarchy of controls, the MOST preferred way to deal with a fall hazard at an unprotected edge is to:

- A. Eliminate the hazard or redesign the task so the edge exposure is removed **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: elimination/design-out sits at the top of the hierarchy.
- B. Issue harnesses and rely on PPE  
  _Rationale:_ PPE is the least preferred control, used when higher controls are not feasible.
- C. Post a warning sign only  
  _Rationale:_ Administrative signage alone does not remove the hazard.
- D. Ask workers to be careful  
  _Rationale:_ Relying on behaviour is the weakest control.

**MST-0329-Q0002** (single, Select ONE) The main reason to investigate a near-miss is to:

- A. Find and fix the causes before an actual injury occurs **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: near-misses reveal causes that can be corrected proactively.
- B. Assign blame to a worker  
  _Rationale:_ Investigations seek causes, not blame.
- C. Avoid any paperwork  
  _Rationale:_ Investigation produces useful records.
- D. Close the site  
  _Rationale:_ A near-miss does not automatically close a site.

**MST-0329-Q0003** (multiple, Select TWO) Select TWO controls that rank ABOVE personal protective equipment in the hierarchy of controls.

- A. Engineering controls **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: engineering controls rank above PPE.
- B. Substitution **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: substitution ranks above PPE.
- C. Hard hats  
  _Rationale:_ A hard hat is PPE, the lowest tier.
- D. Safety glasses  
  _Rationale:_ Safety glasses are PPE.
- E. High-visibility vests  
  _Rationale:_ Hi-vis clothing is PPE.
