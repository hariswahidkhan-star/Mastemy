## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0060-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In the financial-planning process, gathering a client's goals and relevant data should occur:

- A. Before developing and presenting recommendations **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: understanding the client and gathering data precede analysis and recommendations.
- B. After the plan has already been implemented  
  _Rationale:_ Implementing before gathering data reverses the process.
- C. Only if the client requests it  
  _Rationale:_ Data gathering is a required step, not optional.
- D. After monitoring the plan for a year  
  _Rationale:_ Monitoring is a later step, not before data gathering.

**MST-0060-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) For risk management, purchasing insurance is an example of which risk-handling technique?

- A. Risk transfer **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: insurance transfers the financial consequences of a risk to the insurer.
- B. Risk avoidance  
  _Rationale:_ Avoidance means not undertaking the activity at all, not insuring it.
- C. Risk retention  
  _Rationale:_ Retention means bearing the risk yourself, the opposite of insuring it.
- D. Risk reduction  
  _Rationale:_ Reduction lowers likelihood or impact directly; insurance transfers the cost.

**MST-0060-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO tools commonly used in estate planning.

- A. A will directing the distribution of assets **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a will is a foundational estate-planning document.
- B. A revocable living trust **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a living trust is a common estate-planning vehicle that can avoid probate.
- C. A short-term commodity futures contract  
  _Rationale:_ Commodity futures are a trading instrument, not an estate-planning tool.
- D. A corporate expense report  
  _Rationale:_ Expense reports are unrelated to estate planning.
- E. A payroll tax filing  
  _Rationale:_ Payroll filings are a business-tax matter, not estate planning.
