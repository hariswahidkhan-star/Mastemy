## Learning outcomes

1. Principles and prohibitions
2. Profit-and-loss sharing
3. Sale and lease-based contracts
4. Capital markets and insurance
5. Governance and practice

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses conceptual knowledge through selection items; this is general education, not a Shariah ruling or financial advice, and it does not assess the Shariah-compliance of a specific product.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1733-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Riba is prohibited in Islamic finance. Riba most directly refers to:

- A. Interest or a guaranteed return on a loan of money **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: riba centres on interest/usury on money.
- B. Any profit from trade  
  _Rationale:_ Lawful trade profit is permitted; riba is not trade profit.
- C. Paying employees a salary  
  _Rationale:_ Salaries are permitted and unrelated to riba.
- D. Charging a fair price for goods  
  _Rationale:_ Fair trade pricing is permitted.

**MST-1733-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select ALL that apply) Which two statements about Sukuk are correct? (Select TWO) (Select TWO)

- A. They represent ownership of or entitlement to underlying assets or projects **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: Sukuk convey asset-linked entitlements.
- B. Returns should derive from asset performance, not interest **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: Sukuk returns stem from assets, not riba.
- C. They are simply conventional interest-bearing bonds  
  _Rationale:_ Sukuk are structured to avoid interest.
- D. They guarantee a fixed interest coupon  
  _Rationale:_ A fixed interest coupon would be non-compliant.

**MST-1733-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A Shariah supervisory board's main role is to:

- A. Review and approve products for Shariah compliance **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the board provides Shariah oversight and approval.
- B. Set the central bank's interest rate  
  _Rationale:_ That is a monetary-policy function, not the board's role.
- C. Guarantee investors a profit  
  _Rationale:_ No profit guarantee is given; outcomes depend on assets.
- D. Manage the firm's marketing  
  _Rationale:_ Marketing is not the board's function.
