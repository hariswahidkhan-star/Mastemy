## Integrative case

A claims department takes three weeks to approve routine payouts and no one knows why. Act as the business analyst: identify the real business need, elicit requirements from adjusters and managers, model the as-is process to expose handoffs and bottlenecks, design a to-be process, and specify testable, traceable requirements with acceptance criteria.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1096-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which requirement statement is best written?

- A. The system shall email the customer within 2 minutes of claim approval **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it is specific, measurable and testable.
- B. The system should be fast  
  _Rationale:_ 'Fast' is not measurable or testable.
- C. The system shall use a dropdown menu on the approval screen  
  _Rationale:_ This specifies design, not a requirement of business need.
- D. The system shall be user-friendly  
  _Rationale:_ 'User-friendly' is subjective and not verifiable as written.

**MST-1096-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which statements about non-functional requirements are correct? (Select TWO)

- A. They describe qualities such as performance, security or availability **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: non-functional requirements constrain how well the system behaves.
- B. They should still be specified in testable terms **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a target like '99.9% uptime' makes them verifiable.
- C. They define the specific features the system provides  
  _Rationale:_ That describes functional requirements.
- D. They can never be measured  
  _Rationale:_ Well-written non-functional requirements include measurable targets.

**MST-1096-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) An analyst presents a 'current' process map that actually shows how work should ideally flow. Why is this a problem?

- A. Improvement decisions will be based on a process that does not reflect reality **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an inaccurate as-is hides the real bottlenecks and risks wrong fixes.
- B. Ideal processes can never be drawn  
  _Rationale:_ To-be maps are valid; the error is mislabelling it as as-is.
- C. Swimlanes are not allowed in as-is maps  
  _Rationale:_ Swimlanes are standard and appropriate.
- D. As-is maps must omit handoffs  
  _Rationale:_ Handoffs are exactly what an as-is map should expose.
