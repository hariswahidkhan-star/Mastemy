## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1268-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A market with a single dominant supplier and high barriers to entry is a:

- A. Perfect competition  
  _Rationale:_ Perfect competition has many suppliers and no barriers.
- B. Monopoly **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a monopoly has one dominant supplier and high entry barriers.
- C. Oligopoly  
  _Rationale:_ An oligopoly has a few dominant suppliers, not one.
- D. Monopsony  
  _Rationale:_ A monopsony is a market with a single dominant buyer, not seller.

**MST-1268-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which analysis tool scans political, economic, social, technological, legal and environmental factors?

- A. PESTLE **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: PESTLE scans these macro-environmental factors.
- B. Kraljic matrix  
  _Rationale:_ Kraljic positions purchases by risk and value.
- C. RACI chart  
  _Rationale:_ RACI assigns responsibility for tasks.
- D. Five forces  
  _Rationale:_ Five forces analyses competitive forces within an industry, not the macro-environment.

**MST-1268-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO practices keep a tender evaluation fair and defensible?

- A. Setting and publishing weighted criteria before bids open **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: fixed, pre-published criteria ensure fairness.
- B. Scoring all bids against the same criteria **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: consistent scoring is essential to a defensible award.
- C. Adjusting the weightings to favour a preferred bidder  
  _Rationale:_ Changing weightings to favour a bidder is improper and indefensible.
- D. Discarding the audit trail after award  
  _Rationale:_ An audit trail must be retained to justify the decision.
