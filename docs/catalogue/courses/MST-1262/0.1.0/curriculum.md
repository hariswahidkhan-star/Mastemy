## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1262-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which specification type states the outcome a supplier must achieve rather than exactly how to achieve it?

- A. Performance specification **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a performance specification defines required outputs/outcomes and leaves method to the supplier.
- B. Conformance specification  
  _Rationale:_ A conformance spec dictates detailed inputs, materials or methods.
- C. Technical drawing  
  _Rationale:_ A drawing is a detailed conformance document.
- D. Brand specification  
  _Rationale:_ A brand spec names a product, not an outcome.

**MST-1262-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) When comparing two machines, which approach captures purchase, running, maintenance and disposal costs together?

- A. Lowest purchase price  
  _Rationale:_ Purchase price ignores downstream costs.
- B. Whole-life costing **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: whole-life/total cost of ownership sums all costs across the asset's life.
- C. Marginal costing  
  _Rationale:_ Marginal costing is about the cost of one more unit, not asset life.
- D. Standard costing  
  _Rationale:_ Standard costing sets expected unit costs for control, not life-cycle comparison.

**MST-1262-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO of Porter's five forces are being described when many substitute products exist and buyers can switch easily?

- A. Threat of substitutes **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: readily available alternatives is the threat of substitutes.
- B. Bargaining power of buyers **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: easy switching increases buyer bargaining power.
- C. Threat of new entrants  
  _Rationale:_ New entrants concern barriers to entering the market, not switching.
- D. Rivalry among existing competitors  
  _Rationale:_ Rivalry concerns competition intensity between current players.
