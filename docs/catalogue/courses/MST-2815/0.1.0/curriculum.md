## Learning outcomes

1. Understand a fraction as equal parts of a whole or a set
2. Recognise and name common fractions such as halves, quarters and thirds
3. Find equivalent fractions using diagrams
4. Compare and order simple fractions
5. Understand tenths and hundredths as decimals
6. Connect simple fractions to their decimal forms

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2815-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which fraction is the same as one half?

- A. 2/4 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: two quarters cover the same amount as one half.
- B. 1/3  
  _Rationale:_ One third is less than one half.
- C. 1/4  
  _Rationale:_ One quarter is half of a half, not a whole half.
- D. 2/3  
  _Rationale:_ Two thirds is more than one half.

**MST-2815-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO amounts that are equal to one half. (Select TWO.)

- A. 0.5 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 0.5 is the decimal form of one half.
- B. 3/6 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: three sixths simplifies to one half.
- C. 0.2  
  _Rationale:_ 0.2 is two tenths, which is less than one half.
- D. 1/3  
  _Rationale:_ One third is less than one half.

**MST-2815-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which fraction is larger, 3/4 or 1/4?

- A. 3/4, because it is three of the same-sized parts and 1/4 is only one **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: with the same denominator, more parts means a larger fraction.
- B. 1/4, because 1 is a smaller number  
  _Rationale:_ A smaller top number does not make the fraction larger here.
- C. They are equal  
  _Rationale:_ Three quarters is three times one quarter.
- D. You cannot compare them  
  _Rationale:_ Same-sized parts can be compared by counting them.
