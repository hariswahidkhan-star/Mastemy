## Learning outcomes

1. Summarise a contract's key terms accurately
2. Flag risky or missing clauses against a checklist
3. Route issues to legal and respect the tool's limits
4. Apply confidentiality, accuracy and human-review controls to the workflow

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every course outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Hands-on operation of ChatGPT, the quality of live AI outputs, and professional judgement on accepting AI suggestions are taught by video demonstration and model-answer analysis; the certificate assessment uses single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only and never requires uploads, recorded work, graded prompts or peer review.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0486-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the correct role of ChatGPT in contract review?

- A. Support and triage, with a qualified lawyer accountable for advice **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the tool can summarise and flag issues, but legal judgement and advice remain with a lawyer.
- B. A replacement for legal counsel  
  _Rationale:_ It cannot provide accountable legal advice.
- C. A source of binding legal decisions  
  _Rationale:_ AI output is not a binding legal determination.
- D. A guarantee the contract is safe  
  _Rationale:_ No tool can guarantee a contract is risk-free.

**MST-0486-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why review a contract against a clause checklist with ChatGPT?

- A. To systematically flag missing or risky clauses for a human **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a checklist drives consistent issue-spotting that a human then evaluates.
- B. To finalise the contract without legal input  
  _Rationale:_ A checklist does not replace legal review.
- C. Because AI never misses anything  
  _Rationale:_ AI can miss or misread clauses, so human review is needed.
- D. To avoid reading the contract at all  
  _Rationale:_ The reviewer must still understand the document.

**MST-0486-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are appropriate uses of AI in contract work? (Select TWO)

- A. Drafting a plain-language summary for a business reader **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: summarising is a safe, useful support task.
- B. Preparing a question list for the legal team **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: surfacing questions for legal is appropriate triage.
- C. Giving the counterparty final legal advice  
  _Rationale:_ Providing accountable legal advice is beyond the tool and the user's role.
- D. Signing off risk without any lawyer  
  _Rationale:_ Risk sign-off requires qualified legal judgement.
