## Learning outcomes

1. Explain why cash and profit differ and why cash management matters
2. Interpret the cash flow statement and build a short-term cash forecast
3. Measure and manage the cash conversion cycle
4. Apply levers across receivables, payables and inventory
5. Recognise liquidity risk and the role of financing buffers
6. Communicate a cash position and funding need clearly

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2488-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why can a growing, profitable company still face a cash crisis?

- A. Growth ties up cash in receivables and inventory faster than profit converts to cash **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: rapid growth absorbs working capital.
- B. Because profit and cash are legally required to be equal  
  _Rationale:_ They are not required to be equal.
- C. Because growth always reduces revenue  
  _Rationale:_ Growth does not reduce revenue.
- D. Because depreciation uses up cash each month  
  _Rationale:_ Depreciation is a non-cash charge.

**MST-2488-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO actions shorten the cash conversion cycle? (Select TWO.)

- A. Collecting receivables faster **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: faster collection reduces days sales outstanding.
- B. Negotiating longer payment terms with suppliers **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: longer payables terms extend the cash held.
- C. Holding more slow-moving inventory  
  _Rationale:_ More inventory lengthens the cycle.
- D. Paying suppliers earlier than due  
  _Rationale:_ Paying early shortens payables and uses cash sooner.

**MST-2488-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A 13-week cash forecast is most useful because it:

- A. Shows the timing of receipts and payments so a shortfall can be anticipated and managed **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it is about timing, not period profit.
- B. Replaces the need for an annual budget  
  _Rationale:_ It complements, not replaces, the budget.
- C. Guarantees the company will not run out of cash  
  _Rationale:_ It informs decisions but guarantees nothing.
- D. Measures long-term profitability  
  _Rationale:_ It is a short-term liquidity tool.
