## Learning outcomes

1. Choose the right ChatGPT model, mode and interface features for a professional task
2. Construct prompts that produce consistent, reviewable professional output
3. Use ChatGPT with uploaded files and browsing while keeping outputs verifiable
4. Apply privacy, accuracy and review controls to AI-assisted work

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every course outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Hands-on operation of ChatGPT, the quality of live AI outputs, and professional judgement on accepting AI suggestions are taught by video demonstration and model-answer analysis; the certificate assessment uses single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only and never requires uploads, recorded work, graded prompts or peer review.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0471-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A user needs ChatGPT to work through a multi-step logic problem and show careful reasoning. Which choice best fits the task?

- A. A reasoning-optimised model **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reasoning-optimised models are designed for multi-step problems where accuracy matters more than speed.
- B. The fastest general chat model  
  _Rationale:_ Speed-optimised models trade depth of reasoning for latency and are weaker on multi-step logic.
- C. Voice mode  
  _Rationale:_ Voice changes the input/output channel, not the model's reasoning depth.
- D. A larger context window alone  
  _Rationale:_ A bigger context window holds more text but does not by itself improve step-by-step reasoning.

**MST-0471-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Before emailing an AI-drafted proposal to a client, what is the most important final step?

- A. A human review for accuracy and confidentiality **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a human-in-the-loop review catches factual errors, tone issues and any leaked confidential data before it leaves the organisation.
- B. Regenerating the draft three more times  
  _Rationale:_ Regeneration may change wording but does not guarantee accuracy or confidentiality.
- C. Switching to a newer model  
  _Rationale:_ Changing models does not substitute for verifying the specific draft being sent.
- D. Increasing the response length  
  _Rationale:_ Length is unrelated to whether the content is correct and safe to send.

**MST-0471-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO practices reduce the risk of acting on a hallucinated ChatGPT answer? (Select TWO)

- A. Ask the model to cite sources and then check them **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: requesting and verifying sources lets a human confirm claims independently.
- B. Verify key facts against an authoritative source **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: independent verification is the direct control against fabricated details.
- C. Trust answers that sound confident  
  _Rationale:_ Confidence of tone is not evidence of accuracy; hallucinations are often stated confidently.
- D. Use a longer prompt  
  _Rationale:_ Prompt length does not prevent fabrication of facts.
