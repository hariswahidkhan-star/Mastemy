## Learning outcomes

1. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Monitoring, logging, and remediation' to exam-style problems
2. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Reliability and business continuity' to exam-style problems
3. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Deployment, provisioning, and automation' to exam-style problems
4. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Security and compliance' to exam-style problems
5. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Networking and content delivery' to exam-style problems
6. Apply the knowledge and techniques of 'Cost and performance optimization' to exam-style problems

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Official AWS examinations may use item formats (for example scenario, performance-based or constructed-response tasks) that this platform's MCQ/MR forms do not reproduce; see the exam-version record. Domain structure here is a design assumption pending official confirmation.
