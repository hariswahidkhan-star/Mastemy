## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0043-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under IFRS 15, when should revenue from a contract with a customer be recognised?

- A. When (or as) each performance obligation is satisfied by transferring control of a good or service **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: IFRS 15 recognises revenue as performance obligations are satisfied through the transfer of control.
- B. When the customer's cash is received  
  _Rationale:_ Cash receipt is not the recognition trigger; revenue follows transfer of control.
- C. When the contract is signed  
  _Rationale:_ Signing a contract alone does not transfer control of any good or service.
- D. Evenly over the entity's financial year  
  _Rationale:_ Straight-line over the year is not a basis in IFRS 15 unless it reflects satisfaction of an obligation.

**MST-0043-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under IFRS 16, how does a lessee generally account for a lease (other than short-term or low-value leases) at commencement?

- A. Recognise a right-of-use asset and a corresponding lease liability **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: IFRS 16 requires lessees to recognise a right-of-use asset and a lease liability for most leases.
- B. Charge all payments to profit or loss as an operating expense with no asset or liability  
  _Rationale:_ That is the superseded operating-lease treatment; IFRS 16 removed it for lessees except for exemptions.
- C. Recognise the leased asset at its full market value and no liability  
  _Rationale:_ The asset is measured at the lease liability plus certain costs, and a liability is recognised.
- D. Recognise only a liability and no asset  
  _Rationale:_ A right-of-use asset is recognised alongside the liability.

**MST-0043-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO conditions that must both be met for an investor to consolidate an investee as a subsidiary under IFRS 10.

- A. The investor has power over the investee **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: power over the relevant activities of the investee is a control criterion.
- B. The investor is exposed to variable returns from its involvement **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: exposure or rights to variable returns is a control criterion.
- C. The investor holds exactly 50% of the voting shares  
  _Rationale:_ Control is about power and returns, not a fixed 50% shareholding.
- D. The investee operates in the same country as the investor  
  _Rationale:_ Geography is irrelevant to the control assessment.
- E. The investor accounts for the investee using the equity method  
  _Rationale:_ The equity method applies to associates, not consolidated subsidiaries.
