## Learning outcomes

1. Explain health data content, structure and the legal health record
2. Describe classification systems, coding and reimbursement methodologies
3. Explain data integrity, information governance, privacy and security
4. Describe healthcare statistics, quality and health information technology

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0354-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A patient asks for a copy of their record. Which concept determines exactly which documents must be released?

- A. The definition of the legal health record **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the legal health record defines the documents disclosed for legal and release purposes.
- B. The size of the hard drive  
  _Rationale:_ Storage capacity is unrelated to what constitutes the legal record.
- C. The patient's insurance plan  
  _Rationale:_ The insurer does not define the legal health record's contents.
- D. The color of the file folder  
  _Rationale:_ This is irrelevant to record content.

**MST-0354-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) How do 'privacy' and 'security' differ in health information management?

- A. Privacy governs who may access data; security is the controls that protect it **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: privacy is about permitted access/use, security is the safeguards.
- B. They are identical terms  
  _Rationale:_ They are related but distinct concepts.
- C. Privacy applies only to paper and security only to electronic records  
  _Rationale:_ Both apply across paper and electronic records.
- D. Security governs who may access data and privacy protects it  
  _Rationale:_ This reverses the two definitions.

**MST-0354-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO systems used to classify or code healthcare data.

- A. ICD-10-CM for diagnoses **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ICD-10-CM is a diagnosis classification system.
- B. CPT for procedures and services **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: CPT codes procedures and services.
- C. A building's fire-evacuation plan  
  _Rationale:_ An evacuation plan is not a coding system.
- D. The staff parking policy  
  _Rationale:_ A parking policy is unrelated to clinical coding.
- E. The cafeteria menu  
  _Rationale:_ A menu is not a health-data classification system.
