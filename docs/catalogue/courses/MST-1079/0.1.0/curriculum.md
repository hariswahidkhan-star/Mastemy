## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1079-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An 'async-first' team treats which approach as the default?

- A. Real-time video calls for every discussion  
  _Rationale:_ That is sync-first and scales poorly across time zones.
- B. Written, documented communication that others read on their own schedule **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: async-first defaults to durable writing, reserving sync for what truly needs it.
- C. Instant replies expected within minutes  
  _Rationale:_ Expecting instant replies recreates always-on pressure, not async work.
- D. No written records at all  
  _Rationale:_ Async-first depends on strong written records.

**MST-1079-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) The healthiest way to manage a remote report's performance is to focus on:

- A. Hours shown as active in the chat tool  
  _Rationale:_ Activity signals measure presence, not value, and invite gaming.
- B. The outcomes and deliverables they are accountable for **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: managing by outcomes respects autonomy and measures real results.
- C. How quickly they answer messages  
  _Rationale:_ Response speed is not a measure of meaningful output.
- D. Webcam-on time during the day  
  _Rationale:_ Surveillance erodes trust without improving results.

**MST-1079-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO steps reduce proximity bias on a hybrid team? (Select TWO)

- A. Make sure remote members get the same access to informal information and sponsors **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: equal access counters the in-office information advantage.
- B. Base recognition and promotion on documented outcomes, not visibility **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: outcome-based decisions reduce bias toward whoever is seen most.
- C. Favour in-office staff for stretch projects because they are easier to reach  
  _Rationale:_ That is exactly the proximity bias to avoid.
- D. Hold key conversations in the hallway where only on-site staff hear them  
  _Rationale:_ Hallway decisions exclude remote colleagues and entrench bias.
