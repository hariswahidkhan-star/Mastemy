## Learning outcomes

1. Explain commercial contract management and the governing commercial law framework (knowledge practice)
2. Apply formation, terms and risk allocation in commercial contracts
3. Describe performance, changes and remedies under commercial contracts
4. Analyse closeout, compliance and ethics in the commercial environment

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1300-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A buyer and seller contract for the sale of goods. Which body of law most directly governs in the US commercial context?

- A. Federal acquisition regulation for agencies  
  _Rationale:_ That governs US federal government procurement, not commercial sales between private parties.
- B. The Uniform Commercial Code (sale-of-goods framework) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the UCC governs the sale of goods in the US commercial context.
- C. International maritime salvage law  
  _Rationale:_ Maritime salvage is a narrow, unrelated area.
- D. Criminal sentencing guidelines  
  _Rationale:_ Criminal law is unrelated to a commercial sale of goods.

**MST-1300-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which set of elements is generally required to form a valid commercial contract?

- A. Only a verbal promise with no exchange  
  _Rationale:_ A bare promise without consideration generally is not enforceable.
- B. Offer, acceptance and consideration (with capacity and lawful purpose) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: offer, acceptance and consideration, with capacity and legality, form a valid contract.
- C. A notarised seal and nothing else  
  _Rationale:_ A seal alone does not supply the required elements.
- D. Mutual dislike between the parties  
  _Rationale:_ Sentiment is irrelevant to contract formation.

**MST-1300-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO statements about remedies for breach of a commercial contract are correct?

- A. A material breach may excuse the non-breaching party's further performance **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a material breach can discharge the other party's remaining obligations.
- B. Damages generally aim to put the non-breaching party in the position it expected **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: expectation damages seek the benefit of the bargain.
- C. Any minor breach automatically terminates the contract  
  _Rationale:_ Minor breaches generally give a damages claim, not automatic termination.
- D. The breaching party is always entitled to keep all payments  
  _Rationale:_ That is not a general rule of remedies.
