## Learning outcomes

1. How insurance works
2. Core principles
3. Main types of cover
4. The policy and claims
5. Pricing and conduct

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses conceptual knowledge through selection items; this is general education, not insurance advice, and it does not assess suitability of a specific policy for an individual.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1724-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) The principle of indemnity means insurance aims to:

- A. Restore the insured to their position before the loss, not better **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: indemnity restores, it does not enrich.
- B. Pay the insured more than the loss as a reward  
  _Rationale:_ Profiting from a loss breaches indemnity.
- C. Pay a fixed amount regardless of the loss  
  _Rationale:_ That describes certain benefit policies, not the indemnity principle.
- D. Refund all premiums if no claim is made  
  _Rationale:_ Premiums are the cost of cover, not refundable by default.

**MST-1724-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select ALL that apply) Which two must generally be true for a valid insurance contract? (Select TWO) (Select TWO)

- A. The insured has an insurable interest in the subject **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: insurable interest is required.
- B. Material facts are disclosed in good faith **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: utmost good faith / disclosure is required.
- C. The insured is guaranteed to make a claim  
  _Rationale:_ No claim is guaranteed or required.
- D. The policy pays out even for deliberately caused losses  
  _Rationale:_ Deliberate self-inflicted loss is typically excluded.

**MST-1724-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A policy has a sum insured of 10,000 and an excess of 500. A covered loss of 3,000 would pay:

- A. 2,500 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the insurer pays the loss less the 500 excess.
- B. 3,000  
  _Rationale:_ The excess of 500 is deducted.
- C. 500  
  _Rationale:_ 500 is the excess retained by the insured, not the payout.
- D. 10,000  
  _Rationale:_ The sum insured is a ceiling, not the payout for a 3,000 loss.
