## Learning outcomes

1. Explain occupational health, hygiene and safety concepts and the technician's role
2. Apply recognition, evaluation and control of workplace health hazards
3. Apply sampling, monitoring and exposure-assessment methods
4. Explain safety programs, regulatory compliance and record-keeping

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0330-Q0001** (single, Select ONE) A single short 'grab' air sample should NOT be treated as a full exposure assessment because it:

- A. May not represent the worker's full-shift, time-weighted exposure **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a short sample can miss variation across the shift.
- B. Is always above the limit  
  _Rationale:_ A grab sample is not inherently high.
- C. Requires no laboratory analysis  
  _Rationale:_ Analysis is still needed.
- D. Measures noise, not chemicals  
  _Rationale:_ Grab sampling here concerns airborne agents.

**MST-0330-Q0002** (single, Select ONE) The preferred way to control a solvent overexposure is to:

- A. Apply engineering controls such as local exhaust ventilation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: engineering controls rank above PPE in the hierarchy.
- B. Issue respirators as the first and only step  
  _Rationale:_ Respirators are a lower-tier control.
- C. Rotate workers through the exposure faster  
  _Rationale:_ Rotation spreads exposure, it does not control the hazard.
- D. Post a sign about the smell  
  _Rationale:_ Signage does not reduce exposure.

**MST-0330-Q0003** (multiple, Select TWO) Select TWO examples of physical (non-chemical) workplace health hazards.

- A. Noise **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: noise is a physical agent.
- B. Heat stress **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: heat is a physical agent.
- C. Solvent vapour  
  _Rationale:_ Solvent vapour is a chemical agent.
- D. Bacterial contamination  
  _Rationale:_ Bacteria are a biological agent.
- E. Lead dust  
  _Rationale:_ Lead dust is a chemical/particulate agent.
