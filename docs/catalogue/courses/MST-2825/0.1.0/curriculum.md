## Learning outcomes

1. Understand function notation and evaluate functions
2. Plot and interpret linear graphs
3. Find the gradient and intercept of a straight line
4. Recognise and sketch quadratic and other non-linear graphs
5. Interpret graphs that model real situations
6. Find where graphs intersect to solve problems

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2825-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) For f(x) = 3x - 2, what is f(4)?

- A. 10 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: substitute x = 4 to get 3 x 4 - 2 = 12 - 2 = 10.
- B. 12  
  _Rationale:_ That forgets to subtract 2 after multiplying.
- C. 5  
  _Rationale:_ That computes 3 + 4 - 2 instead of 3 times 4 minus 2.
- D. 14  
  _Rationale:_ That adds 2 instead of subtracting it.

**MST-2825-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO features you can read directly from the equation y = 2x + 3. (Select TWO.)

- A. The gradient is 2 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: in y = mx + c, the coefficient of x is the gradient.
- B. The y-intercept is 3 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the constant term c is where the line crosses the y-axis.
- C. The gradient is 3  
  _Rationale:_ 3 is the y-intercept, not the gradient.
- D. The line passes through the origin  
  _Rationale:_ It crosses the y-axis at 3, not at 0.

**MST-2825-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A straight line passes through (0, 1) and (2, 5). What is its gradient?

- A. 2 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: gradient is change in y over change in x, (5 - 1)/(2 - 0) = 4/2 = 2.
- B. 4  
  _Rationale:_ 4 is the change in y only; gradient divides by the change in x.
- C. 1  
  _Rationale:_ 1 is the y-intercept, not the gradient.
- D. 0.5  
  _Rationale:_ That inverts the ratio; gradient is rise over run, 4/2 = 2.
