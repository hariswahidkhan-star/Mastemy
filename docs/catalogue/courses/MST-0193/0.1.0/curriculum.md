## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0193-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) You want incoming messages from your manager automatically moved to a specific folder. Which Outlook feature should you use?

- A. A rule **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: rules automatically act on incoming or outgoing messages, such as moving mail from a sender to a folder.
- B. A Quick Step  
  _Rationale:_ Quick Steps apply actions you trigger manually, not automatically on arrival.
- C. A signature  
  _Rationale:_ Signatures add text to outgoing messages.
- D. A category  
  _Rationale:_ Categories color-tag items but do not move them automatically.

**MST-0193-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the difference between an appointment and a meeting in Outlook?

- A. An appointment can invite attendees; a meeting cannot  
  _Rationale:_ This is reversed.
- B. A meeting invites other attendees; an appointment involves only you **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a meeting includes invited attendees and responses, whereas an appointment is a block of your own time.
- C. They are identical  
  _Rationale:_ They differ in whether attendees are invited.
- D. Only meetings can recur  
  _Rationale:_ Both appointments and meetings can be recurring.

**MST-0193-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO actions can you perform when managing a contact group? (Select TWO)

- A. Add multiple members to send to all with one address **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a contact group lets you address many recipients at once.
- B. Remove a member from the group **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: members can be removed from a contact group.
- C. Automatically archive the mailbox  
  _Rationale:_ Archiving is unrelated to contact groups.
- D. Convert the group into a calendar  
  _Rationale:_ A contact group cannot become a calendar.
- E. Encrypt the entire Outlook profile  
  _Rationale:_ Profile encryption is not a contact-group action.
