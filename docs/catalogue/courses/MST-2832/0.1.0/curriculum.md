## Learning outcomes

1. Name the main parts of a plant and state each part's job
2. Describe what a seed needs to germinate and grow
3. Explain in simple terms how leaves make food using sunlight
4. Trace how water travels from roots to leaves
5. Describe how flowers lead to seeds and new plants
6. Plan a fair test of one condition a plant needs

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A class plants bean seeds in identical pots but changes one thing per pot (light, water or warmth), records growth for two weeks, explains results using plant parts and how leaves make food, and recommends the best conditions for a healthy plant.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2832-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the main job of a plant's roots?

- A. To take in water and hold the plant in the ground **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: roots anchor the plant and absorb water.
- B. To make food from sunlight  
  _Rationale:_ Leaves make food; roots do not.
- C. To attract insects with colour  
  _Rationale:_ That is the flower's job.
- D. To make the plant smell nice  
  _Rationale:_ Scent usually comes from flowers, not roots.

**MST-2832-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A seedling kept in a dark cupboard grows pale and weak. What is the best explanation? (Select ONE)

- A. Without light its leaves cannot make enough food **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: light is needed for leaves to make food.
- B. Seeds do not need any water  
  _Rationale:_ This is about light, and water is still needed.
- C. Roots stop holding the plant in the dark  
  _Rationale:_ Darkness does not disable the roots' grip.
- D. Plants only grow at night  
  _Rationale:_ Plants need light to make food during the day.

**MST-2832-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO things a seed usually needs to start growing (germinate). (Select TWO)

- A. Water **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: water is needed to start germination.
- B. Warmth **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a suitable temperature helps seeds germinate.
- C. Bright sunlight on the seed itself  
  _Rationale:_ Many seeds germinate in the dark soil before needing light.
- D. Salt added to the soil  
  _Rationale:_ Salt harms most seeds rather than helping them.
