## Learning outcomes

1. Identify where AI helps across the marketing funnel
2. Use generative AI to draft and refine campaign content responsibly
3. Apply AI to audience segmentation and personalisation
4. Interpret AI-driven marketing analytics and recommendations
5. Recognise brand, privacy and disclosure risks in AI marketing

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1374-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An AI tool drafts ad copy that includes a specific product statistic. Before publishing, what should the marketer do first?

- A. Verify the statistic against a reliable source **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI can fabricate figures, so verification protects against false claims.
- B. Publish immediately to hit the deadline  
  _Rationale:_ Publishing unverified claims risks false advertising.
- C. Add more adjectives to make it persuasive  
  _Rationale:_ Persuasiveness does not fix an unverified claim.
- D. Assume the AI checked the fact already  
  _Rationale:_ AI does not reliably verify facts.

**MST-1374-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO actions support responsible use of AI in a marketing campaign? (Select TWO.)

- A. Disclose AI-generated content where it could mislead **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: disclosure maintains trust and honesty.
- B. Handle customer data within consent and privacy rules **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: lawful data use is a baseline obligation.
- C. Scrape personal data without consent for targeting  
  _Rationale:_ Unlawful data collection creates legal and trust risk.
- D. Present AI-invented testimonials as real  
  _Rationale:_ Fabricated testimonials are deceptive.

**MST-1374-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) An AI dashboard shows a channel with high clicks but near-zero conversions. What is the most reasonable interpretation?

- A. Clicks alone do not prove the channel drives valuable outcomes **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a vanity metric can hide poor downstream performance.
- B. The channel is definitely the best performer  
  _Rationale:_ High clicks without conversions is not strong performance.
- C. Conversions do not matter if clicks are high  
  _Rationale:_ Conversions are the outcome that typically matters.
- D. The dashboard must be broken  
  _Rationale:_ The pattern is common and interpretable, not necessarily an error.
