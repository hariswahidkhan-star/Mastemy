## Module and lesson plan

### M01 — Consolidation principles  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 26%)_

- M01L01 — Control, the group and the scope of consolidation (120 min)
- M01L02 — The consolidation process and worksheet overview (120 min)
- Worked applications: Decide whether an investee is a subsidiary, associate or neither from the facts; Walk a simple parent and subsidiary through a consolidation worksheet
- Common misconception addressed: Consolidating based on ownership percentage alone rather than control

### M02 — Acquisition accounting  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 26%)_

- M02L01 — Fair value of net assets at acquisition and goodwill (120 min)
- M02L02 — Non-controlling interests at acquisition (120 min)
- Worked applications: Calculate goodwill from consideration, NCI and net assets acquired; Measure NCI and its share of post-acquisition reserves
- Common misconception addressed: Forgetting to restate the subsidiary's assets to fair value at acquisition

### M03 — Intercompany eliminations  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 24%)_

- M03L01 — Intercompany balances, loans and trading (120 min)
- M03L02 — Unrealised profit in inventory and non-current assets (120 min)
- Worked applications: Eliminate an intercompany receivable and payable in full; Remove unrealised profit on inventory still held within the group
- Common misconception addressed: Eliminating only the buyer's side of an intercompany balance

### M04 — Consolidated statements  _(MASTEMY-DESIGN 24%)_

- M04L01 — Consolidated statement of financial position (120 min)
- M04L02 — Consolidated profit, reserves and disclosure basics (120 min)
- Worked applications: Assemble a consolidated statement of financial position from the worksheet; Split consolidated profit between the parent and the NCI
- Common misconception addressed: Adding the parent's cost of investment to assets instead of cancelling it

## Integrative case

Prepare consolidated financial statements for a small group: determine control and consolidation scope, eliminate intercompany balances and unrealised profit, measure non-controlling interests and goodwill at acquisition, and assemble the consolidated statement of financial position.
