## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0366-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A certified occupational therapy assistant (COTA) identifies that a client needs a change to the intervention plan. The COTA should:

- A. Communicate the finding to the supervising occupational therapist **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the OTA collaborates with the supervising OT, who is responsible for the intervention plan.
- B. Change the plan independently  
  _Rationale:_ Changing the plan is outside the OTA's independent scope.
- C. Stop seeing the client entirely  
  _Rationale:_ Discontinuing service is not the indicated or independent action.
- D. Wait until the next quarterly review  
  _Rationale:_ A needed change should be communicated promptly, not deferred.

**MST-0366-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A client has difficulty with buttoning due to limited fine-motor control. Which intervention is most appropriate first?

- A. Provide a buttoning aid and adapt the task **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: adapting the task with assistive equipment supports independence in self-care.
- B. Advise the client to avoid dressing  
  _Rationale:_ Avoidance reduces participation and is not therapeutic.
- C. Refer the client for surgery  
  _Rationale:_ Surgical referral is not an OT intervention for this limitation.
- D. Discharge the client  
  _Rationale:_ Discharge is premature when an adaptive strategy can help.

**MST-0366-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are appropriate contributions of a COTA to the evaluation process?

- A. Collecting client data delegated by the OT **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the OTA gathers delegated assessment data.
- B. Reporting observations to the supervising OT **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: communicating findings to the OT is a core contribution.
- C. Independently writing the final evaluation and plan  
  _Rationale:_ The OT is responsible for the evaluation and plan.
- D. Determining the client's discharge independently  
  _Rationale:_ Discharge decisions rest with the OT.
- E. Interpreting standardized test results as the primary evaluator  
  _Rationale:_ The OT interprets and is the responsible evaluator.
