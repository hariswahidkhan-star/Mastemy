## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0037-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In enterprise risk management, 'risk appetite' is best defined as:

- A. The amount and type of risk an organisation is willing to accept to pursue its objectives **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: risk appetite is the level of risk the organisation chooses to take in pursuit of objectives.
- B. The maximum possible loss the organisation could ever suffer  
  _Rationale:_ That is closer to maximum exposure, not chosen appetite.
- C. The probability that any risk will occur  
  _Rationale:_ That describes likelihood, a component of risk assessment, not appetite.
- D. The cost of the organisation's insurance premiums  
  _Rationale:_ Insurance cost is a treatment expense, not risk appetite.

**MST-0037-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A company wants to protect against a rise in the price of a commodity it must buy in six months. Which instrument most directly hedges this?

- A. Buying a forward or futures contract to fix the purchase price **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: buying forward/futures locks in the purchase price and hedges the upward price risk.
- B. Selling a forward contract on the commodity  
  _Rationale:_ Selling forward hedges a seller, not a buyer facing rising prices.
- C. Buying the company's own shares  
  _Rationale:_ Share buybacks do not hedge commodity price risk.
- D. Increasing the dividend payout  
  _Rationale:_ Dividend policy is unrelated to commodity price hedging.

**MST-0037-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO components of a widely used internal-control framework.

- A. The control environment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the control environment is a core component of internal-control frameworks such as COSO.
- B. Risk assessment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: risk assessment is a core component of internal-control frameworks.
- C. Dividend policy  
  _Rationale:_ Dividend policy is a financing matter, not an internal-control component.
- D. Share issuance  
  _Rationale:_ Issuing shares is a financing activity, not a control component.
- E. Product pricing strategy  
  _Rationale:_ Pricing strategy is a commercial matter, not an internal-control component.
