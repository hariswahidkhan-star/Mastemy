## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the elements of an effective compliance and ethics program
2. Apply risk assessment, policy and training practices to build program components
3. Evaluate investigations, discipline and reporting mechanisms
4. Analyse compliance program effectiveness and continuous improvement

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0111-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A company has a detailed written compliance policy but employees routinely ignore it and leadership never references it. Against the elements of an effective program, what is the core deficiency?

- A. Lack of oversight, culture and enforcement making the program ineffective in practice **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an effective program requires genuine oversight, culture and enforcement, not just documents.
- B. The policy is simply too long  
  _Rationale:_ Length is not the governing deficiency here.
- C. Nothing, because a written policy satisfies the standard  
  _Rationale:_ A written policy alone does not make a program effective.
- D. The company needs more policies  
  _Rationale:_ Adding documents does not fix a culture-and-enforcement gap.

**MST-0111-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) When conducting a compliance risk assessment, what makes the output most useful for program design?

- A. Prioritising risks by likelihood and impact so resources target the highest exposure **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a risk-ranked assessment directs limited resources to the greatest exposure.
- B. Listing every conceivable risk without prioritisation  
  _Rationale:_ An unranked list does not guide resource allocation.
- C. Copying another company's risk register unchanged  
  _Rationale:_ Risk is entity-specific; a copied register may miss real exposures.
- D. Assessing risk only after an incident occurs  
  _Rationale:_ Risk assessment should be proactive.

**MST-0111-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) A whistleblower reports suspected kickbacks. Which TWO actions best protect the integrity of the compliance response? (Select TWO)

- A. Protect the reporter from retaliation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: anti-retaliation protection is essential to an effective reporting mechanism.
- B. Preserve relevant documents and plan an impartial investigation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: document preservation and impartial investigation protect integrity.
- C. Tell the accused manager to handle it informally  
  _Rationale:_ Letting the subject manage the inquiry destroys independence.
- D. Close the matter quickly to avoid disruption  
  _Rationale:_ Rushing to close undermines a credible investigation.
- E. Identify the reporter to the accused to 'clear the air'  
  _Rationale:_ Exposing the reporter invites retaliation.
