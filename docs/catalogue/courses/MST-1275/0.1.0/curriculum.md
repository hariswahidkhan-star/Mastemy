## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1275-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A federal court's power to hear a case between citizens of different states exceeding the amount in controversy is based on:

- A. Diversity jurisdiction **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: diversity jurisdiction covers suits between citizens of different states above the statutory amount.
- B. Personal jurisdiction  
  _Rationale:_ Personal jurisdiction concerns power over the parties, not subject matter.
- C. Supplemental jurisdiction  
  _Rationale:_ Supplemental jurisdiction attaches related claims to an existing anchor claim.
- D. Removal  
  _Rationale:_ Removal is a procedure to move a case from state to federal court, not a jurisdiction basis itself.

**MST-1275-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under the mailbox rule, acceptance of an offer is generally effective when:

- A. It is dispatched (e.g. posted) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: under the mailbox rule, acceptance is effective on dispatch, not receipt.
- B. It is received by the offeror  
  _Rationale:_ Receipt governs revocations and most other communications, not acceptance under this rule.
- C. The offeror signs a new contract  
  _Rationale:_ No new signature is required for acceptance to be effective.
- D. A court later enforces it  
  _Rationale:_ Effectiveness is at dispatch, not at later enforcement.

**MST-1275-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO elements must a plaintiff establish in a negligence claim?

- A. A duty of care owed to the plaintiff **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: duty is a required element of negligence.
- B. Breach of that duty **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: breach of the duty is a required element.
- C. The defendant's intent to cause harm  
  _Rationale:_ Intent is required for intentional torts, not negligence.
- D. A prior criminal conviction of the defendant  
  _Rationale:_ A criminal conviction is not an element of civil negligence.
