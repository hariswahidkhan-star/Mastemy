## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0370-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) During nutrition assessment, which step best supports an accurate nutrition diagnosis?

- A. Integrating anthropometric, biochemical, clinical and dietary data together **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a nutrition diagnosis draws on multiple data domains, not one value alone.
- B. Relying solely on body mass index  
  _Rationale:_ A single value cannot establish a nutrition diagnosis on its own.
- C. Using the patient's self-reported goal weight only  
  _Rationale:_ A goal weight is not assessment data for a diagnosis.
- D. Skipping the diet history  
  _Rationale:_ Dietary intake is essential assessment data.

**MST-0370-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A patient with advancing chronic kidney disease (not yet on dialysis) typically needs which dietary adjustment?

- A. Controlled protein intake appropriate to the stage **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: protein is managed to the disease stage in pre-dialysis CKD to limit uraemic burden.
- B. Unrestricted high-protein intake  
  _Rationale:_ Unrestricted protein is generally not appropriate in pre-dialysis CKD.
- C. Elimination of all carbohydrate  
  _Rationale:_ Carbohydrate elimination is not the standard CKD modification.
- D. Maximised potassium intake  
  _Rationale:_ Potassium often needs limiting, not maximising, as CKD advances.

**MST-0370-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are critical control points in preventing foodborne illness in a foodservice operation?

- A. Holding hot foods at or above the required temperature **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: temperature control during holding is a critical control point.
- B. Cooking foods to the required internal temperature **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: cooking to the specified internal temperature is a critical control point.
- C. Choosing the colour of serving trays  
  _Rationale:_ Tray colour has no food-safety role.
- D. Printing the menu in two fonts  
  _Rationale:_ Menu formatting is unrelated to food safety.
- E. Playing background music during service  
  _Rationale:_ Ambience does not control foodborne hazards.
