## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the sales function, process and modern buyer behaviour
2. Prospect and qualify leads effectively
3. Run discovery to understand needs and build value
4. Present solutions and handle objections
5. Close, follow up and manage the pipeline and relationship

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

A new account executive at a software company inherits a messy pipeline of mixed-quality leads. Qualify the pipeline, run structured discovery on the best opportunities, tailor a value-based pitch, handle the common objections, and build a follow-up and forecasting routine that keeps deals moving.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1825-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the main goal of the discovery stage in a sale?

- A. To understand the buyer's needs, pain and decision criteria **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: discovery builds the understanding needed to sell value, not features.
- B. To present the full product demo immediately  
  _Rationale:_ Demos come after needs are understood.
- C. To give the lowest possible price  
  _Rationale:_ Price is not the focus of discovery.
- D. To close the deal on the first call  
  _Rationale:_ Closing follows discovery and value-building.

**MST-1825-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) A prospect says 'your product is too expensive'. Which TWO responses handle the objection well? (Select TWO.)

- A. Clarify what they are comparing the price to **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: understanding the reference point reframes the conversation.
- B. Reconnect the price to the value and outcomes they cared about **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: value framing addresses the real concern behind price.
- C. Immediately offer the largest available discount  
  _Rationale:_ Discounting first erodes margin and signals the price was inflated.
- D. End the call because they cannot afford it  
  _Rationale:_ Assuming inability to pay abandons a workable objection.

**MST-1825-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why should every sales interaction end with an agreed next step?

- A. It keeps the deal moving and makes the pipeline forecastable **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: clear next steps prevent stalls and improve forecasting accuracy.
- B. It guarantees the deal will close  
  _Rationale:_ It improves momentum but does not guarantee closing.
- C. It replaces the need for discovery  
  _Rationale:_ Discovery is still essential earlier in the process.
- D. It lets you skip follow-up  
  _Rationale:_ Follow-up executes the agreed next step.
