## Learning outcomes

1. Apply the CAIA professional standards and the fundamentals of alternative investing
2. Analyse real assets, hedge funds and private capital as asset classes
3. Evaluate structured products, digital assets and portfolio construction with alternatives

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Knowledge-practice forms use MCQ/MR only; official item formats not reproducible in this format are listed in the exam-version record.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0054-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An investor is told a commodity index earns a positive 'roll yield'. Under what futures-curve condition does this occur?

- A. Contango, because distant futures are priced above spot  
  _Rationale:_ In contango the roll is negative, not positive.
- B. Backwardation, because expiring futures roll up toward a higher spot **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: in backwardation futures are below spot, so rolling forward captures a positive yield.
- C. A flat curve with equal prices across maturities  
  _Rationale:_ A flat curve produces roughly zero roll yield.
- D. Whenever spot volatility is high  
  _Rationale:_ Volatility does not determine the sign of roll yield.

**MST-0054-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which statement about a private equity fund's J-curve is correct?

- A. Net returns are highest in the first year  
  _Rationale:_ Early years show negative net returns from fees and draws.
- B. Committed capital equals invested capital at inception  
  _Rationale:_ Capital is drawn over time, so the two differ.
- C. Early negative net returns are followed by gains as investments are realised **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: fees and early draws depress returns before exits lift them.
- D. The J-curve only applies to hedge funds  
  _Rationale:_ The J-curve is characteristic of private capital drawdown funds.

**MST-0054-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO responsibilities that the CAIA professional standards place on a member making an investment recommendation.

- A. Disclose conflicts of interest that could affect the recommendation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: disclosure of conflicts is a core duty under the standards.
- B. Guarantee the recommended return to the client  
  _Rationale:_ Guaranteeing returns breaches the standards.
- C. Have a reasonable basis for the recommendation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: recommendations must rest on reasonable and adequate analysis.
- D. Prioritise the firm's revenue over suitability  
  _Rationale:_ Client interest and suitability take priority, not firm revenue.
- E. Avoid documenting the rationale to stay flexible  
  _Rationale:_ The standards favour a documented, defensible basis.
