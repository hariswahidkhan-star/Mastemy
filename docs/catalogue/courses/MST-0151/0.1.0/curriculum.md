## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0151-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Predictability across the ART has fallen for two consecutive PIs. Which forum is designed to identify and solve the systemic causes?

- A. The Inspect & Adapt event, including the problem-solving workshop **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: I&A includes a structured problem-solving workshop to address systemic issues and improve the ART.
- B. The daily stand-up of a single team  
  _Rationale:_ A single team's stand-up cannot address ART-wide systemic causes.
- C. An ad hoc email thread  
  _Rationale:_ An email thread is not the structured improvement mechanism SAFe defines.
- D. The next PI Planning only  
  _Rationale:_ PI Planning plans work; the I&A event is where improvement actions are generated.

**MST-0151-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the main purpose of the ART sync (e.g., Coach Sync and PO Sync) facilitated by the RTE?

- A. To track progress toward PI objectives and surface impediments and dependencies **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ART sync keeps the train aligned by tracking progress and raising issues across teams.
- B. To assign individual tasks to developers  
  _Rationale:_ Task assignment is a team self-organization matter, not the ART sync purpose.
- C. To replace PI Planning  
  _Rationale:_ ART sync complements, not replaces, PI Planning.
- D. To approve expense reports  
  _Rationale:_ Administrative approvals are not an ART sync function.

**MST-0151-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are responsibilities of the RTE when preparing for PI Planning? (Select TWO)

- A. Ensuring readiness of scope, context and facilities for the event **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the RTE drives event readiness including content, logistics and alignment.
- B. Aligning stakeholders on the planning scope and business context **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: establishing shared context and alignment is a key RTE preparation task.
- C. Writing every team's detailed iteration plans in advance  
  _Rationale:_ Teams plan their own iterations during PI Planning; the RTE does not pre-write them.
- D. Setting individual developers' salaries  
  _Rationale:_ Compensation is not an RTE responsibility.
- E. Deciding the product's final pricing  
  _Rationale:_ Pricing is a product management/portfolio concern, not an RTE task.
