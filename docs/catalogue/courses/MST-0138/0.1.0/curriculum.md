## Learning outcomes

1. Describe advanced Scrum Master stances and servant-leadership in depth
2. Apply coaching and facilitation to teams, Product Owners and the organisation
3. Evaluate impediment removal and organisational change for agility
4. Analyse measurement of value and evidence-based improvement

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0138-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A leader praises a team for its high velocity. An experienced Scrum Master is cautious because velocity:

- A. Measures output, not the value or outcomes delivered **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: velocity is an output measure and can rise while value falls.
- B. Is the only true measure of success  
  _Rationale:_ Velocity is not a measure of value or success on its own.
- C. Should always be maximised at any cost  
  _Rationale:_ Maximising velocity can harm value and sustainability.
- D. Proves customer satisfaction  
  _Rationale:_ Velocity says nothing directly about customers.

**MST-0138-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A Scrum Master keeps assigning tasks and making decisions for the Developers. This behaviour most likely:

- A. Undermines the team's self-management and growth **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a Scrum Master should foster self-management, not assign tasks.
- B. Is required by Scrum  
  _Rationale:_ Scrum does not require the Scrum Master to assign tasks.
- C. Improves long-term capability  
  _Rationale:_ It reduces the team's capability and ownership.
- D. Is the Product Owner's job being done well  
  _Rationale:_ Neither role assigns tasks to Developers.

**MST-0138-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO measures best reflect whether a product is succeeding, rather than how busy the team is? (Select TWO)

- A. Customer or user outcomes achieved **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: outcomes reflect real value delivered.
- B. Business value or impact realised **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: realised value measures success.
- C. Lines of code written  
  _Rationale:_ Code volume is an output, not value.
- D. Number of meetings held  
  _Rationale:_ Meeting count does not reflect success.
- E. Hours logged by the team  
  _Rationale:_ Logged hours measure effort, not value.
