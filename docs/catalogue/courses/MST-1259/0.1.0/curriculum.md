## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the purpose, principles and core components of a governance system for enterprise information and technology
2. Distinguish governance from management and describe the associated objective domains
3. Describe the governance and management objectives and their supporting components
4. Explain performance management, design factors and the goals cascade

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1259-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which activity best represents governance rather than management in a COBIT-style model?

- A. The board setting risk appetite and directing investment priorities **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: setting direction, risk appetite and monitoring outcomes are Evaluate-Direct-Monitor (governance) activities.
- B. A team deploying a patch to production servers  
  _Rationale:_ Deploying patches is an operational management (build/run) activity.
- C. An analyst writing a test plan for a release  
  _Rationale:_ Test planning sits within management domains, not governance.
- D. A developer fixing a defect found in testing  
  _Rationale:_ Defect fixing is a management/operational task.

**MST-1259-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) In the goals cascade, what triggers the flow toward alignment goals and objectives?

- A. Vendor product roadmaps  
  _Rationale:_ Vendor roadmaps are external inputs, not the cascade trigger.
- B. Stakeholder needs and enterprise goals **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: stakeholder drivers and enterprise goals initiate the cascade down to alignment goals and governance/management objectives.
- C. The internal audit schedule  
  _Rationale:_ Audit scheduling is downstream assurance, not the cascade origin.
- D. Available IT budget for the year  
  _Rationale:_ Budget constrains delivery but does not originate the goals cascade.

**MST-1259-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are among the components of a governance system in a COBIT-style model?

- A. Processes **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: processes are one of the seven components of a governance system.
- B. Organisational structures **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: organisational structures are a governance-system component.
- C. Quarterly revenue targets  
  _Rationale:_ Revenue targets are a business outcome, not a governance-system component.
- D. Individual employee bonuses  
  _Rationale:_ Bonuses are HR incentives, not a defined governance-system component.
