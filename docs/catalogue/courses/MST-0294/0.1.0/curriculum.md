## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0294-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) An employee alleges harassment by a supervisor. What is the most defensible first HR action?

- A. Begin a prompt, impartial investigation and document each step **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a prompt, impartial, documented investigation is the defensible first action.
- B. Immediately terminate the supervisor  
  _Rationale:_ Terminating before investigating creates legal risk and skips due process.
- C. Ask the complainant to resolve it informally first  
  _Rationale:_ Pushing informal resolution can be seen as discouraging a complaint.
- D. Wait for a written complaint before acting  
  _Rationale:_ Delaying action pending paperwork breaches the duty to respond promptly.

**MST-0294-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Pay compression occurs when:

- A. New hires earn close to or more than longer-tenured staff in similar roles **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: compression is the narrowing of pay differences between new and tenured employees.
- B. The company freezes all salaries  
  _Rationale:_ A freeze is a separate action, not the definition of compression.
- C. Benefits costs rise faster than wages  
  _Rationale:_ That describes benefit inflation, not pay compression.
- D. Overtime exceeds base pay  
  _Rationale:_ High overtime is unrelated to the compression concept.

**MST-0294-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) HR must improve the quality of a talent-acquisition pipeline. Select TWO metrics that best measure hiring quality rather than speed.

- A. New-hire 12-month retention rate **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: retention of new hires is a direct quality-of-hire signal.
- B. Hiring-manager satisfaction with new hires **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: hiring-manager satisfaction reflects fit and quality.
- C. Time-to-fill  
  _Rationale:_ Time-to-fill measures speed, not quality.
- D. Number of applications received  
  _Rationale:_ Application volume is a top-of-funnel count, not quality.
- E. Cost-per-hire  
  _Rationale:_ Cost-per-hire measures efficiency, not quality.
