## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0367-Q0001** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) Applying the ALARA principle to a described radiographic exposure means the technologist should:

- A. Use the lowest exposure that still yields a diagnostic image **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ALARA keeps dose as low as reasonably achievable while preserving diagnostic quality.
- B. Always use the highest available exposure  
  _Rationale:_ Higher-than-needed exposure increases dose without benefit.
- C. Avoid collimation to be safe  
  _Rationale:_ Collimation reduces dose and scatter; avoiding it increases exposure.
- D. Repeat images routinely for certainty  
  _Rationale:_ Unnecessary repeats add dose and violate ALARA.

**MST-0367-Q0002** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) On a digital radiograph, increasing kVp while compensating mAs will most directly:

- A. Decrease image contrast (longer grey scale) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: higher kVp lengthens the grey scale and lowers contrast.
- B. Increase image contrast  
  _Rationale:_ Higher kVp reduces, not increases, contrast.
- C. Have no effect on the image  
  _Rationale:_ kVp is a primary controller of contrast and penetration.
- D. Remove all image noise  
  _Rationale:_ kVp changes do not eliminate quantum noise on their own.

**MST-0367-Q0003** (multiple-answer-selection, Select TWO) Which TWO are the technologist's responsibilities for patient care and safety?

- A. Verify patient identity before the examination **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: confirming identity prevents wrong-patient errors.
- B. Apply appropriate infection-control precautions **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: infection control protects patient and staff.
- C. Interpret the radiograph and issue a diagnosis  
  _Rationale:_ Diagnosis is the radiologist's role, not the technologist's.
- D. Prescribe medication for the patient  
  _Rationale:_ Prescribing is outside the technologist's scope.
- E. Skip shielding to save time  
  _Rationale:_ Shielding should not be skipped; it reduces dose.
