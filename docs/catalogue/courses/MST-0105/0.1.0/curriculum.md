## Learning outcomes

1. Explain European data protection law and its institutions
2. Apply the GDPR legislative framework to processing
3. Demonstrate compliance and lawful data transfers

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0105-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under the GDPR, pseudonymised data is:

- A. Outside the scope of the GDPR  
  _Rationale:_ Pseudonymised data can still identify individuals with additional information, so it remains personal data.
- B. Still personal data subject to the GDPR **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: pseudonymised data is personal data because re-identification remains possible.
- C. The same as anonymised data  
  _Rationale:_ Anonymised data cannot be re-identified; pseudonymised data can.
- D. Only regulated outside the EU  
  _Rationale:_ The GDPR applies regardless.

**MST-0105-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A company must respond to a data-subject access request within what default period?

- A. One month of receipt, extendable in limited cases **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the GDPR sets a one-month default, extendable by two months for complex requests.
- B. 72 hours  
  _Rationale:_ 72 hours is the breach-notification deadline, not the DSAR deadline.
- C. One year  
  _Rationale:_ That exceeds the statutory period.
- D. There is no deadline  
  _Rationale:_ A statutory deadline applies.

**MST-0105-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO lawful bases for processing personal data under the GDPR.

- A. Consent of the data subject **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: consent is one of the six lawful bases.
- B. The data is stored in the EU  
  _Rationale:_ Storage location is not a lawful basis.
- C. Performance of a contract with the data subject **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: contractual necessity is a lawful basis.
- D. The controller prefers not to ask  
  _Rationale:_ Preference is not a lawful basis.
- E. The data is encrypted  
  _Rationale:_ Encryption is a safeguard, not a lawful basis.
