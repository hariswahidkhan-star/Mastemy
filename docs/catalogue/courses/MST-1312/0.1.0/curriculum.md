## Learning outcomes

1. Clarify the work problem before selecting a tool
2. Compare AI tools against practical criteria
3. Assess data, privacy and security fit
4. Estimate cost, effort and value of adoption
5. Make and justify a tool recommendation

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1312-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What should come first when choosing an AI tool for work?

- A. A clear statement of the problem and desired outcome **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: define the problem before shopping for tools.
- B. Picking the most advertised product  
  _Rationale:_ Advertising is not a selection basis.
- C. Buying the cheapest option immediately  
  _Rationale:_ Price alone is not the first criterion.
- D. Choosing whatever a competitor uses  
  _Rationale:_ Imitation ignores your actual need.

**MST-1312-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO belong in a data and security check for an AI tool? (Select TWO.)

- A. Where the tool stores and processes your data **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: data location is a key concern.
- B. Whether vendor terms allow reuse of your data for training **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reuse terms matter for privacy.
- C. The colour of the tool's logo  
  _Rationale:_ Branding is irrelevant to security.
- D. How many awards the vendor advertises  
  _Rationale:_ Awards do not establish data safety.

**MST-1312-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why pilot an AI tool before a full rollout?

- A. To test real fit and surface problems at low risk **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a pilot limits risk and reveals issues.
- B. To guarantee the tool will be free  
  _Rationale:_ A pilot does not change pricing.
- C. Because pilots skip all evaluation  
  _Rationale:_ A pilot is part of evaluation, not a skip.
- D. To avoid ever making a decision  
  _Rationale:_ A pilot informs the decision, not avoids it.
