## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the ancient foundations of Chinese, Korean and Japanese civilisation
2. Explain the classical and imperial Chinese order and its influence on the region
3. Analyse the distinct political and cultural development of Korea and Japan
4. Evaluate the effects of Western and Japanese imperialism on the region
5. Explain the revolutions and wars that reshaped 20th-century East Asia
6. Assess the economic transformations and tensions of the region since 1945

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2105-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why is it misleading to treat the whole of East Asia as culturally identical to China?

- A. Korea, Japan and others adapted shared influences into distinct institutions and identities **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: borrowing was selective and reworked, producing real divergence.
- B. Because China had no influence on the region  
  _Rationale:_ Its influence was large; the error is erasing local difference.
- C. Because the societies never interacted  
  _Rationale:_ They interacted extensively; the point is they still differed.
- D. Because they shared no writing or ideas  
  _Rationale:_ They shared much, yet developed distinctly.

**MST-2105-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are associated with the imperial Chinese civil-service examination system? (Select TWO.)

- A. Selection of officials partly through competitive examination **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the system recruited officials via examinations.
- B. A route to status based on mastery of classical texts **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: success rewarded classical learning.
- C. Hereditary-only appointment with no testing  
  _Rationale:_ The system's point was examination, not pure heredity.
- D. Election of emperors by popular vote  
  _Rationale:_ No such practice existed.

**MST-2105-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A student explains rapid post-1945 East Asian growth 'purely because of culture'. What is the weakness?

- A. It ignores specific policies, institutions, global conditions and historical contingency that shaped growth **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: culture-only explanations overlook state policy, timing and external factors.
- B. Culture had no role whatsoever  
  _Rationale:_ Culture may matter, but it cannot be the sole explanation.
- C. Growth did not actually occur  
  _Rationale:_ Substantial growth occurred; the issue is explaining it.
- D. All East Asian economies grew identically  
  _Rationale:_ Paths varied, which a single-cause story cannot capture.
