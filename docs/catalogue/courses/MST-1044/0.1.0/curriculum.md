## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1044-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Under the cost-to-cost method, progress is measured as:

- A. Costs incurred to date divided by total estimated costs **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: cost-to-cost expresses progress as costs incurred over total estimated costs.
- B. Cash received divided by contract price  
  _Rationale:_ Cash receipts do not measure progress.
- C. Months elapsed divided by total months  
  _Rationale:_ Elapsed time is not the cost-to-cost measure.
- D. Invoices issued divided by budget  
  _Rationale:_ Billings are not a progress measure under cost-to-cost.

**MST-1044-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) A contract is now expected to make an overall loss. Which TWO actions are correct? (Select TWO)

- A. Recognise the entire expected loss immediately **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an expected contract loss is recognised in full at once.
- B. Reassess the cost-to-complete estimate that produced the loss **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the estimate should be validated as part of recognising the loss.
- C. Spread the loss evenly over the remaining term  
  _Rationale:_ Expected losses are not spread; they are recognised immediately.
- D. Defer the loss until the contract ends  
  _Rationale:_ Deferring a known loss is not permitted.

**MST-1044-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A revision to the estimated total cost of a contract is accounted for as:

- A. A change in accounting estimate, applied prospectively through a cumulative catch-up **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: estimate revisions are prospective with a cumulative catch-up to revenue/margin.
- B. A prior-period error requiring restatement  
  _Rationale:_ A genuine estimate change is not an error.
- C. A change in accounting policy  
  _Rationale:_ It is a change in estimate, not policy.
- D. An event ignored until completion  
  _Rationale:_ The revision is reflected in the current period.
