## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1295-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In LEED, what is the difference between a prerequisite and a credit?

- A. Prerequisites are mandatory and earn no points; credits are optional and earn points **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: prerequisites must be met for certification and award no points, while credits are optional and contribute points.
- B. Prerequisites earn the most points; credits earn none  
  _Rationale:_ Prerequisites earn no points; this is incorrect.
- C. They are identical and interchangeable  
  _Rationale:_ They differ in whether they are mandatory and whether they earn points.
- D. Credits are mandatory and prerequisites are optional  
  _Rationale:_ This reverses the definitions.

**MST-1295-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A LEED BD+C project cannot demonstrate it meets the minimum energy performance prerequisite. What is the consequence?

- A. The project cannot be certified until the prerequisite is met **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: all prerequisites are mandatory; failing one blocks certification regardless of credit points.
- B. It automatically achieves Platinum  
  _Rationale:_ Failing a prerequisite blocks certification, not achieves the highest level.
- C. The prerequisite can be ignored if enough credits are earned  
  _Rationale:_ Credits cannot substitute for mandatory prerequisites.
- D. It only affects Innovation points  
  _Rationale:_ A missed prerequisite blocks the whole certification, not just Innovation.

**MST-1295-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO statements that are TRUE about pursuing LEED credits across categories.

- A. A credit scorecard helps a team plan which credits to pursue to reach a target level **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a scorecard is used to plan feasible credits toward a certification target.
- B. Documentation is required to substantiate each credit claimed **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: credits must be documented to be awarded.
- C. Any expensive material automatically earns a Materials & Resources credit  
  _Rationale:_ Cost alone does not earn credits; specific documented requirements must be met.
- D. Credits can be claimed without any supporting evidence  
  _Rationale:_ Credits require documentation and cannot be claimed unsupported.
- E. Earning innovation points removes the need for prerequisites  
  _Rationale:_ Prerequisites remain mandatory regardless of innovation points.
