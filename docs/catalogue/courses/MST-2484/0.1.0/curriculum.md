## Learning outcomes

1. Explain how the income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement connect and what each one tells a manager
2. Interpret common profitability, liquidity and leverage measures in plain business language
3. Translate operational decisions into their financial effect on profit, cash and the balance sheet
4. Read a departmental budget and explain variances to a non-financial audience
5. Use basic finance vocabulary correctly when working with finance colleagues
6. Recognise the limits of financial figures and where judgement is still required

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2484-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A manager says the business 'made a big profit so we must have plenty of cash'. Why can that be wrong?

- A. Profit is measured on accruals, so it can rise while cash is tied up in receivables, inventory or capital spend **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: profit and cash differ because of timing and non-cash items.
- B. Profit and cash are always identical under the accounting rules  
  _Rationale:_ They are not; accruals and timing make them diverge.
- C. Profit is only ever negative  
  _Rationale:_ Profit can be positive or negative; that is not the point.
- D. Cash can never be lower than profit  
  _Rationale:_ Cash is frequently lower than reported profit.

**MST-2484-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO items appear on the balance sheet rather than the income statement? (Select TWO.)

- A. Trade receivables owed by customers **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: receivables are an asset on the balance sheet.
- B. A bank loan still outstanding **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a loan is a liability on the balance sheet.
- C. Sales revenue for the period  
  _Rationale:_ Revenue belongs on the income statement.
- D. Staff salaries expensed this month  
  _Rationale:_ Salary expense belongs on the income statement.

**MST-2484-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A department's travel cost is above budget. What is the most useful first question for the manager to ask?

- A. What drove the variance, and was it a one-off or a change in the underlying run-rate? **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: understanding the cause and whether it recurs guides the response.
- B. Who can we blame for the overspend?  
  _Rationale:_ Blame does not explain or fix the variance.
- C. Should we stop all travel immediately?  
  _Rationale:_ A reaction before understanding the cause is premature.
- D. Does the overspend mean the company is insolvent?  
  _Rationale:_ A single variance does not indicate insolvency.
