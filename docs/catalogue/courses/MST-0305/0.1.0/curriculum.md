## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0305-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) When comparing freight options, why is 'total landed cost' preferred over the quoted freight rate alone?

- A. It includes transport, duties, handling, inventory and other costs to the destination **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: total landed cost captures all costs of getting goods to their destination, not just freight.
- B. It is always lower than the freight rate  
  _Rationale:_ Landed cost is typically higher because it adds more cost elements.
- C. It ignores customs and duties  
  _Rationale:_ Landed cost specifically includes duties and customs charges.
- D. It applies only to domestic shipments  
  _Rationale:_ Landed cost is especially relevant for international shipments.

**MST-0305-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A shipper must move low-value, non-urgent bulk goods across an ocean at the lowest cost. Which mode best fits?

- A. Ocean freight **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ocean is the lowest-cost mode for non-urgent, high-volume international cargo.
- B. Air freight  
  _Rationale:_ Air is fastest but far more costly, unsuited to low-value non-urgent goods.
- C. Expedited courier  
  _Rationale:_ Expedited courier is for urgent, time-critical parcels at premium cost.
- D. Same-day van delivery  
  _Rationale:_ Same-day local delivery cannot cross an ocean and is costly.

**MST-0305-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO factors that raise an item's reorder point.

- A. Longer supplier lead time **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: more time to replenish means ordering sooner, raising the reorder point.
- B. Higher average demand during lead time **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: greater demand over the lead time increases the quantity needed before resupply.
- C. Shorter, more reliable lead time  
  _Rationale:_ A shorter lead time lowers, not raises, the reorder point.
- D. Falling demand for the item  
  _Rationale:_ Lower demand reduces the reorder point.
- E. Unlimited free storage  
  _Rationale:_ Storage cost does not change the demand-and-lead-time reorder calculation.
