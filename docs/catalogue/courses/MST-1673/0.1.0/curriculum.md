## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the purpose and structure of an ISO/IEC 27001 information security management system
2. Describe the role of risk assessment and the Statement of Applicability
3. Identify the categories of controls in the standard's annex
4. Explain certification, audits and continual improvement
5. Relate the standard to day-to-day security operations

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1673-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is the primary purpose of the Statement of Applicability in an ISMS?

- A. To record which controls apply and justify inclusions and exclusions **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the SoA documents control selection against assessed risk.
- B. To list every employee's password  
  _Rationale:_ The SoA does not contain credentials.
- C. To replace the risk assessment  
  _Rationale:_ The SoA follows from, not replaces, the risk assessment.
- D. To guarantee no incidents will occur  
  _Rationale:_ No document guarantees the absence of incidents.

**MST-1673-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO activities help sustain an ISMS over time? (Select TWO.)

- A. Regular internal audits **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: internal audits check the ISMS keeps working.
- B. Management review and continual improvement **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: review and improvement keep the system current.
- C. Writing policies once and never revisiting them  
  _Rationale:_ Static policies drift out of date and lose value.
- D. Hiding nonconformities from auditors  
  _Rationale:_ Concealing issues undermines improvement and trust.

**MST-1673-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why is risk assessment central to control selection in ISO/IEC 27001?

- A. Controls are chosen to treat assessed risks, not applied arbitrarily **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the standard ties control selection to identified risks.
- B. Because every control must always be applied in full  
  _Rationale:_ Controls are selected based on applicability to risk.
- C. Because risk assessment replaces the need for controls  
  _Rationale:_ Assessment informs, it does not replace, controls.
- D. Because the standard ignores risk  
  _Rationale:_ Risk is explicitly central to the standard.
