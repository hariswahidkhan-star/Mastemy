## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0317-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A quality manager wants to focus improvement on the defects that cause most of the losses. Which tool best identifies them?

- A. Pareto chart **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a Pareto chart ranks causes so the vital few driving most loss are visible.
- B. Control chart  
  _Rationale:_ A control chart shows process stability over time, not ranked contribution.
- C. Scatter diagram  
  _Rationale:_ A scatter diagram explores relationships between two variables.
- D. Check sheet  
  _Rationale:_ A check sheet collects raw tally data but does not rank or prioritise it.

**MST-0317-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which step comes first when deploying a strategic plan using a policy-deployment approach?

- A. Align objectives to the organisation's strategy and define measures **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: deployment starts by aligning objectives and measures to strategy.
- B. Audit suppliers for conformance  
  _Rationale:_ Supplier audits are an execution activity, not the first deployment step.
- C. Purchase new measurement equipment  
  _Rationale:_ Buying equipment is premature before objectives are defined.
- D. Celebrate results  
  _Rationale:_ Recognition comes after deployment and results.

**MST-0317-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are appropriate uses of the voice of the customer (VOC)?

- A. Translating customer needs into measurable product requirements **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: VOC turns stated needs into design and process requirements.
- B. Prioritising improvement efforts by customer impact **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: VOC data helps rank what matters most to customers.
- C. Setting internal defect targets without any customer input  
  _Rationale:_ That ignores the customer, defeating the purpose of VOC.
- D. Replacing financial planning entirely  
  _Rationale:_ VOC informs quality decisions; it does not replace financial planning.
- E. Eliminating all measurement  
  _Rationale:_ VOC drives measurement rather than removing it.
