## Learning outcomes

1. Explain what an AI management system is and the management-system pattern it follows
2. Describe the Plan-Do-Check-Act lifecycle as applied to an AI management system
3. Identify the organisational context, interested parties and scope needed to start an implementation
4. Plan the policies, objectives, roles and controls an AI management system typically requires
5. Design the operational controls, documentation and records for AI risk and impact handling
6. Plan internal audit, management review and continual improvement for the system

- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1881-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A startup documents one model thoroughly and says it has 'implemented ISO/IEC 42001.' What is missing?

- A. A management system is organisation-wide: policy, roles, planning, operation, audit and review, not a single model's documentation **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 42001 defines a management system, not a per-model artefact.
- B. Nothing, one strong model file is sufficient  
  _Rationale:_ A management system spans the organisation and its lifecycle.
- C. It also needs a published research paper  
  _Rationale:_ Publication is unrelated to a management system.
- D. Only a privacy policy is required  
  _Rationale:_ A privacy policy alone is not an AI management system.

**MST-1881-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO activities fit the 'Check' stage of Plan-Do-Check-Act for an AI management system? (Select TWO.)

- A. Conducting an internal audit against the system's requirements **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: internal audit is a Check activity.
- B. Holding a management review of objectives and performance **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: management review is a Check activity.
- C. Writing the AI policy and objectives  
  _Rationale:_ That is a Plan activity.
- D. Operating the model-change control process  
  _Rationale:_ That is a Do activity.

**MST-1881-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) An auditor finds a control is defined but never executed. What is the correct management-system response?

- A. Raise a nonconformity and open a corrective action to fix the cause **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: nonconformity and corrective action drive continual improvement.
- B. Delete the control so there is no gap on paper  
  _Rationale:_ Removing a needed control hides the problem rather than fixing it.
- C. Ignore it because the control exists in writing  
  _Rationale:_ A control that is not performed is a real nonconformity.
- D. Wait for the next certification cycle  
  _Rationale:_ Corrective action should not be deferred by default.
