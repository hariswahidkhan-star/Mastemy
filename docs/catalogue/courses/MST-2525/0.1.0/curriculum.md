## Learning outcomes

1. Explain which entities are eligible to apply the SME standard
2. Describe how the SME standard simplifies full IFRS
3. Identify key recognition and measurement differences from full IFRS
4. Apply simplified treatments in common areas such as goodwill, borrowing costs and development costs
5. Explain the reduced disclosure requirements
6. Explain transition to and from the SME standard

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2525-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which type of entity is generally NOT eligible to apply the IFRS for SMEs standard?

- A. An entity with public accountability, such as one whose shares are publicly traded **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: publicly accountable entities are not eligible.
- B. A small private company with no public accountability  
  _Rationale:_ Such an entity is typically eligible.
- C. A family-owned manufacturer that does not file with a securities regulator  
  _Rationale:_ This is the kind of entity the SME standard is designed for.
- D. A private start-up with few owners  
  _Rationale:_ A private start-up without public accountability can qualify.

**MST-2525-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO treatments differ in the IFRS for SMEs standard compared with full IFRS? (Select TWO.)

- A. Goodwill is amortised over its useful life rather than only tested for impairment **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the SME standard amortises goodwill, unlike full IFRS.
- B. Development costs are expensed rather than capitalised when criteria are met **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the SME standard expenses development (and borrowing) costs, simplifying full IFRS.
- C. SMEs must apply every disclosure required by full IFRS  
  _Rationale:_ The SME standard reduces disclosures, it does not require all of them.
- D. SMEs must revalue all property, plant and equipment every year  
  _Rationale:_ No such annual revaluation requirement applies.

**MST-2525-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A company argues that because it is small, it automatically qualifies for the IFRS for SMEs standard. Why may this reasoning be wrong?

- A. Eligibility depends on the absence of public accountability, not merely on size **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a small but publicly accountable entity is not eligible.
- B. Only very large entities can use the SME standard  
  _Rationale:_ That is the opposite of its purpose.
- C. Size is the only criterion that matters  
  _Rationale:_ Public accountability, not size alone, governs eligibility.
- D. The SME standard is mandatory for all companies  
  _Rationale:_ It is not mandatory for all companies.
