## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0062-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A finance team wants to compare the cost of leasing versus buying a machine over five years. Which technique is most appropriate?

- A. Net present value of the cash flows under each option **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: discounting the cash flows of each option to present value allows a like-for-like comparison.
- B. Comparing only the first-year cash outflow  
  _Rationale:_ A single year ignores the multi-year cash flows and the time value of money.
- C. Choosing whichever has the lower sticker price  
  _Rationale:_ Sticker price ignores financing, maintenance and timing of cash flows.
- D. Picking the option with the higher accounting depreciation  
  _Rationale:_ Depreciation is a non-cash allocation and is not the deciding cash-flow criterion.

**MST-0062-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) In a rolling forecast, how often is the forecast horizon typically updated?

- A. Once at the start of the fiscal year only  
  _Rationale:_ That describes a static annual budget, not a rolling forecast.
- B. Continuously, adding a new period as each one closes **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: a rolling forecast always extends the horizon as each period completes.
- C. Only when actuals differ from budget by more than 20%  
  _Rationale:_ Rolling forecasts update on a regular cadence, not only on large variances.
- D. Never; forecasts are fixed once approved  
  _Rationale:_ Fixed forecasts are the opposite of the rolling approach.

**MST-0062-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO items that are drivers of free cash flow.

- A. Operating cash flow **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: operating cash flow is a core input to free cash flow.
- B. Capital expenditures **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: capital expenditure is subtracted when computing free cash flow.
- C. Par value of issued shares  
  _Rationale:_ Par value is an accounting label and does not drive free cash flow.
- D. The chart of accounts numbering scheme  
  _Rationale:_ Account numbering is organizational and has no cash impact.
- E. The color of the budget dashboard  
  _Rationale:_ Presentation choices do not affect cash flow.
