## Learning outcomes

1. Set up and configure a Shopify store
2. Manage products, collections and inventory
3. Configure checkout, payments, shipping and taxes
4. Operate orders, fulfilment and store analytics

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

An operator must launch and run a Shopify store for a small brand. They must configure store settings, build the product catalogue and inventory, set up payments, shipping and taxes, and operate orders and analytics, defending shipping and inventory choices against margin and stockout risks.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1138-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) In Shopify, product variants should be used for:

- A. Options of the same product such as size or colour **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: variants model options under one product with shared inventory logic.
- B. Completely unrelated products  
  _Rationale:_ Unrelated items are separate products.
- C. Customer accounts  
  _Rationale:_ Variants are not customers.
- D. Payment gateways  
  _Rationale:_ Unrelated to variants.

**MST-1138-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO should be configured before launching a Shopify store? (Select TWO.)

- A. Payment provider and checkout settings **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: customers cannot buy without payments configured.
- B. Shipping rates and store policies **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: rates and policies are required to sell properly.
- C. The founder's personal social media bio  
  _Rationale:_ Not required to launch the store.
- D. A finished television advertisement  
  _Rationale:_ Not a launch prerequisite.

**MST-1138-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A checkout conversion report is most useful for:

- A. Finding the step where buyers abandon the purchase **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: it reveals drop-off points to fix.
- B. Designing the product photography  
  _Rationale:_ Not what the report shows.
- C. Writing the refund policy text  
  _Rationale:_ Unrelated to conversion analytics.
- D. Setting up the domain name  
  _Rationale:_ Domain setup is separate.
