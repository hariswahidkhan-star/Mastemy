## Learning outcomes

1. Name the five senses and the body part used for each
2. Match everyday objects to the sense that notices them
3. Describe how sight, hearing, smell, taste and touch help us stay safe
4. Sort things by how they feel, sound, look, smell or taste
5. Explain that some living things use senses differently from people
6. Ask a simple question about the world and answer it by observing

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A class visits a garden: the children use each of their five senses to notice flowers, birds, soil and fruit, then draw and tell which body part noticed each thing and which sense kept them safe near the bees.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2829-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which body part do we use to hear sounds?

- A. Our ears **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ears are the sense organ for hearing.
- B. Our eyes  
  _Rationale:_ Eyes are for seeing, not hearing.
- C. Our nose  
  _Rationale:_ The nose is for smelling.
- D. Our hands  
  _Rationale:_ Hands are mostly for touching.

**MST-2829-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) A child wants to find out if a drink is sweet or sour. Which sense should they use? (Select ONE)

- A. Taste, using the tongue **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: taste tells us sweet, sour, salty and bitter.
- B. Sight, using the eyes  
  _Rationale:_ Looking cannot tell you the taste.
- C. Hearing, using the ears  
  _Rationale:_ Sound does not reveal taste.
- D. Touch, using the skin  
  _Rationale:_ Touch tells texture, not flavour.

**MST-2829-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO senses you would use to enjoy a slice of warm toast at breakfast. (Select TWO)

- A. Smell, to notice the toasty smell **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: smell notices the aroma of the toast.
- B. Taste, to notice the flavour **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: taste notices how the toast tastes.
- C. Hearing, to find out the colour  
  _Rationale:_ Hearing cannot tell colour.
- D. Sight, to measure the temperature  
  _Rationale:_ Sight does not measure how hot something is.
