## Integrative case

Two founders have savings and a polished app idea but no evidence anyone wants it. Guide them: adopt an experimental mindset, validate that the problem is real, design a workable business model, test the riskiest assumptions with an MVP, and avoid funding or scaling before they have found product-market fit.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1822-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why is validating the problem more important than perfecting the solution early on?

- A. A great solution to a problem nobody has still fails **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: demand depends on a real, painful problem, not solution polish.
- B. Solutions never matter  
  _Rationale:_ Solutions matter, but only for a validated problem.
- C. Problems are always obvious  
  _Rationale:_ Problems must be tested, not assumed.
- D. Perfecting the solution guarantees sales  
  _Rationale:_ Polish does not create demand that was never there.

**MST-1822-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which actions reflect a lean, experimental approach to a startup? (Select TWO)

- A. Building a minimal version to test a key assumption **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an MVP tests demand cheaply before full build-out.
- B. Being willing to pivot when evidence contradicts the plan **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: responding to evidence is central to lean practice.
- C. Building the complete product before talking to any customer  
  _Rationale:_ This risks large investment in something unwanted.
- D. Ignoring metrics that contradict the founders' beliefs  
  _Rationale:_ Dismissing disconfirming evidence defeats the purpose of experiments.

**MST-1822-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A startup hires aggressively and opens new markets before confirming customers love the product. What risk is this?

- A. Premature scaling before product-market fit **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: scaling an unproven product multiplies losses.
- B. A guaranteed path to success  
  _Rationale:_ Scaling too early is a common cause of startup failure.
- C. Evidence that the product is validated  
  _Rationale:_ Hiring does not validate demand.
- D. A sound way to reduce risk  
  _Rationale:_ It increases burn and risk before fit is found.
