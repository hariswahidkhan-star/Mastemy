## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-0153-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A sponsor asks the BA to show which requirements are affected if a particular business rule changes. Which BABOK practice most directly supports this?

- A. Requirements traceability **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: traceability links requirements to one another and to designs, so impact of a change can be analyzed.
- B. Stakeholder onboarding  
  _Rationale:_ Onboarding concerns engaging stakeholders, not impact analysis of a change.
- C. Elicitation preparation  
  _Rationale:_ Elicitation preparation gathers information; it does not trace change impact.
- D. Backlog grooming only  
  _Rationale:_ Grooming orders work but does not establish the traceability needed for impact analysis.

**MST-0153-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) Which elicitation technique is most appropriate to quickly gather quantitative input from several hundred geographically dispersed users?

- A. Survey/questionnaire **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: surveys efficiently collect structured, quantitative input from large dispersed groups.
- B. One-on-one interviews with each user  
  _Rationale:_ Interviewing hundreds of users individually is not efficient for this scale.
- C. A single co-located workshop  
  _Rationale:_ A single workshop cannot reach several hundred dispersed users.
- D. Document analysis of meeting minutes  
  _Rationale:_ Document analysis reviews existing records, not new quantitative input from users.

**MST-0153-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO elements are typically part of defining a business analysis approach? (Select TWO)

- A. The level of formality and planned BA deliverables **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: formality and deliverables are core choices in the BA approach.
- B. How requirements will be prioritized and traced **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the approach sets how requirements are prioritized, traced and managed.
- C. The vendor's final contract price  
  _Rationale:_ Contract pricing is a procurement outcome, not part of the BA approach.
- D. Individual developer performance ratings  
  _Rationale:_ Performance appraisal is unrelated to the BA approach.
- E. The data center hardware model  
  _Rationale:_ Infrastructure selection is not a BA approach element.
