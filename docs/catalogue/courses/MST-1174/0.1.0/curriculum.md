## Learning outcomes

1. Explain GHG accounting principles, gases and CO2-equivalent
2. Set organisational and operational boundaries for an inventory
3. Quantify Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions using activity data and factors
4. Build and quality-check a GHG inventory
5. Develop and evaluate carbon reduction and offset strategies

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

A manufacturer must publish its first carbon inventory and a reduction plan. Set organisational and operational boundaries, quantify Scope 1, 2 and the material Scope 3 categories, quality-check the inventory and its largest uncertainties, then rank reduction levers before any offsets and set a credible, baselined target.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1174-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A gas has a 100-year global warming potential (GWP) of 25. Emitting 2 tonnes of it equals how many tonnes of CO2-equivalent?

- A. 50 tonnes CO2e **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: CO2e = mass x GWP = 2 x 25 = 50 tonnes.
- B. 25 tonnes CO2e  
  _Rationale:_ This ignores the 2-tonne quantity; multiply mass by GWP.
- C. 2 tonnes CO2e  
  _Rationale:_ This ignores the GWP of 25.
- D. 12.5 tonnes CO2e  
  _Rationale:_ This divides instead of multiplies; CO2e is mass x GWP.

**MST-1174-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Under the GHG Protocol, which TWO sources are typically Scope 1 (direct) emissions? (Select TWO.)

- A. Fuel burned in the company's own boilers **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: on-site combustion in owned/controlled equipment is Scope 1.
- B. Fuel burned in company-owned vehicles **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: direct combustion in owned fleet is Scope 1.
- C. Purchased grid electricity used on site  
  _Rationale:_ Purchased electricity is Scope 2, not Scope 1.
- D. Emissions from a supplier making purchased materials  
  _Rationale:_ Upstream supplier emissions are Scope 3.

**MST-1174-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) In a credible carbon-management strategy, where do offsets belong relative to direct reductions?

- A. After prioritising reductions at source, for residual emissions **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: the mitigation hierarchy puts reduction first and uses quality offsets for the remainder.
- B. Before any reduction, as the main lever  
  _Rationale:_ Leading with offsets instead of reducing is widely seen as low-integrity.
- C. Instead of reductions entirely  
  _Rationale:_ Offsets are not a substitute for cutting actual emissions.
- D. Only for Scope 2 electricity  
  _Rationale:_ Offsets are not restricted to one scope in this way.
