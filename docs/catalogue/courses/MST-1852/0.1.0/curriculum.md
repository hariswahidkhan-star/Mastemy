## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1852-Q0001** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) The Hangul syllable 한 is read as:

- A. 'han' **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: ㅎ(h) + ㅏ(a) + ㄴ(n) combine to 'han'.
- B. 'nah'  
  _Rationale:_ The initial consonant is ㅎ (h), so it is not 'nah'.
- C. 'ha'  
  _Rationale:_ The final ㄴ adds an 'n' sound, giving 'han', not 'ha'.
- D. 'hang'  
  _Rationale:_ The final consonant is ㄴ (n), not ㅇ (ng).

**MST-1852-Q0002** (single-answer-mcq, Select ONE) Choose the correct subject particle: '학생___ 공부해요.' (The student studies.)

- A. 이 **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: 이 is the subject particle after a consonant-final noun like 학생.
- B. 가  
  _Rationale:_ 가 is the subject particle after a vowel-final noun, not a consonant-final one.
- C. 를  
  _Rationale:_ 를 marks an object, not a subject.
- D. 에  
  _Rationale:_ 에 marks place or time, not the subject.

**MST-1852-Q0003** (multiple-answer-selection, Select TWO) Which TWO are reading/listening (receptive) tasks that TOPIK I assesses?

- A. Understanding a short notice **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: reading comprehension of notices is assessed.
- B. Listening for a key detail in a dialogue **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: listening comprehension is assessed.
- C. Delivering a spoken presentation  
  _Rationale:_ TOPIK I has no speaking section.
- D. Writing an essay  
  _Rationale:_ TOPIK I has no productive writing section.
- E. Recording a pronunciation sample  
  _Rationale:_ Pronunciation production is not assessed in TOPIK I.
