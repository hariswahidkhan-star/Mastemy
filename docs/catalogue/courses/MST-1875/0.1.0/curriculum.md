## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the principles of animation
2. Apply timing, spacing and weight
3. Plan and stage animated action

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Integrative case

An animator must bring a short character action to life. They must apply core principles, use timing and spacing to convey weight, and stage and plan the action, defending the use of anticipation, follow-through and planning over unplanned straight-ahead animation.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1875-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) Anticipation in animation is used to:

- A. Prepare the viewer for a coming action **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: anticipation signals and sells the main action.
- B. Increase the file's resolution  
  _Rationale:_ Unrelated to anticipation.
- C. Replace the need for timing  
  _Rationale:_ Timing still applies.
- D. Remove all motion from a scene  
  _Rationale:_ Anticipation adds, not removes, motion.

**MST-1875-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO help convey weight in animation? (Select TWO.)

- A. Varying spacing to show acceleration and deceleration **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: spacing communicates weight and force.
- B. Follow-through and overlapping action **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: parts settle at different rates, showing mass.
- C. Perfectly even spacing on every frame  
  _Rationale:_ Even spacing feels weightless.
- D. No easing anywhere  
  _Rationale:_ Lack of easing reads as mechanical.

**MST-1875-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Thumbnailing an action before animating helps by:

- A. Planning clear staging and key poses in advance **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: planning strengthens readability and efficiency.
- B. Rendering the final frames  
  _Rationale:_ Thumbnails are planning sketches.
- C. Setting the export codec  
  _Rationale:_ Unrelated to planning.
- D. Colouring the character  
  _Rationale:_ Not the purpose of thumbnails.
