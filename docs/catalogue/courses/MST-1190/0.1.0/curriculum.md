## Learning outcomes

1. Explain realistic AI use cases across project delivery
2. Use AI tools and ground their output in project data
3. Use AI to support planning and documentation with review
4. Use AI to support stakeholder communication responsibly
5. Govern AI use for confidentiality, accuracy and risk
6. Lead responsible, ethical AI adoption in delivery

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1190-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What is a realistic expectation for AI in project delivery?

- A. It can assist with drafting and summarising, with human review and accountability **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: AI assists; the PM stays accountable and reviews output.
- B. It can run the project without human oversight  
  _Rationale:_ AI does not replace project leadership or accountability.
- C. It guarantees schedules will be accurate  
  _Rationale:_ AI does not guarantee accuracy.
- D. It removes the need for stakeholder management  
  _Rationale:_ Stakeholder management remains human work.

**MST-1190-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO are good practices when using AI on project documents? (Select TWO.)

- A. Grounding summaries in real project artifacts **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: grounding reduces fabrication.
- B. Reviewing AI drafts before they are shared **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: human review catches errors and tone issues.
- C. Entering client-confidential data into a public tool  
  _Rationale:_ That risks a confidentiality breach.
- D. Sharing an AI-drafted plan with no validation  
  _Rationale:_ Unvalidated plans are a delivery risk.

**MST-1190-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Who is accountable for an AI-assisted status report sent to a client?

- A. The project manager, who remains accountable for the output **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: accountability stays with the human, not the tool.
- B. The AI tool vendor  
  _Rationale:_ The vendor is not accountable for your report.
- C. No one, because AI produced it  
  _Rationale:_ AI involvement does not remove accountability.
- D. The client who receives it  
  _Rationale:_ The recipient is not accountable for its accuracy.
