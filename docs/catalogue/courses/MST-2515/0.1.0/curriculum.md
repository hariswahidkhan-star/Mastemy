## Learning outcomes

1. Explain the definition of fair value and the concept of an exit price
2. Identify the principal or most advantageous market and market participants
3. Apply the fair value hierarchy and classify inputs across its three levels
4. Select an appropriate valuation technique for an asset or liability
5. Apply highest-and-best-use and other concepts for non-financial assets
6. Prepare the disclosures that explain fair value measurements

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2515-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) How does IFRS 13 define fair value?

- A. The price that would be received to sell an asset or paid to transfer a liability in an orderly transaction between market participants at the measurement date **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: fair value is an exit price in an orderly transaction.
- B. The amount the entity originally paid for the asset  
  _Rationale:_ That is historical cost, not fair value.
- C. The amount the entity hopes to receive at some future date  
  _Rationale:_ Fair value is a current exit price, not a hoped-for future amount.
- D. The replacement cost of the asset less tax  
  _Rationale:_ That is not the IFRS 13 definition.

**MST-2515-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO inputs would be classified as Level 1 or Level 2 in the fair value hierarchy? (Select TWO.)

- A. A quoted price in an active market for an identical asset (Level 1) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: an unadjusted quoted price for an identical asset in an active market is Level 1.
- B. A quoted price for a similar asset in an active market (Level 2) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: observable inputs other than Level 1 quoted prices are Level 2.
- C. A management forecast with no observable market data (Level 3)  
  _Rationale:_ An unobservable input is Level 3, not Level 1 or 2.
- D. An internal cash-flow estimate based solely on entity assumptions  
  _Rationale:_ Entity-specific unobservable inputs are Level 3.

**MST-2515-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A company plans to keep a plot of land vacant, but market participants would develop it, which would command a higher price. On what basis is the land's fair value measured?

- A. Its highest and best use by market participants, which is the developed use **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: fair value of a non-financial asset reflects highest and best use by market participants.
- B. The entity's own intended use, leaving it vacant  
  _Rationale:_ Fair value uses market participants' highest and best use, not the entity's intention.
- C. Its original purchase cost  
  _Rationale:_ Historical cost is not fair value.
- D. A value of zero because it is unused  
  _Rationale:_ An unused asset still has a market exit price.
