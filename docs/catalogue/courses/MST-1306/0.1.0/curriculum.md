## Learning outcomes

1. Outline the major eras of AI research
2. Identify landmark systems and why they mattered
3. Explain what caused AI winters and revivals
4. Describe the shift to data-driven and deep learning
5. Place today's AI in historical context

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1306-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What primarily caused the 'AI winters'?

- A. Hype outran results, so funding and confidence collapsed **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: unmet expectations cut funding and interest.
- B. Computers were physically destroyed  
  _Rationale:_ Hardware was not destroyed; expectations failed.
- C. AI was legally banned worldwide  
  _Rationale:_ No global ban occurred.
- D. All researchers retired at once  
  _Rationale:_ Researchers remained; funding and optimism fell.

**MST-1306-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO factors drove the deep learning breakthroughs? (Select TWO.)

- A. Much larger datasets **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: data scale enabled modern models.
- B. Greatly increased computing power **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: GPUs and compute were pivotal.
- C. Abandoning all mathematics  
  _Rationale:_ Deep learning relies heavily on mathematics.
- D. Removing data from training  
  _Rationale:_ Data is essential, not removed.

**MST-1306-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) Why does knowing AI's history matter for today's decisions?

- A. It tempers hype by showing past cycles of over-promising **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: history guards against repeating hype mistakes.
- B. It proves AI will always fail  
  _Rationale:_ History shows revivals too, not only failures.
- C. It makes current tools obsolete  
  _Rationale:_ History does not make tools obsolete.
- D. It is irrelevant to modern systems  
  _Rationale:_ Context informs realistic expectations.
