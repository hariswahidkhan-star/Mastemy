## Integrative case

A company rolls out a new expense system and six weeks later half the staff still email spreadsheets. Apply change management: diagnose the human-side failures, choose a suitable model, understand and address resistance, communicate the 'why' effectively, and build reinforcement so the new way is actually adopted.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1754-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A well-designed new system is rejected by staff who keep using the old method. What does this most illustrate?

- A. Adoption depends on the human side of change, not just the solution's quality **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: technical excellence does not guarantee people will change behaviour.
- B. The system must be technically defective  
  _Rationale:_ The scenario states it is well designed; the gap is adoption.
- C. Resistance is always irrational  
  _Rationale:_ Resistance often has legitimate, addressable causes.
- D. Communication is never necessary  
  _Rationale:_ The failure points to a need for engagement, not less of it.

**MST-1754-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which actions help address resistance to change constructively? (Select TWO)

- A. Understanding the specific concerns behind the resistance **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: diagnosing the cause lets you respond to real worries.
- B. Engaging influential stakeholders early **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: early engagement builds advocates and surfaces issues.
- C. Dismissing resisters as simply obstructive  
  _Rationale:_ This ignores legitimate concerns and hardens opposition.
- D. Withholding information to avoid pushback  
  _Rationale:_ Secrecy increases distrust and resistance.

**MST-1754-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A project team celebrates 'success' at the go-live date. Why might this be premature?

- A. Change is successful only when the new way is actually adopted and sustained **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: go-live is a milestone; adoption and reinforcement follow.
- B. Go-live always equals full adoption  
  _Rationale:_ Go-live is availability, not behaviour change.
- C. Celebrations are forbidden in change projects  
  _Rationale:_ Recognition is fine; the issue is measuring success too early.
- D. Adoption cannot be measured  
  _Rationale:_ Adoption can be tracked with usage and outcome metrics.
