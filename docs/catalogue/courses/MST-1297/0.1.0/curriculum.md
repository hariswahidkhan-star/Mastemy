## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-1297-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) What primarily distinguishes the WELL Building Standard from LEED?

- A. WELL focuses on occupant health and well-being; LEED focuses on building environmental performance **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: WELL centres on human health and well-being, while LEED centres on environmental/sustainability performance.
- B. They measure exactly the same things  
  _Rationale:_ Their focus areas differ; they are complementary, not identical.
- C. WELL only applies to factories  
  _Rationale:_ WELL applies across many building and space types.
- D. LEED measures only lighting  
  _Rationale:_ LEED covers many environmental categories, not only lighting.

**MST-1297-Q0002** (single-answer, Select ONE) In WELL, what is the relationship between preconditions and optimisations?

- A. Preconditions are required for certification; optimisations are optional and add points toward higher levels **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: preconditions are mandatory, and optimisations are optional features that contribute to higher certification levels.
- B. Optimisations are mandatory and preconditions are optional  
  _Rationale:_ This reverses the definitions.
- C. Both are optional  
  _Rationale:_ Preconditions are mandatory, not optional.
- D. Neither affects certification  
  _Rationale:_ Both are central to achieving and raising certification.

**MST-1297-Q0003** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Select TWO statements that are TRUE about achieving WELL certification.

- A. Performance verification (on-site testing/review) is part of the process **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: WELL uses performance verification to confirm features perform as intended.
- B. All applicable preconditions must be met before certification is awarded **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: preconditions are mandatory for certification.
- C. Design intent alone is sufficient, with no verification  
  _Rationale:_ WELL requires verification, not design intent alone.
- D. Optimisations are mandatory for a basic certification  
  _Rationale:_ Optimisations are optional and add points; preconditions are the mandatory part.
- E. Documentation is unnecessary for WELL features  
  _Rationale:_ Features must be documented to be credited.
