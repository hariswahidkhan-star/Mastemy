1. Explain and apply the in-scope objectives of 'The Test Analyst's tasks in the test process' as published by ISTQB for the stated exam version.
2. Explain and apply the in-scope objectives of 'Risk-based testing for the Test Analyst' as published by ISTQB for the stated exam version.
3. Explain and apply the in-scope objectives of 'Test analysis and test design' as published by ISTQB for the stated exam version.
4. Explain and apply the in-scope objectives of 'Test quality characteristics' as published by ISTQB for the stated exam version.
5. Explain and apply the in-scope objectives of 'Software defect prevention' as published by ISTQB for the stated exam version.
6. Explain and apply the in-scope objectives of 'Test tools and automation' as published by ISTQB for the stated exam version.
