## Learning outcomes

1. Read and notate pitch and rhythm in standard notation
2. Build scales, intervals and key signatures across major and minor keys
3. Construct and label diatonic chords and common progressions
4. Analyse and write voice-leading and simple harmonic progressions
5. Shape melody, phrase and form in a short composition
6. Compose and notate a short original piece applying these tools

## What this course assesses / does not assess

- Assesses: knowledge and applied reasoning on every in-scope outcome, using single-answer MCQ and multiple-answer selection only.
- Does not assess: Assesses knowledge and applied reasoning through selection items; does not assess hands-on performance.

## Sample items (original, draft, unreviewed)

**MST-2430-Q0001** (single-answer, Select ONE) A major scale is built with the sequence of intervals:

- A. Whole, whole, half, whole, whole, whole, half **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: W-W-H-W-W-W-H defines the major scale.
- B. Whole, half, whole, whole, half, whole, whole  
  _Rationale:_ That is a minor-type pattern, not major.
- C. All whole steps  
  _Rationale:_ That is a whole-tone scale.
- D. All half steps  
  _Rationale:_ That is a chromatic scale.

**MST-2430-Q0002** (multiple-answer, Select TWO) Which TWO chords are the dominant and subdominant triads in the key of C major? (Select TWO.)

- A. G major (the V chord) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: G is the dominant (V) in C major.
- B. F major (the IV chord) **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: F is the subdominant (IV) in C major.
- C. C major (the I chord)  
  _Rationale:_ C is the tonic, not dominant or subdominant.
- D. A minor (the vi chord)  
  _Rationale:_ A minor is the submediant, not those functions.

**MST-2430-Q0003** (single-answer, Select ONE) A perfect authentic cadence most strongly creates a sense of resolution because it moves:

- A. From the dominant (V) to the tonic (I) with the tonic in the melody and bass **(key)**  
  _Rationale:_ Correct: V-I with tonic on top gives the strongest closure.
- B. From IV to V  
  _Rationale:_ That is a half-cadence feel, not full resolution.
- C. From I to IV  
  _Rationale:_ That moves away from, not toward, rest.
- D. From vi to iii  
  _Rationale:_ That is not a strong closing cadence.
