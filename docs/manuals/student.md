# Student Manual

## Account
Register with email and password, then sign in. Switch language (English/العربية) and light/dark from the header.

## Finding and watching courses
Browse by the 14 subject domains. Course videos play from YouTube and are free to watch; you do not need to buy anything to watch available videos. Ads or YouTube recommendations may appear and are controlled by YouTube.

## Notes
Open the notes panel beside the player. Add a private note: it records the current video timestamp. Click a note's timestamp to jump back. Only you can see your notes. Instructor study notes are shown separately.

## Practice and exams
- Practice mode may show explanations after each question.
- Exam mode has a server-side deadline; the timer continues if you disconnect, and answers submitted after the deadline are not accepted. Results and explanations appear at the review point.
- Multiple-select questions are labelled; read the scoring rule before starting.

## Paid packages
Packages add services such as advanced MCQ banks, mock exams and study packs. Paying is via Stripe checkout; access is granted once payment is confirmed by the server (not on redirect). Refunds or expiry remove package services only, never video access.

## Certificates
Earned by meeting the approved MCQ pass threshold, not by watch time. Each certificate has an ID that anyone can check on the Certificate Verification page. A certificate shows knowledge assessed by MCQs; it is not a professional licence or external credential.
