# Instructor Manual

External instructor registration is off at launch; internal and invited authors use the studio.

## Create a course
1. Studio → New course: title, domain, audience, prerequisites, outcomes.
2. Add modules and lessons; reorder as needed.
3. For each lesson, paste the YouTube link or video ID of a video already uploaded to the approved channel (see `docs/youtube-operations.md`). Mastemy validates the link and shows video status. A video must be Ready before publication; private-only or embedding-disabled videos are not.
4. Write lesson notes (study material for learners).

## Questions
- Add SingleChoice or MultipleSelect questions with an explanation for every option.
- Bulk import: download `templates/mcq-import-template.csv`, fill it (UTF-8), upload, review the preview and row errors, then commit. Nothing is saved until commit, and a file with errors is not partially imported.
- Editing a published question creates a new version; past attempts keep the version they used.

## Review and publishing
Submit for review (Draft → In Review). A reviewer approves or requests changes; you cannot approve your own course. Admin publishes. To change a published course it moves to Updating.

## Rules
Videos must be free to watch. Do not ask learners to like/subscribe. Use only original or licensed content; no exam dumps. Keep your original video files; Mastemy does not store them.

## Earnings
Earnings come from paid packages per the commission ledger, never from YouTube views.
