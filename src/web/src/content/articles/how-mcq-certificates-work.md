Some Mastemy courses end with an assessment that counts toward a certificate. This article explains how that certificate is earned, how anyone can check it, and, just as important, what it does **not** say about its holder.

## How a certificate is earned

1. The course includes one or more assessments that the instructor marked as counting toward the certificate.
2. Those assessments are built from multiple-choice questions. Questions go through review before they become active, and every option carries an explanation shown after the attempt.
3. The learner takes the assessment. Timed exams use the server's clock, and grading happens on the server, not in the browser.
4. If the score meets the pass threshold set for the assessment, Mastemy issues a certificate with a unique code.

## How anyone can verify it

Each certificate has a code. Anyone, such as an employer, can enter it on the [verification page](/verify) to see whether it was issued by Mastemy, who it was issued to, for which course, when, and whether it has since been revoked.

## What a certificate proves

A valid Mastemy certificate shows that its holder **passed an MCQ knowledge assessment** for a specific course on a specific date.

## What it does not prove

- It does **not** verify practical, hands-on or professional competence. Multiple-choice questions test knowledge and reasoning, not the ability to carry out a task in a real setting.
- It is **not** an official certification from any exam body, professional association or government. Where a Mastemy course prepares for an external certification, passing the Mastemy assessment does not grant that certification.
- It does **not** record how much of the course video was watched. Watch progress is saved only as a convenience and never affects certificates.

## Why we are explicit about this

A credential is only useful if people can trust what it means. By stating the limits plainly on the certificate, the verification page and here, we hope employers and learners can give a Mastemy certificate exactly the weight it deserves, no more and no less.
