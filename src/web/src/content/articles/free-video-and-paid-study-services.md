Mastemy is built on one simple rule: **lesson videos are always free to watch**. Paying for something on Mastemy never unlocks a video, and no video is ever hidden behind a login.

## The free part

Every published course on Mastemy is made of lessons, and each lesson's video plays through YouTube's official embedded player. That means:

- You can open any lesson of a published course and watch it without an account and without paying.
- Videos stay on YouTube and remain subject to YouTube's terms. Mastemy does not control the advertisements or recommendations YouTube shows.
- If a video cannot play in the embedded player (for example because its owner disabled embedding), the lesson page explains why and offers a "Watch on YouTube" link.

Instructors can also publish **free study notes** for a lesson. Those sit next to the video and do not require a purchase.

## The paid part: study services

Instructors may propose **study packages** for a course. A package sells original study services that Mastemy hosts, such as:

- premium study notes written for the course,
- larger or more advanced banks of multiple-choice practice questions,
- mock or timed exams.

Each package lists its included services in plain language on the course page, together with its price and access period. Packages are reviewed by Mastemy staff before they go on sale, and reviewers check that a package does not sell or unlock video access.

## What an account adds

A free account lets you keep private notes on lessons, save your watch position, take practice questions and track your progress. None of this changes whether you can watch the video.

## Why we work this way

Video teaching is most useful when anyone can reach it. The study services are where Mastemy and its instructors add work that goes beyond the video: structured notes, reviewed questions with explanations for every option, and assessments. Keeping the two apart lets you judge a course by watching it first, and decide later whether the extra study services are worth it to you.
