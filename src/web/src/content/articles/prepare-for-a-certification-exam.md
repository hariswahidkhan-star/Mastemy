Many learners use Mastemy to study for an external certification exam. Mastemy is **not** the exam provider and does not claim any partnership with, or endorsement by, the bodies that run these exams. This guide explains how to use what Mastemy does offer.

## 1. Start from the official source

Open the certification in the [certification directory](/certifications). Each entry shows the issuer, exam code, version, the date Mastemy staff last checked the details, and a link to the **official source**. Always read the official page first: it is the authority on eligibility, fees, exam format and the current syllabus. If the official information differs from what you see on Mastemy, the official information is correct.

Entries are only listed publicly after a reviewer has checked them recently. If an entry has not been re-checked for too long, it is removed from the public directory until someone checks it again.

## 2. Read the blueprint and any non-MCQ disclosure

Where available, the entry lists the exam's objectives with their weights. Use the weights to plan your time. If the real exam includes tasks that are not multiple-choice (such as practical labs, essays or simulations), the entry says so. Mastemy's practice is multiple-choice, so plan separate practice for those parts.

## 3. Choose a preparation course

The entry lists live Mastemy courses mapped to that certification. Every lesson video in those courses is free to watch. Work through the lessons in the order the course suggests and take notes as you go.

## 4. Practise actively

Use the practice questions attached to lessons and modules. Read the explanation for every option, including the ones you got right. Come back to topics where you were unsure.

## 5. Check yourself under exam conditions

If the course offers timed exams, take one only after you have finished the material, and treat the result as a signal of which objectives to revisit, not as a prediction of your real exam score.

## 6. Keep the limits in mind

- Passing a Mastemy assessment does not grant the external certification.
- Exam syllabi change. Check the "last checked" date and the official source shortly before you book your exam.
- A Mastemy certificate confirms a passed MCQ knowledge assessment only. See [what our certificates do and don't prove](/articles/how-mcq-certificates-work).
